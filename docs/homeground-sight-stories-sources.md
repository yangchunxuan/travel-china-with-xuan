# Sight stories: sources

The writing in `lib/sightStories.ts` keeps to facts that do not date quickly,
such as history, sizes and names. Hours, prices and booking steps belong to
the reservation rules and the Travel Advice guides. Each fact below names the
source it was checked against.

Judgments are Homeground's own and are written as advice. Examples: when to
arrive, what to skip, and how long to give a sight.

Checked on 2026-10-04.

## Forbidden City

| Fact | Source |
| --- | --- |
| The palace was finished in 1420. 24 emperors (14 Ming, 10 Qing) ruled from it until the 1912 abdication. | [Wikipedia: Forbidden City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbidden_City); [Palace Museum](https://www.dpm.org.cn/) |
| It covers 720,000 m² (72 ha). Rooms: "over 9,000" is the usual official figure, and a 1973 survey counted 8,704. We write "close to 9,000". | [China Daily, 2023](https://china.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202301/28/WS63d4ff21a3102ada8b22cc9d.html); [Baidu Baike: 北京故宫](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E5%8C%97%E4%BA%AC%E6%95%85%E5%AE%AB/345415) |
| The Hall of Supreme Harmony is the largest hall in the palace and was used for enthronements and imperial weddings. Its roof carries ten beasts behind the immortal on a phoenix, the only building with ten. | [Wikipedia: Hall of Supreme Harmony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hall_of_Supreme_Harmony); [Wikipedia: roof decorations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_roof_decoration) |
| The Ningshou Palace area was rebuilt by Qianlong (1771–1776) for his retirement and now houses the Treasure Gallery. The Nine Dragon Screen (1771) is 29.4 m long and made of glazed tiles. | [Wikipedia: Nine-Dragon Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine-Dragon_Wall); [Palace Museum](https://www.dpm.org.cn/) |
| Visitors enter at the Meridian Gate and leave by the north gate (Shenwumen) or the east gate. | `content/guides/forbidden-city-for-foreign-visitors/source-log.md` |
| Jingshan Park faces Shenwumen across the road. Its top pavilion, Wanchun Ting, is the classic viewpoint over the palace. | [Wikipedia: Jingshan Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jingshan_Park); [Beijing Bendibao](http://bj.bendibao.com/tour/2023112/353288.shtm) |
| The Treasure Gallery is ticketed separately. | `forbidden-city-for-foreign-visitors` guide ("individual galleries may require additional ticket") |
| Zijincheng: 紫 stands for the Pole Star (the emperor of heaven's seat), and 禁 for forbidden. Puyi lived in the inner court until he was evicted in 1924, and the Palace Museum opened in 1925. | [Wikipedia: Forbidden City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbidden_City) |
| Courtyards widen up to the square below the Hall of Supreme Harmony (about 30,000 m², the largest in the palace, with bronze rank markers for officials) and narrow after the Gate of Heavenly Purity (乾清门). | [Baidu Baike: 太和殿前广场](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E5%A4%AA%E5%92%8C%E6%AE%BF%E5%89%8D%E5%B9%BF%E5%9C%BA/5986850); [Baidu Baike: 品级山](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E5%93%81%E7%BA%A7%E5%B1%B1/10895622) |
| The tenth roof beast, hangshi (行什), is monkey-faced and winged and appears only on the Hall of Supreme Harmony. | [Palace Museum lemma](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/241413.html) |
| Qianlong rebuilt the Ningshou courts for his retirement but never moved in. The jade mountain "Yu Taming the Flood" (about 5,350 kg, 2.24 m tall), the largest jade carving in the palace, stands in the Hall of Joyful Longevity (乐寿堂). | [World Monuments Fund: Qianlong Garden](https://www.wmf.org/monuments/qianlong-garden); [Palace Museum: 乐寿堂](https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236470.html); [Baidu Baike: 大禹治水图玉山](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E6%B8%85%E5%A4%A7%E7%A6%B9%E6%B2%BB%E6%B0%B4%E5%9B%BE%E7%8E%89%E5%B1%B1/1548501) |
| The Treasure Gallery is ticketed separately, and the guide says to secure it before the day. | [Palace Museum notice](https://www.dpm.org.cn/singles_detail/257830.html); `forbidden-city-for-foreign-visitors` guide |
| For slower walkers, use the museum's two-hour route and don't add Jingshan automatically. | `forbidden-city-for-foreign-visitors` guide (parents callout, route cuts) |
| 65–75% of visitors enter between 08:30 and 10:30, so the entrance is busiest at opening; inside, the halls are busiest from about 10:00 to the early afternoon. Cixi lived in the Hall of Joyful Longevity from 1894, so the courts were used, though not by Qianlong. | [Beijing government, 2023](https://www.beijing.gov.cn/renwen/cshd/202310/t20231025_3286234.html); [Palace Museum: 乐寿堂](https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236470.html) |
| Korean: Gyeonghoeru has 11 japsang (the most in Korea) and Geunjeongjeon has 7. Counting the lead figure, the Hall of Supreme Harmony also has 11. | [Namu wiki: 경회루](https://namu.wiki/w/%EA%B2%BD%EB%B3%B5%EA%B6%81%20%EA%B2%BD%ED%9A%8C%EB%A3%A8); [OhmyNews](https://www.ohmynews.com/NWS_Web/View/at_pg.aspx?CNTN_CD=A0000880174) |

## West Lake

| Fact | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Inscribed in 2011 as the "West Lake Cultural Landscape of Hangzhou". UNESCO says it influenced garden design in China, Japan and Korea. | [UNESCO 1334](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1334/); [ICOMOS evaluation](https://whc.unesco.org/document/152404) |
| The lakeside is open and free; the shore is about 15 km. The southern parks opened free in 2002. Some sites inside charge. | [Hangzhou government, 2025](https://www.hangzhou.gov.cn/art/2025/6/24/art_812270_59114316.html); [Wikipedia (zh): 西湖](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E8%A5%BF%E6%B9%96) |
| Su Causeway was built in 1090, when Su Shi governed Hangzhou, from dredged lake mud. It is nearly 3 km long and has six arched bridges. | [West Lake Administration: 苏轼筑堤](https://westlake.hangzhou.gov.cn/art/2022/7/8/art_1639430_59037825.html); [The Paper: 苏堤六桥](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_19443734) |
| Three Pools Mirroring the Moon has three stone pagodas in the water. The island is reached by boat. The view is on the back of the 1-yuan note (5th series). | [Wikipedia (zh): 西湖](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E8%A5%BF%E6%B9%96); [Hangzhou Bendibao](https://hz.bendibao.com/tour/201393/ly37400_5.shtm) |
| Leifeng Pagoda was built in 977 and collapsed in 1924. The new pagoda was finished in October 2002 over the old site; the ruins of the old base are shown inside. | [Wikipedia (zh): 雷峰塔](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E9%9B%B7%E5%B3%B0%E5%A1%94); [Urban China: 重建雷峰塔](http://www.urbanchina.org/content/content_7709546.html) |
| Bai Juyi (governor 822–824) built a dyke that is long gone; the Bai Causeway predates him. Su Shi built the Su Causeway in 1090. The Ten Scenes were named in the Southern Song (13th century), and Kangxi changed two of the names in 1699 ([Wikipedia (zh): 西湖十景](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E8%A5%BF%E6%B9%96%E5%8D%81%E6%99%AF); [Hangzhou News, 2024](https://hznews.hangzhou.com.cn/chengshi/content/2024-03/23/content_8705401_0.htm)). UNESCO notes its influence on Qing imperial gardens. Qianlong modelled the West Causeway of the Summer Palace on the Su Causeway. | [ICOMOS evaluation](https://whc.unesco.org/document/152404); [Travel China Guide: Summer Palace West Causeway](https://www.travelchinaguide.com/attraction/beijing/summer/western-bank.htm) |
| From 2002 Hangzhou took down the lakeside park fences and stopped charging ("还湖于民"); by October 2003 the lakeside parks were free day and night. | [Tide News](https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=2505269); [Hangzhou News, 2012](https://hznews.hangzhou.com.cn/chengshi/content/2012-10/08/content_4413060_2.htm) |
| Hills enclose three sides of the lake, with the city on the fourth. | [UNESCO 1334](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1334/) |
| Leifeng fell on 25 September 1924, partly because people had long been taking bricks as charms (see Lu Xun's essay). | [Wikipedia (zh): 雷峰塔](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E9%9B%B7%E5%B3%B0%E5%A1%94); [Hangzhou News, 2024](https://hznews.hangzhou.com.cn/wenti/content/2024-09/26/content_8792570_0.htm) |
| Longjing village lies in the hills south of Lingyin, on Longjing Road back towards the lake. | Map check (Longjing Road from Lingyin to Manjuelong) |

## Hongyadong

| Fact | Source |
| --- | --- |
| It was built in 2006 in the style of Chongqing's stilt houses, with 11 storeys on the Jialing River cliff. Floor 1 opens onto Jiabin Road by the river and floor 11 onto Cangbai Road. | [Wikipedia (zh): 洪崖洞民俗风貌区](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E6%B4%AA%E5%B4%96%E6%B4%9E%E6%B0%91%E4%BF%97%E9%A3%8E%E8%B2%8C%E5%8C%BA); [Chongqing housing commission, 2025](https://zfcxjw.cq.gov.cn/cqcjdag/csjy/202504/t20250416_14526589.html) |
| Inside are food, souvenir and bar streets. | [Wikipedia (zh): 洪崖洞民俗风貌区](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E6%B4%AA%E5%B4%96%E6%B4%9E%E6%B0%91%E4%BF%97%E9%A3%8E%E8%B2%8C%E5%8C%BA) |
| The best views are from the middle of the Qiansimen Bridge footpath, the north bank across the Jialing, and the road below, the angle compared with *Spirited Away*. | [Chongqing Bendibao](https://m.cq.bendibao.com/tour/77329.shtm); [Baidu Jingyan](https://jingyan.baidu.com/article/3d69c5517a67efb1ce02d74f.html) |
| Chaotianmen is where the Jialing meets the Yangtze; the two-river night cruises leave from there. Jiefangbei is uphill nearby. | [Wikipedia: Chaotianmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaotianmen) |
| The complex opened on 29 September 2006 and takes its name from the Hongya cave in this cliff. Where the original cave was is disputed (a 1960s witness places it below the old cableway station), so the story says only that the name comes from it. | ([Chongqing civil affairs bureau, 2024](https://mzj.cq.gov.cn/sy_218/bmdt/mzyw/202401/t20240119_12841031.html)) | [Wikipedia (zh): 洪崖洞民俗风貌区](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E6%B4%AA%E5%B4%96%E6%B4%9E%E6%B0%91%E4%BF%97%E9%A3%8E%E8%B2%8C%E5%8C%BA) |
| Walking across Qiansimen Bridge is about 800 m. | [Chongqing Bendibao](https://m.cq.bendibao.com/tour/76191.shtm) |
| The lights follow a fixed schedule (around 19:00 in winter, later in summer) that has changed more than once. | [Chongqing Bendibao](http://cq.bendibao.com/tour/2019419/76188.shtm); [NetEase](https://c.m.163.com/news/a/JV5DMQLG05565JWF.html) |
| A riverside path to Chaotianmen (2.4 km, "20-odd minutes") opened in December 2025, with the Hongyadong section then closed for works, so the story says only "about twenty minutes along the river". The Jialing is usually clearer than the Yangtze, but the colours can swap after heavy rain upstream, hence "usually". | [Xinhua Chongqing](http://www.cq.xinhuanet.com/20251208/9eec344fd4cc4aeb89694374416a65ce/c.html); [China Daily](https://tech.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202306/14/WS64894110a310dbde06d234ca.html); [Chongqing government](https://www.cq.gov.cn/zt/yyztls/lsjcx/202512/t20251205_15213290.html) |
| Chongqing is called the "8D city" (Trip.com KR: 충칭 8D 매직 시티). | [Trip.com KR](https://kr.trip.com/hotspoi/8d-magic-city-of-chongqing-150025879) |
