# Sight stories: sources

The writing in `lib/sightStories.ts` keeps to facts that do not date quickly,
such as history, sizes and names. Hours, prices and booking steps belong to
the reservation rules and the Travel Advice guides. Each fact below names the
source it was checked against.

Judgments are Homeground's own and are written as advice. Examples: when to
arrive, what to skip, and how long to give a sight.

Checked on 2026-10-04.

## Forbidden City

| Fact | Source |
| --- | --- |
| The palace was finished in 1420. 24 emperors (14 Ming, 10 Qing) ruled from it until the 1912 abdication. | [Wikipedia: Forbidden City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbidden_City); [Palace Museum](https://www.dpm.org.cn/) |
| It covers 720,000 m² (72 ha). Rooms: "over 9,000" is the usual official figure, and a 1973 survey counted 8,704. We write "close to 9,000". | [China Daily, 2023](https://china.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202301/28/WS63d4ff21a3102ada8b22cc9d.html); [Baidu Baike: 北京故宫](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E5%8C%97%E4%BA%AC%E6%95%85%E5%AE%AB/345415) |
| The Hall of Supreme Harmony is the largest hall in the palace and was used for enthronements and imperial weddings. Its roof carries ten beasts behind the immortal on a phoenix, the only building with ten. | [Wikipedia: Hall of Supreme Harmony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hall_of_Supreme_Harmony); [Wikipedia: roof decorations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_roof_decoration) |
| The Ningshou Palace area was rebuilt by Qianlong (1771–1776) for his retirement and now houses the Treasure Gallery. The Nine Dragon Screen (1771) is 29.4 m long and made of glazed tiles. | [Wikipedia: Nine-Dragon Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine-Dragon_Wall); [Palace Museum](https://www.dpm.org.cn/) |
| Visitors enter at the Meridian Gate and leave by the north gate (Shenwumen) or the east gate. | `content/guides/forbidden-city-for-foreign-visitors/source-log.md` |
| Jingshan Park faces Shenwumen across the road. Its top pavilion, Wanchun Ting, is the classic viewpoint over the palace. | [Wikipedia: Jingshan Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jingshan_Park); [Beijing Bendibao](http://bj.bendibao.com/tour/2023112/353288.shtm) |
| The Treasure Gallery is ticketed separately. | `forbidden-city-for-foreign-visitors` guide ("individual galleries may require additional ticket") |

## West Lake

| Fact | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Inscribed in 2011 as the "West Lake Cultural Landscape of Hangzhou". UNESCO says it influenced garden design in China, Japan and Korea. | [UNESCO 1334](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1334/); [ICOMOS evaluation](https://whc.unesco.org/document/152404) |
| The lakeside is open and free; the shore is about 15 km. The southern parks opened free in 2002. Some sites inside charge. | [Hangzhou government, 2025](https://www.hangzhou.gov.cn/art/2025/6/24/art_812270_59114316.html); [Wikipedia (zh): 西湖](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E8%A5%BF%E6%B9%96) |
| Su Causeway was built in 1090, when Su Shi governed Hangzhou, from dredged lake mud. It is nearly 3 km long and has six arched bridges. | [West Lake Administration: 苏轼筑堤](https://westlake.hangzhou.gov.cn/art/2022/7/8/art_1639430_59037825.html); [The Paper: 苏堤六桥](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_19443734) |
| Three Pools Mirroring the Moon has three stone pagodas in the water. The island is reached by boat. The view is on the back of the 1-yuan note (5th series). | [Wikipedia (zh): 西湖](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E8%A5%BF%E6%B9%96); [Hangzhou Bendibao](https://hz.bendibao.com/tour/201393/ly37400_5.shtm) |
| Leifeng Pagoda was built in 977 and collapsed in 1924. The new pagoda was finished in October 2002 over the old site; the ruins of the old base are shown inside. | [Wikipedia (zh): 雷峰塔](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E9%9B%B7%E5%B3%B0%E5%A1%94); [Urban China: 重建雷峰塔](http://www.urbanchina.org/content/content_7709546.html) |

## Hongyadong

| Fact | Source |
| --- | --- |
| It was built in 2006 in the style of Chongqing's stilt houses, with 11 storeys on the Jialing River cliff. Floor 1 opens onto Jiabin Road by the river and floor 11 onto Cangbai Road. | [Wikipedia (zh): 洪崖洞民俗风貌区](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E6%B4%AA%E5%B4%96%E6%B4%9E%E6%B0%91%E4%BF%97%E9%A3%8E%E8%B2%8C%E5%8C%BA); [Chongqing housing commission, 2025](https://zfcxjw.cq.gov.cn/cqcjdag/csjy/202504/t20250416_14526589.html) |
| Inside are food, souvenir and bar streets. | [Wikipedia (zh): 洪崖洞民俗风貌区](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E6%B4%AA%E5%B4%96%E6%B4%9E%E6%B0%91%E4%BF%97%E9%A3%8E%E8%B2%8C%E5%8C%BA) |
| The best views are from the middle of the Qiansimen Bridge footpath, the north bank across the Jialing, and the road below, the angle compared with *Spirited Away*. | [Chongqing Bendibao](https://m.cq.bendibao.com/tour/77329.shtm); [Baidu Jingyan](https://jingyan.baidu.com/article/3d69c5517a67efb1ce02d74f.html) |
| Chaotianmen is where the Jialing meets the Yangtze; the two-river night cruises leave from there. Jiefangbei is uphill nearby. | [Wikipedia: Chaotianmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaotianmen) |
