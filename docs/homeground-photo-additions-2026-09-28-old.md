## Processing and scene checks

- All outputs were made with Sharp: EXIF orientation applied, center crop to 1600 × 1000, WebP quality 84 and effort 5. The three outputs are 528,226, 438,916 and 170,802 bytes, respectively.
- Xi'an: The Commons file shows Small Wild Goose Pagoda in the Xi'an Museum heritage area, which is named in the D4 itinerary. Its 1920 × 1280 official Wikimedia thumbnail was used because the original file request returned HTTP 429. Commons API original-file SHA-1: `b0997d9618a04da552f7a6972d79d6432d6b1e21`; this is not the SHA-256 of the downloaded thumbnail. File-page photographer and CC0 status were confirmed, including Commons' Flickr license review. The photo was taken in 2011, so it should not be presented as a current operating or access condition.
- Wulong: The source depicts a Fairy Mountain meadow, distinct from the existing Wulong canyon photo. Its original 3008 × 2000 JPEG was used. Commons API original-file SHA-1: `0eaa6cac45d7345356a9af12c2541bb84ec55228`. The image represents **one possible D4 landscape**; the customer has not selected Fairy Mountain over Furong Cave. Suggested caption: “Fairy Mountain in Wulong shows one possible Day 4 landscape. The included Fairy Mountain or Furong Cave option will be stated in your written confirmation.” In Chinese: “武隆仙女山是第 4 天可能看到的一种景观；实际包含仙女山还是芙蓉洞，以书面确认单为准。”
- Guilin: The source shows Guilin city and its surrounding karst peaks from Solitary Beauty Peak, different from the product's existing Li River, Yulong River, Elephant Trunk Hill and Sun and Moon Pagodas photos. Its original 4290 × 2860 JPEG was used. Commons API original-file SHA-1: `bb21c2310b440f89286a5d50779afbe60f16f019`. Suggested caption: “A view of Guilin for departure-day context; no sightseeing is scheduled on Day 5.” This viewpoint is illustrative and is not an included stop.
