## Scope and method

This batch owns six narrow Destination / Explore China decisions. It does not own a generic city guide, accommodation-area comparison, pure transport execution, multi-city itinerary order or a list of attractions. Candidate discovery checked current Google results, visible People Also Ask and related-search language, current traveller questions, `origin/main`, unmerged `origin/article/*` and `origin/codex/*` guide identities, Search Map reservations and current official-source readiness. Search-result observations are demand and competition evidence, not traffic-volume claims.

The six selected pages must independently answer the landing task, state their canonical exclusions, separate stable geography from dynamic operations, provide failure recovery and use only accurate, rights-cleared real-location images or clearly labelled editorial diagrams.

## Shared canonical exclusions

- No page targets “X Travel Guide”, “best things to do in X” or a generic first-trip city overview.
- No page chooses accommodation areas, gives door-to-door public-transport execution, orders a multi-city itinerary or sells a fixed product.
- Live hours, fares, inventory, weather operation, temporary closure and foreign-document workflows are included only when a current primary source supports them; otherwise the page gives a source-of-record and a recovery decision.
- Traveller posts establish question wording and unresolved friction only. They never establish operating facts.
- Translations share one canonical identity; EN/ZH/KO block ID, type, order, numerical facts and conclusions must match.
