## Fact levels

- **Independent interpretation:** museum and academic sources support viewing face-changing as one element within a larger actor-centred form.

## Search/user-question findings

Current results often sell “face-changing shows” without identifying a troupe, play or excerpt. Traveller discussions ask whether such a show counts as Sichuan opera, whether an evening is understandable without Chinese, and whether a tourism format is necessarily inauthentic. The packet answers by separating format from evidence: a mixed visitor programme can contain skilled, identifiable Sichuan-opera work, while a prestigious building or dramatic mask photograph alone proves little.
