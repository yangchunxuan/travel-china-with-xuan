## Hero

- Subject: a real working tea landscape where land form, tea plants and human-scale access can all be identified; preferred target is a Jingmai old tea forest with canopy and path visible, or a Wuyi village tea garden where the exact garden is documented.
- Required record: exact property/village/garden, GPS or management-unit identity, capture date, photographer, who authorized access, licence and permitted crops.
- Crop: 1600 × 1000, leaving a safe central landscape band. Do not crop a no-derivatives institutional image.
- EN alt: “Tea trees growing beneath a taller forest canopy in a documented old tea forest on Jingmai Mountain.”
- ZH alt: “景迈山一处有明确记录的古茶林，茶树生长在较高林冠之下。”
- KO alt: “징마이산의 기록된 오래된 차숲에서 차나무가 높은 수관 아래 자라는 모습.”

- No shop display as evidence of origin.
- No AI documentary landscape, staged “tea farmer,” scraped social post or unlicensed UNESCO/ICH slideshow image.
- Do not identify a tea variety, processing stage or historic building from appearance alone.
