## Fact levels

- **Official/institutional fact:** inscription dates, named heritage systems, documented landscape components and described processing stages.
- **Independent cross-check:** UNESCO and FAO are used to test local claims about Jingmai’s landscape ecology and management; no unverified economic-impact claim is carried into the copy.
- **Local or community interpretation:** Tea Ancestor beliefs and community governance are attributed specifically to the Jingmai World Heritage documentation, not generalized to Yunnan or China.
- **Homeground editorial judgment:** the four-region choice matrix, the “map → plant → process → cup → label” field sequence and recovery advice.

## Dynamic facts
