## Working vocabulary for integration

- **Surviving historic fabric / 原存、遗存 / 현존 역사 재료:** material or structure documented as physically surviving from an earlier period; may still include later maintenance.
- **Conservation repair / 修缮、保护性维修 / 보수·보존 수리:** intervention intended to stabilize or maintain historic fabric; new compatible material may be present.
- **Reconstruction / 复建、重建 / 복원 건립·재건:** a lost element built again, sometimes on its former site and using records; silhouette or location does not make the material old.
- **Archaeological trace / 遗址、考古遗存 / 유적·고고학 흔적:** buried, fragmentary or exposed evidence used to understand a former wall, gate or road.
- **Interpretive addition / 展示、标识 / 해설 시설:** modern walkway, lighting, model, paving or outline that makes evidence legible but is not the historic structure itself.

## Fact levels

- **Official fact:** named protected components, documented repair/reconstruction dates and identified surviving fragments.
- **Scholarly interpretation:** gates helped order access and social space; the importance and mechanism vary by city, period and evidence.
- **Homeground editorial judgment:** the five-label material key, outside-to-inside walking sequence and photo-note method.
- **Prohibited inference:** colour, neat brickwork or a traditional silhouette cannot date fabric without a conservation or archaeological record.
