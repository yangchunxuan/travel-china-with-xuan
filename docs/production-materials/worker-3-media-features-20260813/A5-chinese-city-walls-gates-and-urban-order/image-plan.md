# Image plan — A5 `chinese-city-walls-gates-and-urban-order`

Status: **ASSETS NEEDED — no photograph is included or cleared.**

## Hero

- Subject: a documented Nanjing wall section in which the historic wall body, a visible conservation interface and surrounding urban terrain can be identified from the same frame.
- Required record: exact wall section/gate, management unit, capture date, photographer, permitted use, and the conservation source used to caption material status.
- Crop: 1600 × 1000; preserve the wall-to-terrain relationship rather than isolating picturesque brick texture.
- EN alt: “A documented surviving section of Nanjing City Wall, with repaired masonry identified in the site record.”
- ZH alt: “南京城墙一处有明确记录的原存墙段，现场资料另有标注修缮砖体。”
- KO alt: “현존 구간과 보수 벽돌이 현장 기록으로 구분된 난징 성벽.”

## Required comparison set

| Evidence class | Exact target | Caption requirement | Rights/status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Surviving fabric | Named Nanjing or Beijing surviving wall segment | State the exact segment and what the heritage authority identifies as surviving; do not date every brick from appearance | Commissioned/local photographer or compatible CC licence; management rules checked |
| Conservation repair | A section covered by a published Nanjing or Xi’an repair record | Name the intervention date and purpose; identify old/new interface only if the report does | Photographer permission plus management/conservation documentation |
| Reconstruction | Yongdingmen tower or a specifically documented Datong rebuilt component | Caption must state demolition/loss and reconstruction date | Use a rights-cleared current photo; architecture copyright/venue terms checked; no “ancient tower” shorthand |
| Archaeological trace | A managed exposed wall line, gate foundation or road alignment | State whether exposed, outlined or reconstructed for display | Archaeological authority/photographer permission required |
| Urban-form view | View through a named gate toward the documented street or axis | Separate historical route evidence from modern streetscape observation | Local reporting preferred; avoid identifiable faces unless releases are secured |

## Exclusions

- No generic “ancient Chinese wall” photograph as proof of period or authenticity.
- No visual dating based only on colour, weathering, brick neatness or vegetation.
- No AI reconstruction presented as documentary evidence.
- No tourist photo of a complete circuit captioned “original” without a component-level source.

## Keep as HTML

The five material-status labels, function/evidence table, four-site comparison and seven-minute field check must stay in HTML. A fixed infographic would hide uncertainty and become stale when conservation records change.
