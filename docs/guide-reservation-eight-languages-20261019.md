# Six reservation-linked guides in eight languages

## Scope

The six approved guides now have full bodies in English, Chinese, Korean,
Japanese, French, Spanish, German and Arabic (48 published routes). Thirty
complete editions were added for the last five languages. The reservation
offers already approved in PR #393 are localized in the other seven languages.

- `food-plants-and-animal-products-into-china`
- `yangshuo-town-or-yulong-river-where-to-stay`
- `shanghai-where-to-stay-first-trip`
- `tianmen-mountain-tickets-and-routes`
- `li-river-cruise-tickets-piers-booking`
- `forbidden-city-for-foreign-visitors`

Spanish guide paths use `/es/guias/`; other translated editions use
`/<language>/guides/`. Reciprocal language links, sitemap alternatives and
the relevant guide indexes expose the complete editions. Existing homepage
Yangshuo guide cards now lead to their native-language equivalents.

## Preserved boundaries

Original guide block structure, sources, factual review dates, images and
licensed credits remain intact. Translations retain source facts rather than
inventing new booking rules. Product cards retain their original products,
images and selection parameters. Published prices, itineraries, service scope,
central fee rules and guarantee conditions are unchanged. Tianmen promotion
continues to use its existing private-tour/day-guide destinations, not an
unsupported standalone reservation service.

No real inquiry, customer email or WhatsApp message is part of acceptance.
There are no new images, dependencies, advertising or monitoring changes.

## Review and verification

Two rounds of source/content review caught and corrected translation nuance,
missing original commercial modules, incorrect author/contact links, guide-index
labels and homepage links. Independent visual review inspected 60 screenshots,
covering all 48 mobile guide cards and desktop samples in all eight languages.
Premature screenshots were replaced and reviewed again rather than counted as
passes. No open visual blockers remained.

Browser checks covered all 48 routes at 360, 390 and 1440 pixels (144 geometry
checks), FAQ opening/closing on every route, actual eight-language switching,
and reservation-link selection transfer in all eight languages. These checks
are distinct from production acceptance.

Automated coverage includes full-body/FAQ consistency, reciprocal canonical and
hreflang links, sitemap entries, localized fees/offers, original product cards,
selection parameters and every rendered internal link on the 48 pages. Existing
analytics events and personal-data filtering are preserved. Japanese-only source
files follow the existing system-font exclusion; production font coverage checks
remain enabled. Locale export assertions now include the added pages.

Local results: 1,585 inquiry tests, seven guide-search tests and 16 affected
homepage tests passed. The full static build and export checks are required
before release; GitHub quality checks and the exact deployed release marker must
also pass. Production and standby are verified separately against the merged
commit, including 48 guides and 16 homepage/reservation-service control pages.

Detailed screenshots and review records are retained in the task's local
