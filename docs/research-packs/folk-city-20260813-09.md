## Direct-answer hypothesis for the first 150 words

Do not frame the choice as “Which place is more authentic?” Yizhou is the registered location and current protection base of the national-ICH item `刘三姐歌谣`; it is the stronger place to investigate song types, “speaking through song” and present-day transmission, but a specific bearer, song gathering or non-festival performance must be verified for the actual date. Yangshuo offers the Li River landscape, memories created by the 1960 film and a large modern outdoor production. That production is written, directed, lit, ticketed and commercially operated: it uses traditional material but is not equivalent to the full registered song repertoire, a community song-gathering context or any specific ritual practice. Travellers with no verified Yizhou encounter, no interest in a ticketed spectacle or too little time can learn the four layers and see Yangshuo's landscape without chasing an unclassified “folk show.”

This is a drafting hypothesis, not body copy.

## Narrative spine

Follow one name through four media:

`Yizhou and wider southern song practice → variable Liu Sanjie / Liu Sanmei legend → cǎidiào, musical theatre and the 1960 film's national image → the modern Yangshuo landscape show`

At every stop ask what the reader is seeing: oral song practice, a circulating legend, an authored film or a tourism production. The governing turn is that the ICH item registers a repertoire and practice, not Liu Sanjie's historic biography; Yangshuo's most visible “site” is not an ancient monument but film memory, karst landscape and a modern production system. The article ends with a three-way decision: verified Yizhou encounter, Yangshuo production, or background only.

A non-fiction opening may contrast the Xiajian River's open song-stage area with the ticketed Li River theatre, but must not invent singing, weather, a personal visit or dialogue.

### Required Yizhou observation set

1. Look for the exact item name/number `刘三姐歌谣 Ⅰ-23`, the full protection-unit name and a named bearer rather than a “birthplace” claim.
2. Ask what language, tune and one of the seven stated song categories is being performed.
3. Ask whether the event is a scheduled attraction programme, a documented public song gathering, a workshop or an explained improvisational exchange.
4. Check whether a stage, courtyard or “former residence” is labelled as reconstruction or tourism presentation.
5. Read the Xiajian River/village setting as social and geographic context, not evidence of a biography.
6. Use a documented 2025 government-outreach adaptation only as evidence that mountain-song form can be reused for new public messaging; do not treat it as proof of ICH transmission, a community tradition or an unchanged ancient lyric.

### Required Yangshuo observation set

1. See the actual Li River/karst setting before the show so “natural theatre” remains separate from plot evidence.
2. Read operator, ticketing, entrance and seat controls as the boundary of a modern product.
3. Identify lighting, blocking, costume and mass choreography as contemporary authorship.
4. Visit the culture-impression museum only if its current opening/access is confirmed; classify its displays.
5. Treat Big Banyan Tree as film-location memory when the official location record supports it, not as Liu Sanjie's historic home.
6. A non-linear, image-led production is not evidence that the visitor “missed” one definitive legend plot.
