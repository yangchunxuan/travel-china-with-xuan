## Executive decision

The evergreen cultural argument is strong enough for a future article, but the traveller-facing and asset gates are not closed.

The evidence supports four distinct layers:

1. a living song practice registered as the national intangible-cultural-heritage project `刘三姐歌谣 / Liu Sanjie ballads`;
2. variable Liu Sanjie or Liu Sanmei legends rather than one authenticated biography;
3. a nationally influential 1960 film and other authored stage adaptations;
4. a modern, ticketed landscape performance in Yangshuo.

However, research completed on 2026-08-13 did **not** locate all of the following in publishable first-party form: a currently documented ordinary-visitor cultural anchor in Yizhou; a Yizhou public event whose organiser, performers, song form, public-participation rules and photography rules are all stated; a complete current operator policy for the Yangshuo show; and publishable rights for the required event, display and operator-sign images. The planned “Yizhou half-day experience” is therefore withdrawn. Until those gates close, Yizhou can only be framed as a **background-understanding option whose individual stops must be confirmed directly**, not as an executable half-day promise.

## Direct answer for the future article

Do not ask whether Yizhou is the “true” Liu Sanjie and Yangshuo the “false” one. Ask which medium you want to understand. Yizhou is the registered place associated with the national ICH song project and its protection work; it is the better research direction for song types, oral variation and contemporary transmission **when a real public programme can be confirmed**. Yangshuo offers the karst setting, film memory and a modern commercial landscape show. The show can be culturally meaningful without being an unchanged ritual. If no Yizhou public programme can be verified, travellers should learn the four-layer background and avoid adding a city merely for an unconfirmed encounter.

## Narrative spine

The same name moves through different media:

`local and regional mountain-song practice → variable Liu Sanjie/Liu Sanmei legends → authored opera, musical and 1960 film image → modern Yangshuo tourism performance`

The article's recurring question should be: **What kind of evidence is in front of me?** A national ICH project page can establish the protected practice and protection unit; it cannot certify the heroine's biography. A film archive can establish a film's date, makers and plot; it cannot establish that the plot is the oldest oral version. A ticket office, lighting rig and operator notice establish a commercial production; they do not make the production culturally empty, nor do they turn it into an intact traditional rite.

## Revised outline (held, not approved for drafting)

Target only after gates close: English 2,300–2,500 words, Chinese and Korean equivalent in information, 9–11 major H2 sections. At least half of the article must serve on-site reading, choice, verification and recovery.

### 1. One name, four different things

- Open with two documentable spaces: a Yizhou song-cultural setting and Yangshuo's ticketed riverside theatre, without inventing sound, weather or first-person experience.
- Give the direct answer within 150 English words.
- State immediately that the page does not arbitrate a single birthplace.

### 2. What does the national ICH registration actually recognise?

- Explain the project number, 2006 listing, Yizhou application and current protection unit.
- Introduce the seven song categories in the official record.
- Correct the first misconception: the registered object is a song tradition, not a historic-person certificate.

### 3. Why do Liu Sanjie and Liu Sanmei stories refuse one map?

- Explain widespread variants and the changing form of the name.
- Present scholarly disagreement as disagreement, not a verdict.
- Keep the retelling short and functional.

### 4. What did the 1960 film change for later travellers?

- Use the China Film Archive for date, credits, running time and official synopsis.
- Place landlord conflict, singing contests and romance in the film layer.
- Explain that screen circulation helped standardise a nationally familiar image.
- Do not quote lyrics, dialogue or use stills/posters.

### 5. What is the Yangshuo show—and what is it not?

- Establish the modern project, operator, natural-stage concept and opening history.
- Read ticketing, seating, lighting and mass choreography as evidence of an authored tourism production.
- Do not call it a complete traditional ceremony or dismiss it as “fake.”

### 6. How should a visitor test a Yizhou claim?

- Require a current official page or direct confirmation for the exact venue and date.
- At any display, look for the project name/number, protection unit, named bearer and song category.
- Ask: Who is singing? What form is being sung? Is this a protected-practice programme, community event or scenic-area show? May visitors participate, photograph or record?
- Unless gate 1 and gate 2 close, keep this as a verification method, not a scheduled half-day.

### 7. How should a visitor read the Yangshuo venue?

- Separate daytime karst landscape, film-location memory, venue exterior/operating signs and the performance itself.
- Explain what stage technology and ticket rules reveal about authorship and operations.
- Require same-day operator confirmation for running status and venue rules.

### 8. Should you choose Yizhou, Yangshuo or background only?

- Choose Yizhou only when a dated, public cultural programme and visitor mechanics are directly confirmed and song practice is a major reason for the trip.
- Choose Yangshuo when already on a Guilin–Yangshuo route and interested in modern landscape theatre, after checking current operating terms.
- Choose background only when access or event evidence is missing, time is limited or a large commercial show is not wanted.
- Choose both only for a deliberate “practice to modern stage” study; do not make it the default.

### 9. What is the safe visit order and failure recovery?

- Conditional Yizhou sequence: confirmed public display → labelled landscape/reconstruction → confirmed public programme.
- Yangshuo sequence: real landscape in daylight → four-layer card → current external operator signs → performance if running and terms accepted.
- Recovery: if Yizhou activity is unverified, delete the live-contact promise; if the Yangshuo show stops, retain landscape and interpretive background; never substitute an unidentified “ethnic show.”

### 10. What should you not photograph or assume?

- Obtain explicit permission for identifiable performers, bearers, children, backstage work, close audio and ritualised activity.
- Follow the operator's current rules; in the absence of first-party rules, default to no recording and no publication of identifiable close-ups.
- Do not use film frames or show screenshots.
- Do not call unfamiliar music primitive, mysterious or unchanged.

### 11. Five conclusions not to carry home

- ICH registration is not a birth certificate.
- The film plot is not every oral version.
- A film location is not proof that events happened there.
- A modern landscape show is not an intact ritual.
- A Yizhou attraction carrying the name is not automatically a protection-unit programme.
