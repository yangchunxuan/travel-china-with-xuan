## 2. Eight opened official sources

### S1 — China Railway 12306: booking document and boarding

URL: https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html?item=2

Publisher: China Railway 12306. Publication/update date: not displayed; accessed 2026-09-10.

- Confirmed: e-ticket passengers must show the valid identity document used to buy the ticket for entering/exiting the station and boarding. The English service accepts eligible valid foreign passports. An itinerary sheet or reimbursement receipt is not a ticket.
- Safe packing action: keep the physical original passport/accepted booking document accessible in hand baggage, and keep itinerary/offline copies separately as backups. The source supports the actual booking document, not substitution by an itinerary screenshot.
- Do not claim: every passenger may use a passport regardless of which document was booked; a scan replaces the document; one passport-validity rule applies to every entry route. The FAQ mixes historical and newer material, so do not import its unrelated child-ticket, refund or restricted-item details.

### S2 — Air China: ticket and fare determine checked baggage

URL: https://webresource.airchina.com.cn/en-US/content/c/2024-02-09/16521.shtml

Title: Announcement on the Implementation of Branded Fares in Economy Class on Air China's International and Regional Routes.

Publisher: Air China. URL date: 2024-02-09; body does not show a separate publication date. Accessed 2026-09-10.

- Confirmed: Air China differentiates international/regional economy fares; some Economy Standard routes have no free checked baggage. The notice says standards may change and the purchased ticket controls final details.
- Safe packing action: check cabin size/weight and checked allowance against each actual ticket and operating carrier, including a separately bought China domestic flight. International-route allowance is not evidence of a separate domestic ticket's allowance.
- Do not claim: every international fare has a free suitcase, every China domestic fare includes 20 kg, or a single weight/piece rule applies to all airlines or codeshares. Do not use the legacy Air China English passenger notice's internally conflicting battery sentence as a new power-bank rule.

### S3 — CAAC: domestic-flight CCC restriction

URL: https://www.caac.gov.cn/English/News/202507/t20250709_227894.html

Title: CAAC Bans Passengers from Carrying Power Banks Without CCC Markings or from Recalled Models on Domestic Flights.

Publisher: Civil Aviation Administration of China. Page displays `09/07/2025`; URL identifies 2025-07-09. Accessed 2026-09-10.

- Confirmed: effective 2025-06-28, the notice prohibits power banks without CCC, with unclear CCC, or in recalled models/batches on domestic flights.
- Safe packing action: inspect the physical device label and exact model before departure when the itinerary includes a China domestic flight; consult the existing power-bank owner and current carrier/airport instructions for cabin placement, energy, quantity and use restrictions.
- Do not claim: CCC alone guarantees acceptance; “international itinerary” removes the rule from its domestic segment; international flights have no battery restrictions; this aviation notice establishes railway rules. This notice alone is not a complete international battery-allowance table.

### S4 — CAAC: electricity and sockets

URL: https://www.caac.gov.cn/ZTZL/RDZT/YTHYWZ/CHRY/SHZN/Electricity/index.html

Title: Electricity. Publisher: CAAC conference Living Guide. Publication/update date: not displayed; accessed 2026-09-10.

- Confirmed: the page specifies 220V, 50Hz and describes two flat parallel pins and a three-pin arrangement with angled flat prongs/grounding. An adapter may be needed.
- Editorial action consistent with the existing electrical owner: read the device/charger INPUT label and manufacturer instructions, then choose a compatible plug adapter. Do not infer voltage compatibility from the plug fitting.
- Do not claim: every device is dual voltage, every hotel socket accepts every plug, or a shape adapter changes voltage. Do not repeat the page's broad hotel-bathroom converter statement as a hotel guarantee. The source does not certify a traveller's particular device or a medical appliance.

### S5 — Shanghai government / General Administration of Customs: food and medicine verification route

URL: https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-CustomsImmigration/20231212/9a3c7f512d464e0798be3fc76c8dd6f2.html

Title: Customs clearance guide for inbound passengers. Publisher: Shanghai government; credited source: General Administration of Customs. Body updated 2025-10-13; accessed 2026-09-10. URL date is not its latest body update.

- Confirmed: the guide identifies animal/plant and restricted-article controls, includes food/medicine risk categories, and provides a related official pathway titled “How can I bring psychotropic medications into China?”.
- Safe packing action: for snacks and medicines, verify the exact product, ingredients/active ingredient, quantity, intended use and entry route through current Chinese Customs guidance. For medicine, confirm the applicable entry requirements and supporting documents before packing; use the linked specialist pathway where relevant. Original labelled packaging and a prescription/doctor's information are useful for verification, not a permission certificate.
- Do not claim: packaged, cooked, small-quantity, personal-use or prescription-labelled items are universally permitted; a prescription automatically overrides controlled-drug or quarantine rules; a universal supply limit applies to all medicines.
- Source-quality boundary: the opening declaration wording in this overview is broader than the 2025 declaration rule cited by the existing red/green owner. Use it as the targeted official verification path, not a replacement declaration-law source. The related medication page was visible as a link but was not separately opened; no dosage/quantity claim is derived from it.

### S6 — Heilongjiang Culture and Tourism: Harbin's previous-season announcement

URL: https://wlt.hlj.gov.cn/wlt/c114169/202512/c00_31897123.shtml

Title: 官宣！第二十七届哈尔滨冰雪大世界12月17日开园！

Publisher: Heilongjiang Department of Culture and Tourism; source: Harbin Culture and Tourism. Published 2025-12-16 09:42:36; accessed 2026-09-10.

- Confirmed historical fact: this is the 27th edition's announcement for 2025-12-17 opening, with ice/snow art, performances and interactive experiences. It advises cold-weather preparation.
- Safe winter action: choose Harbin when ice/snow is the trip's main purpose and travellers accept substantial time outdoors; leave flexibility until the relevant season's official operating announcement is available.
- Do not claim: the 28th/2026–27 opening or closing date is confirmed; last season's ticket amount is current; all attractions share the park's date; any slide or event is guaranteed. No 2026–27 date or price was confirmed in this research.

### S7 — Shanghai government: city winter alternatives

Exact opened URL (redirected to a literal apostrophe): https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Editor'sPick-StudyinShanghai/20250116/ff320a15c405454d90fa190c67426c01.html

Title: Discover Shanghai's enchanting winter. Publisher: Shanghai government. Body dated 2025-01-22, despite `20250116` in URL; accessed 2026-09-10.

- Confirmed: this winter overview includes museums, galleries, food and city scenery; it describes heavy snow as uncommon. This supports a city/culture winter branch with indoor substitutions.
- Safe winter action: combine shorter outdoor neighbourhood/sightseeing blocks with museum/gallery/food time, and confirm the named venue's current hours and booking rules. Beijing–Shanghai is an editorial route choice; this source directly supports Shanghai, not Beijing weather or museum rules.
- Do not claim: snow will fall on the visit, 2025 festivals repeat on the same 2026–27 dates, a museum has walk-in availability, or every indoor venue is open daily. No temperature numbers are needed.

### S8 — State Council English site / Xinhua: tropical Sanya and route contrast

URL: https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202402/01/content_WS65baee01c6d0868f4e8e3af5.html

Title: Essential travel guide to Chinese New Year for foreigners. Publisher: State Council English website, reporting by Xinhua. Updated 2024-02-01 09:04; accessed 2026-09-10.

- Confirmed historical/general context: the article distinguishes Harbin winter activities, tropical coastal Sanya as a respite from cold, and urban/cultural options including Beijing and Shanghai. It explicitly advises checking attraction and restaurant opening hours during the festival.
- Safe winter action: use Sanya/Hainan as the third branch for travellers whose main aim is a tropical coastal break. The three-branch comparison is editorial synthesis from sources S6–S8 and existing site owners, not an official recommended package.
- Do not claim: winter is low season throughout China; a guaranteed swimming day, sunshine, temperature or sea condition; 2024 visa/events/discounts remain valid. This research did not verify a low-altitude Yunnan destination, so the supported warm branch is Hainan/Sanya; do not silently substitute all of Yunnan or high-altitude routes.
