## Method and hard gates

This manifest records twelve independently researched candidates, not twelve promised pages. The current Google result pages were sampled in English on 2026-09-01 using one task-shaped query per candidate. Visible organic results, People Also Ask prompts, related searches and recent public traveller discussions were used only to establish wording and unresolved demand. No search volume, click-through rate, conversion rate or purchase probability is inferred. Forums are not evidence for a law, medical action, platform capability or operating rule.

The owner audit covered all current `content/guides` metadata, the generated and legacy registries, the latest Search Map and do-not-repeat ledger. The remote audit covered 181 `origin/article/*` and `origin/codex/*` refs. It found 60 Search-Map-reserved durable drafts and 11 additional central-review drafts that are absent from main but still block synonym recreation. A remote branch not being an ancestor of main was never treated as proof of non-publication.

Every selected identity must pass these gates before it is committed:

1. Four or more non-image primary or official sources, with every dynamic claim dated 2026-09-01 unless a row documents a different fact-level recheck.
2. A direct answer, a decision path, exceptions, failure recovery and a final verification step in EN, ZH and KO.
3. Identical block IDs/types and identical table dimensions across locales; natural locale writing rather than sentence-by-sentence translation.
4. `reviewedAt`, `freshnessClass`, review triggers and an explicit official-conflict rule in the article pack.
5. A traveller blind review and a separate SEO/technical adversarial review; all P1/P2 findings fixed and re-reviewed before the next article starts.
6. No edits to main, Search Map, the guide registry, homepage, templates, sitemap or production settings. New collection assignments remain central decisions.

## Selected canonical boundaries

The six selected pages are mutually exclusive at the moment a traveller lands on them:

- Rabies owner: condition-specific immediate exposure and continuity record. It does not own the ambulance/hospital dispatch system.
- Weather owner: source priority, exposure reduction and trip-level rebuilding after an alert. It does not decide refunds for a carrier or attraction.
- Driving owner: legal/practical go/no-go before driving. It does not rank rentals or plan road trips.
- Medical-emergency owner: dispatch, scene communication and administrative handoff. It does not diagnose or prescribe.
- Medicine owner: pre-travel ingredient/status/documents/declaration decision. It does not advise a dose, substitution or treatment plan.
- Pet-entry owner: accompanied dogs/cats, documents, declaration, port and quarantine decision. It does not own commercial import, other species, airline acceptance, local dog registration or the destination country's return rules.

## Official conflict policy for the batch

1. Use the latest applicable Chinese primary rule for legal and administrative requirements in mainland China.
2. Treat official English pages as translations or service summaries, not as permission to erase scope, date or local differences in the Chinese source.
3. If current Chinese and official English text materially differ, display both positions, identify the date and authority, and route the traveller to the responsible authority. Do not silently choose the more convenient reading.
4. WHO or a traveller's home-government health guidance may add an international safety baseline; it does not replace a China-specific clinical or entry decision. Any difference is disclosed and referred to a qualified clinician or authority.
5. A city page may demonstrate local implementation only. It is never generalized to every city.
6. When the official platform, destination authority, carrier, venue or medical provider cannot confirm a required step, the safe default is to stop the regulated activity, preserve evidence and use the documented fallback.
