## Distinct page purpose

The 10-day page answers first-trip route selection with a 10-day/9-night Beijing–Xi'an–Shanghai example and two slower or nature-focused alternatives. It links to the existing Beijing–Zhangjiajie–Shanghai article, whose main worked example is 11 days/10 nights and whose slower recommended default is 12 nights; it does not rename or duplicate that specialist article.

The southwest page answers the exact three-city combination using a 12-day/11-night example: four Chengdu nights, three Chongqing nights plus one Wulong night, and three Zhangjiajie villa nights. Both intercity transfers have their own days. Shorter versions identify what must be removed. The Wulong leg returns to Chongqing before the Zhangjiajie transfer.

The examples require their own confirmed itinerary and quote. Linked city products retain their own duration and inclusions. No combined price, guaranteed reservation, new transport inclusion or continuous guide is implied.
