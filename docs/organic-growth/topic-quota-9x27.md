## Quota guardrails

- All 42 observed existing identities are retained before selecting new mapped identities.
- Higher-maintenance tool, service and timing families have lower ceilings than destination and practical task families.
- A quota does not justify a weak page. Every raw seed excluded from the final count has an explicit merge/reject owner and reason in the conflict ledger; no candidate is cut only to satisfy a quota.
- No city × month × audience, nationality, day-count or route-direction matrix is allowed to fill a quota.
