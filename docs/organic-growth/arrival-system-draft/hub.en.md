## Direct answer

Treat the first day as one chain with seven handoffs: establish the correct
entry basis, protect the passport, complete the current official arrival-card
task, make an honest Customs decision, get one working connection and one
working payment fallback, reach the exact accommodation, and close the right
registration process. This Hub tells you which task comes next. Open the linked
owner for the actual rules and steps; do not use a generic checklist to overrule
an embassy, the National Immigration Administration, Customs, a bank, a carrier
or the exact airport and hotel involved.

## At the port: keep the order simple

1. **Read the signs before joining a queue.** Separate immigration, baggage and
   Customs; an arrival card is not a visa and is not a Customs declaration.
2. **Use the current official arrival-card route if required.** Keep the
   Homeground owner labelled “merged code, not live” until central verifies its
   public URL. A saved submission is preparation, not admission.
3. **Present the exact entry evidence for your path.** A visa-free round trip,
   a visa and a 240-hour third-country transit are different paths. If staff
   need more evidence, show the current transport and accommodation records and
   follow their instructions rather than debating from a screenshot.
4. **Collect baggage before deciding the Customs lane.** If the item or limit is
   uncertain, use red and ask. Do not abandon unidentified medicine, food or a
   regulated object in a public area.
5. **Create minimum communications before moving.** Save the property name and
   address in Chinese, an offline map pin, the flight record and an emergency
   contact. One working method is enough to leave the terminal safely; optimize
   the SIM later.
6. **Test one small payment, then keep the backups separate.** A successful app
   login does not prove every merchant, transit gate or person-to-person scene
   will accept the same method.

## At the accommodation: hotel and non-hotel stays split here

For a hotel stay, the hotel registers the foreign guest and submits the hotel
accommodation information. Use the [hotel owner](/guides/foreigners-china-hotel/)
to separate the official rule, the platform record and what the front desk can
actually process.

For a home, private residence or other non-hotel stay, the foreign guest or host
must complete accommodation registration with the local public-security organ
within 24 hours. As checked on 20 August 2026, NIA’s online service was still a
pilot in seven provincial-level regions—not a nationwide online promise. The
official fallback is the local exit-entry service window or police station; NIA
12367 can explain the process. If an apartment, guesthouse or minsu has an
unclear operating model, establish how it registers guests before paying, and
do not assume a platform label changes the legal category.

If the online registration page rejects the address or identity check, save a
privacy-safe record of the attempt, ask the host to help, and switch to the
local in-person route within the deadline. Do not wait for an app fix and do not
post passport or address screenshots publicly.

## Two first-day scenarios

### Ordinary round trip, hotel, evening landing

Use the passport-specific owner rather than the transit page; complete the
official arrival-card task if required; keep questionable baggage for Customs;
activate only the minimum connection; test one payment; then go to the exact
hotel branch. If the room is refused despite written confirmation, ask for the
specific reason once, open the platform case without self-cancelling, challenge
any no-show classification and move to a staffed replacement. The hotel-registration duty
belongs to the hotel, but the traveller should retain the check-in record.

### 240-hour route, non-hotel address, phone not compatible with eSIM

First use the 240-hour owner to verify nationality, A–China–C route, port,
permitted area, document validity and confirmed onward evidence. Keep the NIA
arrival-card task separate. Use roaming or airport Wi-Fi to contact the host,
then buy a local SIM only after an operator confirms the phone and identity
process. The host should help with the initial non-hotel registration. If the
online pilot is unavailable or fails, go to the local public-security route;
do not let a connectivity problem consume the registration window.

## Before the first night ends

- Confirm that the passport is physically secure and that no essential record
  exists only inside one phone.
- Confirm the correct accommodation-registration path and retain a privacy-safe
  record; do not photograph another guest’s screen or expose a passport number.
- Test the route from the property entrance to tomorrow’s first fixed booking.
- Keep a small independent payment reserve and a way to contact the property if
  the phone or primary app fails.
- Recheck any next-day ticket, terminal, station or attraction against its
  official operator. Do not use this Hub as live timetable evidence.
