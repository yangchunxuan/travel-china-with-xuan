## Recommended hero

Create an original, documentary-neutral workflow diagram rather than an airport
photograph or a screenshot. A single route moves through seven labelled gates:

1. entry basis and passport;
2. official arrival card;
3. baggage and Customs;
4. connection;
5. payment backup;
6. airport-to-address transfer;
7. hotel or non-hotel registration.

Each gate has a small side arrow to a neutral “ask official staff / use the
fallback owner” node. The graphic communicates relationships only; it is not
evidence that a form, lane, device, app or counter looks a particular way.

## Production specification

- Master: editable SVG, 1600 × 1000, with shapes and text on separate layers.
- Runtime proposal: WebP 1600 × 1000; derive 1200 × 630 social crop only after

- Style: Homeground editorial palette, high contrast, simple line icons, no
  brand colours that imply endorsement.
- Text: preferably no embedded body copy. If labels remain, create separately
  reviewed EN/ZH/KO variants rather than one mixed-language asset.
- Required alt ideas:
  - EN: “Seven-step China arrival workflow from entry documents to accommodation registration, with a fallback at every step.”
  - ZH: “从入境证件到住宿登记的七步中国抵达流程，每一步都有失败兜底。”
  - KO: “입국 서류부터 숙박 등록까지 이어지는 중국 도착 7단계와 각 단계의 대안.”

## Privacy and accuracy prohibitions

- No passport, visa, boarding pass, arrival-card field, declaration form,
  booking, bank card, phone number, QR code or barcode—real or plausibly real.
- No precise home address, map location, hotel room, live itinerary, face,
  child, staff name, account balance, transaction or device identifier.
- No immigration officer, Customs officer or hotel employee presented as
  endorsing Homeground.
- No app, carrier, bank, eSIM, visa agent, hotel platform or transfer logo.
- No screenshot that can become stale and no recreation of a government form.
- No AI-generated documentary airport or border scene. AI may not be used to
  fabricate evidence of an operational process.

## QA and provenance record required at import

Central must record the creator, creation date, source file, edit history,
rights owner, output dimensions, SHA-256 values, alt text, crop decision and
whether any AI-assisted tool touched the asset. The SVG must contain no external
links, scripts, hidden metadata, rasterized personal data or unoutlined
unlicensed font dependency.
