# Shanghai Destination Hub image plan

- Artifact status: central review required
- Image review date: 2026-08-15
- Proposed page paths: `/destinations/shanghai/`, `/zh/destinations/shanghai/`, `/ko/destinations/shanghai/`
- Documentary rule: every photograph must depict the named Shanghai location. No AI-generated or AI-expanded documentary city image may be used.
- Map rule: use original editorial diagrams only. Do not reproduce a commercial map, transit map, satellite image or unlicensed screenshot.

## Visual system

The page should explain Shanghai as a set of spatial decisions rather than decorate a list of attractions. The visual sequence therefore moves from the two banks of the Huangpu River, to a simplified airport-and-station network, and then to accurately identified neighbourhood and venue photographs.

Preferred source order:

1. Homeground-owned original photography with original files and capture metadata.
2. Newly commissioned photography with a written perpetual web licence covering editing, crops and all three language versions.
3. A file-specific Wikimedia Commons image only when the individual file page confirms a compatible licence, author and attribution. A Commons category page is not sufficient evidence.
4. An official Shanghai government, airport, museum or attraction image only after written reuse permission or an explicit reusable licence is recorded. Publication on an official website does not by itself grant reuse rights.

## Diagram content rules

### Huangpu relationship diagram

Include:

- west/east orientation;
- Puxi and Pudong labels;
- Bund–Old City–Yuyuan, People’s Square–East Nanjing Road, Jing’an–Former French Concession and Lujiazui clusters;
- Hongqiao as a transport/stay task, not a central sightseeing cluster;
- Disneyland/International Resort as an independent eastern task;
- a clear “schematic, not to scale” note.

Exclude:

- walking-time promises;
- exact district boundaries for the Former French Concession travel label;
- decorative bridges or ferry routes that could be mistaken for operating advice;
- an attraction ranking.

### Airport and station schematic

Include:

- PVG, SHA T1, SHA T2 and Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station as distinct labels;
- Airport Link between PVG and SHA T2;
- Metro Line 2 as the east–west city spine;
- Maglev ending at Longyang Road rather than continuing to the Bund;
- Shanghai Railway Station, Shanghai South and Shanghai Songjiang as separate railway nodes;
- “verify live timetable and exact station in official channels.”

Exclude:

- exact first/last trains, fares and journey minutes;
- future Shanghai East or Baoshan stations as operational nodes;
- a copied operator map, logo or trademarked station graphic;
- a line that visually suggests SHA T2 and Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station are one platform.

## File, performance and accessibility requirements

- Keep the hero derivative near or below 300 KB where visual quality permits; supporting photographs near or below 220 KB.
- Provide responsive `srcset` widths suitable for the existing image component; do not upload only a desktop master.
- Preserve meaningful focal points in 16:9, 4:5 and narrow mobile crops.
- Put explanatory text in HTML beside diagrams where practical; do not make the image the sole carrier of critical information.
- Use high-contrast labels and line patterns that remain understandable for colour-vision deficiencies.
- Do not place text in photographs. Locale-specific diagram text must be rendered as separate assets or accessible SVG layers.
- Record width, height, byte size, format, source URL, author, licence, attribution and crop notes in the eventual production asset manifest.

## Rights and factual QA checklist

- [ ] The exact location and direction of every photograph are recorded.
- [ ] The source file is original or has a file-level reusable licence/permission.
- [ ] Attribution wording is stored exactly as required by the licence.
- [ ] Cropping and derivative use are permitted.
- [ ] No official-site image is treated as reusable merely because it is public.
- [ ] No AI-generated, AI-expanded or materially composited documentary city image is used.
- [ ] Yuyuan Garden is not confused with the surrounding bazaar.
- [ ] Shanghai Museum People’s Square and East Museum are not interchanged.
- [ ] Hongqiao airport and railway-station signage is correctly identified.
- [ ] Both diagrams state that they are schematic and not to scale.
- [ ] All three locale versions have accurate alt text.
