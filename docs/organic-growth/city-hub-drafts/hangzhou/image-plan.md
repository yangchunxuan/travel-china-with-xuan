## Asset-production rules

1. Download the original-resolution source from the recorded Commons file page, not a search-engine thumbnail.
2. Record source filename, author, capture date, licence URL, source SHA-256, final SHA-256, dimensions and processing.
3. Use only crop, resize, rotation correction, colour-profile conversion and WebP encoding. Do not add or remove buildings, people, vegetation, water, sky, signs or archaeological fabric.
4. Strip EXIF, GPS and device metadata from public derivatives while retaining the provenance record in the repository.
5. Preserve CC BY/CC BY-SA attribution and modification notices. Apply ShareAlike to each altered image derivative where required.
6. Do not enlarge identifiable people or make them the subject. Choose a different crop when privacy or crowd framing is weak.
7. Keep relationship diagrams clearly labelled as editorial schematics. Do not present them as official maps or live transit information.
8. Inspect every crop at mobile and desktop widths. The hero must still show both lake and city; supporting images must retain enough context to identify the actual place.
9. Do not reuse a picture to represent a different location: Liangzhu Museum is not the park; Hangzhou East is not Hangzhou West; a Longjing hillside is not proof of a particular tea product.
10. No AI tourism photograph, AI reconstruction, generative fill, synthetic weather, invented crowd or fake night lighting.
