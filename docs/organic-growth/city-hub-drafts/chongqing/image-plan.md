## 1. Visual purpose

The page must explain Chongqing as a usable destination rather than sell a list of viral camera positions. The visual sequence should answer four questions:

1. How do the two rivers, peninsula and opposing banks shape the city?
2. Why can nearby map pins belong to different height levels and routes?
3. Which airport and railway hubs serve different travel directions?
4. Why are Wulong and Dazu full distant extensions rather than downtown attractions?

A night image may appear only as a secondary, accurately captioned photograph. The hero should not be a generic neon montage.

## 4. Diagram 1 — vertical-city orientation schematic

### Content

Create an editorial cross-section/topology, not a tourist map and not a scale drawing. It should include:

- Jiangbei on the north side of the Jialing River;
- the Yuzhong ridge/peninsula between the Jialing and Yangtze rivers;
- Nan’an on the south side of the Yangtze;
- Nanshan above the south-bank riverfront but not shown as a direct upstairs walk;
- example stacked roads and a multi-level building entrance;
- possible connector types: bridge, tunnel, rail, lift/escalator, stairs/ramp and vehicle approach;
- a four-part callout: **bank → level → entrance → connection**.

### Prohibitions

- no precise elevation, gradient, walking minute or straight-line distance;
- no implication that every illustrated connector is operating;
- no universal “upper city/lower city” boundary drawn as if the whole municipality had two fixed floors;
- no accessibility icon unless the whole illustrated route has been verified end to end;
- no “8D” branding as a substitute for orientation.

### Accessibility and localisation

- Supply text labels in EN/ZH/KO as separate layers or data, not baked into one raster.
- Ensure reading order and contrast work without relying on colour alone.
- Suggested caption: `A non-scale orientation diagram: river bank, street level, usable entrance and connector determine the real route.`

## 5. Diagram 2 — transport-hub topology

### Content

Create a simplified, explicitly non-scale hub diagram with:

- CKG north of the central-city core;
- Chongqing North as the largest established rail hub;
- Shapingba as a smaller urban station with selected Chengdu-corridor value;
- Chongqing West as a west-side long-distance/high-speed hub;
- Chongqing East in Nan’an/Chayuan for the southeast corridor;
- Chongqing Station at Caiyuanba marked **under reconstruction / not a current passenger alternative as checked 2026-08-15**;
- Wulong South outside the central-city frame, with a second transfer required to the scenic visitor system;
- directional route arrows for Chengdu, Qianjiang/Zhangjiajie and the Yangtze cruise handoff;
- a note that exact trains, terminals, station exits and cruise piers are dynamic.

### Prohibitions

- no geographic-scale claim;
- no guaranteed transfer time;
- no future metro or future Qianjiang–Jishou railway shown as open;
- no fixed Chaotianmen pier for all cruises;
- no claim that Wulong South is a scenic-area gate.

### Suggested caption

`Chongqing’s transport hubs serve different corridors. The ticket, airline record or cruise notice—not the city name alone—decides the handoff.`

## 7. Processing and provenance requirements

For every selected photograph:

1. Download the original from the recorded source page, not a search-result thumbnail.
2. Save the original URL, author, date, licence URL and retrieval date in the final asset ledger.
3. Record the original checksum before editing.
4. Apply only editorial crop, modest exposure correction if needed, resize and WebP conversion. Do not add or remove documentary elements.
5. Strip unnecessary metadata from the published derivative while preserving the provenance record separately.
6. Record the derivative dimensions and SHA-256.
7. Include attribution adjacent to the image or in the site’s photo-credit system as required by the licence.
8. For ShareAlike assets, ensure the derivative and downstream publication comply with the applicable licence.
9. Write factual alt text that describes what is visibly present; do not use alt text for SEO keyword stuffing.
10. Recheck the Commons file page immediately before publication in case the licence or deletion status has changed.

## 8. Rejected visual directions

Do not use:

- AI-generated Chongqing night scenes, river panoramas, stations, tourists, heritage carvings or Wulong landscapes;
- composite skylines that merge unrelated riverbanks or buildings;
- drone images with unclear permission;
- social-media screenshots or travel-platform thumbnails;
- a viral “train through building” image without an explicit reusable licence;
- a tourist map that converts straight-line distance into walking time;
- a transport diagram that presents future infrastructure as current;
- more than one decorative night image before the orientation and transport explanations.
