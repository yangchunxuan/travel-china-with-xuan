# Guangzhou destination hub image plan

**Draft scope:** planning only; no media files are added in this patch
**Rule:** Documentary-looking Guangzhou photographs must be real, attributable photographs. AI-generated or AI-altered documentary scenes are prohibited.

## 1. Visual narrative

The page should communicate one idea: Guangzhou is not one compact centre. The reader must understand the relationship among the western old city, the central historic/commercial core, Tianhe and Zhujiang New Town, Pazhou in Haizhu, Panyu/Chimelong and the northern airport.

The visual sequence should therefore be:

1. a real Guangzhou hero that includes the Pearl River and contemporary city, without reducing the page to a night-view list;
2. an original editorial relationship map showing old city → central core → Tianhe/new axis → Pazhou → Panyu, with Baiyun Airport north of the main city;
3. a real Liwan heritage photograph;
4. a real Shamian streetscape;
5. a real major transport hub photograph;
6. a real airport/intercity photograph.

The page should not show five near-identical skyline pictures. At least two supporting images must explain the older city and at least two must explain transport or spatial separation.

## 3. Original relationship map

### Working filename

`guangzhou-city-zones-relationship-map.svg`

### Type

Original editorial diagram, not a geographic navigation map and not an AI image.

### Required labels

Northern gateway:
- Baiyun Airport
- T2: Airport North / Baiyun Airport North
- T3: Baiyun Airport East
- T1: passenger service suspended as of the review date

Main west-to-south city chain:
- Liwan / Shamian
- Beijing Road / Yuexiu
- Tianhe / Zhujiang New Town
- Haizhu / Pazhou
- Panyu / Chimelong
- Guangzhou South

Railway hubs:
- Guangzhou South
- Guangzhou East
- Guangzhou Baiyun
- Guangzhou Railway Station
- Guangzhou North

External route arrows:
- Foshan / Shunde
- Shenzhen
- Hong Kong SAR
- Macao SAR
- Changsha / Zhangjiajie direction

### Diagram logic

Use a schematic spatial arrangement rather than false precision:

```text
                   Baiyun Airport / Huadu
                         │
        Guangzhou North  │  Guangzhou Baiyun
                         │
Liwan/Shamian — Yuexiu — Tianhe/Zhujiang New Town — Pazhou
        │                  │                         │
   Foshan/Shunde      Guangzhou East            intercity
                         │
                    Panyu/Chimelong — Guangzhou South
                         │
                Shenzhen / Hong Kong / Macao chains
```

The final artwork must make clear that:

- Liwan and Shamian are west of the new central axis;
- Zhujiang New Town and Canton Tower face each other across the Pearl River;
- Pazhou is further east in Haizhu;
- Chimelong and Guangzhou South are in the southern Panyu task zone;
- the airport is north of the central city;
- Hong Kong and Macao are cross-boundary destinations, not Guangdong child cities.

### Accessibility

The diagram must not rely on colour alone. Use line style, arrows, node shapes and labels. Provide a text description immediately below it in all three locales. Every label should remain readable at a 320-pixel viewport; otherwise provide a mobile stacked version.

### Suggested text alternative

- **English:** `Schematic relationship map placing Liwan and Shamian in western Guangzhou, Yuexiu in the central historic area, Tianhe and Zhujiang New Town on the modern axis, Pazhou farther east, Panyu and Chimelong to the south, and Baiyun Airport to the north.`
- **简体中文:** `广州片区关系示意图：荔湾与沙面位于城市西部，越秀是中部历史核心，天河与珠江新城位于现代中轴，琶洲更靠东，番禺与长隆在南部，白云机场在北部。`
- **한국어:** `광저우 공간관계 도식으로, 서부의 리완·사몐, 중앙 역사권의 웨슈, 현대축의 톈허·주장신청, 동쪽의 파저우, 남쪽의 판위·창룽, 북쪽의 바이윈공항을 배치한다.`

## 6. Prohibited visual choices

- AI-generated “realistic” Guangzhou skyline, old street, family, airport or station scenes.
- AI removal or replacement of people, signage, buildings, vehicles or weather in documentary photographs.
- Commercial stock thumbnails without a traceable licence.
- Hotel marketing photography used as a district illustration.
- Restaurant food photography that turns the destination hub into a dining ranking.
- A main visual showing only an aeroplane, terminal hall or trade-fair booth.
- More than one near-duplicate Canton Tower night photograph.
- A geographic map that implies false walking distances or exact travel times.
- A route diagram that places Hong Kong or Macao inside Guangdong.

## 7. Asset production checklist

Before central implementation:

1. Open every Wikimedia file page and record the current author, original file, licence and licence URL.
2. Download the original file, not a search-engine thumbnail.
3. Preserve a copy of the unmodified original outside the production asset folder.
4. Record crop, resize and WebP conversion.
5. Keep attribution in page metadata or visible caption according to the licence.
6. For CC BY-SA derivatives, preserve the same or compatible licence and state that the image was cropped/converted.
7. Test 320-pixel and desktop crops.
8. Confirm that signs, station names and terminal labels remain accurate and readable.
9. Confirm that the relation map has a text equivalent.
10. Reject any asset whose provenance or licence cannot be verified.
