# Chengdu Destination Hub — image plan

**Draft:** `city-hub-chengdu`
**Asset state:** planning only; no image binary is added by this patch
**Documentary rule:** use real, licensable photographs with place-level provenance. Do not generate a panda, tea house, street, skyline, airport, railway station, museum interior or city-life scene with AI. An original editorial relationship diagram is allowed because it is explanatory graphic design, not simulated documentary photography.

## Visual narrative

The visual system must support the Hub’s central distinction:

1. Chengdu is a living city, not only a panda stop.
2. TFU, CTU, central stay areas and railway stations occupy different directions and solve different transport tasks.
3. Dujiangyan, Guanghan, Leshan and Jiuzhaigou are branches or independent routes, not downtown Chengdu attractions.

The page should therefore avoid a hero that is only a close panda portrait. A panda may appear in a child guide, but the city Hub needs an image that communicates the slower urban rhythm and gives the supporting diagram room to explain the regional network.

## Required relationship diagram — original editorial graphic

**Working title:** `Chengdu city, transport hubs and Sichuan branches`

This is the most important explanatory asset after the hero. It must be designed as an original schematic, not generated as a fake satellite map and not copied from an operator’s copyrighted map.

### Required nodes

- Central Chengdu: Chunxi Road/Taikoo Li, Tianfu Square, Kuanzhai/People’s Park side
- Airports: CTU southwest; TFU southeast
- Railway hubs: Chengdu East, South and West; Chengdu Station shown with a `dynamic project / verify current operation` note
- City-edge task: Chengdu Panda Base
- Western branch: Dujiangyan and Panda Valley
- Northern branch: Guanghan and Sanxingdui Museum
- Southern branch: Leshan; Mount Emei shown as a separate continuation, not the same-day default
- Independent northern Sichuan route: Jiuzhaigou, with `rail/flight + road handoff + own nights`
- Intercity edges: Xi’an and Chongqing

### Diagram rules

- Use directional relationships and broad relative position only; do not draw exact administrative boundaries.
- Do not print journey times, fares, train numbers, metro first/last times or live service frequency.
- Airports must be identified by both name and code: `TFU` and `CTU`.
- “Chengdu” must not appear as the destination label for every rail or airport connection; name the actual hub.
- Jiuzhaigou must be visually separated from the day-trip branches.
- Provide trilingual legend text or three localized exports from one shared source file.
- Include a note: `Planning schematic — not live navigation. Recheck the ticketed airport, station and transfer for the travel date.`
- Accessibility: every relationship expressed by line or colour must also be expressed by labels, arrowheads or line style.

## Panda-image rule

No new panda photograph is required for this Hub. The dedicated panda comparison already owns venue imagery. If central review later requests a supporting panda image:

- use a real, correctly identified venue photograph with explicit rights;
- record whether it is Chengdu Panda Base, Panda Valley or the separate Dujiangyan base;
- do not use a generic social-media repost;
- do not imply touching, feeding, a guaranteed cub, guaranteed activity or a particular crowd level;
- never generate a documentary panda photograph with AI.

## Production and rights checklist

Before an image is implemented:

1. Reopen the exact source page and confirm creator, licence and file identity.
2. Record the source URL, creator, licence URL, capture date, processing, final dimensions and SHA-256 in the central photo-provenance ledger.
3. Inspect the original and final crop visually; do not rely on filename alone.
4. Strip EXIF/GPS/device metadata from the public derivative unless location metadata is intentionally retained in the provenance record only.
5. Avoid close identifiable faces unless the licence and editorial use are appropriate.
6. Translate alt text and captions semantically; do not copy an English location label into Chinese or Korean.
7. For CC BY-SA assets, preserve attribution and mark the derivative; central review must confirm the project’s share-alike handling.
8. Do not use official airport, metro, railway or tourism maps as page art without a separate rights basis. Build the relationship diagram from verified facts in original design.
9. No photograph may be used to prove current operating conditions, routes, crowd levels or availability.
