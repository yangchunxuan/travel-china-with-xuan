# Image plan — Xi’an Destination Hub

Status: `DRAFT ASSET PLAN — REAL PHOTOGRAPHY ONLY`

Reviewed: 2026-08-15

This content-only draft does not add public assets. It defines the visual package for central production. Documentary-looking AI images are prohibited.

## Visual job

The page must explain Xi’an as four different planning fields:

1. the old-city core inside and around the City Wall;
2. the south-side Dayanta/Xiaozhai museum-and-pagoda cluster;
3. Lintong as a separate eastward archaeological excursion;
4. Mount Hua as an external Huayin/Weinan-direction extension.

A Terracotta-only hero would misrepresent the hub as a single-attraction page. The hero should show recognisable urban Xi’an and reserve the Terracotta image for the Lintong section.

## Relationship diagram specification

The diagram is an editorial planning graphic, not a scale map and not documentary photography.

### Required nodes

- northwest: XIY Airport, explicitly labelled as the Xianyang-side airport serving Xi’an;
- north: Xi’an North Railway Station;
- north edge of old city: Xi’an Railway Station;
- east/southeast: Xi’an East Railway Station;
- centre: City Wall, Bell/Drum Towers and Muslim Quarter approaches;
- south: Xiaozhai/Shaanxi History Museum and Dayanta;
- east: Lintong — Terracotta Warriors + Lishan Garden;
- farther east, outside the Xi’an field: Huashan North + Mount Hua, labelled Huayin/Weinan direction.

### Relationship rules

- use directional lines, not invented minute estimates;
- show Lintong inside Xi’an municipality but outside the central hotel field;
- show Mount Hua beyond the Xi’an city field;
- do not place XIY inside central Xi’an;
- do not imply one station serves every onward city;
- include “not to scale / verify live transport” in all three locales.

## Accuracy and contamination checks

Reject an image when:

- another city wall is labelled as Xi’an;
- an AI-generated or heavily composited cityscape is presented as a photograph;
- the Qin-Han Gallery is labelled as the Xiaozhai Main Building;
- a modern Tang-themed street is captioned as surviving Tang Chang’an;
- Mount Hua is presented as central Xi’an;
- a replica shop figure or performer is labelled as an excavated Terracotta Warrior;
- a dated event entrance is presented as a permanent City Wall rule;
- author, license version, attribution or share-alike processing is missing.

## Processing and handoff

1. Download only from the verified original file page.
2. Preserve a source copy outside the public asset directory.
3. Record source URL, author, license, access date and original dimensions.
4. Crop without removing the identifying landmark.
5. Export WebP at the required dimensions and strip unnecessary metadata.
6. Calculate SHA-256 for original and derivative.
7. Add source disclosure in all three locales where required.
8. Run desktop and mobile visual QA.
9. Do not add public assets in this content-only draft PR.
