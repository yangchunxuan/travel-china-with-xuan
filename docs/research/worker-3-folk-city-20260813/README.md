## Evidence method

- `history`: verifiable biography, dated material remains, institutional history, conservation or reconstruction record.
- `folklore`: belief tradition, local legend, ritual narrative, or community-transmitted account.
- `literature`: a work's setting, characters, narration, reception, or adaptation.
- `tourism-presentation`: modern naming, reconstruction, performance, IP use, visitor packaging, or operator claim.
- `editorial-judgment`: Homeground's proposed decision rule or route; never presented as an external fact.

Official tourism copy is used to document how a place presents itself, not as sole proof of literary or historical truth. Dynamic schedules, prices, access rules, ferry operations, performances, reservations, filming rules, and crowd controls are isolated in dynamic-fact tables.
