# Image-rights note

This research-only package includes no publishable image.

If central later approves a visitor module, obtain first-party or commercially licensed photography from the exact confirmed public venue. The frame must show a recognizable visitor floor or demonstration with the operator's permission and a capture date after the latest product change.

Do not use:

- conference news frames as evidence of everyday access;
- social-media reposts or screenshots from booking mini-programs without permission;
- factory, laboratory or training-base interiors photographed during a delegation;
- an AI-generated ‘documentary’ humanoid-robot showroom;
- a generic robot image that implies a specific Beijing venue.

An exterior wayfinding image is useful only if the official venue name and entrance are current. Avoid faces unless consent/release status is documented.
