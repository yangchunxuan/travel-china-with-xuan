## Fact classes

- **Official current fact:** a June 2026 Robot World route included a stated foreign-passport booking workaround.
- **Operator current fact with gap:** Robot Mall is promoted as a public ticketed experience, but a complete foreign identity/payment path was not published in sources reviewed.
- **One-off fact:** an organized delegation or public science day entered a facility on a named date.
- **Unsupported extrapolation:** ‘the factory is open to tourists’, ‘foreigners can book normally’, or ‘two robot venues can always be combined’.
- **Homeground judgement:** without direct confirmation, the access friction and product overlap outweigh the value of an evergreen guide page.
