## Decision module

### Can an independent foreign visitor currently plan a robot-industry day in Beijing?

**Not as an open industrial-district tour.** Plan at most one confirmed public-facing E-Town experience. Treat laboratories, pilot-production platforms, data-training bases and company facilities as non-public unless an official notice for the exact date says otherwise.

| Status | Place type | What the traveller should do |
|---|---|---|
| Conditional public access | Robot World | Use the current official reservation route. The June 2026 government instructions say a Chinese-ID holder must assist with the booking for a foreign passport holder, who then brings the original passport. Reconfirm this process before relying on it. |
| Public product, foreign workflow unverified | Robot Mall / Robot 4S experience | Ask the operator whether the exact venue is separate from Robot World and whether passport, foreign phone and payment details are accepted. Do not buy transport around an unconfirmed slot. |
| Event-only | World Robot Conference or a published open day | Follow that event's dated ticket, security and identity rules. The event does not establish year-round access. |
| Not a visitor site on present evidence | Innovation centre, pilot-production platform, data/training base, factory or laboratory | Do not approach expecting entry. A press visit, delegation or public-science event is not an evergreen operating rule. |

### Minimum confirmation script

Ask the operator in one message:

> I am an individual visitor using a foreign passport. For [date], which public venue can I reserve, what is its exact Chinese address and entrance, can I book without a mainland ID card or phone number, and what original document and payment method must I bring?

Save the operator's reply and a screenshot of the current booking page. If the reply is incomplete, build the day around another confirmed Beijing attraction and treat the robot venue as optional.
