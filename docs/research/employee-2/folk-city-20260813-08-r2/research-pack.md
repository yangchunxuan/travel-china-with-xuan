## 1. Completion decision

The cultural interpretation gate is closed, and two limited operating facts can be stated:

- A July 2026 national-government article says that Tianlong's “Yanwutang” presents timed Dixi excerpts every day and that the tourism excerpts are about ten minutes long. It does not publish the clock times or make a no-cancellation guarantee.
- The Guizhou Development and Reform Commission's 2026 government-price list says the Tianlong old-town ticket includes Dixi and tea. Inclusion in the priced ticket does not establish that a performance will occur at any particular arrival time or what remedy applies if it does not.

The article is still blocked because the following required facts remain unavailable from a current operator, protection body or other direct institution:

1. an accurate current performance timetable;
2. Dixi-specific cancellation, rescheduling and ticket-remedy rules for weather, performer absence and technical failure;
3. a current visitor-executable location for the active Yanwutang, distinguished from the old and new halls;
4. the official foreign-passport purchase, collection and admission workflow;
5. ordinary-visitor English interpretation, plot sheet, subtitles or other foreign-language support;
6. ordinary-visitor photography, video, flash, tripod, full-recording and commercial-publication rules;
7. a complete publishable image set showing the current venue, a close forehead/veil arrangement and a current rule, programme or official display.

This is not a judgement that no daily performance exists. It is a judgement that “daily timed” is not enough to build the continuous first-visit workflow required by this assignment. No public guide directory, metadata file or trilingual body has been created.
