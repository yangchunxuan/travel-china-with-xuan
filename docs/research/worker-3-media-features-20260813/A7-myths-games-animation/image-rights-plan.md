## Hero selection rationale

The selected Met image is a precisely catalogued historical object with explicit public-domain/Open Access status and a stable source record. It avoids unlicensed modern-IP imagery while giving the article an evidence-led example of adaptation across time and culture. Its complete portrait-format artwork is preserved inside a solid-colour landscape canvas rather than destructively cropped.

A game screenshot, film poster, celebrity image, generic temple, cosplay photograph or AI recreation must not be added as a substitute. The hero must never imply that this Japanese print is the sole “original” form of Sun Wukong, or that it depicts Nezha.

## Useful supporting assets, if separately cleared

1. A catalogue-level image of a specific Journey to the West edition or illustrated leaf, paired with an explanation that publication history and visual traditions changed over time.
2. A catalogued Nezha object with date, provenance, material and institutional interpretation visible in the caption.
3. A commissioned photograph of a named exhibition label beside its exact object, provided both are permitted and legible.
4. A rights-cleared photograph from a modern adaptation's official exhibition or production archive, explicitly labelled as that production's interpretation rather than historical evidence.

## What should remain HTML, not an image

- source tradition → adaptation choice → modern effect comparison;
- “confirmed / interpretation / promotional claim / unresolved” evidence labels;
- game, film, animation and tourism-intent split;
- location-claim verification flow;
- a compact chronology of selected adaptations;
- rights status and final-review dates.

These components will change as works and location campaigns appear. Keeping them as accessible text avoids freezing unstable claims inside an infographic.

## Capture and credit fields required for every approved asset

- exact object, production, performance or site identity;
- institution, venue or rights holder;
- creator/photographer and creation or capture date;
- catalogue/accession number where applicable;
- source URL and rights URL;
- licence or written permission record, including commercial/editorial scope;
- crop allowance and required credit line;
- confirmation that the crop does not remove evidence needed for interpretation;
- model/performer/venue permissions where relevant;
- review date and reviewer name.

Three-language alt text must be written only after the exact asset is approved. It should describe visible evidence, not infer mythology, identity, age, authenticity or emotion from appearance.
