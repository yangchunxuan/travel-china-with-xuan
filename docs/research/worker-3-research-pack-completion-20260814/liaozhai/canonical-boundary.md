# Liaozhai and Zibo — canonical boundary

The future page owns one visitor decision: how to read the integrated Pu Songling former-residence/memorial-museum core, *Liaozhai Zhiyi*, Liaozhai liqu and the specific modern Liaozhai City attraction without collapsing them into one “haunted” experience. Liuquan and the cemetery remain optional context.

It does not own:

- a ghost-story anthology or plot-summary page;
- claims that fictional people lived in Zibo;
- a complete Pu Songling biography;
- a general Zibo itinerary, transport guide or hotel guide;
- a horror-attraction ranking;
- screen adaptations, modern illustrations or long translated excerpts;
- a claim that the modern attraction reconstructs Qing daily life.

A latest-main and remote-reference scan on 2026-08-14 found no public guide or article branch with this exact owner. The r1/r2 research branches are research predecessors, not public-page owners.
