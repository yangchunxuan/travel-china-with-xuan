## Why each page earns an independent canonical

Each owner changes a concrete visit decision and needs evidence that cannot fit safely into a city-hub paragraph: at least four site-specific observation points, a bounded evidence problem, and a recoverable on-site sequence. A city hub may recommend the stop, but it cannot own the interpretation. Conversely, transport, tickets, hotel choice and complete city itineraries remain with their existing owners.

No exact owner was found for the ten locked tasks. The current Guangzhou hub mentions Chen Clan Academy as a stop but does not own a seven-craft, original-fabric-versus-museum-display reading. That is a reference relationship, not canonical duplication.
