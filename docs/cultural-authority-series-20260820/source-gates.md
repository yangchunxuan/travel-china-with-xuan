# Source and claim gates

Checked: 2026-08-20

## Authority order

1. Site management body, museum, archaeological institute or UNESCO property file.
2. National/provincial cultural-heritage authority, government gazette or public collection catalogue.
3. Peer-reviewed or university-press scholarship for interpretation that an institution does not establish.
4. Current operator notice only for access, closures, ticketing, photography and visitor flow.
5. Tourism marketing only as evidence of how a site presents itself.

Search snippets, copied attraction listings and social posts are discovery leads, never core proof. A World Heritage designation does not mean every visible component is ancient fabric. A museum reconstruction is identified as a reconstruction even when it is useful.

## Per-package minimum

| Package | Institutional anchors | Interpretive requirement | Claims that must stay bounded |
|---|---|---|---|
| Du Fu Cottage | Museum history, archaeological-site page, current notices | Museum/academic account of Chengdu period | Reconstructed hut is not Du Fu's surviving house |
| Jiangnan Gongyuan | Museum history, Nanjing local records, current museum notice | Exam-space scholarship or museum scholarship | Painting/model/rebuilt cells are not a complete surviving Ming-Qing compound |
| Imperial Kiln | Museum, Jingdezhen government, Palace Museum archaeology | Archaeological context for sherds and kiln remains | A repaired vessel is evidence, not an untouched court object found whole |
| Stele Forest | Museum catalogue/notices, Shaanxi heritage statistics | Curatorial or academic explanation of rubbings | Text, handwriting, carved stone and ink rubbing are different evidence objects |
| Rishengchang | UNESCO Pingyao file plus peer-reviewed piaohao studies | Network/accounting scholarship | Avoid “first modern bank” and market-share superlatives unless directly sourced |
| Tianyi Pavilion | Museum, Palace Museum, National Library | Collection-history scholarship | Present display access does not imply open consultation of originals |
| Turpan Karez | ICID WHIS record, Turpan government, site operator | Water-engineering explanation | ICID World Heritage Irrigation Structure is not UNESCO World Heritage |
| Qufu | UNESCO property, Qufu heritage administration, operator | Property-function history | Do not treat visitor ritual presentation as proof of one timeless practice |
| Shenyang Palace | Museum research/visitor pages and UNESCO file | Building-phase chronology | Later west-route additions are not evidence of the 1620s court layout |
| Chen Clan Academy | Museum and Guangzhou/Guangdong cultural authorities | Craft identification by institution | A current museum display is not automatically an original clan-academy object |

## Dynamic-fact rule

Every operational statement carries the review date 2026-08-20 in its package log. Bodies avoid fixed prices, opening hours, temporary-room promises and performance schedules. The traveler is directed to the exact operator page shortly before the visit. Where an official page is silent, the copy stays conditional rather than importing a blog's claim.
