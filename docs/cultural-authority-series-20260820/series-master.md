## Series purpose

This series helps an overseas visitor read one real cultural site in one city. Every page begins with a visit decision, separates evidence layers, and ends with a usable on-site sequence. It does not treat a city as scenery for a generic history essay.

## Shared evidence labels

- `history`: dated events, surviving fabric, archaeology, institutional records.
- `later-interpretation`: a defensible scholarly or curatorial explanation, named as such.
- `literature-or-tradition`: poetry, remembered story, ritual meaning or inherited narrative; not proof of a dated event.
- `tourism-presentation`: reconstruction, display, visitor route, performance or current marketing.
- `editorial-judgment`: Homeground's visit choice or reading method, never disguised as institutional consensus.

## Package gate

Each finished package contains `metadata.json`, natural EN/ZH/KO bodies, `source-log.md`, `dynamic-facts.md`, `canonical-boundary.md` and `image-plan.md`. The hero is a real, identified site image with a reusable licence. Additional images remain requests until the exact object, source and right are recorded; a generic old building never proves age, function or authenticity.
