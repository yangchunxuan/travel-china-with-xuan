# Canonical boundary — beijing-peking-opera-first-performance

## Owner

This page owns the traveller task: **choose one credible first Peking Opera (`京剧`, Jingju)
performance in Beijing, verify what the ticket actually buys, and watch it without depending on a
permanent subtitle promise**.

## Included

- Decision between a visitor-oriented highlights showcase, a named `折子戏` excerpt programme, a
  clearly labelled `全本/整本` production, and a gala or hybrid programme.
- A pre-purchase evidence checklist: exact title, producer/troupe, cast, format, synopsis, expected
  duration/intermission, language support, seat relationship, venue rules, entry documents and
  change/refund terms.
- A first-audience viewing method based on `唱念做打`, role types, orchestra, percussion, symbolic
  action and minimal stage objects.
- Failure recovery for absent/blocked subtitles, programme changes, wrong format, late admission,
  inaccessible language support and recording restrictions.

## Excluded

- A permanent Beijing event calendar, theatre ranking, ticket-reseller list or venue-by-venue buying
  tutorial.
- A general history of all Chinese opera, a Mei Lanfang biography, or a face-colour personality
  cheat sheet.
- Claims that short programmes are fake, that full plays are automatically better, or that any
  venue always provides English subtitles, a fixed duration or permissive photography.
- Sichuan Opera, Anshun Dixi, Mongolian long song and other performance traditions already owned by
  their dedicated guides.
