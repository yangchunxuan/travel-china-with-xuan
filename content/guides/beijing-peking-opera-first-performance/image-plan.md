## Explanatory assets for central acquisition

1. **Programme evidence:** a current official poster or venue-board photograph showing title,
   troupe, date and format. Obtain organiser reuse permission; do not reproduce a commercial poster
   merely because it is publicly visible online.
2. **Stage grammar:** a licensed performance photograph with the exact play, troupe, scene and date
   supplied by the rights holder. Caption only gestures, props or role type that the source confirms.
3. **Orchestra/percussion:** a venue-authorised image showing `文场` and/or `武场`, with musicians'
   consent and no claim that every seating plan places them identically.
4. **Subtitle sightline:** an empty auditorium or organiser-provided image showing the actual subtitle
   display for one named production; caption must state date, language and seat section.
5. **Audience rules:** photograph the exact venue's current no-photo/phone/late-admission notice in a
   public area; avoid tickets, QR codes and personal data.

## Use HTML instead of images

- Product-format comparison, pre-purchase checklist, role-and-sound guide and failure recovery stay
  as editable tables/cards. Do not create a face-colour infographic: colour meanings are contextual
  and a simplified chart would overclaim.
- No AI-generated documentary actors, theatre interiors, audiences, tickets or stage scenes.
- No unlicensed production stills, commercial posters, livestream frames or recognisable audience
  close-ups.
