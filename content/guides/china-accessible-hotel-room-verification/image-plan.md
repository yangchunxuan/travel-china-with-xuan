# Image plan

Status: **COPY READY — ASSETS NEEDED — CENTRAL WILL EDIT**

## Required hero

Commission or license one real, recent, property-authorised documentary photograph that demonstrates a verifiable accessibility feature without implying that the whole hotel is certified. Preferred options, in order:

1. an empty step-free hotel entrance showing the usable door and approach; or
2. an empty accessible bathroom detail showing the shower entrance and securely installed grab bars; or
3. an empty lift-to-room doorway sequence where the location and usage rights can be documented.

Record the property's full name and city privately in the asset ledger, photographer, capture date, written property permission or licence, original URL/file, crop, edits, dimensions and final SHA-256. The visible caption must state exactly what the photograph shows and must not extrapolate to the entire route.

## Privacy and security exclusions

- No guest, staff face, medical document or mobility-user portrait without explicit releases.
- No passport, booking number, payment screen, phone number, room number, key card or access credential.
- Do not show a complete evacuation plan, full floor geometry, CCTV view or restricted access-control detail.
- Crop or blur incidental personal data before editorial review; retain the unedited licensed original only in the controlled asset workflow.
- Do not stage a loose chair or temporary ramp as if it were a permanent accessible installation.
- No AI-generated hotel, room, street or documentary-looking accessibility scene.

Keep the measurement checklist, route chain, scenarios and recovery matrix as localised HTML rather than text baked into an image.
