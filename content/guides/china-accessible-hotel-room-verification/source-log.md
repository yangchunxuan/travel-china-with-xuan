## Editorial boundary

The copy keeps three evidence layers separate:

1. law and official standards describe duties, scope and technical baselines;
2. a platform label is listing information, not legal or functional certification;
3. only current, property-supplied evidence can describe the assigned room and today's route.

The article does not claim that every existing hotel must physically match every value quoted from a current engineering code. GB 55019-2021 applies to new, extended and renovated construction; the national accessibility law separately addresses existing facilities, planned alteration, maintenance and necessary alternative measures. Dimensions from the code are presented as an evidence floor, never as a prescription for every traveller's body or mobility device.

## Official sources reviewed

1. China Disabled Persons' Federation — *Accessible Environment Development Law of the People's Republic of China*
   - https://www.cdpf.org.cn/ztzl/zxzt1/2024/cjrqybzflfggzxxgkxt/xfjfl/lfstl/dbcd52526ef14aa0ba649e7feb25a18d.htm
   - Supports the scope of people with accessibility needs; standards and connection duties for new/altered/expanded public buildings; treatment of existing buildings; maintenance responsibility; and prohibition on occupying or damaging accessible facilities.

2. Huai'an Disabled Persons' Federation — full text of **GB 55019-2021**, *General Code for Accessibility of Buildings and Municipal Engineering Projects*
   - https://cl.huaian.gov.cn/col/8349_118464/art/w/17144928/1715582990210UdXHUSlL.html
   - Supports continuous routes, passages, doors, lifts, accessible-room location and connection, wheelchair turning, call devices, accessible bathrooms, bed-side passage of at least 1.20 metres, multi-sensory information, and detailed bathroom elements.

3. Beijing Municipal Government mirror of Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development Announcement No. 174 of 2021
   - https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/qtwj/202204/t20220412_2674451.html
   - Confirms that GB 55019-2021 is a mandatory engineering construction code, effective 2022-04-01, and supersedes conflicting mandatory provisions.

4. National Public Service Platform for Standards Information — **GB/T 47543-2026**, *Specification on accessible tourism service — Tourism hotel*
   - https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=11FB862766387228085E1B8F974590D7
   - The page showed **current** on 2026-08-20; published 2026-04-30 and effective 2026-08-01; supervised by the Ministry of Culture and Tourism.
   - Qualification: this is a recommended `GB/T` standard and non-equivalently adopts ISO 21902:2021. The official page withholds the full adopted text for copyright reasons. No unviewed clause is asserted in the copy.

5. China Disabled Persons' Federation — Ministry deployment to implement the accessibility law and construction standards
   - https://www.cdpf.org.cn/xwzx/clyw2/5536a8ea36af499c8c4196f18aa6815e.htm
   - Supports enforcement focus on the full life cycle, linkage of facilities, acceptance and maintenance.

6. China Disabled Persons' Federation — 2026 accessibility law awareness activity
   - https://www.cdpf.org.cn/xwzx/dfdt1/2fc60d439f794270856a7f5d697ee897.htm
   - Provides official context for the practical gap between a facility existing and being usable. Used for editorial framing only, not as a hotel-policy source.

## Property evidence still required from the traveller

For a real booking, request dated evidence tied to the actual property and room type: legal drop-off, kerb and slope, entrance hours, door clear openings and thresholds, lift door/cabin, corridor, room layout, bed transfer space and height, bathroom door, toilet transfer side, installed grab bars, shower threshold and seat, hand shower, basin clearance, alert/call equipment, and the backup room or route. The article does not certify any property.

## Depth and scenario audit

- Independent actionable information objects: **28 core objects**, plus sensory-access and public-area extensions.
- Scenarios: left-side wheelchair transfer; late-night walker arrival with entrance/lift failure; deaf solo traveller needing non-audio alert and text contact.
- Recovery chain: written must-have list → dated room evidence → protected/cancellable booking → pre-arrival reconfirmation → inspect before unpacking → verified replacement room/route → cancellation and relocation → privacy-safe evidence preservation.
- Dynamic maintenance: property condition, lift status, staff hours and room allocation should be reconfirmed close to arrival. Review official standard status at least every six months and whenever a replacement notice appears.
