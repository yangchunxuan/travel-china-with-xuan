# SEO brief — Shanghai to Suzhou day trip

Internal editorial record, prepared 2026-09-23. This is not public page copy.

## Measured search signal

- Semrush Keyword Magic, desktop database, read live 2026-09-23. Singapore: `shanghai to suzhou day trip` estimated 210 searches/month, KD 21%; `suzhou day trip` 140/month, KD 9%. Malaysia: corresponding estimates 20/month and 10/month, KD unavailable. Australia: 20/month and 40/month, KD unavailable. These are tool estimates with differing update dates, not additive forecasts of Homeground traffic or sales.
- Google Search Console, domain property, Web search, all countries/devices, 2026-08-24 to 2026-09-20: the existing English Suzhou-garden guide had 20 impressions and 0 clicks, average position 6.7. The query table is privacy-filtered and did not expose a reliable Suzhou query row. Do not infer zero demand from that absence.
- Existing garden guide owns garden choice and cultural interpretation; the four-city route-order guide owns multi-city sequencing. Neither owns a complete Shanghai-to-Suzhou same-day return decision. This new page owns that narrow task.

## Reader outcome and product boundary

- Answer whether one focused Suzhou day is viable, how to choose the exact four station names, which access legs and margins count, what to omit, and when one Suzhou night is preferable.
- Keep current fares, frequencies, last trains and garden admission date-specific. Source them to 12306 and garden authorities rather than embedding a stale number.
- The published five-day Shanghai–Suzhou tour contains the day trip; it is not sold on this page as a standalone one-day tour. The six-day three-city tour stays overnight in Suzhou. Do not transfer its museum inclusion to the five-day route.
- EN/ZH/KO answer the same decision and link to their matching product pages. One real existing Suzhou photo is reused with its existing provenance and required licence credit; no new/generated image.

## Follow-up measurement

After deployment and crawl, compare this exact URL in Search Console with a stable page/country/device/date filter. Track impressions, clicks and query mix separately from product clicks and enquiries. Publishing alone does not establish indexing or a traffic lift.
