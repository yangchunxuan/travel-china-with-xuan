# Dynamic facts — hostel-or-budget-hotel-china

Reviewed 2026-08-22, Asia/Taipei.

This page is deliberately built so that almost nothing in it is dynamic. The method is the
deliverable; the facts are pushed onto the property. What follows is the short list of items that
still need watching, and the much longer list of things the page refuses to state.

## Explicitly refused rather than published

Each of the following is a live, frequently asked question that this page **does not answer**,
because the honest answer is property-level or locality-level and any national statement would be
a guess:

- Whether hostels accept foreign guests.
- Minimum or maximum age for a dormitory bed.
- Gender arrangements in dormitories.
- Deposits, their size and their refund mechanics.
- Front-desk staffed hours and whether a night arrival can be registered.
- Curfews and locked front doors.
- Whether a private bathroom is included.
- Luggage storage availability, security and hours.
- Lift availability.
- Cancellation and no-show terms.
- Any price, any nightly rate and any claim about which type is cheaper.

The draft converts every one of these into a checklist question and states, in its own words, that
the answer belongs to one property in one place at one time.

## Freshness note for central

This page ages unusually well by construction. The realistic triggers for a rewrite are: an
amendment to the Measures; a new national regulation on accommodation registration; or a change to
the neighbouring guides' boundaries that would let this page take back some of what it currently
pushes out. A yearly review is sufficient.
