## Homeground editorial judgments

- The four-role classification and every threshold belong to Homeground, not to a railway, venue or hotel.
- A day trip here means no local sleep and must protect its local purpose plus a recoverable return or forward edge after the complete chain is written.
- One overnight must unlock a named cross-night, split-visit, recovery or forward-route gain; it receives no fixed time penalty.
- A true base usually needs several independent priorities or repeated local starts. “At least two” is a review heuristic, not an official night rule.
- Removal means the stop does not earn time in this route, not that the destination lacks value.
- Unknown inputs produce a conservative role range and a recheck field, never a forced category.
