# Image plan — Shanghai 24-hour parks reality check

Status: `COPY READY — ASSETS NEEDED — CENTRAL WILL EDIT`

## Hero requirement

Use a rights-clear **real nighttime photograph of a named Shanghai park's officially night-open public route or entrance**. Century Park is preferred because the body contains a sourced implementation example, but the exact route and current status must be rechecked before the shoot.

The image must show park-level evidence—not a generic Shanghai skyline or an unidentifiable dark garden. Include enough wayfinding, entrance context or recognisable public-route detail to support an accurate caption without implying that the entire park is lit or safe.

Required asset record:

- exact park, entrance and route/zone;
- capture date and local time;
- same-day confirmation that the photographed zone was officially open;
- photographer and assignment/licence record, original-file provenance and model-release assessment;
- no identifiable visitor used as an unconsented night-safety claim;
- 1600 × 1000 WebP derivative plus retained original.

Suggested alt text pattern: `The officially open public route near [entrance] in [named Shanghai park] at night.` Avoid `safe`, `fully lit`, `open everywhere` or `open permanently` unless the image and current official source support the narrower statement.

## Supporting original infographic

Create a Homeground-original verification stack with seven fields: current status → zone → entrance → lighting → toilet/facility → return transport → same-day notice. It must be drawn from the article's editorial method, carry `Checked 2026-08-13`, and say `Verify again on the intended day`.

The infographic must not reproduce a citywide park list, invent a safety score or imply that passing the checklist guarantees safety. Keep the editable source and export a 1600-pixel WebP with documented authorship.

## Optional evidence image

A separate rights-clear original photograph of an official park sign can show split hours for a zone or facility. The sign, park, capture date and surrounding context must be recorded. Do not use an old sign photo as current operating proof without a same-day source check.

## Prohibited substitutions

- no AI-generated documentary park, lighting, toilet or visitor scene;
- no generic skyline, Bund nightscape or stock garden presented as a 24-hour park;
- no municipal, district, operator or newspaper photograph unless reuse rights are documented separately from public webpage access;
- no long-exposure image that makes an actually dark route appear brightly usable;
- no caption that converts one photographed route into a whole-park safety or availability guarantee.
