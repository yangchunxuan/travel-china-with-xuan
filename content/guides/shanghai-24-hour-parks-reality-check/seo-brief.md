The sampled SERP was heavily official/local and Century-Park-led, with dated government announcements, park profiles, encyclopedic listings and older forum uncertainty. Search results can establish that a park was designated 24-hour, but rarely expose zone/facility parentheses or a same-day verification workflow.

## Result gap and Homeground information gain

- State immediately that 24-hour can mean a public route or zone, not every facility.
- Use the smallest operating unit: exact entrance, loop, toilet, playground, court, indoor venue.
- Preserve the official-current-channel handoff to Suishenban/Green Shanghai rather than publishing a permanent list.
- Keep the Minhang parenthesis as a concrete example: park 24 hours, lake loop 05:00–21:00 on the checked official page.
- Separate opening designation from lighting, facilities, weather/works, personal safety and return transport.
- Give clean failure exits rather than encouraging improvised entry or dark-route exploration.
