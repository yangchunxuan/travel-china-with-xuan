## Homeground editorial judgments

- Official listing is necessary but insufficient for a late visit.
- Verify the smallest operating unit the plan depends on: exact zone, gate, route, toilet, playground, court or indoor facility.
- If route lighting, toilet or return transport is unknown, move earlier, shrink the activity or leave.
- A ride-hail estimate is not a guaranteed vehicle, and park opening does not extend metro service.
- Never bypass a barrier, improvise beside water or works, or use the 24-hour label as a personal-safety promise.
