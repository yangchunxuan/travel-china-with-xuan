## Current passenger-station roster

The 2026-02-24 Chongqing transport authority report records actual holiday passenger departures at Chongqing North, Chongqing West, Chongqing East and Shapingba. All four therefore enter the principal urban-hub decision table. The same report includes other municipality stops, so the article explicitly tells travellers to follow any different station printed on an issued ticket. Chongqing Station at Caiyuanba stopped passenger service in 2022 and remained an active construction site in the official 2026-07-15 update, so it is explained as a misleading old label rather than a current option.
