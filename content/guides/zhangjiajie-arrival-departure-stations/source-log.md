# Source log — Zhangjiajie tour start and finish

Reviewed: 2026-09-20. Status: internal editorial record. This file is not rendered on the public page.

## Editorial purpose

Opened Chinese traveller posts and comments repeatedly treated the final station, luggage, hotel access and pickup handoff as part of the itinerary rather than an afterthought. Those human accounts were used only to select the questions this page answers. Dynamic transport facts and official names were verified separately.

The internal human-research set is the same five opened Xiaohongshu originals recorded in the companion `zhangjiajie-furong-fenghuang-route-order/source-log.md`. The public page does not expose or attribute that internal research.

## Official sources and fact boundaries

1. China Railway 12306: https://www.12306.cn/en/index.html
   - Authority for the exact Chinese station name and date-specific service, stops, fares and availability.
2. Hunan Provincial Government — current English station directory, used for the government English names and the Furong Town station/scenic-area distance: https://www.enghunan.gov.cn/hneng/Services/Live/Transportation/RailwayStations/202607/t20260703_34019377.html
3. Hunan Provincial Government — station order and approximate Furong Town/Fenghuang attraction distances: https://www.hunan.gov.cn/hnszf/hnyw/zwdt/202112/t20211206_21247807.html
4. Hunan Provincial Government — Zhangjiajie Hehua International Airport English name: https://enghunan.gov.cn/hneng/Services/Live/Transportation/Airports/202606/t20260604_33993921.html
5. Hunan Department of Transportation — DYG airport code: https://jtt.hunan.gov.cn/bsfw/ggfw/gjfw/jcfw/201904/t20190429_5325073.html

The page uses the official names Zhangjiajie Hehua International Airport (DYG), Zhangjiajie West Railway Station, Furong Town Railway Station and Fenghuang Ancient City Railway Station. It does not promise a train number, journey time, fare, seat, local shuttle, traffic time or vehicle stopping point. Those remain date- and booking-specific.
