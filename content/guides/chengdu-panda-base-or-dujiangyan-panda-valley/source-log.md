Other queries, including Jiuzhaigou versus Huanglong, Guilin versus Yangshuo and Huangshan city versus the mountain area, were useful but ranked below these three on the combined test of search clarity, first-party support and immediate content-tree value.

## Claim boundaries used in the copy

- Official fact: identity, address, current published ticket price, dated city-base hours, booking/document rules, maps and published services.
- Disclosed source conflict: Panda Valley's Chinese and English pages currently disagree on seasonal hours and limited walk-up handling, while the Chinese page omits the English page's seven-day booking window. The article sends readers to the official WeChat channel for the exact date instead of choosing one as truth.
- Homeground planning judgment: which venue fits a route, how much schedule space to protect and why visiting both on one day is usually a poor trade.
- Not claimed: current animal counts, guaranteed cubs or feeding, guaranteed activity, guaranteed outdoor viewing, fixed crowd levels, fixed taxi time, universal accessibility, or every facility being open.
