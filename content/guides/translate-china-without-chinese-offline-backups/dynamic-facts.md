## Durable rule

Preserve the original, translate one task at a time, and confirm consequential meaning with the responsible human operator.

## Never freeze

- a universal best translator
- a claim that every downloaded language supports every offline feature
- automatic accuracy for menus, safety signs or interfaces
- a translated medical, legal, immigration or allergy guarantee
