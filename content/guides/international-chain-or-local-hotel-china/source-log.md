## Claim ledger

### Official facts used

1. The State Council’s 2025 guide says foreign hotel guests present passports, Foreign Permanent Resident ID Cards or other valid documents at reception for registration. It describes RMB cash, bank cards, Alipay and Weixin Pay as hotel payment routes and specifically tells international-card users to ask the hotel in advance whether its card network is accepted.
2. The July 2024 seven-department accommodation measures say local departments and platforms should not use qualification requirements to restrict accommodation operators from receiving overseas guests, and platforms and operators should not illegally publish refusal notices. The body cites this only as a boundary and sends the full legal/refusal task to `foreigners-china-hotel`.
3. The State Administration for Market Regulation says the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System publishes registered business and credit information. The article explicitly says this does not certify a room, service quality, foreign-guest execution or a remedy.
4. SAMR’s official consumer guide confirms the 12315 platform and local 12315 channels for consumer complaints. The body does not describe them as emergency room-finding services or guaranteed compensation.

### Exact-property and group examples

- The official Four Points by Sheraton Qingtian page displayed that property’s address, telephone, check-in and check-out times, credit-card acceptance, 24-hour reception and Chinese and English service when checked on 2026-08-13.
- The Marriott reservation FAQ says third-party reservations may need the original booking channel for changes or cancellation and describes early/late check-in requests as subject to availability. These are Marriott’s own channel statements, not industry-wide rules.
- The official H World page for Crystal Orange Beijing Qianmen displayed that branch’s telephone, address, listed payment methods and the text `内宾` in the property title when checked on 2026-08-13. The copy treats this only as page evidence that triggers direct confirmation. It is not presented as law or a national hotel category.

### Editorial judgments, not official standards

- Better branch-specific evidence should outweigh brand familiarity when route fit is otherwise comparable.
- A late arrival, fixed train, accessibility need or complex deposit raises the value of documented execution and a backup property.
- Brand, property, operator and booking channel can own different parts of a failure.
- A safe-room deadline is a Homeground recovery recommendation, not an official time standard.

### Facts intentionally not frozen

- Any brand-wide level of English, foreign-card acceptance, foreign-guest handling or recovery authority.
- Property staffing, desk hours, room inventory, bed dimensions, deposits and payment terminals.
- The continuing wording of `内宾` or any other property/platform label.
- Current group participation, franchise or management arrangements.
