# Image plan

## Required hero

Commission or license a real documentary photograph at one identifiable, consenting hotel property in China. The frame should show the actual reception approach and a readable Chinese branch-name sign or property directory so the image supports the article’s “verify the exact property” task. A staffed desk may appear only if the staff member has given a model release; a people-free frame is preferred.

Deliver a 1600 × 1000 WebP crop with a clean text-safe area. Record:

- exact property and city;
- capture date;
- photographer and original-file reference;
- copyright owner;
- written property/photographer permission or the exact reusable licence;
- crop and colour adjustments;
- final SHA-256 after export.

The selected property is an editorial illustration only. Do not imply Homeground recommends it or that its current services represent its whole brand.

## Privacy and safety exclusions

The hero and any optional inline photographs must not show:

- passports, document numbers or faces on copied identity pages;
- booking confirmations, QR codes, phone numbers, payment cards or receipts;
- room numbers, key-card numbers or access codes;
- readable guest names on a screen, registration form or luggage tag;
- CCTV monitors, back-office systems or security procedures;
- identifiable guests without releases.

Do not photograph a staged passport handover. Do not use screenshots from Marriott, H World or a booking platform as the hero. Do not use a generic stock lobby with an unknown location or rights chain.

## Prohibited substitutions

- No AI-generated hotel, lobby, room, street or traveller documentary image.
- No invented international-versus-local split scene.
- No fake brand signage, altered property name or composited guest record.
- No asset copied from a property page without an explicit reusable licence.

If the rights and location evidence cannot be completed, retain the missing-asset status rather than supplying a plausible-looking replacement.
