# Image plan

1. Hero: A licensed real photograph of one of Beijing's eight major railway stations with its full Chinese station name clearly readable above or beside travellers. Landscape 16:10. The purpose is to make the station name—not generic high-speed-train imagery—the first visual decision.
2. Body 1: Place after the eight-station matrix. Use the real photograph of the subway entrance inside Beijing Chaoyang Railway Station's integrated transport hub. The caption may explain the railway–subway connection, but must not claim a visible floor, gate or route that the photograph does not show.
3. Body 2: Place near the wrong-station or luggage section. Use the real photograph of Beijing Fengtai Railway Station's central hall. The caption should support the need to confirm the station, hall and live signs without claiming that the image shows Qinghe, a taxi zone or a specific floor.
   - Hero selected: [Beijing Chaoyang Railway Station (20251220131259)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beijing_Chaoyang_Railway_Station_(20251220131259).jpg), N509FZ, CC BY-SA 4.0.
   - Body 1 selected: [Entrance of Metro Chaoyang Railway Station (20241215172322)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Entrance_of_Metro_Chaoyang_Railway_Station_(20241215172322).jpg), N509FZ, CC BY-SA 4.0.
   - Body 2 selected: [Central lobby of Beijing Fengtai Railway Station (20220620140021)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Central_lobby_of_Beijing_Fengtai_Railway_Station_(20220620140021).jpg), N509FZ, CC BY-SA 4.0.
4. Images that must be real photographs: Every railway station, station sign, departure board, ticket or phone screen, passenger, suitcase, subway entrance, taxi area and ride-hailing pickup zone. Hide personal ticket and passport data. Use only Homeground-owned, railway/municipal-authorised or properly licensed material; do not generate AI station or traveller scenes.
5. Infographic recommendation: One human-designed, source-checked schematic is useful: place Beijing, South, West, North, Chaoyang, Fengtai, Qinghe and Tongzhou by broad city quadrant, add the direct subway lines and typical rail corridors, and label it “orientation only—not to scale.” Do not ask an image model to invent a Beijing map, timetable, fare or travel time.
