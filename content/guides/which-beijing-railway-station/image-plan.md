# Image plan

1. Hero: A licensed real photograph of one of the five Beijing railway stations with its full Chinese station name clearly readable above or beside travellers. Landscape 16:10. The purpose is to make the station name—not generic high-speed-train imagery—the first visual decision.
2. Body 1: Place after the five-station matrix. Use a licensed real photograph of Beijing Chaoyang Railway Station's Line 3 transfer hall or B1 railway entrance, with both the metro line and railway-station wayfinding legible. This supports the updated direct-subway fact.
3. Body 2: Place near the wrong-station or luggage section. Use a licensed real photograph at Beijing Fengtai or Qinghe showing the railway entrance level, taxi/ride-hailing pickup direction or integrated metro transfer. The frame should reveal the physical handoff rather than simply show the building exterior.
4. Images that must be real photographs: Every railway station, station sign, departure board, ticket or phone screen, passenger, suitcase, subway entrance, taxi area and ride-hailing pickup zone. Hide personal ticket and passport data. Use only Homeground-owned, railway/municipal-authorised or properly licensed material; do not generate AI station or traveller scenes.
5. Infographic recommendation: One human-designed, source-checked schematic is useful: place South, West, Chaoyang, Fengtai and Qinghe by broad city quadrant, add the direct subway lines and typical rail corridors, and label it “orientation only—not to scale.” Do not ask an image model to invent a Beijing map, timetable, fare or travel time.
