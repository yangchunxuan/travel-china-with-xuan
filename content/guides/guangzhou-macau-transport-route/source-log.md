## Canonical boundary and search gap

This page owns the Guangzhou-address-to-Macau-hotel decision through either Zhuhai Station/Gongbei or Hengqin Station/Hengqin Port, including luggage nodes and failure recovery. It does not own Hong Kong–Macau transport, multi-entry eligibility, national rail boarding, Macau hotel rankings or a port timetable.

Search results commonly compare only the railway duration from Guangzhou South or call one port “closer.” They often omit the trip from a Tianhe/central Guangzhou hotel to Guangzhou South, the Macau-side endpoint, self-managed luggage through immigration, different port hours, and the risk that a 24-hour crossing outlives useful rail or hotel transport.

- Macao Customs listed the Border Gate as 06:00–01:00 and Hengqin Macau Port Area as 24 hours. Scope: Macau-side published hours only; temporary controls and mainland arrangements can change. Checked 2026-08-13.
- Macau official transport information identifies buses/taxis at the Border Gate and the Hengqin LRT connection. Scope: mode existence, not all-night operation or a hotel-specific shuttle. Checked 2026-08-13.
- NIA advises travellers to verify document/visa validity and use 12367 for assistance. No case-specific eligibility conclusion is made. Checked 2026-08-13.
