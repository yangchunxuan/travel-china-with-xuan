# Image plan

Status: **COPY READY — ASSETS NEEDED — CENTRAL WILL EDIT**

## Required hero

Obtain one real, licensed, property-authorised documentary photograph of an empty hotel corridor with a continuous illuminated emergency-exit sign and no visible obstruction. The image should teach “recognise the route from the assigned room” without revealing a hotel's full circulation plan.

Acceptable alternative: a tight, privacy-safe crop of a generic room-door evacuation instruction with the room number, “you are here” position and full floor geometry completely obscured. The caption must not name the property unless location and permission have been verified.

Record photographer, property/city evidence, capture date, licence or written permission, original URL/file, crop, edits, dimensions and final SHA-256 in the central asset ledger.

## Privacy and security exclusions

- No guest, staff face, child or identifiable reflection without explicit releases.
- No room number, key card, access credential, booking screen, name, phone number or passport data.
- No full evacuation map, complete floor geometry, fire-control room, CCTV field of view, restricted door-control detail or alarm panel close-up.
- Do not stage a blocked exit, prop open a fire door, move safety equipment or trigger an alarm for the photograph.
- Do not photograph an active emergency or interfere with evacuation.
- No AI-generated hotel corridor, exit, fire scene or documentary-looking hotel image.

Keep the timed checklist, night set-up, smoke decision, mobility matrix and recovery chain as accessible, selectable HTML in all three languages.
