## Safety and editorial boundary

This is a traveller-usability workflow, not professional fire inspection, legal advice or a substitute for on-site fire-rescue direction. The copy deliberately does not instruct a traveller to test an alarm, extinguisher, sprinkler, smoke detector, fire door or restricted exit. During an active alarm, smoke or fire, life-safety action and official direction replace the booking-recovery workflow.

The text separates:

1. operator and authority duties stated in law and standards;
2. what a guest can observe from the assigned room without interfering with equipment; and
3. what must be decided from current smoke, heat, staff and fire-rescue direction during an incident.

## Official sources reviewed

1. Ministry of Emergency Management — 2025 holiday travel safety press conference
   - https://www.mem.gov.cn/xw/xwfbh/2025n09y29xwfbh/
   - Identifies hotels and restaurants as crowded places and highlights locked safety exits, damaged fire equipment and other risks.
   - Related official release: https://www.mem.gov.cn/xw/xwfbh/2025n09y29xwfbh/fbyd_4258/202509/t20250930_560468.shtml
   - Supports the public reminder to notice hotel/guesthouse exits, fire facilities and evacuation routes.

2. National Fire and Rescue Administration / Beijing Fire and Rescue — *How to stay safely in a hotel*
   - https://www.119.gov.cn/kp/hzyf/bgfd/2022/18836.shtml
   - Supports reading the room-door route diagram, walking the route, recognising equipment, observing blocked passages and fire-door condition, reporting a hazard, following staff, leaving possessions and sheltering/signalling if smoke makes exit impossible.
   - Qualification: stable public education first published in 2021. Incident actions are framed by current conditions and official/on-site direction rather than as a rigid universal sequence.

3. National Fire and Rescue Administration — 2023 holiday safety notice
   - https://www.119.gov.cn/qmxfgk/tzgg/2023/39693.shtml
   - Supports keeping hotel exits and routes clear; guests noting exits and equipment; calling 119; and not taking lifts, returning for possessions or jumping blindly.

4. Ministry of Emergency Management — *Public-place fire self-rescue guidance*
   - https://www.mem.gov.cn/kp/shaq/201904/t20190401_366015.shtml
   - Supports observing layout, fire signs, routes, exits and stairs on arrival; calling 119; following staff/signs; avoiding panic; and lowering posture in smoke.
   - Qualification: public education from 2019, used only for stable preparedness principles and never to override an incident command.

5. National Fire and Rescue Administration — *Ten provisions for hotel and restaurant fire-safety management*
   - https://www.119.gov.cn/site1/kp/hzyf/ggylcs/2022/1251.shtml
   - Supports room-door evacuation diagrams, safety signs, unobstructed exits/routes, staff education, emergency plans and drills.
   - Qualification: primarily an operator-management document. The article uses it to explain operator responsibility, not to invite guest enforcement.

6. National Public Service Platform for Standards Information — **GB 13495.3-2026**, *Fire safety signs — Part 3: Requirements for installation*
   - https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=E430E8F3B52A6CD5AB7E2DEC00F5C1EE
   - On 2026-08-13 the official page showed **current**; published 2026-01-28 and effective 2026-08-01; supervised by the National Fire and Rescue Administration.
   - Official SAMR interpretation: https://www.samr.gov.cn/bzjss/bzjd/art/2026/art_0c3eff0c14854bd6a508f8bf5db6ee9d.html
   - Confirms scope across placement, installation and maintenance and replacement of GB 15630-1995. The copy does not repeat stale search-index wording that called the standard “forthcoming.”

7. Hunan Provincial Government mirror of the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development announcement — **GB 55037-2022**, *General Code for Building Fire Protection*
   - https://www.hunan.gov.cn/zqt/zcsd/202301/t20230131_29211615.html
   - Confirms a mandatory engineering code effective 2023-06-01 and its relationship to conflicting older mandatory provisions.
   - Qualification: provides the professional construction framework; it is not used to judge a property from a guest walk-through.

8. National Database of Laws and Regulations — *Fire Protection Law of the People's Republic of China* (current 2021 amendment)
   - https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?fileId=&id=ff8081817ab22e0c017abd909312060a&title=%E4%B8%AD%E5%8D%8E%E4%BA%BA%E6%B0%91%E5%85%B1%E5%92%8C%E5%9B%BD%E6%B6%88%E9%98%B2%E6%B3%95&type=
   - Supports operator duties for facilities, signs, maintenance, clear evacuation routes/exits, plans and drills, and the duty to report a fire.

## Depth and scenario audit

- Independent actionable information objects: **30**.
- Distinct structure: timed five-minute check → night set-up → active-smoke decision → mobility/sensory/family variants → pre-incident fault recovery → privacy boundary.
- Scenarios: night alarm with smoke outside; wheelchair user above ground level; deaf solo guest; family with children or older relatives.
- Recovery before an emergency: define the exact fault → duty-manager inspection → walk the cleared route → inspect a replacement room's route → relocate if no route works → preserve narrow, redacted evidence after safety is restored.
- Active incident rule: call 119 and follow current emergency direction; do not wait for platform support, film evidence, collect luggage or return.

## Dynamic maintenance

Recheck the Fire Protection Law and the two current standards at least every six months or after an amendment/replacement notice. Hotel route usability is more volatile than the law: renovation, stored items, room moves, equipment faults and shift procedures can change it on the day. The article tells readers to repeat the five-minute check after every room move.
