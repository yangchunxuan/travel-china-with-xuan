## Editorial use and crop boundaries

- The Hero may be used in cards and social previews with the full metadata credit. The museum name must remain legible; no claim that the pictured entrance is the only current gate.
- Body 1 keeps enough of the case and adjacent label context to show a museum display. Do not crop it into an unlabelled “Song robe” image.
- Body 2 is a texture close-up. The caption must identify that the structural term comes from the object record, not visual guesswork.
- Body 3 keeps damaged edges visible; they are evidence of a surviving fragment, not a defect to retouch away.
- Body 4 keeps the uneven surface visible and does not colour-correct it into a more saturated blue.
- None of the four object photographs should be used in a “what is on today” carousel without a fresh onsite confirmation.

## Optional future acquisitions, not launch blockers

1. **Sericulture Hall process sequence:** exact current display of cocoon/reeling/thread preparation, photographed with museum permission and a 2026 date. Capture the adjacent label; do not substitute a live village or factory.
2. **Weaving Hall loom:** one named loom or model plus its original/model/reconstruction label. Rights must cover publication; a working demonstration requires separate participant and performer consent.
3. **Conservation Gallery:** public-side viewing window or interpretation panel. Do not photograph staff, treatment records or objects under active care without institutional permission.
4. **Current campus route sign:** useful for building-to-building orientation after verifying the date and reuse right.

Official website photographs are reference-only unless the museum grants reuse permission. Social-media reposts and travel-blog images are not acceptable substitutes.
