## 1. Beijing Municipal Administration for Market Regulation — instant-boiled mutton guide: **VERIFIED, read in full**

- **Institution:** 北京市市场监督管理局 (Beijing Municipal Administration for Market Regulation)
- **Page title:** 天凉好涮肉！老北京涮羊肉全攻略
- **URL:** https://scjgj.beijing.gov.cn/zwxx/scjgdt/202512/t20251215_4342165.html
- **Published:** 2025-12-13 (page timestamp 2025-12-15) · **Retrieved:** 2026-08-22
- **Supports:**
  - **Broth base: plain water with ginger slices, spring onion sections, goji berries and dried shrimp.**
  - **Cuts named for the pot: 羊腿肉 (leg) and 羊上脑 (upper shoulder), 肥瘦相间** — fat interleaved
    with lean.
  - **Sides: 大白菜 (Chinese cabbage), 茼蒿 (crown daisy), 冻豆腐 (frozen tofu).**
  - **Classic dip: 芝麻酱 + 腐乳 + 韭菜花** (sesame paste, fermented bean curd, chive-flower sauce).
  - **Cooking order: 涮煮时可遵循"先菜后肉"的顺序** — vegetables before meat.
  - **Long-boiled hotpot broth is relatively high in purines and fat; people with gout or high blood
    lipids should be cautious about drinking it.**
  - Freshness marks when buying: fresh mutton is bright red or pink with fine muscle fibre and
    evenly distributed glossy fat; dark or blackened meat may not be fresh; a mild gamey smell is
    normal but a fishy or pungent one is not; on pre-packed rolls, check production date, shelf life
    and manufacturer.
- **Cannot support:** any cooking time in seconds; any restaurant's individual practice; prices;
  which houses are good.
- **Dynamic:** low. This is guidance, not an operating status. Re-check annually.

## 2. Beijing Municipal Administration for Market Regulation — buying beef and mutton: **VERIFIED, read in full**

- **Page title:** 关于选购牛羊肉的消费提示
- **URL:** https://scjgj.beijing.gov.cn/hdjl/xfts/202001/t20200120_1619155.html
- **Published:** 2020-01-20 · **Retrieved:** 2026-08-22
- **Supports:**
  - **纯牛羊肉:** slaughtered, then divided and trimmed with **nothing added**, sold directly; cut
    physically into blocks, rolls or slices; **100 per cent pure**; a primary agricultural product.
  - **调理牛羊肉:** beef or mutton (or edible by-products) as the main ingredient, with **other
    auxiliary materials and food additives added** after initial processing; **not fully cooked,
    non-ready-to-eat**; stored, transported and sold chilled or frozen; **调理羊肉卷 is named as an
    example**.
  - **纯羊肉卷** is mutton divided, quick-frozen and then sliced, and can be 100 per cent mutton;
    **调理羊肉卷** may have other auxiliaries and additives worked in and is **not** 100 per cent
    mutton.
  - **"调理牛、羊肉制品中的原料肉含量，当前还没有相关标准进行规定"** — there is currently **no
    standard governing raw-meat content** in these products, so content varies greatly between
    manufacturers and cheaper rolls appear on the market; do not judge on price alone.
  - Label reading under GB 7718: the name must reflect the food's true attribute in a prominent
    position; **the words 调理 or 风味 indicate a prepared product**; the ingredient list must be
    shown in descending order of quantity, and pure mutton generally lists only mutton.
  - Named contributing experts from 中国肉类食品综合研究中心 (China Meat Research Centre).
- **Cannot support — and this is stated explicitly in the body:** **anything about a restaurant
  menu.** This advisory is about *packaged retail food*. The page says so and tells the reader to
  ask rather than to assume the vocabulary transfers.
- **Dynamic:** medium. The "no standard yet" statement is the item most likely to change; if a
  minimum-content standard is issued, the menu callout must be rewritten.

## 4. Beijing Center for Disease Prevention and Control — food safety: **VERIFIED, read in full**

- **Page title:** 北京疾控提醒您：美味佳肴 别忘了食品安全
- **URL:** https://www.beijing.gov.cn/fuwu/bmfw/sy/jrts/202402/t20240210_3561570.html
- **Published:** 2024-02-10 · **Publisher of record:** 北京市疾病预防控制中心 · **Retrieved:** 2026-08-22
- **Supports:**
  - **Definition of 生熟分开:** "raw" means raw material still requiring heat treatment (raw meat,
    poultry, eggs, seafood, vegetables); "cooked" means food eaten directly. **Utensils and
    containers for the two must be separate**, including knives and boards.
  - **烧熟煮透 general principles:** poultry until the juices run clear and no pink remains inside;
    eggs and seafood until fully boiling; soups and stews boiled for **at least one minute**, with
    large pieces such as a whole chicken needing longer; reheated cooked food heated right through.
  - **"绝大多数微生物喜欢室温的环境，高于70℃，或者低于4℃就很难存活"** — most micro-organisms
    struggle to survive above 70 °C or below 4 °C.
- **Cannot support:** any per-slice timing for thin mutton in a copper pot. **No such figure exists
  in any source consulted, and none is published on this page.**
- **Dynamic:** low.

## 5. Beijing Municipal Health Commission — shared serving: **VERIFIED, read in full**

- **Page title:** 餐桌文明——分餐制 · **Article source credited on the page:** 北京朝阳医院
- **URL:** https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/bmfw_20143/jkzs/jksh/202106/t20210623_2419757.html
- **Published:** 2021-06-23 · **Retrieved:** 2026-08-22
- **Supports:** shared-plate eating spreads pathogens via utensils, naming hand-foot-and-mouth
  virus, **Helicobacter pylori**, viral hepatitis and norovirus; **China's average H. pylori
  infection rate is given as 59 per cent, and 40 per cent in children**; H. pylori is described as
  classed by the WHO as a group one carcinogen; the practical rule **"公筷（勺）夹菜，私筷（勺）
  进食"** — serve with the communal pair, eat with your own.
- **Used with restraint:** the body cites the 59 per cent figure and the serving-chopstick rule, and
  nothing else. The 40 per cent figure for children and the WHO carcinogen classification are **not
  used in any of the three languages** — both were cut during the length edit and not restored, and
  a grep for "carcinogen", "致癌" and "발암" across the three bodies returns zero. Nothing beyond the
  page is claimed.
- **Dynamic:** medium — an epidemiological figure with a publication date. It is attributed to its
  publisher and its date in the source list rather than presented as a current measurement.

## 6. Dongcheng District People's Government, Beijing — Donglaishun technique: **VERIFIED, read in full**

- **Page title:** 东来顺涮羊肉制作技艺
- **URL:** https://www.bjdch.gov.cn/ztzl/cyqn/jsmsjy/202407/t20240730_3762490.html
- **Published:** 2024-07-16 (page timestamp 2024-07-30) · **Retrieved:** 2026-08-22
- **Supports:** Donglaishun founded in Guangxu 29 (1903) at the north gate of the old Dong'an
  Market; founder 丁德山; a master knife chef recruited from 正阳楼 outside Qianmen, **whose
  distinctive expertise covered the sheep's place of origin, the cut of meat used and the slicing
  technique**; slices laid on blue-and-white porcelain thin enough for the plate's pattern to show
  through, and watching the master slice becoming a sight in itself; **the old Beijing habit of
  cooking in a copper pot has been kept**; a cloisonné pot was also developed; **the charcoal used
  is machine-made, burns a long time and does not pollute**; **the technique entered the national
  intangible cultural heritage list in 2008**.
- **Cross-checked** against 非遗智造局之舌尖上的东城,
  https://www.beijing.gov.cn/renwen/bjgk/dcgk/fy/202211/t20221111_2856995.html (Beijing Municipal
  Government portal, source 东城区政务服务管理局, 2022-11-11, retrieved 2026-08-22), which carries the
  same account in the same wording.
- **Cannot support:** anything about Donglaishun today — its branches, prices, menu or quality. The
  body names it only as a documented method, and says so.
- **Dynamic:** low. This is a heritage record.

## 7. Beijing Municipal Government portal — Yangfang Shengli: **VERIFIED, read in full**

- **Page title:** 阳坊胜利涮羊肉 (Changping district intangible heritage)
- **Source credited on the page:** 昌平区政务服务管理局
- **URL:** https://www.beijing.gov.cn/renwen/bjgk/cpgk/cpfy/202304/t20230403_2965858.html
- **Published:** 2023-04-03 · **Retrieved:** 2026-08-22
- **Supports:** Yangfang lay on an important north-western route into the capital region from the

  rather than plain water, which is the page's central point about house variation.
- **Cannot support:** current operations, branches, prices or quality.
- **Dynamic:** low.

  flatbread); **2007: the technique entered the Beijing municipal intangible heritage list**.

### Audit note on the halal sentence

Source 8 states 清真 explicitly for Yitiaolong. Source 7 states that Yangfang's sheep market grew out
of a **Hui community area**, which is context rather than a statement about the restaurant company
today. Because "two of the three are halal Muslim restaurants" could not be verified to the same
standard for all three, the sentence in all three languages is written as a statement about **who
built this tradition in Beijing**, is immediately followed by an explicit refusal to verify anyone's
certification, and names no certification body. **Central review is invited to tighten or remove
this sentence** if it prefers a stricter standard; nothing else in the article depends on it.

## Absolute expressions audited after drafting

- **No cooking time in seconds appears in any language.** The one place a reader expects one is a
  callout whose entire subject is the absence of such a figure.
- **No price, no queue time, no opening hour, no branch address.**
- **No claim that any house is best, most authentic or original.** The four-way table is explicitly
  labelled as not a ranking in all three languages.
- **No national generalisation from one operator.** Every house-specific fact in the comparison
  table is attributed to that house's own record in the same cell.
- **No allergen or dietary guarantee.** Refused explicitly.
- The word "always" does not carry any factual load anywhere in the body; the three "not specified"
  cells in the comparison table are deliberate, and record that a source is silent rather than
  filling the gap.

## Sources deliberately not used

Restaurant rankings, food-media listicles, OTA pages, recipe blogs and social posts were found in
quantity for this topic and **none was used**. In particular, the widely repeated claim that mutton
is cooked "in seven seconds" or "as soon as it changes colour" traces to no authority found here and
is contradicted in the body. The hero image's own Commons caption on a sibling file
("Raw mutton changes color after a few seconds") is a photographer's caption, **not a source**, and
is not used as one.
