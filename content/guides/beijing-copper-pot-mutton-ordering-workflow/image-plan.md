### Why this image

It shows the article's specific entities rather than a generic food or city picture. Visible in one
frame are the copper pot and its charcoal chimney, the clear broth this page describes, mutton
presented **both** as flat hand-laid slices and as rolls — which is exactly the menu distinction the
article teaches — the sesame-paste bowl the page calls the seasoning, pickled garlic, and the sesame
flatbread named in Yitiaolong's Ministry of Commerce record as the staple. A reader who has read the
page can name almost everything on the table, which is the test a hero should pass.

It is also **honest about the moment it shows**: the broth is up but nothing has been cooked yet,
which matches the page's argument that the first round is a decision rather than a spread.

### Disclosure: the restaurant is identifiable

The Commons file names the location as **Yangfang Shengli**, and the printed paper placemats on the
table carry that operator's branding. Two things follow, and both are deliberate:

1. **The location is stated rather than hidden.** Concealing it would be the dishonest option when
   the file name and the placemats both carry it.
2. **The body does not recommend this or any restaurant.** Yangfang Shengli appears in the article
   only where the Beijing municipal government's Changping heritage record documents its method, and
   the article states in all three languages that naming a house is not recommending it. The
   `heroCredit` line describes the subject as an instant-boiled mutton table rather than as a
   restaurant endorsement.

If central review considers the visible placemat branding to be advertising, the correct fix is a
different Commons file, not a crop that hides the branding while keeping the venue — the alternative
candidates are listed at the end of this file.

### Licence and provenance checks performed

- Licence, author, description and date read from the Commons file page's own `extmetadata` via the
  MediaWiki API. **Nothing was inferred from a search thumbnail or a reverse-image result.**
- CC BY-SA 4.0 requires attribution and share-alike on the derivative. Attribution appears in the
  `heroCredit` block of all three locales and here; the derivative is stated as CC BY-SA 4.0 in each
  place.
- The image is a photograph. **No AI-generated image is used anywhere in this guide**, and no
  AI-generated image was accepted from any source — including source 3 in `source-log.md`, whose
  own footer discloses that some of its illustrations were AI-generated, and from which no image was
  taken.

### Privacy and content checks

- **No people are visible.** No hands, no faces, no reflections carrying a person.
- No identity documents, bank cards, tickets, order screens, receipts or telephone numbers.
- The placemats carry the operator's decorative print and illustrations. These were inspected at
  full resolution: they contain brand text and drawings, and **no telephone number, address, order
  number or personal data is legible**.
- The only other legible text is a Chinese label on a small finger bowl.

### A caption that is not a source

The Commons description of a sibling file by the same photographer reads "Raw mutton changes color
after a few seconds." That is a **photographer's caption, not an authority**, and it is the exact
claim this article refuses to publish. It is recorded here so that a future editor does not mistake
it for corroboration.

### Crop review

- **Desktop (wide):** the pot sits dead centre with its plume of steam in the upper middle, and the
  surrounding plates ring it symmetrically. The 6 per cent vertical trim removes only the outer
  edges of the topmost plates and the near ends of the chopsticks; the pot, the steam, both styles
  of mutton, the dip bowls and the flatbread all stay in frame.
- **Mobile (390 px):** the shared template renders the same 1.6:1 ratio. Because the subject is
  centred and radial rather than horizontal, nothing essential falls into the outer margins that a
  narrow viewport can clip. Verified in the browser pass recorded in `canonical-boundary.md`.

### Age of the photograph

Taken January 2020. It is used for the arrangement of a copper-pot table, which is stable, and for
no claim that depends on a date. No fact in the article rests on this image.
