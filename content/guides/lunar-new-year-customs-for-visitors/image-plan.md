# Image plan — Spring Festival, Chunyun and visitor boundaries

Status: `COPY READY — ASSETS NEEDED — CENTRAL WILL EDIT`

## Hero requirement

Use a rights-clear **real photograph of a named Chinese railway station's public concourse during that year's Chunyun period**. The preferred commission is a Homeground original showing wayfinding, luggage and public passenger movement without using compressed crowds as spectacle.

Required asset record:

- exact station and concourse/entrance;
- capture date and confirmation that it fell inside the officially announced Chunyun period;
- photographer, licence or assignment agreement, model-release assessment and original-file provenance;
- confirmation that station security, ticket screens and identity documents remain legible only where lawful and privacy-safe;
- 1600 × 1000 WebP derivative plus the retained original.

Suggested alt text pattern: `Travellers in [named station] during the [year] Spring Festival transport period.` Do not describe the image as “the world's largest migration”, a unique-person count or a private family reunion.

## Optional supporting visual

An original editorial diagram can separate four clocks—official holiday, Chunyun, venue schedule and host-family schedule—without entering annual dates. It must be clearly labelled as an explanatory model, not an official calendar.

## Prohibited substitutions

- no AI-generated documentary crowd or station scene;
- no generic red-lantern stock image standing in for Chunyun;
- no private reunion meal, ancestor practice, child or red-envelope exchange without specific informed consent and publication rights;
- no government or news photograph unless reuse rights are documented separately from public webpage access;
- no crowd image cropped or captioned to imply danger, uniform behaviour or a world-record headcount.
