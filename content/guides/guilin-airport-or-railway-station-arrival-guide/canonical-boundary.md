# Canonical boundary — guilin-airport-or-railway-station-arrival-guide

## Owns

- Identity and choice among Guilin Liangjiang International Airport (`桂林两江国际机场`, KWL), Guilin Railway Station (`桂林站`), Guilin North (`桂林北站`) and Guilin West (`桂林西站`).
- The complete arrival chain from the booked gateway to the exact Guilin hotel or onward base: exit process, baggage, station/terminal-side connection, waiting, road or rail handoff, final address and check-in.
- How exact hotel pin, onward direction, luggage, group mobility and late arrival change the gateway decision.
- Error prevention for mistranslated or truncated station names.
- Recovery after booking or reaching the wrong station, after a late arrival, or when a luggage-dependent connection fails.
- A first-time arrival checklist and a dynamic-fact release checklist for these four gateways.
- Yangshuo Station (`阳朔站`) only as a disambiguation item and a referral into the existing Guilin–Yangshuo canonical guide.

## Does not own

- `guilin-yangshuo-transport-route`: rail versus road versus Li River cruise between Guilin and Yangshuo, in either direction, plus the detailed Yangshuo final-leg comparison.
- A future Longji/Longsheng route guide: village, terrace entrance and route-specific mountain transfer decisions.
- `china-high-speed-train-first-time-guide`: national 12306 booking, passport, ticket collection, station-entry, security and boarding procedure.
- `china-private-transfer-or-public-transport`: the general China-wide private-transfer versus public-transport decision.
- `china-last-night-before-international-flight`: where to spend the last night before an international departure.
- `china-hotel-near-metro-or-attraction`: general hotel-area selection.
- Train or bus timetables, fixed fares, first/last services, driver rankings, private-driver lists or a catalogue of every hotel–gateway combination.
- A claim that Yangshuo Station, any Guilin station or the airport is universally “best.”
- A new or stronger paid-service call to action.

## Merge rule

Future queries whose primary question is “KWL, Guilin, Guilin North or Guilin West?” or “How do I turn that arrival into my real hotel/onward-base chain?” merge into this page. Queries whose primary question is “train, car, bus or cruise between Guilin and Yangshuo?” merge into `guilin-yangshuo-transport-route`.

Do not create separate pages for each of the three Guilin railway stations, an airport-versus-station micro-comparison, late-night Guilin arrival, wrong-Guilin-station recovery or luggage at these gateways unless a genuinely distinct user intent and owner is documented first.
