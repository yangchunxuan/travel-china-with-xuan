## Honest gaps recorded

- Bank of China: no current dedicated retained-card procedure page found on official domains during this review; only a 2016 media-reprint item describing a self-retrieval password window. NOT cited in the body.
- No PBoC regulation page specific to 吞卡 found publicly; the regulatory-grade anchors used are the bank charters themselves.
- The ICBC FAQ states 30 natural days while another ICBC product guide mentions 20 working days before destruction — version differences exist between bank documents; the body therefore presents windows as "about thirty days" per-bank rules rather than a universal figure.

## Editorial decisions

- Retrieval procedures always attributed to their specific banks; explicitly framed as non-uniform across banks.
- Fraud-protection section enforces PIN/code secrecy, independent number verification, refusal of stranger retrieval offers, and no remote-control app installs.
- Same-day fallbacks presented with setup-time caveats; nothing promised instant.
- No real card numbers, PINs, verification codes or passport numbers appear anywhere.
