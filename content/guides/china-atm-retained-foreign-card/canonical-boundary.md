## Why a new canonical instead of updating an old page

The existing ATM guide owns the no-cash money failure. Retained-card recovery is a physical-object problem with a different actor set (machine-owning bank's retention box, passport-based retrieval at a specific outlet) and different fraud surface (strangers offering to extract cards). Folding it in would blur two distinct first questions users ask.
