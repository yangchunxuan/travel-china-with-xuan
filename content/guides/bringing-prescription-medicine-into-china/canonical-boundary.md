# Canonical boundary — bringing-prescription-medicine-into-china

## Canonical owner

This page owns one pre-trip decision for a short-stay foreign traveller personally carrying prescription medicine through a mainland China passenger port: identify the active ingredient, separate ordinary from potentially controlled or otherwise special items, prepare supporting evidence, obtain an official answer where needed, and choose a truthful Customs disclosure route.

The primary intent is **pre-trip**. A reader should be able to reach one of four outcomes without another page: carry a bounded personal-use supply with evidence; pause and confirm an ingredient; change the medicine or travel plan with the prescriber; or disclose it to Customs for an on-site decision.

## It does not own

- Diagnosis, prescribing, dose changes, treatment interruption, substitution safety or advice to stop medicine.
- A brand-name whitelist, a universal days-of-supply limit, a clearance probability or an individual legal conclusion.
- Commercial import, courier/postal shipment, pharmacy procurement in China, drug registration or NMPA business licensing.
- Blood products, biological products, syringes or other medical devices where separate health-quarantine or aviation rules may apply.
- General red/green-channel rules, food/plant/animal-product biosecurity, airline cabin/baggage rules, or destination-country rules on the return trip.
- Hong Kong or Macao, which have separate jurisdictions and must be sourced separately.

## Conflict handling

The General Administration of Customs and current national regulations are the controlling sources for passenger supervision and controlled medicines. The 2026 Chongqing Government English FAQ, attributed to Chongqing Customs, is useful traveller-facing interpretation: it advises supporting documents and proactive declaration, but it does not create a universal brand list or fixed quantity for every medicine.

If a general Customs page, the local English FAQ, a controlled-drug list, a prescriber or an airline uses different wording, record the authority, jurisdiction, date, ingredient and dosage form. Do not silently choose one version. Customs makes the port decision; the page must narrow its conclusion and direct the traveller to 12360 or the actual arrival Customs office before travel.

If 12360 cannot be reached from abroad, the direct port contact cannot be located, language fails or no formal answer arrives, silence is not permission. The medicine remains unresolved and the traveller returns to the prescriber-led no-carry or alternative plan without changing treatment independently.
