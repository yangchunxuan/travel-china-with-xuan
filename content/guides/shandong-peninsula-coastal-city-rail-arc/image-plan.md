# Image plan — Shandong Peninsula coastal rail arc

**Status: licensed, location-accurate real assets needed; no files bundled.**

## Hero

- Exact location: a clearly identified passenger railway station in Qingdao, Yantai or Weihai, preferably a composition that shows the station name and travellers without readable private ticket data.
- Required elements: rail as the organising device, an unambiguous station identity, ordinary intercity travel rather than a generic beach.
- Forbidden: a train from another province; a composited route sign; readable passports or tickets; an unnamed seafront used to represent all three cities; AI documentary imagery.
- Crop: 1600 × 1000 landscape; retain station identity and allow text-safe space without cloning or removing infrastructure.
- Alt EN: `A location-verified Shandong Peninsula railway station with travellers beginning a coastal city journey`
- Alt ZH: `经地点核实的山东半岛铁路车站与开始海岸城市旅行的乘客`
- Alt KO: `산둥반도의 위치가 확인된 철도역에서 해안 도시 여행을 시작하는 승객들`
- Rights: Homeground-owned original or directly licensed work with author, date, exact station, licence, original and derivative hashes.

## Supporting set

1. Qingdao: exact old-city/Zhanqiao-side urban coast, with date and location recorded.
2. Yantai: exact Zhifu axis—Yantai Hill, Chaoyang Street or Suochengli; do not use Penglai or another district as central Yantai.
3. Weihai: exact managed public coast, with any seasonal access state visible but not claimed evergreen.
4. Optional original schematic: three city nodes with removable middle/end node and station-pair labels. It must say `conceptual rail arc—check 12306`, contain no timetable or copyrighted map tiles, and remain supporting rather than documentary.

Each city photo identifies only its city role; it cannot prove current weather, rail operation or crowd level.
