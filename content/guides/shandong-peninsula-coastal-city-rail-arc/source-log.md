# Research and source log

Reviewed: 2026-08-13 (Asia/Shanghai).

## Scope and evidence

- Canonical task: assign non-repeating roles to Qingdao, Yantai and Weihai, then connect or delete them through exact station pairs.
- China Railway 12306 is the only source used for actual date-specific train search. Municipal service notices prove current network connectivity or a dated adjustment, not permanent frequency.
- City-government tourism pages establish the editorial role of Qingdao's old-city-to-bay coast and Yantai's Zhifu port-history axis.
- Weihai's April 2026 notice is used as a dated example of seasonal coastal access control. Its April–October 2026 dates must not be copied into a later season without a fresh notice.
- Homeground judgment: a city earns a hotel change by adding a different action; remove a stop if its only role is another beach, sunset and seafood meal.

## Dynamic recheck before publication

Exact train date, number, station pair and inventory; station construction or changed pickup zones; hotel-to-station transfer; coastal road restrictions; island/boat operations if later added; severe-weather notices. Never convert a temporary service or restriction notice into an evergreen claim.
