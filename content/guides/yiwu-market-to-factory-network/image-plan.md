## Hero

- Exact location: Yiwu International Trade Market; the source does not identify the current district or booth.
- Required elements: aisle scale, product displays and booth-scale trade; no confidential screens, documents or readable personal contact details.
- Avoid: a Guangzhou wholesale market, generic warehouse, staged handshake, unidentified factory, empty promotional rendering or an image implying supplier endorsement.
- Crop: 16:9 with a clear aisle axis; protect signs from edge crops.
- Alt EN: `Souvenir plates and mugs displayed inside Yiwu International Trade Market in August 2014`.
- Alt ZH: `2014年8月义乌国际商贸城内陈列的纪念盘和杯具`.
- Alt KO: `2014년 8월 이우 국제상무성 내부에 진열된 기념 접시와 머그잔`.

## Supporting images

1. Official wayfinding or category signage photographed in the exact current district; record capture date.
2. Public logistics interface viewed lawfully from outside operational/loading space.
3. A simple editorial diagram: booth → trading/legal counterparty → production → consolidation → logistics. This should be an original information graphic, not a supplier map.

Rights record: creator, date, exact district/aisle, licence, releases where faces are identifiable, and permission for any close product photograph. Do not use vendor social posts, trade-fair news frames or an arranged factory tour without explicit publication rights.
