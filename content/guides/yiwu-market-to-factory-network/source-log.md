## Fact boundaries

- Official current fact: Yiwu's market economy spans multiple market blocks and service layers.
- Long-term pattern: display, trade, production, consolidation and logistics may be handled by different organizations and places.
- Homeground judgement: one product-family evidence chain teaches more than a superficial full-complex tour.
- Never inferred: supplier quality, factory ownership, labour conditions, compliance or transaction safety.

Recheck before publication: current district/category directory, public opening, service-point location and photography rules. The page is educational, not supplier due diligence, ranking, lead generation or legal/commercial advice.
