## Freshness note for central

This page's highest-risk items are 3, 4 and 9. Items 3 and 4 describe an airport arrangement barely
a year old that is still settling; item 9 is weather-driven and can change within a day. A
quarterly review is the minimum, and the Lanzhou section should be re-read whenever the Gansu
airport group publishes a terminal notice.
