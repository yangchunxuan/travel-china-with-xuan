## Direct editorial answer

Yangshan Phase IV automates and remotely controls much of the routine container movement within a controlled terminal. TOS plans the work, ECS coordinates executable equipment tasks, cranes and AGVs move containers, and people plan, monitor, maintain, control exceptional operations and recover failures. “Automated” must not be converted into “no humans,” “AI decides everything” or “every movement is autonomous.”

## Public-access audit
