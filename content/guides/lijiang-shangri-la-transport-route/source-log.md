## Canonical boundary and search gap

This page owns the Lijiang-to-Shangri-La transport choice plus the station-to-lodging handoff, first-night pacing and downgrade path. It does not duplicate the published Yunnan route-order page, prescribe a number of nights, rank attractions, publish a live timetable or give individual medical advice.

Search results now explain that the railway exists, but many stop at station-to-station duration. The missing decision is that the official railway description places Lijiang Station at about 2,400 m and Shangri-La Station at about 3,274 m. The final vehicle, lodging access and first night therefore belong to the same transport decision.

- Lijiang–Shangri-La railway opened on 2023-11-26. Official station elevations are approximately 2,400 m and 3,274 m. Scope: stable route/elevation facts, not current train frequency. Checked 2026-08-13.
- Yunnan transport authorities report station-to-scenic connections, but exact routes/times are not republished. Scope: existence of local connection planning only. Checked 2026-08-13.
- Diqing government published authorised scenic direct-service information from Dukezong. Scope: evidence that the base matters; live bookings still require current official confirmation. Checked 2026-08-13.
- NHC expert guidance says high-altitude travellers should consider gradual transition, reduce activity after arrival, avoid alcohol, keep warm and seek urgent care for severe breathing difficulty, severe headache or altered consciousness. Scope: general safety guidance, not diagnosis. Checked 2026-08-13.
- Weather and warnings are deferred to CMA on the travel date; rail inventory/calls are deferred to 12306.
