# Canonical boundary — bringing-dog-or-cat-into-china

## Canonical owner

This page owns one task: a traveller carrying **one domestic dog or one domestic cat in the cabin or as checked baggage** into mainland China decides whether the animal, official documents, microchip, origin branch and actual arrival port form a workable Customs/quarantine plan before the animal flies. It also owns the humane response when Customs may quarantine, retain, return or dispose of the animal.

Primary intent: `execute`

Section / family: `essentials / task`

Freshness class: `critical-on-gacc-country-lab-port-or-quarantine-change`

The exact slug and task were absent from the baseline guide directory and generated registry. The batch owner audit also checked locally available `origin/article/*` and `origin/codex/*` candidates; no live or reserved canonical pet-entry owner was found. The existing food/biosecurity owner explicitly excludes the formal live-pet process.

## Non-negotiable editorial boundaries

- Do not freeze the designated-country/region, recognised-laboratory or quarantine-port lists in body copy. GACC says those lists are updated; verify the current official list and the actual port for the travel date.
- Do not convert a conditional quarantine exemption into guaranteed release. Every exemption branch still depends on a valid chip/documents and passing on-site quarantine inspection.
- Do not treat an airline booking, agent email, veterinarian note or municipal explainer as Customs pre-clearance.
- Do not invent clearance duration, quarantine cost, return transport, storage, airline acceptance or destruction procedure.
- Do not soften the official return-or-destruction consequence. Put a humane no-fly and return-custody plan before booking.
- Do not choose between conflicting official versions silently. Show the dated national announcement, current service guide and local English summaries, narrow the conclusion, and send the exact facts to 12360 and the arrival Customs office.
- Do not treat an incomplete general summary as repeal of the pet-specific Announcement No. 5; use its conditional branches plus current dynamic lists without promising release.
- Apply Announcement No. 133's facility-port gate only when the carrier confirms the itinerary is its defined international-through-flight service; do not infer that status from an ordinary connection.

## Publication gate

Central should not publish until a fresh check confirms the current GACC designated-origin list, recognised-laboratory list and ports with isolation facilities, the current declaration/registration forms, and the actual arrival port's handling of the chosen branch. For an Australia-origin animal, recheck the current Micor dog/cat entry; for a claimed No. 133 itinerary, obtain the carrier's classification and first-port handoff. Failure to reach an official contact is not approval; the plan remains no-go until a humane, document-complete route is confirmed.
