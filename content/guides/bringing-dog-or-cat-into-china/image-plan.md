## Privacy and accuracy

The diagram uses abstract animal silhouettes, dots and generic document/chip shapes. It contains no real pet, breed, microchip number, vaccination record, health certificate, traveller identity, passport, booking reference, route, airport, port, QR code, official seal or Customs interface. The fork represents a decision, not guaranteed release or quarantine.

Do not caption it as an official form, valid chip example, current port sign, accepted certificate or actual inspection result. The editable SVG lets Central revise the flow without inventing documentary evidence.
