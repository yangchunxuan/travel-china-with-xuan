# Source log — bringing-dog-or-cat-into-china

Official sources were opened or rechecked on **2026-09-04**. This page deliberately does not reproduce a supposedly permanent country, laboratory or quarantine-port list: the official update mechanism is dynamic, and accessible official reproductions contain names that differ from a later official port-visa contact record. That record is not a complete Customs-port or pet-facility directory.

## Official sources and what each supports

1. **GACC Announcement No. 5 (2019), official MOFCOM mirror**

   https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2019/art_71739ec848d14deca4ec330fad835774.html

   Primary, unrepealed pet-specific framework used for one dog/cat per person per entry, official quarantine and rabies-vaccination certificates, chip, 30-day quarantine, the three conditional exemption branches, the narrow named-working-dog vaccination application, dynamic-list ownership, return/destruction triggers and retention-voucher recovery. Its annexes include the dog/cat information-registration form and the detailed examination/test timing used below.

2. **GACC current service guide: quarantine of articles carried by entry/exit passengers**

   https://online.customs.gov.cn/static/pages/guides/000629014001/000629014001.html

   Current administrative-check owner, necessary passenger baggage declaration, and an executable consultation route: the page links to the relevant directly subordinate Customs websites or 12360. Its embedded older general carried-item provisions use blanket 30-day/rabies-occurrence language and `chip or other valid identification`; this incomplete general display is not evidence that the later pet-specific Announcement No. 5 was withdrawn.

3. **Beijing Government English translation of GACC Announcement No. 5**

   https://english.beijing.gov.cn/livinginbeijing/lawsandpolicies/202104/t20210414_2355600.html

   Accessible official English rendering of the branch and consequence rules, with downloadable annex links. The page itself says Chinese is authentic and English is for reference; differences remain visible rather than silently normalised.

4. **International Services Shanghai: How to bring a pet to Shanghai**

   https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-DailyLife-Pets/20260813/76277aba545f43e78632b5d7b611741d.html

   Official operational explainer, marked updated 2025-10-16 and attributed to Shanghai Customs, Pudong Airport Customs and Huangpu Customs. It currently repeats Annex 1's within-14-day examination, vaccination/blood-draw sequence, one-year result and within-validity revaccination exception, and supports certificate fields, chip/reader guidance and Shanghai arrival flow. Its Australia-vaccination FAQ conflicts with both No. 5 and Australia's newer export instructions and is not used as an exception.

5. **Chongqing Government: Bringing Pets into China**

   https://english.cq.gov.cn/services/entryandexit/EntryRequirements/202606/t20260609_15740129.html

   Dated 2026-06-09. Confirms the basic one-pet, official-certificate, identification, quarantine and return/destruction framing, while not expanding the pet-specific origin/lab exemption branches. This is treated as an incomplete summary, not a repeal, new ban or guarantee of quarantine.

6. **GACC Announcement No. 64 (2019), official MOFCOM mirror**

   https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2019/art_f39e30c03fac42c89d3809d93d81023f.html

   States that GACC publishes updates when the designated-rabies-free-origin, recognised-laboratory or quarantine-facility port lists change. This is why the page routes users to a dated official check and does not freeze a list.

7. **Implementation Regulation of the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine Law, SAMR**

   https://www.samr.gov.cn/fgs/zcfg/art/2023/art_1f6a53fff3364f50b00abab82ec5c013.html

   Official legal text for declaration/inspection, official export quarantine certificate, vaccination certificate for dogs/cats, release only after passing quarantine, and return/destruction/retention-voucher consequences.

8. **GACC Customs Clearance Guide for International Passengers**

   https://english.customs.gov.cn/statics/88707c1e-aa4e-40ca-a968-bdbdbb565e4f.html

   General passenger declaration context and the dog/cat exception from the broader live-animal restriction. It does not by itself establish the pet exemption route.

9. **Hainan Provincial Government: accompanied-pet requirements**

   https://en.hainan.gov.cn/englishsite/qli/202508/a56d0b8530fb47039455b57be1a248be.shtml

   Updated 2025-08-21. Reproduces the 19-origin and old 10-port reference lists and renders the titer threshold as inclusive. It is used to expose list/translation risk, not to promise present port capability.

10. **GACC Announcement No. 31 (2023), official Fujian Commerce mirror**

    https://swt.fujian.gov.cn/xxgk/jgzn/jgcs/dtgxdc/zcdt/202304/t20230424_6154486.htm

    Repeals eight designated-supervision-site notices, including No. 108, which had published the static pet-isolation-site list, and states that relevant site lists are dynamically managed. It does **not** repeal Announcement No. 5 and does not confirm capacity at a particular terminal.

11. **National Immigration Administration: major port-visa contacts and closure note**

    https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741542/c1633769/content.html

    This is a contact page for major **port-visa authorities**, not a complete Customs-port or pet-isolation directory. Its listed names and 2024-07-31 railway-port closure note show why a copied name may age; it does not identify the pet Customs office or prove pet-isolation capacity.

12. **GACC Announcement No. 133 (2019), official MOFCOM mirror**

    https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2019/art_b2850e5820014567931e8187d78723a6.html

    Defines the specific `international through flight` service. For travellers carrying pets in that service, every pet must enter through a first mainland port with isolation facilities, submit certificates and undergo the clinical inspection there; a pet that requires isolation is isolated at that same port. This is not generalised to an ordinary self-built connection, and through-checked baggage never skips the gate.

13. **Australian Government Micor: dogs exported to China**

    https://micor.agriculture.gov.au/live-animals/china/dogs

    Entry last reviewed 2026-08-18. The current Australian export workflow requires an Australian Government-endorsed health certificate, rabies vaccination certificate, electronic chip and veterinary inspection within 14 days of arrival. It is the competent exporting-side source for the dog scenario.

14. **Australian Government Micor: cats exported to China**

    https://micor.agriculture.gov.au/live-animals/china/cats

    Entry last reviewed 2026-08-18. It gives the same four current requirements for cats and is used with No. 5 to override reliance on the conflicting Shanghai FAQ, not to make an admission guarantee.

## Port-list durability audit

The Hainan page still reproduces a ten-port list that includes Beijing West Railway Station, Shanghai Railway Station and Urumqi Diwopu International Airport. Announcement No. 31 specifically repealed No. 108, the old static pet-isolation-site notice, but not No. 5. The later NIA **port-visa contact** page notes that the two rail ports closed on 2024-07-31 and uses Urumqi Tianshan International Airport as the renamed airport; it is not evidence of Customs pet capacity or a complete operating-port list. Nothing on that contact page proves current pet-isolation capability at a terminal under the new name. The article therefore names no recommended port and requires confirmation of **facility + terminal + date + branch**, not a city alone.

## Material official conflicts and conservative resolution

| Conflict | What the sources say | Editorial treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Quarantine branches | Unrepealed, pet-specific Announcement No. 5 gives three conditional exemptions; the current online guide embeds earlier general wording and the 2026 Chongqing summary does not expand every branch | Use No. 5 plus current dynamic lists as the specific execution baseline, still conditioned on documents, chip and on-site inspection; do not turn it into guaranteed release |
| Identity | Announcement No. 5 requires an electronic chip and each exemption branch requires a valid chip; older/current guide text says chip or other valid identification | Use a readable matching chip as the safe planning gate; never claim another identifier secures the exemption |
| Titer threshold | Chinese uses `0.5 IU/ml以上`; official English/local pages vary between “above” and inclusive wording | A lab may verify report identity/result/unit, but legal acceptance belongs to Customs; do not plan around exactly 0.5 and ask 12360/actual port |
| Australia vaccination | No. 5 and both Australian Micor entries require a rabies vaccination certificate; the Shanghai FAQ says an Australian-origin pet may not need rabies vaccine | Prepare the certificate. Deviate only if the Australian export authority and actual arrival Customs jointly provide a current, verifiable alternative for the exact file |
| Country/lab/port lists | GACC says all three lists change; No. 31 repealed No. 108's static pet-site notice but not No. 5, while a local reproduction retains names that differ from a later port-visa contact record | Do not reproduce list names or counts as current availability; verify facility, terminal and date directly with Customs |
| English authority | Beijing's official English translation is useful but expressly subordinate to Chinese | Keep both language versions and cite dates; do not erase disagreement during translation |

## Claims intentionally not made

- No guarantee of admission, exemption, chip readability, laboratory acceptance or quarantine capacity.
- No fixed Customs clearance time, return deadline, airline check-in time, fee, deposit, quarantine cost or refund.
- No promise that 12360, a port or carrier offers English service or written pre-clearance.
- No global export-certificate template, vaccination schedule, fitness-to-fly diagnosis or veterinary treatment advice.
- No claim that a transit stop changes the exporting origin, that an airline booking changes Customs classification, or that an ordinary connection is automatically the No. 133 service.
- No claim that an air-waybill or manifest-cargo movement uses this accompanied cabin/checked-baggage workflow merely because the owner travels too.
- No claim that mainland entry completes local registration, breed/keeping rules, onward transport or later export.

## Recheck protocol

At publication and before every traveller departure, reopen the official pages—including both Micor entries for an Australia-origin animal—obtain the current GACC lists, use the service guide's consultation link or 12360 to identify the actual airport/terminal Customs office, and ask using the exact origin/transit, species, chip, certificates, report, forms, transport document and port. Ask the carrier whether No. 133's defined service applies. If sources conflict, contact fails or the intended port cannot confirm a humane isolation/return route, keep the plan unresolved and do not fly the animal.
