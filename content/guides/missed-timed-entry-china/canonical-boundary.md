## Why a new canonical instead of updating an old page

The venue guides teach how to book and tour; none owns the moment after the window closes. The two-path recovery structure, nine-field rule card, strike-ladder awareness and lawful-action list form a distinct task that would dilute every venue page if folded into them. A dedicated recovery page completes the cluster without duplicating any tutorial content.
