## Seen and deliberately not published

- **Ticket prices for both types, and the half-price tiers.** They are on the operator's page and
  are high-volatility; the article states only that two types exist and that they are not the same
  visit.
- **Guided-commentary fee tiers.**
- **The exact daily window for concession processing**, given as a stated window in the body rather
  than as printed times.
- **Bus route numbers and journey times** from Tianshui's stations and airport.
- **Any figure for how many caves a given ticket admits you to.**
