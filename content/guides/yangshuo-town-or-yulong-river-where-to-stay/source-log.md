## Stable geography versus editorial judgement

Supported stable relationships:

- West Street is a mapped node in Yangshuo town and has an official evening-consumption role.
- The Yulong River tourism area spans many villages and named nodes over a large corridor.
- Yulong Bridge, Jiuxian, Jima and Gongnong Bridge are not one interchangeable point.

Homeground planning judgements, stated as conditional rather than official facts:

- a quieter road-edge town property is the resilient first-trip default for a short stay;
- countryside value comes from repeated morning/slow-stay use, not one activity;
- town usually absorbs rain, luggage and plan changes better, while a specifically verified countryside property can reverse that conclusion;
- a hotel move should earn more than one photo stop or one slightly shorter ride.

No unverified taxi time, ride-hailing supply, shuttle, hotel meal, lift, sound level or road-access promise appears in the copy.
