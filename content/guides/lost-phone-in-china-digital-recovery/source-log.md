## Official sources

- Apple Support: [If your iPhone or iPad has been stolen](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120837) — reviewed 2026-08-13. Supports iCloud.com/find without a verification code, Lost Mode, the no-confrontation and anti-phishing boundaries, serial-number/police and carrier steps, Find My/Activation Lock retention, offline erase, and the iOS 15-or-later post-erase location distinction.
- Apple Support: [How to use account recovery when you can't reset your Apple Account password](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118574) — reviewed 2026-08-13. Supports using a trusted device, Apple Support app or recovery contact first; the several-days-or-longer recovery boundary; and that Apple Support cannot shorten the waiting period.
- Google Support: [Find, secure or erase a lost Android device](https://support.google.com/android/answer/6160491?hl=en) — reviewed 2026-08-13. Supports Find Hub prerequisites, guest sign-in, location uncertainty, Mark as lost, IMEI access, permanent device-data deletion, possible SD-card exception, loss of Find Hub location after erase and the password requirement after recovery of an erased device.
- Google Account Help: [Fix common issues with 2-Step Verification](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185834?hl=en) — reviewed 2026-08-13. Supports signed-in devices, alternate number, backup code, security key, passkey, trusted device and carrier-number-transfer options; it also supports the 3–5-business-day ownership-review statement in the page's stated no-backup scenario.
- Google Account Help: [Secure a hacked or compromised Google Account](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/6294825?hl=en) — reviewed 2026-08-13. Supports reviewing security events, devices and recovery settings, changing exposed credentials and contacting a bank or authorities when financial or identity information may be affected.
- State Council of China / Ministry of Commerce: [Guide to working and living in China](https://english.www.gov.cn/2025special/bizexpatsinchina2025) — reviewed 2026-08-13. Supports calling 110 for property crimes. The article does not direct routine misplaced-item cases to the emergency line.

## Claims deliberately not made

- No device is promised to be locatable, remotely lockable or recoverable; power, connectivity, settings, platform and version remain explicit dependencies.
- No mainland or foreign carrier is promised to provide same-day SIM/eSIM replacement, number transfer or a reusable QR code.
- No wallet, card issuer, travel provider or insurer is described as sharing one universal lock, correction or reimbursement process.
- Apple and Google's published account-recovery time statements are not generalised to every case or to other platforms.
- A map pin is not treated as proof of possession or permission to confront, enter property or arrange a private handover.
