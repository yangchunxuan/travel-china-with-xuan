# Research and source log

Reviewed: 2026-08-13 (Asia/Shanghai).

## User task and scope

This page helps travellers distinguish the mechanisms, conditions and limits of straw checkerboards, shelterbelts and photovoltaic fields in China's drylands. It does not certify ecological restoration, score projects, prescribe engineering dimensions, create an attraction list or imply access to rail, power, reserve or research work zones.

## Evidence architecture

- Chinese Academy of Sciences material supplies the documented Shapotou research context.

## Official and primary sources

1. [National Desertification Prevention and Control Plan (2021–2030)](https://www.forestry.gov.cn/c/www/lczc/521554.jhtml) — the National Forestry and Grassland Administration's official publication page, with the plan attached; approved by the State Council and issued by national agencies. Used for protection-first, region-specific management, appropriate use and water-resource-capacity boundaries. Its targets are not presented as measured outcomes.
2. [How China prevents and controls desertification](https://www.forestry.gov.cn/c/www/zhzs/571450.jhtml) — National Forestry and Grassland Administration. Used for precise-control and locally suitable, lower-coverage approaches rather than a one-model-fits-all narrative.
3. [Sand-control approaches that originated from Shapotou](https://www.cas.cn/zt/kjzt/kjzlzqzl/kjzlzq/202405/t20240531_5016345.shtml) — Chinese Academy of Sciences. Used for the local railway-protection history and the documented 1 m × 1 m partly buried checkerboard optimization.
4. [Notice on orderly development of photovoltaic desert-control projects](https://zfxxgk.nea.gov.cn/2024-05/17/c_1310776162.htm) — National Energy Administration and National Forestry and Grassland Administration. Used for project siting, control planning, facility management, inspection, water recycling and necessary ecological-water requirements. Its conditional reference to tourism is not treated as blanket public access.
5. [Why photovoltaic panels alone do not control desertification](https://www.forestry.gov.cn/lyj/1/zhzs/20260417/655639.html) — National Forestry and Grassland Administration hosting a science explainer. Used to separate the panel's energy function and microenvironment effects from the companion ecological measures required for `photovoltaic + desert control`.

## Independent peer-reviewed cross-checks

1. [Drivers and impacts of changes in China's drylands](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43017-021-00226-z) — *Nature Reviews Earth & Environment*. Supports the ecological value of drylands, mixed programme outcomes and substantial water pressure that can accompany afforestation.
2. [Characteristics of turbulent aeolian sand movement over straw checkerboard barriers](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2017JD027786) — *Journal of Geophysical Research: Atmospheres*, DOI 10.1029/2017JD027786. Cross-checks the mechanism involving turbulence, erosion and deposition. It does not justify transferring one grid size to every site.
3. [Sand barriers of straw checkerboards for habitat restoration in extremely arid desert regions](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925857406000966) — *Ecological Engineering*, DOI 10.1016/j.ecoleng.2006.05.020. Supports the distinction between physical sand stabilization and the longer habitat-recovery process.
4. [Effects of photovoltaic panels on soil temperature and moisture in desert areas](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33400111/) — *Environmental Science and Pollution Research*, DOI 10.1007/s11356-020-11742-8. Supports a measured local microclimate effect; no site-specific percentage is generalized.
5. [Ecological construction status of photovoltaic power plants in China's deserts](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/environmental-science/articles/10.3389/fenvs.2024.1406546/full) — *Frontiers in Environmental Science*, DOI 10.3389/fenvs.2024.1406546. A field/remote-sensing survey of multiple plants, used for the variation in companion measures and operating inputs.
6. [Environmental impacts of photovoltaic power plants in northwest China](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2213138823001133) — *Sustainable Energy Technologies and Assessments*. Used as a cross-check that benefits and burdens are local and include soil, vegetation and microclimate changes.

## Official performance-data decision

- The official Shapotou account's 1 m × 1 m mechanism is paired with peer-reviewed aeolian-flow research. The article states the local optimization and causal process, not a universal efficiency percentage.
- No national `area treated`, greening percentage, dust-storm reduction, tree count, carbon benefit or photovoltaic-restoration total is repeated. Independent science does not provide a like-for-like audit of those administrative totals, so using them would imply a stronger cross-check than exists.
- No vegetation response is attributed to panels alone. Water input, construction, barriers, grazing control, prior land condition and maintenance remain explicit alternative causes.

## Causal and ecological boundaries

- `Desert`, `desertified land` and `degraded dryland` are not used as synonyms.
- Sand control can protect infrastructure without restoring a mature ecosystem.
- A planted shelterbelt can reduce wind yet still create water or survival trade-offs.
- More vegetation cover is not used as a universal ecological-success measure.
- A photovoltaic field is first an energy facility. Any ecological claim requires a baseline, comparison, water budget, construction record and monitoring.
