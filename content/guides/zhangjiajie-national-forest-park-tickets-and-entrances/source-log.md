## Gate terminology note

The five identities are East/Wujiayu, South/National Forest Park, West/Yangjiajie, North/Tianzi Mountain and Zimugang. Older official English pages call Zimugang “Central,” while a Chinese tourism page describes it as the southeast entrance. The guide tells readers to use `梓木岗门票站` and an exact map pin rather than choosing from a translated compass label.
