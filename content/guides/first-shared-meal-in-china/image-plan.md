## Hero

Selected source: Wikimedia Commons file [`Chinese meal.jpg`](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chinese_meal.jpg), photographed and uploaded by ZiCheng Xu. The file page identifies the scene as a shared meal in Suzhou and licenses it under [CC BY-SA 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/). The image shows several central dishes and individual rice bowls; no face is visible in the published crop.

The 1600 × 1200 original was centre-cropped to 1600 × 1000 and converted to WebP with conventional pixel processing. It was not generatively edited. Final SHA-256: `db9ad5c8ff8ae4689987b2e2068c85fa6a55011d0020348c21b2cff654a5381e`.

Approved caption: “A shared meal in Suzhou, with several dishes placed in the centre and individual bowls of rice.” Use the locale-specific metadata alt text, which describes only visible content and the source-recorded city.

Do not use a Guangzhou morning-tea table, dim sum steamers, a formal business banquet, a wedding table, a hotpot-only setup or an exaggerated ceremonial toast. Those images would change the article's scope.

## Optional documentary details

1. A real menu or staff-assisted ordering moment, with prices and personal phone information unreadable.
2. A shared dish with clearly distinguishable serving chopsticks or a serving spoon beside personal place settings.
3. A real end-of-meal moment such as packed leftovers or an itemised bill, with restaurant, payment and personal data obscured.

One strong hero is sufficient. No additional documentary image was added because the article's ordering, serving and bill scenarios are better kept as accessible HTML rather than illustrated with unverified or staged moments.

## Screenshots and brands

No app screenshot is necessary. Ordering and payment interfaces change, can expose account data and may require brand permission. If an editor later adds a restaurant QR-menu example, use an authorised demo account, crop all identifiers and confirm the restaurant or platform's reuse terms.

## Keep as HTML

The meal timeline, seating choices, ordering balance, sharing defaults, bill scenarios and useful phrases should remain HTML tables, lists or callouts. They need editorial updates and should not be turned into infographics.

AI-generated people, restaurants or documentary meal scenes are not acceptable.
