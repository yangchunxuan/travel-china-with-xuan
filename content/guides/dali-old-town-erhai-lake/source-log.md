## Evidence and exact use

Ctrip [Dali Old Town](https://you.ctrip.com/sight/dalicity1445616/12192.html), read in the owner's Chrome on 2026-10-10: 4A; open all day all year; backs onto Cangshan and faces Erhai; capital area of the Nanzhao and Dali kingdoms; present layout from the Ming-era prefectural city; Bai courtyards with white walls and grey tiles; South Gate tower, Fuxing Road axis, Wuhua Tower, Renmin Road snacks, Foreigner Street bars at night; slow daily life.
2. China Bound (China Daily), [updated 15 Jan 2026](https://www.chinabound.cn/s/202601/15/WS69ba69e3498e23165e06e2ec/the-chongsheng-temple-and-the-three-pagoda-dali.html): Qianxun Pagoda 69.13 m, built in the Nanzhao kingdom; the two smaller pagodas 42.19 m, built 1108–1172; 1.5 km north-west of Dali Old Town; ticket shown as 121 yuan.
3. Ctrip [Three Pagodas](https://you.ctrip.com/sight/dalicity1445616/3037.html): 5A; 1.5 km north of the Old Town; 07:30–18:30 (sales stop 18:20); temple rebuilt in 2005; reflection pond for photographs; concessions. Price about RMB 75 from 2026 third-party guides (search summary), with lower platform packages; labelled as listings.
4. Ctrip [Erhai](https://you.ctrip.com/sight/dalicity1445616/3038.html): open all day; second in Yunnan after Dianchi; more than 40 km north to south; lakeside ecological corridor for cycling and walking; west shore for sunrise; boats; Shuanglang on the east shore.
5. Ctrip [Cangshan](https://you.ctrip.com/sight/dalicity1445616/3039.html): 08:00–17:00 (sales stop 15:00); 18 km path at over 2,000 m with views of the plain and the lake. Dahepiao, [9 Oct 2025](https://www.dahepiao.com/lvyounews1/20251009532901.html): Gantong cable car return RMB 130 with geopark entry.
6. Ctrip [Xizhou](https://you.ctrip.com/sight/dalicity1445616/12193.html): 18 km north of the Old Town; Bai houses (Yan family compound), Sifang Street, Xizhou baba; day trip by car or bicycle.
