# Image plan — hongshan-jade-context-and-symbols

## Hero in this draft

- Real source: [Hongshan Jade Dragon 2](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hongshan_Jade_Dragon_2.jpg) by Gary Todd (CC0).
- Use: documentary lead image only; preserve truthful caption and attribution.
- Processing: 1600 × 1000 WebP, EXIF stripped, no AI generation or scene alteration.

## Future reporting assets

- Capture the exact museum label, venue notice, menu, craft-stage card or structural detail discussed by the article.
- Pair any close-up with a wider context frame so the object, building, dish or process is identifiable.
- Record date, location, creator permission and whether photography is permitted.
- Do not use an attractive generic ancient building, food spread or craft market as proof of age, provenance, recipe or authenticity.

## What should remain HTML

- Keep comparisons, evidence ladders, meal matrices, process sequences and recovery steps as HTML tables, lists and callouts. They change more easily than photographs and must remain accessible and translatable.
- No decorative infographic is required for launch.
