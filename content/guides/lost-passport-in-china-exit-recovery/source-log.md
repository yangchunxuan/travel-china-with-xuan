# Source log — lost-passport-in-china-exit-recovery

- Status: DEPTH-REPAIRED / CENTRAL REVIEW REQUIRED

- Canonical owner: employee 6 / this batch
- Reviewed: 2026-08-13
- Fact boundary: this article owns the emergency chain after a foreign visitor loses a passport in mainland China. The traveller's own embassy or consulate controls the national travel-document decision; the competent public-security exit-entry authority controls the China-side visa, stay, residence or exit document; border inspection controls final departure.
- Locality boundary: the NIA sources support the national sequence and national decision-time limits. The Beijing Municipal Government page is used only as an explicit example of local police intake, local materials and a locally published service time. None of its city-specific details is presented as nationwide.
- Internal-link boundary: the entry-requirements guide owns ordinary eligibility; the passport-name guide owns normal booking-field construction. This page owns loss reporting, replacement, lawful-status restoration, departure recovery and emergency escalation.

## Official sources

- National Immigration Administration: [Damaged and lost passport](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147428/n147498/n147780/n147970/c159250/content.html) — reviewed 2026-08-13. Supports the core order: local passport-loss certificate, home-country mission replacement, then visa renewal or reissuance through the local public-security exit-entry authority.
- National Immigration Administration: [Entry-exit and stay (residence) of foreigners](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147423/n147478/n147715/c158215/content.html) — reviewed 2026-08-13. Supports evidence for lost-visa, stay-permit and residence-permit reissuance; the separate route where a national document cannot be reissued; the 10-day residence-document change rule; and the decision limits of 7 working days for visa/stay permit and 15 working days for residence permit after acceptance. It also states that justified requests for a shorter examination period remain case-specific.
- National Immigration Administration: [China Immigration Service Hotline 12367 now supports French](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147413/c194788/content.html) — reviewed 2026-08-13. Supports 24/7 12367 service and its language/service-channel boundary. The article does not imply that the hotline issues documents.
- Beijing Municipal Government: [Proof on loss of foreigners' passports](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/livinginbeijing/lossofpassports/) — reviewed 2026-08-13. Used only for the Beijing example: local-police incident report first, listed local evidence, Beijing's same-day loss-confirmation service and the page's express Beijing-only scope.
- State Council of China / Ministry of Commerce: [Guide to working and living in China](https://english.www.gov.cn/2025special/bizexpatsinchina2025) — reviewed 2026-08-13. Supports calling 110 for property crime, reporting passport loss to the local police station and the accommodation-registration evidence boundary.

## Claims deliberately not made

- No embassy processing time, emergency-document eligibility, transit acceptance or same-day replacement is generalised across nationalities.
- Beijing's photo size, address, validity statement and same-day service are not presented as nationwide requirements.
- A carrier's check-in decision is not described as immigration approval.
- A police report, document receipt or insurer notification is not described as a guarantee of boarding, exit or reimbursement.
