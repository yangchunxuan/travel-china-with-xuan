## Planning decisions, separate from verified rules

- Classic example: 10 days in China / 9 hotel nights; Beijing 4, Xi’an 2, Shanghai 3. Arrival is day 1, departure day 10, transfers days 5 and 7. No major sight is added to either transfer day.
- Day 2 depends on confirmed required reservations/access; Beijing sightseeing days can swap. Day 3 is specifically Badaling. Day 9 defaults to Shanghai flexibility; Suzhou is an optional replacement requiring its own checks.
- Slower option: Beijing 5 nights + Shanghai 4 nights, one complete intercity transfer day. Nature option: Beijing 4 nights + Zhangjiajie 5 nights, day 5 transfer and weather flexibility during days 6–9.
- The international journey may add calendar days outside the 10 days in China. A departure connection or return to Beijing must be counted explicitly.
- Actual train/flight availability, station/airport, accommodation, reservations and cancellation terms must be checked for the requested dates. No service frequency, fixed transfer duration or live availability was inferred.
