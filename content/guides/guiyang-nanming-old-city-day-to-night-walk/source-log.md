## Evidence and editorial decisions

- Nanming's official 2026 city-walk page and 2025 holiday routes establish that Cao Zhuangyuan Street, Jiaxiu Pavilion, Cuiwei Alley and Qingyun can be connected as an urban visitor sequence.
- The June 2026 Guiyang government page establishes a dated lighting upgrade around Jiaxiu; it is not a permanent lighting schedule.
- Official English visitor information identifies Qingyun Market as a managed cultural/food/retail block and gives No. 83 Qingyun Road as an address reference. Individual tenant hours are not inferred.
- No exact walking time, permanent Jiaxiu entry time, stall hours or guaranteed lighting moment is claimed.
- Homeground judgment: order heritage in daylight, Jiaxiu before dusk, a short public river segment at blue hour, and Qingyun after dark; rain and fatigue justify a vehicle branch.
