# Image plan — nanjing-purple-mountain-route

Prepared and visually checked: 2026-09-01

Status: four location-accurate real photographs prepared. No AI documentary image, film still, unidentified mountain, visitor-map photograph or third-party tour image is used.

All published derivatives are 1600 × 1000 WebP, centre-cropped to 16:10 and encoded at quality 84. Original source files are retained only in the local production staging area, not committed. Creative Commons licensing resolves the stated photographic copyright; it does not automatically clear privacy, personality, trademark or endorsement concerns. The selected frames avoid identifiable close-up people.

## Not selected

- A 2021 photograph of the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum parking-lot bus stop is rights-cleared under CC0 but omitted. Its old route sign cannot provide enough explanatory value without tempting readers to treat it as current operating proof.
- Photographs of old visitor map boards were rejected. A photographer's Creative Commons licence does not clearly resolve the underlying map artwork, and the boards are not current live-route evidence.
- No small custom map is committed. If central editorial later creates one, use separately licensed OpenStreetMap data or an original schematic with accurate attribution and no false claim of official status.
