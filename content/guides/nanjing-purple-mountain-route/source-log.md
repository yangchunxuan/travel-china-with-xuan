# Source log — nanjing-purple-mountain-route

All web sources were reviewed on 2026-09-01 unless stated otherwise. Marketing language is not used as independent evidence of historical significance, accessibility, crowd levels or operating certainty.

## Official and heritage sources used

1. **Nanjing Zhongshan Scenic Area Administration — bilingual Zhongshan Mountain visitor guide (published 2025-06-26)**
   URL: https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/zszx/dtxx/202506/t20250626_5593899.html
   Supports: approximately 31 km²; the core-area distinction; Ming Xiaoling's 2003 World Heritage status; Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum's 2024 Tentative List status rather than inscription; real-name ticket documents including passport; the reservation distinction; official hotline. The page's prices and hours are not frozen in the body.

2. **Nanjing Zhongshan Scenic Area Administration — Ming Xiaoling Scenic Area**
   URL: https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/fjms/jqjd/mxljq/
   Supports: the scenic area's components, including Grand Golden Gate, Square City, Sacred Way and tomb-palace area; its separation from surrounding additions.

3. **Nanjing Zhongshan Scenic Area Administration — Dr. Sun Yat-sen's Mausoleum Scenic Area**
   URL: https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/fjms/jqjd/zsljq/
   Supports: construction dates; 700-metre horizontal ceremonial axis, 70-metre rise, 392 steps and ten platforms; associated structures. These dimensions inform the stamina decision, not an accessibility promise.

4. **Nanjing Zhongshan Scenic Area Administration — Linggu Scenic Area**
   URL: https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/fjms/jqjd/lgjq/
   Supports: Linggu lies about one kilometre east of Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum and contains temple, Beamless Hall, pagoda, cemetery and memorial layers. It does not support calling Linggu merely a temple stop.

5. **UNESCO World Heritage Centre — Imperial Tombs of the Ming and Qing Dynasties**
   URL: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1004
   Supports: Ming Xiaoling was added to this serial World Heritage property in 2003; World Heritage value and the tomb's place in later imperial-mausoleum design. It does not confer World Heritage status on all of Purple Mountain.

6. **Nanjing Zhongshan Scenic Area Administration — 2026 explanation of the complete Ming Xiaoling line (published 2026-08-21)**
   URL: https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/lyzx/202608/t20260821_5897707.html
   Supports: the full Xiama Archway-to-Baoding line is approximately 2.6 km; Gate 5 offers a more direct Stone Elephant Road entry. Used to show why “visit Ming Xiaoling” can describe two different walking commitments, not to prescribe a permanent entry gate.

7. **Nanjing Zhongshan Scenic Area Administration — 2026 transport-service optimisation (published 2026-07-06)**
   URL: https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/zszx/zsdt/202607/t20260706_5871550.html
   Supports: the operator describes the current shuttle arrangement as `1+2+4+N`, evidence that transfers are a managed and changing network. Exact lines and fares remain dynamic.

8. **Nanjing Zhongshan Scenic Area Administration — 2026 Spring Holiday/Qingming service guide (published 2026-04-01)**
   URL: https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/lyzx/202604/t20260401_5816920.html
   Supports: a dated example of cross-area shuttle direction, aggregation entrances and holiday traffic controls. It is not cited as a permanent timetable.

9. **Nanjing Zhongshan Scenic Area Administration — dated reservation reminder (published 2026-06-18)**
   URL: https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/lyzx/202606/t20260618_5862979.html
    Supports: Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum uses time-slot reservation and the operator lists current reservation channels. Its onsite self-service wording mentions Chinese identity cards and therefore does not prove a complete foreign-passport workflow.

10. **Nanjing Zhongshan Scenic Area Administration — foreign-visitor ticket-system upgrade (published 2026-01-08)**
    URL: https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/lyzx/202601/t20260108_5760022.html
    Supports: the scenic-area system can use foreign passports and foreign permanent-residence IDs for ticket purchase and document-swipe entry. It does not explicitly close the separate free Mausoleum timed-reservation workflow.

11. **Nanjing Zhongshan Scenic Area Administration — 2026 Spring Festival service guide**
    URL: https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/lyzx/202602/t20260214_5794915.html
    Supports: date-specific hours and tomb-chamber restrictions, used only as evidence that access rules can vary by notice.

12. **Nanjing Zhongshan Scenic Area Administration — 2026 May Day service guide (published 2026-04-30)**
    URL: https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/lyzx/202604/t20260430_5833519.html
    Supports: another dated example of holiday hours, traffic controls and shuttle adjustments. It is not cited as a permanent timetable.

13. **Nanjing Zhongshan Scenic Area Administration — extreme-weather advisory (published 2026-07-09)**
    URL: https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/lyzx/202607/t20260709_5874031.html
    Supports: temporary area and project changes may follow extreme weather; it is not a forecast for any future date.

14. **Nanjing Zhongshan Scenic Area Administration — official overview map and virtual-tour hub**
    URL: https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/fjms/xnly/
    Supports: the visitor-facing west–centre–east relationship among Ming Xiaoling, Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum and Linggu. It is not a legal-boundary, UNESCO, accessibility or live-transport map.

## Search-demand and question discovery only

- Google results and related-result patterns checked on 2026-09-01 for combinations of `Purple Mountain Nanjing route`, `Ming Xiaoling Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum Linggu same day`, and current official shuttle/reservation queries. Broad route results already include a substantial ChinaGoTrip heritage-day guide; this page is therefore scoped to feasibility, deletion and failure recovery rather than claiming an empty broad SERP.
- Recent traveller discussions ask whether the three named zones fit in one day and report cutting later plans after the first two. These discussions establish question language only; they do not support operating facts, universal walking times or crowd claims.

## Evidence deliberately not promoted

- No estimated search volume, click-through rate, visitor count or “best” claim.
- No third-party timetable, map-pin driving instruction, ticket price or foreign-passport workaround.
- No claim that every Ming Xiaoling gate is always open, that one gate sequence is universally optimal, or that the shuttle will have capacity.
- No claim that all three zones are UNESCO World Heritage. Only Ming Xiaoling is part of the inscribed serial property; the Mausoleum and affiliated buildings were reported by the operator as entering China's Tentative List in 2024.
