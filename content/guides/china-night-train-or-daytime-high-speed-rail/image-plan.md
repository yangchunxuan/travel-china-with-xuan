# Image plan

Status: **COPY DRAFT — EDITORIAL REVIEW AND ASSETS NEEDED**

Use only real, licensed photographs with a traceable source and permission record. Do not generate sleeper compartments, passengers or stations with AI. Do not show a readable passport, ticket, QR code, booking number, phone screen or identifiable child without an appropriate release.

## Required hero

- File: `public/images/guides/china-night-train-or-daytime-high-speed-rail/hero-1600.webp`
- Size: 1600 × 1000 px, WebP, landscape.
- Scene: travellers boarding a genuine overnight sleeper train at a mainland China railway station, ideally during the evening blue hour or under station lighting.
- Editorial purpose: establish that this is a pace-and-cost decision about a real overnight journey, not a generic high-speed-rail tutorial.
- Composition: show enough of the carriage door, platform and luggage to communicate overnight boarding; preserve quiet negative space for responsive crops.
- Preferred sources: China Railway, a railway bureau, rolling-stock manufacturer or licensed editorial library with explicit reuse rights.
- Avoid: luxury-tourist-train interiors, European night trains, staged hotel beds, empty fantasy “capsules”, route-specific signs that imply the article recommends that service, and passengers photographed in vulnerable sleeping positions.

## Optional supporting photographs

1. `sleeper-interior-1200.webp` — 1200 × 800 px. A verified conventional or EMU sleeper interior. Caption must name the train type and must not imply every sleeper has the same layout.
2. `night-station-arrival-1200.webp` — 1200 × 800 px. A real early-morning or late-night station arrival with travellers and luggage, showing the practical problem of onward transport.
3. `wash-area-luggage-1200.webp` — 1200 × 800 px. A verified onboard wash area or luggage arrangement. Use only when the exact train model and licence are known.

The body copy must remain accurate without these optional photographs. Add figure blocks only after the files, licences, captions, train type and exact dimensions have been verified.

## Graphic treatment

Do not publish a route map or a list of “best sleeper routes”; services change and a route list could be copied as a private itinerary. If editorial wants one graphic, use a neutral ledger comparing sleeper fare and night logistics against daytime rail, hotel and usable-day costs. Label it as a worksheet, not a price estimate.

## Asset QA

- Record photographer or owner, source URL, licence, download date and attribution requirement.
- Verify that the image is from mainland China and that the carriage is actually a sleeper.
- Do not identify hard sleeper, soft sleeper or EMU sleeper from appearance alone; confirm from source metadata.
- Check 320 px and desktop crops before approval.
- Strip unnecessary embedded location and device metadata from final exports.
