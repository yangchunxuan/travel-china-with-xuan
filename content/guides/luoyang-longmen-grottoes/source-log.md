## Evidence and exact use

1. UNESCO, [Longmen Grottoes](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1003/), read in the owner's Chrome: inscribed 2000; both sides of the Yi River south of Luoyang; "more than 2,300 caves and niches" over a 1 km stretch; "almost 110,000 Buddhist stone statues, more than 60 stupas and 2,800 inscriptions"; capital during the late Northern Wei and early Tang; most intensive carving from the end of the 5th century to the mid-8th; Guyangdong and the Three Binyang Caves are the earliest on West Hill; "the high point of Chinese stone carving".
