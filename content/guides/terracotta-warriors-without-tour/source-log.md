## Transport schedule boundary

- The museum's official transport page currently names Metro Lines 1 and 9 and local routes 602 and Tourist 613.
- It does **not** publish a stable departure interval or last return for those buses.
- Current search results contain conflicting timetable claims from aggregators and old articles. None was promoted into the copy.
- The article therefore records route identity as an official dated fact and makes the operating window, exact return stop and last service a travel-date check. If a later official operator timetable is supplied, add it with its own `checkedAt` and update trigger.
- Direct sightseeing buses are described as a category only. No city-centre departure point or schedule is claimed without a current official operator source.

## Fact boundaries used in the copy

- Official standing facts: real-name booking, passport eligibility, original-document inspection, current adult price, seasonal inspection hours, two visitor areas under one ticket, free inter-area shuttle, identified boarding points, three pit descriptions and current museum-published transport routes.
- Dated dynamic facts: live booking interface, overseas partner path, inventory, time slots, holiday hours, temporary capacity, pit or exhibition closures, bus operations, shuttle queues and guide-language availability.
- Homeground planning judgment: protect a full day, use alternative pit orders based on priority and current controls, allow specific time bands, return from Lishan to the museum transport zone by default, and hire a guide chiefly for interpretation or adaptive pacing.
- Not claimed: guaranteed ticket inventory, guaranteed payment acceptance, a universal pit order, a fixed public-bus interval, a guaranteed last return, a fixed queue, every Lishan exhibit being open or a guide having access to special tickets.
