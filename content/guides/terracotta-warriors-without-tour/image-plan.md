## Essential supporting photographs

1. **Entrance and wayfinding:** the signed museum North Gate or the official public-transport arrival area. The caption must give the exact gate, capture date and direction of view.
2. **Pit 1:** a broad formation view with the correct pit identification.
3. **Pit 2 or Pit 3:** at least one image that is independently documented as the named pit. Do not infer the pit from visual similarity.
4. **Lishan Garden:** the East Gate shuttle point, Bronze Chariots Museum, K9901, K0006 or the mausoleum landscape, with the exact component identified.
5. **Two-area shuttle:** useful only when the vehicle or boarding signage can be verified as the official included shuttle rather than a commercial sightseeing vehicle.

## Rights and factual record

For every image, record:

- exact pit, gate, exhibit or Lishan location;
- creator and source;
- licence or written permission and permitted channels;
- capture date if known;
- crop and colour adjustments;
- final alt text and caption in all three languages.

Do not remove or add crowds in a documentary image. Do not show readable passports, booking QR codes, faces without appropriate permission or a guide presented as a Homeground customer without a real release.
