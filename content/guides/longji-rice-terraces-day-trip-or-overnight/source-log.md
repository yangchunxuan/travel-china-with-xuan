## Demand and SERP evidence

Current result pages and traveller discussions repeatedly surface four tasks: whether Longji works as a Guilin or Yangshuo day trip, whether one night is worth the extra move, which terrace area to choose, and how to handle weather and luggage. This is qualitative task evidence only. Search Console, keyword volume and purchase-probability data were unavailable for this specific candidate; none are guessed.

Current results contain many thin commercial itineraries with conflicting fixed journey times, prices and seasonal colour promises. This page therefore owns the duration decision and refuses to freeze those facts.

## Stable evidence

- The Ministry of Culture and Tourism locates Longji Rice Terraces in Longji Town, Longsheng Various Nationalities Autonomous County, about 65 kilometres from Guilin, and identifies several distinct viewing areas: Dazhai Yao terraces, Ping'an Zhuang terraces, Longji Ancient Zhuang Village and Xiaozhai Red Yao terraces.
- Guangxi's official 4A scenic-area register lists Longsheng Longji Rice Terraces Scenic Area in Longji Town and gives the scenic area as 70.1 square kilometres. This supports treating Longji as a distributed mountain destination rather than one roadside viewpoint.
- The National Agriculture Museum describes Longji as a living mountain rice-terrace system across 11 administrative villages and records its 2018 recognition within the FAO Globally Important Agricultural Heritage Systems programme.
- Guangxi Natural Resources reporting describes Ping'an, Dazhai and Longji villages as core communities involved in cultivating and maintaining the terraces. The fields are productive land and water infrastructure, not a static stage set.
- The 2026 Guangxi tourism-homestay plan identifies Dazhai, Ping'an and other villages as lodging clusters and calls for continued work on roads, paths, parking, drainage and safety. It supports last-mile verification; it does not certify any particular property.

## Homeground planning judgment

- A day trip is coherent when it starts from Guilin, targets one terrace area, has an early confirmed road plan, and does not carry a same-evening fixed connection.
- One night is useful when the traveller values early/late light, wants weather recovery, walks slowly, or can place Longji between two route segments without an out-and-back hotel move.
- Starting and ending in Yangshuo adds enough road dependency that it must be calculated as a complete door-to-door day, not described as the same product as a Guilin day trip.
- No sunrise, clear view, mirror-like water or golden field is guaranteed. Agricultural and weather conditions decide the view.
