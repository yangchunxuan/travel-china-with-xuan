- Reviewed: 2026-08-13 (Asia/Shanghai)

- Dynamic class: high; points, designs, fees, booklet rules, machines, exhibitions and admission conditions change by venue.

## SERP composition observed on 2026-08-13

The sampled results mixed postage-stamp museums, viral travel posts, dated venue examples and broad trend reporting. Search wording easily collapses philately and souvenir stamping; current English results rarely explain mechanism, queue etiquette or the distinction from seal history.

Representative results:

- [People's Daily Online — tourist stamp trend](https://en.people.cn/n3/2023/1019/c98649-20086221.html) — broad dated reportage on museums and historical sites adding themed stamps.

- [Shanghai Museum — Chinese seal and seal-carving gallery](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/m/article/id/RI00004032) — context for seals while preventing a direct-descent claim.

This is a dated qualitative sample, not a ranking or traffic estimate.
