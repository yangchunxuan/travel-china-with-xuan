## Official / first-party sources

1. [Stamp-collecting trend in Beijing museums](https://www.beijing.gov.cn/renwen/sy/whkb/202211/t20221101_2849254.html) — Beijing Daily carried by Beijing Municipal Government, 2022-11-01.
   - Evidence used: observed collecting practice; Beijing Art Museum six-card route linking motifs to objects and architecture; collector attribution about motivation and attention.
   - Boundary: Dated reportage, not a current service page.
2. [April 2024 Beijing museum education activities](https://wwj.beijing.gov.cn/bjww/wwjzz/wwjapp/zzbsy/zxhd/436421368/index.html) — Beijing Municipal Cultural Heritage Bureau.
   - Evidence used: Xu Beihong Memorial Museum listed 12 activity stamps, one monthly, in the second-floor activity area; venue official account controlled participation detail.
   - Boundary: April 2024 event listing only.
3. [Nanjing Museum 2022 annual report](https://www.njmuseum.com/files/nb/news/files/2023/02/13/89d976f1cd9fff8638ea34ef842df434.pdf) — Nanjing Museum.
   - Evidence used: multiple exhibition-linked stamps and stamp points in an “exhibition + cultural-creative” programme.
   - Boundary: Annual report proves the model was used; it does not locate current points.
4. [Jining Museum stamp notice](https://www.jining.gov.cn/art/2024/9/25/art_65757_2895180.html) — Jining Municipal Culture and Tourism Bureau, 2024-09-25.
   - Evidence used: expressly free stamps based on museum objects and 19 local immovable-heritage subjects.
   - Boundary: Dated mechanism example, not a 2026 availability promise.
5. [Yiyang Museum 2024 work report](https://www.yiyang.gov.cn/wlgt/uploadfiles/202505/2025050610251159177.pdf) — Yiyang Municipal Culture, Tourism, Radio, Television and Sports Bureau, published 2025.
   - Evidence used: one vending terminal and nine self-service stamp machines selling cultural-creative kits; motifs based on collection objects; sales procedure coordination.
   - Boundary: Shows paid/machine mechanism; no current price or operating status is reproduced.
6. [Chinese Seal and Seal Carving Gallery](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/m/article/id/RI00004032) — Shanghai Museum.
   - Evidence used: distinction between seals whose main function was credential/verification and seal carving as an art form.
   - Boundary: Cultural context only; the page does not claim Shanghai Museum currently offers visitor stamps.

## Independent research / cross-checks

7. [Stamp-Based Gamification as Low-Tech Immersive Media in Museums](https://www.jimmux.com/index.php/imue/article/view/10) — *Immersive Media and User Experience* 2 (2026), DOI 10.65638/2978-8811.2026.02.01.
   - Evidence used: stamp-passport systems can structure visitor movement, attention and motivation; effects are context-dependent.
   - Boundary: Qualitative cross-case study outside a national China sample; no causal or nationwide claim.
8. [青年群体博物馆“集章”行为与记忆建构研究](https://pdf.hanspub.org/ass20241312_472398137.pdf) — *Advances in Social Sciences* (2024).
   - Evidence used: participatory observation, interviews and social-media observation at Yunnan Provincial Museum illustrate route-following, physical ritual and sharing.
   - Boundary: Exploratory study with a small selected interview sample; its 2023–2024 venue details are not reproduced as current.

## Reconciliation and dynamic-risk notes

- “Cultural resonance” is separated from “direct historical descent.” No source proves a single ancient origin for modern stamp rallies.
- Free, purchase-linked, machine-based, temporary and shop-operated mechanisms are explicitly separated.
- Every named stamp arrangement is labelled by its historical date and followed by a warning that it is not a 2026 map.
- Current stamp locations, fees, booklet requirements, machines, exhibition dates and shop hours must come from the venue close to the visit.
- Admission, identity-document checks and timed-entry rules are independent of stamp access.
