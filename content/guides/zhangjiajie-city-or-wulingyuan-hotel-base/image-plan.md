- photographer/creator;
- original file or source page;

- source SHA-256;

- faces without releases;
- vehicle plates;
- room numbers, key cards and access codes;
- passports, booking references and payment details;
- readable guest lists, computer screens and CCTV views;
- any full hotel evacuation plan.

The final alt text may identify Zhangjiajie city and Wulingyuan only after both locations are proved. It must not claim current traffic, walking time, hotel quality, availability or weather.
