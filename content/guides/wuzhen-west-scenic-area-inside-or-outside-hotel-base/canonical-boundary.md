# Canonical boundary

## Primary intent

Help a traveller who has already chosen Wuzhen West Scenic Area (西栅景区) decide whether to sleep inside the controlled scenic area or outside it. The decision turns on a dated, written chain: the property's exact identity, whether it is geographically inside, whether it is an operator-listed property, the booked room/package admission entitlement, re-entry method, actual check-in point, luggage handoff, late-arrival procedure and next-day exit.

The page is not a hotel list. It teaches the traveller when an inside stay removes a useful boundary crossing and when an outside stay offers a simpler arrival or departure chain without pretending that either is universally better.

## This owner owns

- Inside-versus-outside West Scenic Area accommodation after the traveller has already chosen West Scenic Area.
- The distinction between a map pin, the official booking site's `景区内` location filter, the separate `旗下酒店` operator label and the exact booked product.
- The verification ledger for admission quantity, first entry, re-entry, visitor-centre check-in, luggage handoff and late arrival.
- The consequences of stone paving, bridges, stairs and a multi-stage luggage/check-in chain for families, older travellers, wheelchair users and people carrying large bags.
- Failure recovery when a booking label conflicts with the operator's current terms, a room does not include the expected admission, the traveller cannot use the stated re-entry method, luggage transfer is unavailable, or arrival misses the workable handoff.
- The trade-off between preserving early/late time inside the scenic area and accepting a more controlled accommodation workflow.

## This owner does not own

- Which water town to visit, or Wuzhen versus Zhujiajiao, Tongli, Nanxun or another canal town.
- Whether Wuzhen is worth an overnight stay, how many days Wuzhen needs, or a general Wuzhen itinerary.
- East Scenic Area (东栅) versus West Scenic Area sightseeing, ticket strategy or attraction ranking.
- A hotel ranking, named-property recommendation, live inventory, rating, fixed room price or value comparison.
- A permanent promise that a room includes admission, permits re-entry, accepts late check-in, transfers luggage, registers a foreign passport or meets an accessibility need.
- General foreign-passport hotel law or refusal recovery; `/guides/foreigners-china-hotel/` owns that national task.
- General accessible-room measurement and usable-route verification; `/guides/china-accessible-hotel-room-verification/` owns that task.

## Legal rule, platform display and property execution

These layers must remain separate in every locale:

1. **Law and national policy:** a hotel registers a foreign guest's accommodation under Article 39; official 2024 policy says a supposed special “foreign guest qualification” must not be used as an access barrier.
2. **Operator classification:** the official Wuzhen booking site currently exposes separate location filters (`景区内`, `景区外`, `乌村`) and a separate `旗下酒店` label. One field does not prove the other.
3. **Booking-channel display:** an OTA pin, “inside” tag, package title or translated amenity is evidence only of what that channel displayed for that offer.
4. **Actual product execution:** admission quantity, named guests, first-entry steps, re-entry credential, check-in desk, luggage custody and late-arrival handling belong to the exact property, room/product, dates and occupants. They require current written confirmation.

The page may explain the conflict between current official pages, but it must never silently choose one page as a universal promise.

## Cannibalization controls

- Keep `inside or outside West Scenic Area` in the title and opening decision. Do not broaden the headline to `Where to Stay in Wuzhen`.
- Do not publish lists of “best” or “top” hotels, neighbourhood overviews or generic attraction copy.
- Do not optimize around `Wuzhen itinerary`, `is Wuzhen worth staying overnight`, `best water town near Shanghai` or `Wuzhen vs Zhujiajiao/Tongli`.
- A map pin cannot prove an admission or re-entry entitlement. An operator-listed property cannot prove that every room/package has the same entitlement.
- An old operator notice, a current marketing page and a dated live-product page must remain visibly dated evidence, not evergreen guarantees.
- No fixed price belongs in public copy. Current price displays on official or commercial pages are intentionally excluded.
