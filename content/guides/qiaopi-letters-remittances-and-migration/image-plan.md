## Brand and archive restrictions

- Do not screenshot an online archive interface unless its reuse terms permit publication; deep-link to the record instead.
- Do not remove institutional watermarks, crop out catalogue labels in a way that erases provenance, or present a museum’s transcription as Homeground’s independent decipherment.
- Avoid decorative collages of unrelated letters. One documented item is more defensible than a generic wall of script.
