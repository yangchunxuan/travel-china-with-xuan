## Sole canonical ownership

The cross-mode traveller decision after an official severe-weather warning in mainland China: identify the issuing authority, hazard, area, time window and defence guidance; protect the traveller first; then verify each transport, accommodation and attraction booking state before choosing to stay, move, replace or seek the applicable remedy.

This page merges these query intents into one owner:

- China weather warning what should a traveller do
- typhoon, heavy-rain, snow, heat or other warning during a China trip
- whether a warning colour means a train, flight or attraction is cancelled
- how to rebuild a multi-booking trip after weather disruption
- how to preserve status, notice and expense evidence

## Boundary with transport and venue pages

This page owns the sequence that separates a safety alert from the operating state of each booking. Once a carrier, station, hotel or attraction publishes a specific status, that provider's official notice and terms own the remedy. The article may link to those owners but must not restate a temporary timetable, inventory or refund promise.

## Official conflict rule

The national four-colour framework is an interpretation aid, not a substitute for the actual alert. Local authorities may use hazard-specific thresholds, signals or response measures. If a national page, local alert, carrier notice and venue notice differ, preserve every issuer, timestamp and scope; follow the competent safety authority for exposure decisions and the booking owner for operating status. Do not silently make one notice answer a different question.
