## Authority and conflict reconciliation

Meteorological authorities own the warning; local emergency and competent authorities own local safety measures; each carrier, ticketing owner, hotel or attraction owns its operating state. These notices may update at different times without being logically inconsistent. The guide tells the reader to retain the original scope and timestamp rather than treating the newest-looking screenshot as universal.

The national colour/level mapping is presented as the general China meteorological-disaster warning-signal framework, not an NMC-specific operating code. If a hazard-specific or local official alert uses different standards, signals or actions, the article displays the difference and follows that competent authority for the affected place. Translations are reader aids, not invented official Korean editions. Hong Kong and Macao are explicitly excluded because their official systems are separate.
