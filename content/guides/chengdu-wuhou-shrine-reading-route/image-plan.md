## Caption boundaries

- A building can be accurately named while its present fabric is still later than the person remembered there.
- “Three Kingdoms heritage” must not become “built in the Three Kingdoms.”
- Statues are memorial images shaped by later artistic conventions, not verified facial portraits.
- A Huiling image cannot demonstrate unopened underground contents or continuous survival of every visible component.
- A red-wall photograph is decorative unless its exact route/object is known; it cannot prove age.
- No film, television, game art or modern illustrated edition will be used.
