## Translation and terminology decisions

- `武侯祠`: “Wuhou Shrine” in reader-facing English; `무후사` in Korean. “Temple of Marquis Wu” appears only when explaining the title.
- `漢昭烈廟` (simplified `汉昭烈庙`): “Han Zhaolie Temple,” followed by the explanation that Zhaolie is Liu Bei’s posthumous imperial title; `한소열묘` in Korean.
- `惠陵`: “Huiling” / `혜릉`; never “excavated tomb.”
- `蜀丞相诸葛武侯祠堂碑`: descriptive English “Stele of the Ancestral Hall of Zhuge, Marquis Wu”; Korean `촉승상 제갈무후 사당비`, with the official “Three-Success Stele” nickname explained rather than treated as the formal title.
- `三义庙`: “Sanyi Temple” / `삼의묘`; the Peach Garden Oath is labelled literature/tradition.
- *Romance of the Three Kingdoms*: italicised English work title; Chinese `《三国演义》`; Korean `《삼국지연의》` on first reference, then `《삼국지》` only where Korean reading convention is explained.
