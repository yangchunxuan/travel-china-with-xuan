## Numbers intentionally not promoted

Dates used to identify layers (223 record, 809 stele, 1390–1391 consolidation, 1671–1672 rebuilding, 1849 statue adjustment, 1998 Sanyi relocation, 2003 museum expansion) are historical, not operational. Counts and dimensions are omitted unless they directly help on-site observation.
