## Freshness note for central

Row 3 is the page's highest-risk item and is also its strongest information gain. When the Xingang
line western extension opens, the Hankou-Station-to-Wuhan-Station callout, the matrix row that
depends on it, and the recovery table row must all be rewritten together. A search for that
opening should be part of every quarterly review of this page. Row 8 also needs a live ferry check;
the 2026 reopening does not guarantee that the upper deck will remain accessible on every date.
