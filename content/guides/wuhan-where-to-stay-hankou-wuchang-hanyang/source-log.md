## 1. Wuhan Municipal Local Chronicles Office — city profile

- **Institution:** 武汉市地方志工作办公室 (Wuhan Municipal Local Chronicles Office)
- **Page title:** 武汉市情 (Wuhan city profile)
- **URL:** https://dfz.wuhan.gov.cn/sqgk/whsq/
- **Published / updated:** live page; content states figures current to the end of 2024
- **Retrieved:** 2026-08-22
- **Supports:** that the Yangtze and the Han cross through the urban area to form the tripartite
  pattern of **Wuchang, Hankou and Hanyang**, collectively "Wuhan's three towns"; that the city
  administers **13 administrative districts** — Jiang'an, Jianghan, Qiaokou, Hanyang, Wuchang,
  Qingshan, Hongshan, Dongxihu, Hannan, Caidian, Jiangxia, Huangpi, Xinzhou — plus development and
  functional zones; that Wuhan sits at the confluence of the two rivers.
- **Cannot support:** anything about transport, stations, metro lines or accommodation.
- **Dynamic:** low. District boundaries change rarely, but the count should be re-read annually.

## 2. Wuhan Municipal Civil Affairs Bureau — current administrative division

- **Institution:** 武汉市民政局, published on the Wuhan Municipal Government portal
- **Page title:** 我市行政区划现状是怎样的？
- **URL:** https://www.wuhan.gov.cn/zwgk/xxgk/zcwd/202111/t20211123_1857620.shtml
- **Published:** 2021-11-17 · **Retrieved:** 2026-08-22
- **Supports:** that of the 13 districts, **seven are central urban districts**: Jiang'an,
  Jianghan, Qiaokou, Hanyang, Wuchang, Qingshan and Hongshan.
- **Cannot support:** transport. Note the 2021 date; source 1 confirms the same 13-district
  structure as of end-2024, so the two are consistent.
- **Dynamic:** low.

**Together, sources 1 and 2 support the page's central correction:** Hanyang and Wuchang appear in
both the three-towns list and the district list; **Hankou appears only in the three-towns list**,
and the area meant by it is covered by Jiang'an, Jianghan and Qiaokou.

## 3. Wuhan Municipal People's Government — rail transit connecting the three stations

- **Page title:** 武汉三大火车站共规划有14条轨道交通线路衔接
- **URL:** https://www.wuhan.gov.cn/sy/whyw/202203/t20220323_1944411.shtml
- **Published:** 2022-03-23, reporting a municipal government press conference on the city's
  14th Five-Year comprehensive transport plan, with a named Wuhan Metro Group deputy chief engineer
  speaking · **Retrieved:** 2026-08-22
- **Supports:** that Wuhan has a three-hub railway pattern of **Wuhan Station, Hankou Station and
  Wuchang Station**; that Wuhan Station has 6 lines planned (4, 5 and suburban express 19, 10, 20,
  25) with **4 and 5 built and operating**; that Hankou Station has 4 planned (2, 12, 10, 13) with
  **2 built** and 12 under construction; that Wuchang Station has 4 planned (4, 7, 11, 12) with
  **4 and 7 built and operating**; that Tianhe Airport, Wuhan Station, Hankou Station and Wuchang
  Station are interconnected through Lines 2, 4 and 11 among others.
- **Cannot support:** the current network. The body says explicitly that the same briefing listed
  further lines as planned or under construction, so the network has grown since 2022.
- **Dynamic:** high.

## 4. Wuhan Municipal Transport Bureau — the eighth cross-Yangtze metro tunnel

- **Page title:** 25分钟，武汉站直达汉口站！武汉穿江地铁+1
- **URL:** https://jtj.wuhan.gov.cn/jtzx/zwdt/202501/t20250110_2516199.shtml
- **Published:** 2025-01-10 · **Retrieved:** 2026-08-22
- **Supports:** the page's most decision-relevant fact — that **there is currently no direct metro
  between Hankou Station and Wuhan Station**, that passengers must change lines, and that the
  **minimum journey is 55 minutes**; that the Xingang line western extension is being built to
  bring this under 25 minutes; that the metro tunnels **currently crossing the Yangtze in operation
  are Lines 2, 4, 7 and 8**, with the circle Line 12 (crossing twice), Line 11 phase 4 and the
  Xingang line western extension under construction.
- **Cannot support:** when the extension will open. The body states only that it is not open.
- **Dynamic:** high. This fact expires the day the extension opens.

## 5. Wuhan Municipal Transport Bureau — Hankou Station to Tianhe Airport

- **Page title:** 汉口站⇋天河机场，只需12分钟！
- **URL:** https://jtj.wuhan.gov.cn/jtzx/zwdt/202311/t20231109_2298362.shtml
- **Published:** 2023-11-09 · **Retrieved:** 2026-08-22
- **Supports:** that commuter high-speed services run Hankou Station to Tianhe Airport in
  **12 minutes**, against **41 minutes** on Metro Line 2 for the same pair; that ticket gates at
  both Hankou Station and Tianhe Airport Station **close 5 minutes before departure** and
  passengers are asked to arrive 10 minutes before.
- **Cannot support:** current frequency, fares or whether the service still runs on a given date.
  The body uses this to establish that the airport leg is short from the Hankou side, and tells
  readers to confirm for their date.
- **Dynamic:** high.

## 6. Wuhan Municipal Transport Bureau — the Yangtze ferry

- **Page title:** 坐武汉轮渡上不了甲板？网友：一块五的快乐少了一半
- **URL:** https://jtj.wuhan.gov.cn/jtzx/zwdt/202410/t20241023_2472496.shtml
- **Published:** 2024-10-23 · **Retrieved:** 2026-08-22
- **Supports:** that the standard cross-river ferry fare is **1.5 yuan**; that a crossing observed
  from Wuhan Guan wharf took about **15 minutes**; that since the 2024 flood season the **upper
  deck has been closed** for safety, with an attributed statement from a ferry operator
  representative; that a separate, higher-priced sightseeing cruise exists and is a different
  product.
- **Cannot support:** current schedules, or whether the deck has since reopened. The body frames
  the ferry as transport rather than a viewing platform and dates the closure.
- **Dynamic:** high.

## 7. China Railway 12306 — station dictionary

## Absolute expressions audited after drafting

The body contains one deliberate categorical statement, and it is a **negative** one: that no bank
of the river is uniformly safe, convenient or good value. That is an editorial refusal to make a
claim, not a factual assertion, and it is framed as such in its own callout. Every minute figure in
the body is attributed to a named authority with a date. There is no room rate, no property name,
no ranking and no promised commute time.

## Sources deliberately not used

OTA area guides, hotel-district blog posts and aggregated commute estimates were all found in
searching and none was used. The "which bank" argument rests entirely on the municipal and
transport-bureau sources above.
