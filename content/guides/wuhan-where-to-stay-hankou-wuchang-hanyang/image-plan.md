### Why this image and not a skyline

The brief forbids substituting a generic city skyline, and rightly: this guide's entire argument is
that the river is a constraint and that crossings cost time. This frame is not a skyline. It shows
the specific object the argument is about — a bridge carrying road and rail across the Yangtze,
with a visibly different urban texture on each bank: low-rise foreground on the Wuchang side, dense
high-rise and the hill-top tower on the far side. A reader who looks at it understands the problem
before reading a word.
