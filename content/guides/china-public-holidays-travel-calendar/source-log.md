## Planning judgments

- Treat long holiday periods as higher-risk dates for a tightly linked multi-city plan because a missing intercity seat, room type or timed entry can break later bookings. This is a route-resilience judgment, not a measured national crowd level.
- Keep one-base plans structurally simpler than hotel-changing multi-city chains during concentrated travel dates. The recommendation follows from fewer dependent bookings; it is not a claim that the base city will be quiet.
- Confirm hotels, attractions and local businesses directly. There is no national operating-hours rule or nationwide hotel-price rule that follows from the State Council calendar.
- Compensatory workdays affect ordinary weekday/weekend assumptions but do not imply that every private business, attraction or transport operator adopts the same schedule.
