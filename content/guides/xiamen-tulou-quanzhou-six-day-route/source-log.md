# Source log: xiamen-tulou-quanzhou-six-day-route

Reviewed 2026-09-26.

- The published [Homeground six-day product](https://homegroundchina.com/tours/xiamen-tulou-quanzhou-6-day-private-tour/) supplies the five-night split and exact route: Xiamen nights 1 and 5, Nanjing night 2, Anxi night 3, Quanzhou night 4; Yongding and Nanjing tulou on separate days. Product itinerary, meal inclusions and current booking terms remain the source of truth. This guide proposes shorter-route tradeoffs as editorial options, not as included package changes.
- [UNESCO Fujian Tulou](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1113/) confirms that the heritage property is spread across multiple buildings and a wide area. Do not treat all tulou as one park or promise two regions in one short day.
- [UNESCO Quanzhou](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1561/) identifies Quanzhou as a distinct maritime-heritage destination; the guide avoids presenting it as a quick tulou add-on.
- [Xiamen Ferry official notices](https://www.xmferry.com/) show that tourist lines and service can change. The arrival-day Gulangyu suggestion depends on actual flight arrival and a confirmed ferry; no timetable or ticket inventory is stated here.
- [Xiamen University visitor instructions](https://baowei.xmu.edu.cn/info/1003/1005.htm) require individual official reservations and do not allow agencies to reserve groups. This page does not promise campus entry. Recheck current rules for dated travel.
- Existing [tulou cluster guide](https://homegroundchina.com/guides/fujian-tulou-cluster-selection/) and [Gulangyu ferry guide](https://homegroundchina.com/guides/xiamen-hubs-to-gulangyu-ferry-terminal/) cover detailed site and boat choices; link rather than duplicate them.
