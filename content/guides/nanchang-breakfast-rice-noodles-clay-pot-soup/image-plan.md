## Supporting photographs

1. **Menu decoder:** one branch’s board showing `拌粉`, `炒粉`, `汤粉` and soup names if all are present. Obtain shop permission; date it; do not imply citywide prices.
2. **Chilli control:** two real orders from the same shop, one side-chilli and one normally mixed, only if staff confirms the preparation. No staged substitution.
3. **Crock identity:** soup beside its receipt/menu name. Photograph after staff confirmation; do not label a closed crock by guessing.
4. **Sell-out evidence:** a dated sold-out marker or staff-updated board can illustrate dynamic stock only with permission. Do not shame a shop for ordinary morning sell-out.
5. **Group portion:** overhead view of the first shared tasting order before diners serve themselves. Obtain consent and do not make a local family perform a staged meal.

## Brand and menu limits
