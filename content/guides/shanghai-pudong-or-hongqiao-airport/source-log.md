## Search and traveller-question review

Google results reviewed on 2026-08-11 for `Shanghai Pudong vs Hongqiao airport`, `which Shanghai airport`, `PVG SHA Hongqiao railway station` and `PVG SHA wrong airport transfer`.

Main gap:

- Ranking pages commonly reduce the decision to “SHA is closer to the centre; PVG has international flights.”
- They often quote the Airport Link's in-vehicle airport-to-airport time without adding immigration, baggage, terminal walking, waiting, rail-station transfer, check-in and recovery margin.
- They rarely distinguish Hongqiao Airport T1, Hongqiao Airport T2 and Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station as three physical endpoints inside the wider hub.
- Traveller questions repeatedly ask whether the Airport Link stops at the railway station, whether a tight PVG-SHA transfer is feasible, what happens to luggage and what to do after reaching the wrong airport.

## Claim ledger

### Official dynamic facts used

1. Shanghai Pudong handles international and domestic flights; Hongqiao primarily handles domestic and regional East Asian flights. Exact route, airport and terminal remain live flight checks. `checkedAt: 2026-08-11`.
2. Pudong is served by Metro Line 2, the Maglev and the Airport Link. Hongqiao T1 is served by Metro Line 10; Hongqiao T2 by Lines 2 and 10 plus the Airport Link. `checkedAt: 2026-08-11`.
3. The Airport Link connects Hongqiao Airport T2 and Pudong Airport T1 & T2. The official Shanghai Metro-derived guide describes the scheduled rail journey as about 40 minutes and lists seven stations. `checkedAt: 2026-08-11`.
4. The April 1, 2026 operating plan lists Hongqiao T2-to-Pudong service from 05:45 to 22:00, Pudong-to-Hongqiao T2 service from 06:00 to 22:15, and a 15-minute interval. `checkedAt: 2026-08-11`.
5. Shanghai Airport Authority's Airport Link page still contains older or internally inconsistent hours and interval text. The later operator-derived April 2026 notice is used for the dated snapshot; readers are told to recheck live. `checkedAt: 2026-08-11`.
6. Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station and Hongqiao Airport T2 are linked through the signed Hongqiao hub connection corridor. The Airport Link station is named Hongqiao Airport Terminal 2—not Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station. `checkedAt: 2026-08-11`.
7. Shanghai Disney's official rail instructions treat Shanghai International Resort Station as an Airport Link stop and require another connection to the park area. This page uses that only to compare airport geometry and hands the detailed route to the existing Disney guide. `checkedAt: 2026-08-11`.
8. An international inter-airport transit requires leaving the designated transit area; travellers relying on 24-hour visa-free transit must apply for a temporary entry permit at border inspection. `checkedAt: 2026-08-11`. This article does not decide individual entry eligibility.

### Editorial judgments, not official promises

- PVG is often the first airport to compare for an east-Pudong or Disney-area stay; SHA is often first for western Shanghai or a Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station connection.
- People's Square, Nanjing Road, the Bund, Jing'an and Xuhui are not decided by a single “city centre” label. The actual hotel entrance, terminal, hour and luggage chain control.
- The whole-trip calculation includes airside or rail arrival, immigration where applicable, baggage, terminal exit, transport wait, in-vehicle time, transfers, final walk and recovery margin.
- A flight with a better schedule or protection can outweigh the geographically easier airport.

### Facts intentionally not frozen

- Airline route inventory, fare, terminal assignment, gate or satellite concourse.
- Metro, Airport Link, Maglev, bus or shuttle first/last service after the review date.
- Taxi and ride-hailing pickup zones, queues, fares or road time.
- A universal airport-to-hotel or airport-to-airport transfer duration.
- Baggage through-checking or missed-connection protection for a specific ticket.
- Individual temporary-entry or visa eligibility.
