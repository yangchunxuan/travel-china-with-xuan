# Canonical boundary — book-china-travel-direct-or-ota

## Search task uniquely owned by this page

Help a foreign traveller decide, before paying for the same China hotel room / domestic flight / train ticket / attraction product, which sales channel to use — by comparing contract owner, supplier-confirmation chain, document compatibility, payment proof, change-and-refund lane, post-sale language support and failure recovery — not merely by comparing prices.

## Near-synonymous queries merged into this page

- "官网直订还是 OTA" / "book direct or OTA for China"
- "should I book China hotels through Trip.com or directly"
- "OTA 订机票靠谱吗" / "is it safe to book China trains through an agent"
- "在哪里买中国火车票/机票/酒店" (channel choice framing, pre-purchase)

## Questions this page explicitly does NOT own

- Whether a specific ticket seller is genuine → `official-or-reseller-china-tickets` (channel authenticity and real-name transfer for attractions).
- How to assemble evidence after a dispute → `china-booking-dispute-evidence-pack`.
- Foreign-passport acceptance at hotels / guest registration → `foreigners-china-hotel`.
- What a fare bundle includes on domestic flights → `china-domestic-flight-fare-bundle-baggage`.
- Handling an airline schedule disruption after purchase (rights and compensation flow) → the existing flight-disruption owner; this page only names the first contact.
- Hotel overbooking / reservation-not-found at the desk → owned by separate recovery guides (this page only points to the correct first caller).

## Why a new canonical instead of updating an old page

No existing Homeground page owns the pre-purchase channel decision across product types: `official-or-reseller-china-tickets` is scoped to attraction-ticket authenticity, not hotel/rail/air contract structure; payment guides cover how to pay, not where contract risk sits. Updating those pages would blur their canonical tasks. A dedicated decision page completes the booking journey without duplicating their reasoning.
