## Editorial decisions

- Rail channel guidance follows China State Railway Group's own statements; third-party purchases are described as agency purchases inside official inventory, not as invalid tickets.
- The 7-working-day refund floor is presented as a regulatory baseline with the bank-processing caveat quoted from carrier conditions.
- Air China's issuing-carrier rule is labeled as one airline's example, never extrapolated industry-wide.
- No platform ranking, no live prices, no discount codes, no "cheapest channel" conclusion anywhere in the page.
- Attraction-channel authenticity is delegated to `official-or-reseller-china-tickets`; dispute evidence assembly to `china-booking-dispute-evidence-pack`; foreign-passport acceptance to `foreigners-china-hotel`.
