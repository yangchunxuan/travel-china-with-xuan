# Image plan — grand-canal-everyday-urban-history

No image asset is included. Use only location-verified, licensable documentary photography. Do not generate an AI travel photograph.

## Hero

- Exact subject: a documented public section of the Grand Canal, preferably Gongchen Bridge/Hangzhou or a verified Tongzhou public bank, showing water, a crossing and ordinary public use in one frame.
- Required evidence: photographer caption, exact coordinates or unmistakable landmark, capture date, and commercial publication rights.
- Avoid: an unrelated Jiangnan water town, generic old bridge, staged period costume, private/restricted wharf, or a crop that hides the water-to-street relationship.
- Crop: 16:9 safe area with waterway as the organizing line and enough upper/side space for responsive crops.
- Alt EN: `Public Grand Canal bank where a bridge, waterway and everyday city movement meet`.
- Alt ZH: `桥梁、水道与日常城市动线交汇的大运河公共岸线`.
- Alt KO: `다리와 물길, 일상 도시 동선이 만나는 대운하 공개 수변`.

## Supporting photographs

1. A legally photographed working-navigation scene from a public viewpoint; vessel and infrastructure must be identifiable, with no trespass.
2. A perpendicular street leading away from a verified canal section, showing how the water connects to the wider city.
3. An ordinary public-space scene, with faces avoided or consent documented; never present residents as an attraction.

Rights: first-party Homeground photography or a documented commercial licence only. News-agency frames, social reposts and unknown-rights tourism-board images are research references, not publishable assets.
