## Official and primary evidence

1. [China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network — Zhenjiang Hengshun aromatic-vinegar brewing technique](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/14372) establishes the precise project name and describes layered solid-state fermentation. This listing is not generalized to every producer.
2. [National Public Service Platform for Standards Information — GB/T 18623 Zhenjiang vinegar record](https://std.samr.gov.cn/gb/search/gbDetailed?id=E116673E2B0BA3B7E05397BE0A0AC6BF) establishes a national geographical-indication standard record.
3. [National standards platform — revision project](https://std.samr.gov.cn/gb/search/gbDetailed?id=3E68BCF716812CAEE06397BE0A0AE36A) listed project `20254914-T-463` as “正在征求意见” (public consultation) on 2026-08-13 and says it would wholly replace GB/T 18623-2011 if completed. A consultation draft is not an effective replacement, so the body gives no new technical rule or effective date.
4. [Ministry of Industry and Information Technology / State Council — reviewed national industrial-heritage list](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202510/P020251023673841442628.pdf) identifies the Hengshun Zhenjiang vinegar traditional brewing area and its core workshops, cellars, drying yard, tools and business records. It does not establish that all are open to visitors.
5. [Jiangsu SASAC — China Vinegar Culture Museum](https://jsgzw.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2023/3/21/art_61556_10839620.html) documents museum collections, craft studios, public science education and demonstrations. It is government-hosted but still reports a company-linked venue's own activity.
6. [National Archives Administration — 2026 Hengshun sauce-and-vinegar culture carnival](https://www.saac.gov.cn/daj/c100199/202602/0999f9676f1d49dfa5bc24226a781424.shtml) is evidence that the culture continues in a dated public event. It is not used as a permanent annual timetable.

## Independent scientific and cultural cross-sources

1. [Duan et al., physicochemical and bioactive changes during brewing](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6864686/), *Foods* 2019, follows acids, sugars, phenolics and measured antioxidant capacity across stages. It supports “time changes chemistry,” not a health benefit for travellers.
2. [Peng et al., inoculation strategies and flavour](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10033837/), *Frontiers in Microbiology* 2023, compares microbial composition, organic acids, amino acids and volatile substances. It supports multi-organism flavour formation, not one universal production recipe.
3. [Seasonal environmental factors and microbial succession](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11335490/), *Foods* 2024, shows that environmental conditions can change communities and flavour measures during acetic fermentation. The page therefore avoids a single fixed temperature or microbial sequence.
4. Liu, Cai and Hu, [“Research on protection and reuse of industrial heritage in Zhenjiang”](https://m.fx361.com/news/2022/0207/10191682.html), 2022, treats the Hengshun traditional brewing area as one Zhenjiang industrial-heritage case and discusses protection/reuse. It is an independent secondary source of modest authority and is used only as a cross-check, not the sole basis for heritage status.

## Evidence levels and editorial decisions

- **Official fact:** the exact ICH project, national standard record/revision project, industrial-heritage core objects and documented museum collection/activity.
- **Independent science:** solid-state microbial succession and measured changes in acids, sugars, amino acids, phenolics and volatiles across studied production conditions.
- **Homeground synthesis:** read grain → alcohol → solid vinegar mash → turning → extraction → aging, then separate craft, product, heritage and visitor presentation.
- **Cannot infer:** one recipe or duration for all producers, that older always means better, that dark colour proves Zhenjiang origin, medical benefit, or guaranteed access to an active factory batch.
