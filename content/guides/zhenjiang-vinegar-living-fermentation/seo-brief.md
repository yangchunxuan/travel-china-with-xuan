## Result gap and Homeground information gain

- Follow grain → alcohol → solid vinegar mash → turning → extraction → aging as transformations, not a universal recipe.
- Separate named ICH technique, standard/product, industrial-heritage site and museum presentation.
- Give six provenance questions for any demonstration or display.
- Explain why colour, age and flavour language cannot authenticate a bottle.
- Keep chemistry as process evidence and exclude traveller health claims.
