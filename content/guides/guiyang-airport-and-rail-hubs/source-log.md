## Canonical boundary and search gap

This page owns identity and recovery across Longdongbao Airport/railway station, Guiyang North, Guiyang East and central Guiyang Railway Station, including hotel and rural-onward effects. It does not publish a city transit timetable, rank hotels, explain national rail boarding or promise rural tourist buses.

Search results often call Guiyang North the default for every high-speed train, ignore high-speed calls at central Guiyang Station, or blur Guiyang East Railway Station with the road coach terminal historically called Guiyang East Coach Station. They also describe the airport’s integrated rail station without warning that only a dated 12306 call makes it useful.

## Dynamic-fact review

- Guiyang North and central Guiyang Station are connected by Metro Line 1; Longdongbao Airport is served by Line 2. Scope: network identity, not first/last trains. Checked 2026-08-13.
- Longdongbao Railway Station is integrated beneath the airport terminal complex. Scope: physical identity; no current individual train is promised. Checked 2026-08-13.
- Guiyang North has organised south/north entry and distinct taxi/ride-hailing zones. Scope: walking/pickup friction; travellers must follow current signs. Checked 2026-08-13.
- Official transport sources report passenger extension services at North/East and date-specific tourist links from multiple hubs. Scope: rural-onward discovery; all routes/times require reconfirmation. Checked 2026-08-13.
- Central Guiyang Station handles conventional and dated high-speed services, but a route must explicitly call there. Scope: station identity. Checked 2026-08-13.
- Live train calls and changes are deferred to 12306; metro/bus/coach operations are deferred to Guiyang/Guizhou official transport channels.
