## Dynamic-fact controls

- Official counts, route coverage, delivery locations, app availability, service hours and locker access can change. No current route is promised.
- The construction plan's 2026 node, route, coverage and SILAS figures are targets, not completion claims.
- “Operating route,” “trial,” “demonstration,” “operation site,” “certified aircraft” and “public ticketed passenger service” are not treated as synonyms.
- Shenzhen's airspace notices can change. This page is about city systems and gives no takeoff point, altitude, application shortcut or tourist-flying recommendation.
- A delivery order may still move partly by merchant staff, rider or courier. The aircraft is one link, not a door-to-door replacement.
