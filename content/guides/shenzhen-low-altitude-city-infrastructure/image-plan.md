## Privacy and safety rules

- No faces without releases; no residential windows framed as surveillance subjects.
- No readable phone screens, delivery names, addresses, order numbers, QR codes or medical labels.
- No image captured from an unauthorised flight and no depiction that functions as a takeoff-location recommendation.
- Do not photograph police, emergency or critical-infrastructure operations in a way that reveals sensitive deployment details.
- Do not label a prototype or static exhibit as a working city service.
