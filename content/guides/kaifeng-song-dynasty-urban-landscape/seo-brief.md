## SERP composition observed on 2026-08-14

The English and Chinese qualitative samples split into five result types: attraction promotion/itineraries, artwork explainers, Zhouqiao archaeology reports, academic authenticity/urban-archaeology studies and encyclopedic summaries. Park pages commonly use “recreate,” “restore” or “1:1” without defining an archaeological standard; artwork results rarely solve park-ticket intent; archaeology results rarely explain the visitor choice. Thin third-party itineraries often freeze performance times or treat themed silhouettes as historical proof.

Representative results:

- [Palace Museum — Zhang Zeduan's *Qingming Shanghe Tu*](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/paint/228226.html) — primary artwork record and painted Hongqiao naming.

- [*Journal of Tourism and Cultural Change* — “Does authenticity exist in cultural theme parks?”](https://doi.org/10.1080/14766825.2018.1437745) — independent research framing visitor/activity authenticity in Millennium City Park; it does not authenticate individual structures.
- [*World Archaeology* — “Urban archaeology in Kaifeng, a capital city of dynastic China”](https://doi.org/10.1080/00438243.2024.2304336) — peer-reviewed context for Kaifeng's layered urban archaeology.

## Result gap and Homeground information gain

- Make the three evidence layers explicit before giving travel advice: **artwork → archaeology → tourism presentation**.
- Name three different bridges instead of letting the shared word `Hongqiao/bridge` imply identity: painted Hongqiao, modern park Hongqiao and Zhouqiao.
- Preserve the archaeological hard boundary: the visible main Zhouqiao bridge is early Ming on Song foundations; the inferred Northern Song flat-bridge body is lost.
- Treat “1:1” as attributed project language and explain that the park expanded in later phases beyond the scroll.
- Convert authenticity into a ticket decision: themed interpretation, material evidence, or a deliberate combination—not a universal “worth it” verdict.
- Give a three-to-four-hour route that still works without a scheduled performance.
- State what official evidence does not close: current price, foreign-passport flow, programme inclusion and Zhouqiao ordinary access.
- Publish image identities and licence routes while refusing to use the Palace Museum web image without permission.
