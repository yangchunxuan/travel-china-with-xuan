## Deliberate exclusions and residual asset opportunities

- **Palace Museum original:** excluded. The museum's image application and website terms require a rights workflow; a public-domain status assertion about the underlying old artwork would not by itself license downloading the museum's web image for this publication.
- **Performances:** no screenshot or performance photograph is used. A future body image would require current event/venue permission plus publication consent for any identifiable performer or child.
- **Zhouqiao evidence:** represented by a 2025 photograph of the excavated area. Its identity is cross-checked against the Commons coordinates/categories and the Henan Provincial Institute of Cultural Heritage and Archaeology site description. It does not establish which parts a visitor can see on any later date.
- **Museum display:** not represented because no verified current gallery/artefact and publication-right combination was closed.
- **Relief material:** the selected source file labels the object as carved stone while the current operator describes a copper scroll display elsewhere/now. The article therefore says only “modern relief” and makes no material identity claim.

## Cropping restrictions

- Keep sufficient modern skyline/context in the Hero to prevent an “intact Song city” impression.
- Do not crop the modern Hongqiao so tightly that water and visitor circulation disappear.
- Keep the Zhouqiao protective roof, bridge fabric, canal walls and surrounding layers in the frame. Do not isolate the arch in a way that suggests it is the lost Northern Song bridge.
- Do not enlarge distant people into recognizable subjects. Replace the asset if a later crop would make an individual identifiable.
