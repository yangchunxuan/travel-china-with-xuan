- Fact boundary: this article provides a document-to-platform workflow. It does not declare that a particular abbreviation, transliteration, truncation or field order will be accepted by every provider.
- Provider boundary: airline field rules and corrections remain carrier-specific; 12306's published foreign-passport rules apply only to 12306; hotel registration and attraction admission follow the responsible property or venue.
- Internal-link boundary: transport, lodging and attraction guides own their full booking workflows. This article owns the cross-system passport identity record, mismatch triage and escalation decision.

## Official sources

- China Railway 12306: [Foreign-passport name and number entry instructions](https://mobile.12306.cn/otsmobile/h5/otsbussiness/passportInstruction/passport-entry-instructions.html) — reviewed 2026-08-13; supports visual-zone-first/MRZ-auxiliary handling, label-specific name order, spaces and separators, Latin-character handling and the 64-character entry limit
- China Railway 12306: [Purchase FAQ](https://kyfw.12306.cn/otn/gonggao/buyTicket.html) — reviewed 2026-08-13; supports accurate passenger identity entry and the current refund-and-repurchase route when name or document information is wrong after purchase
- China Railway 12306: [English real-name ticket FAQ](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html?item=2) — reviewed 2026-08-13; supports use of a valid foreign passport and presentation of the original valid ID used to purchase the ticket
- Civil Aviation Administration of China: [Provisions on public air transport passenger services](https://www.caac.gov.cn/XXGK/XXGK/MHGZ/202103/t20210315_206814.html) — reviewed 2026-08-13; page marks the provisions effective and supports ticketed passenger-name disclosure and check-in with the valid ID consistent with purchase
- State Council of China: [Guide to working and living in China as business expatriates](https://english.www.gov.cn/2025special/bizexpatsinchina2025) — reviewed 2026-08-13; supports foreign-passport use for rail, original-document checks and hotel accommodation registration with valid identification
- The Palace Museum: [Booking and ticket inspection instructions](https://intl.dpm.org.cn/ticket_details.html) — reviewed 2026-08-13; used as an explicitly labeled venue example for full name, passport number and the original valid ID used to book; it is not generalized to every attraction

## Dynamic review notes

- The previous generic 12306 real-name links were retained only where they directly support original-ID and correction workflow. The newly added mobile 12306 instruction is the controlling source for foreign-passport field examples.
- No airline-specific surname, middle-name or punctuation format is presented as universal. The article sends ambiguous airline cases to the ticket issuer and operating carrier.
- Passport renewal is presented as a provider-by-provider escalation, not as a promise that carrying an old passport, a new passport or a change certificate will automatically preserve a booking.
- The 2018 CAAC notice that formerly supported a free-remedy statement is now explicitly marked `失效` (no longer effective) on CAAC's own page. It was removed from all three articles and source lists on 2026-08-13; the guide now makes the narrower, current claim that correction method and cost must be confirmed with the issuer and operating carrier.
