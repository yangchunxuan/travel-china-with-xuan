## Core evidence

1. [Chaoyang Yingge](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/12901.html), China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network: records slow, medium and fast local types, differences in sticks and rhythm, and front/rear performance sections.
2. [Puning Yingge](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/12902.html), China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network: records Puning-specific team-number traditions, formations and the Water Margin/Damingfu narrative association.
3. [Jiazi Yingge](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/12903.html), China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network: records a distinct four-part processional structure and local performance components.
4. [Guangdong cultural highlight on Yingge](https://discoverguangdong.com/cultural-highlights/tu5abna3km5gbrswco1xdpge), official provincial destination channel: current visitor-facing regional orientation; not a permanent timetable.
5. [Shantou 2026 event context](https://stgsl.shantou.gov.cn/stgsl/tpxw/202602/a65ed783eae844c887242068a97c77e2.shtml), official Shantou source: evidence that a named 2026 event occurred, not that it recurs on the same dates.
6. [Shantou regional tourism alliance update](https://www.shantou.gov.cn/wgxj/zwgk/gzdt/content/post_2553236.html), Shantou Municipal Bureau of Culture, Radio, Television, Tourism and Sports: current official regional context.
7. [Official party-building/local troupe style account](https://stszgw.shantou.gov.cn/stszdj/yaowen/202402/eee3e4d18bc84d32837339a823667eb6.shtml), Shantou municipal source: examples of named local teams and styles; not a ranking.
8. [Research on Yingge’s contemporary transmission](https://tyxk.scnu.edu.cn/a/20240802/2929.html), South China Normal University journal: independent academic context for performance and transmission.
9. [China ICH copyright notice](https://www.ihchina.cn/about/206.html): the text records can be cited, but their images are not assumed reusable; commercial reuse requires permission under the site notice.
