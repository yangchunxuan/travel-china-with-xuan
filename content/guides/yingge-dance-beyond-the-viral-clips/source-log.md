## Core evidence

1. [Chaoyang Yingge](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/12901.html), China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network: records slow, medium and fast local types, differences in sticks and rhythm, and front/rear performance sections.
2. [Puning Yingge](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/12902.html), China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network: records Puning-specific team-number traditions, formations and the Water Margin/Damingfu narrative association.
3. [Jiazi Yingge](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/12903.html), China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network: records a distinct four-part processional structure and local performance components.
4. [Guangdong cultural highlight on Yingge](https://discoverguangdong.com/cultural-highlights/tu5abna3km5gbrswco1xdpge), visitor-facing destination guide that describes itself as an official travel guide: regional orientation only; not a first-party event source or permanent timetable.
5. [Shantou 2026 event context](https://stgsl.shantou.gov.cn/stgsl/tpxw/202602/a65ed783eae844c887242068a97c77e2.shtml), official Shantou source: evidence that a named 2026 event occurred, not that it recurs on the same dates.
6. [Shantou regional tourism alliance update](https://www.shantou.gov.cn/wgxj/zwgk/gzdt/content/post_2553236.html), Shantou Municipal Bureau of Culture, Radio, Television, Tourism and Sports: current official regional context.
7. [Official party-building/local troupe style account](https://stszgw.shantou.gov.cn/stszdj/yaowen/202402/eee3e4d18bc84d32837339a823667eb6.shtml), Shantou municipal source: examples of named local teams and styles; not a ranking.
8. [2010 study of adapting Yingge for staged presentation](https://tyxk.scnu.edu.cn/a/20240802/2929.html), *Journal of Physical Education*, South China Normal University: independent academic context for choreography and stage adaptation; not evidence for current schedules or contemporary digital transmission.
9. [China ICH copyright notice](https://www.ihchina.cn/about/206.html): the text records can be cited, but their images are not assumed reusable; commercial reuse requires permission under the site notice.
10. [Hero photograph: Yingge in the 2011 Dahao New Year procession](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2011%E8%BE%BE%E5%9F%A0%E6%AD%A3%E6%9C%88%E5%88%9D%E4%B8%80%E5%B7%A1%E6%B8%B8.jpg), Ixioini / Wikimedia Commons, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/): documented in Dahao, Shantou, with paired sticks visible; it does not identify a team, a named character or a Chaoyang, Puning or Jiazi lineage.

## Evidence levels

- **Official form-level fact:** the three separately recorded projects and the characteristics their records explicitly attribute to each locality.
- **Current organizer/government fact:** a named dated event, team or programme was announced or held; no recurrence is inferred.
- **Independent scholarship:** staged adaptation and choreography context, used as interpretation rather than a timetable or claim about current digital transmission.
- **Homeground editorial judgment:** the verification ladder, three-layer field sequence, two traveller scenarios and recovery actions.
- **Cannot infer:** one universal team size, one story, one stick length, one speed, one costume code or one “most authentic” locality from these records.
