## Answer design

The decision turns on when the old city is valuable and whether the last physical chain is workable. Inside wins for repeat dawn/evening access; outside wins for late arrival, heavy luggage, limited mobility or an onward connection, without pretending that either side is a single hotel zone.

The English, Simplified Chinese and Korean pages use corresponding decision objects but independently written prose.
