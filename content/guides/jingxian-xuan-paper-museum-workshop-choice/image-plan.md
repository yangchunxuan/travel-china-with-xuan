# Image plan — Jingxian Xuan paper

Status: `ASSETS NEEDED`

No image has been added to the repository. Do not use the widely circulated Anhui Museum Xuan-paper diorama as if it were a live Jingxian workshop, and do not use generic East Asian paper-making footage.

## Hero — required

- **Exact subject:** two trained workers forming a wet production sheet with a bamboo screen, or a trained worker separating and brushing a wet sheet at the drying wall.
- **Exact place:** an identified live production area in the China Xuan Paper Culture Park, Wuxi area, Jingxian County, Anhui; the venue must confirm the station.
- **Capture date:** preferably 2025–2026, on a documented production day. Record the date rather than describing the scene as “ancient”.
- **Rights:** Homeground-owned original, written venue permission covering web/SEO use, or an open licence verified on the original file page. Obtain a release or use a composition that does not make an identifiable worker the commercial subject. Do not scrape the museum or government site.
- **Crop:** source at least 2400 px wide; crop to 1600×1000 WebP, keeping both hands, the whole working edge of the screen and enough vat/drying context. Do not crop a visitor into the role of a craftsperson.
- **EN alt:** `Skilled workers forming a wet sheet of Xuan paper with a bamboo screen in Jingxian.`
- **ZH alt:** `泾县熟练工匠用竹帘捞制湿宣纸的真实生产场景。`
- **KO alt:** `징현에서 숙련된 장인이 대나무 발로 젖은 선지 한 장을 뜨는 실제 작업 모습.`

## Supporting photographs

1. **Raw materials:** labelled blue sandalwood bark and Shatian rice straw at the museum or production site. Record whether the display is original material or a reconstruction. Rights and date required.
2. **Museum interpretation:** one process timeline with enough surrounding gallery context to make clear that it is an exhibit. Confirm photography and reproduction permission; do not publish a full copyrighted panel at readable resolution.
3. **Workshop reality:** visitor hands at one named step, such as guided sheet forming. Caption it as a short activity using prepared material, never “making Xuan paper from start to finish”. Written consent is required for identifiable visitors, especially children.
4. **Drying detail:** a close view of wet-sheet separation or brushing can explain skilled failure points. Venue, worker consent and process identity must be logged.

## Screenshots and brand limits

- A screenshot of the museum notice/booking page has only temporary explanatory value. Link to the live page in text instead of embedding a screenshot that will age quickly.
- Museum logos, Red Star branding and interface screenshots are not covered by a photograph’s Creative Commons licence. Obtain brand permission or exclude them from the crop.
