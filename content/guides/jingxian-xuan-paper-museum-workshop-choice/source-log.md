## Core evidence

1. [UNESCO — Traditional handicrafts of making Xuan paper](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-handicrafts-of-making-xuan-paper-00201) establishes the inscribed living craft and Jingxian context.
2. [China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network — project overview](https://www.ihchina.cn/page_special.html) identifies blue sandalwood bark and Shatian rice straw, the broad process and 2006/2009 listing history.
3. [China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network — process](https://www.ihchina.cn/technology.html) groups the sequence into raw-material preparation, pulping, sheet forming, drying and cutting; it states that raw-material processing is the longest stage.
4. [Xuancheng People’s Government — Xuan paper cultural ecology plan](https://www.xuancheng.gov.cn/OpennessContent/show/3017793.html) provides the official geographic/product definition and detailed bark, straw, blending and paper-making stages.
5. [Jing County People’s Government — Xuan Paper Culture Park](https://www.ahjx.gov.cn/About/show/33534.html) confirms three visitor components: China Xuan Paper Museum, the “three-zhang-three” production workshop and the experience garden.
6. [China Xuan Paper Museum](https://bwg.hongxingxuanpaper.com.cn/) is the direct venue channel for current notices. Its live operation, closures, class inventory and booking rules are dynamic.
7. [Xuancheng Market Regulation Bureau — 2026 traditional-knowledge exchange](https://amr.xuancheng.gov.cn/News/show/1718542.html) is the dated local-government source for the approximately two-year production-cycle statement. Other official accounts describe longer ageing for some high-grade paper, so the body does not universalize one duration.

## Evidence levels and editorial decisions

- **Official fact:** material definition, broad and detailed process, heritage status, and the three-part venue structure.
- **Venue-dependent fact:** whether the production area is open to visitors, which station operates, what a workshop includes, collection of a wet sheet, accessibility and reservation. These require reconfirmation close to travel.
- **Homeground editorial judgment:** museum first for process literacy, then a confirmed live station; add a workshop only when the task is named.
- **Cannot infer:** that every labelled product is verified Xuan paper, every heritage experience reproduces an unchanged historic method, or every production stage is visible every day.
