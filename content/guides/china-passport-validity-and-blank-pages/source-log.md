## Editorial decisions

- The article rejects a universal “six months and two pages” shortcut. It routes readers by travel basis before discussing numbers.
- The Ministry of Foreign Affairs visa-free FAQ supports validity through the intended stay for the covered unilateral visa-free route. The NIA announcement separately requires at least three months for the 240-hour transit route.
- Toronto and Norway pages are representative application instructions, not global law. The Azerbaijan page is retained as an official counterexample showing that local blank-page requirements can differ.
- Air China is used only to show that a carrier owns its boarding-document check and may apply a stricter operational rule. Readers are sent to the operating carrier for their own itinerary.
- Passport damage, a new passport, changed identity data, long-validity or multiple-entry visa requests, and transit-country rules are explicit decision-changing conditions.
