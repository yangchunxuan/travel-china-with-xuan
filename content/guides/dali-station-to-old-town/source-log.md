## Canonical boundary and search gap

This page owns the station-to-confirmed-gate-to-lodging execution after arrival at Dali Railway Station. It does not rank Dali hotels, plan Old Town sightseeing, promise a bus timetable or explain national rail boarding.

Search results usually give a route number to “Dali Old Town.” Official material itself distinguishes a South Gate visitor-centre corridor, Erhai Gate/east side, West/Cangshan Gate and North Gate. The missing object is the hotel’s vehicle-accessible entrance; a generic old-town map pin can create a long stone-street luggage walk.
