No account was registered and no order was started.

2. Official visitor information (游客须知), https://www.mutianyugreatwall.com/cnInformation (HTTP 200). Supports: real-name tickets, entry with the original ID, towers 1-20 open (night opening towers 10-15); concession and free-ticket categories, including foreign minors over 6 to 18 and taller than 1.2 m; shuttle and cable car free only for children 1.2 m or shorter, no other concession; admission policy excludes shuttle, cable car, chairlift and toboggan; shuttle station about 3 km from the ticket gate; return cable car holders come back the same way; "缆车公司与索道、滑道公司隶属两家公司，票不通用"; booking up to 30 days ahead, one admission per ID per day (身份证/外国人永久居留身份证/护照); one mobile number per account; refund or change before 24:00 on the visit date for unchecked tickets, money back within 7 working days; cable car holders enter by 北检票口 only, chairlift and toboggan holders by 滑道检票口 only; passport holders need no ticket exchange; seasons and opening hours; ticket sales stop one hour and ticket checks half an hour before closing; cable car hours; weather closures.
