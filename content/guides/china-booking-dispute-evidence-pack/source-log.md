## Editorial and parity review

- English is the authoritative factual baseline and is within the 900–1,500-word production range.
- Chinese and Korean retain every evidence field, status distinction, scenario, privacy warning, escalation boundary, recovery action and final verification item, with natural locale-specific phrasing.
- The master-archive/redacted-copy model prevents the article from encouraging public exposure of passport, card, order, QR-code, contact or location data.
- The guide distinguishes authorised-payment service disputes from unauthorised transactions and tells the reader to report verifiable facts rather than select a more serious label for leverage.
- Regulatory escalation is conservative: first use accountable commercial channels, then select the public route that matches the respondent and issue.

## Dynamic conflicts and limits

- SAMR and PBOC rules can decline a matter already accepted or handled through another specified adjudicative or administrative route. The copy therefore avoids regulator “spray and pray”.
- Platform retention duties do not create a promise that a traveller can instantly download every backend field. The practical instruction is to preserve what is visible and request the rest through the accountable case channel.
- Payment-provider, issuer and card-network rules vary by institution, transaction type and jurisdiction. No universal chargeback category or deadline is supplied.
