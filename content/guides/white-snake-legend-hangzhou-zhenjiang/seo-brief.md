## Search task

The reader is not asking for another plot summary. They need an evidence-aware way to connect a familiar story with real places, avoid treating a modern reconstruction as an untouched relic, and decide whether a second city earns the transfer cost.

## Search-result composition observed

The 2026-08-13 research pass found a mix of plot retellings, single-attraction pages, folklore essays and location photo stories. Many results connected the legend to a place without separating material survival, later literary association and current tourism presentation. Few answered the practical two-city choice.

## Information gain

1. A four-layer model distinguishes material history, folklore, literary/performance development and tourism presentation.
2. Broken Bridge, the old Leifeng site, the modern visitor tower and Jinshan Temple are compared by what each can and cannot prove.
3. The page gives a clear Hangzhou-first decision threshold instead of implying that two cities are required.
4. Visit sequences, access uncertainty and fallbacks remain useful without freezing hours, prices, exhibits or cave access.
5. Real documentary images show the current places and the historical tower condition without film, television or performance stills.
