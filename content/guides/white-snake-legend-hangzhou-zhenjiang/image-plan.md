## 1918 Leifeng copyright review

Conclusion: **usable as a public-domain historical photograph, subject to preserving this record and the integrity limits below.**

- Photographer Sekino Tadashi died in 1935. Under current PRC Copyright Law Article 23, the economic term for a natural-person work is life plus 50 years, ending no later than 1985-12-31. Attribution, modification and integrity rights under Article 22 remain respected.
- Japan's National Diet Library guidance states that photographs published before 1957 are beyond the Japanese term. The source volume was published by Hōzōkan in Japan in 1939; Commons applies `PD-Japan-oldphoto`.
- United States URAA restoration applies only when the work remained protected in its source country on 1996-01-01. This photograph had already entered the Japanese public domain, so Commons also marks it public domain in the United States.
- The generic Commons warning about near-simultaneous United States publication was reviewed; the located bibliographic record is the 1939 Japanese Hōzōkan volume and no contemporaneous US edition was found.
- Required editorial handling: credit Sekino Tadashi, 1918; name the 1939 publication; say public domain; never colourise, AI-restore, extend or label the damaged state as the original Wuyue appearance.
