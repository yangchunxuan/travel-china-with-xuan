## Primary query

## Related questions

- Which of China's three famous literary towers should I visit?

## Search task

The reader needs a choose-one comparison, not three attraction summaries. The page must distinguish an old literary lineage from the age and material of the building now visited, then connect that distinction to route fit and on-site observation.

## Search-result composition observed

The 2026-08-13 research pass found many individual attraction pages and generic “three great towers” lists. Material continuity, dismantling conservation, relocation and modern reinforced-concrete reconstruction were rarely compared in one decision-oriented result.

## Information gain

1. One table compares the current buildings by site, material, date, conservation and claim boundary.
2. The page preserves the official `223` versus `233` Yellow Crane conflict instead of silently choosing a convenient number.
3. Yueyang's retained material is presented with its dismantling-repair caveat; no component is automatically dated to 1880.
4. Yellow Crane Tower and Tengwang Pavilion are read as documented modern reconstructions rather than ancient fabric.
5. A choose-one framework connects literary interest and material evidence to the traveller's existing route, with no invented three-city itinerary.
