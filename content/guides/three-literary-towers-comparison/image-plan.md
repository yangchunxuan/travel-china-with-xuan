## Three-language alt and caption functions

The five figure block IDs, dimensions and source paths match in EN/ZH/KO. Each caption states what the image helps establish and what it cannot prove. Hero alt says “present/modern Yellow Crane Tower”; it does not say “three ancient towers.”

## Rejected or withheld candidates

- Rejected `Tengwang Pavilion, Nanchang.jpg`: noisy night image was visually weaker than the GPS-backed CC0 daytime set.
- Rejected `Pavilion of Prince Teng 20231017 24.jpg`: visual review showed a dougong display model behind glass, not an exterior bracket detail; the photo licence would not clear the underlying model automatically.
- Withheld Tengwang murals, models and digital exhibits: underlying-work rights and current visibility were not both closed.
- Withheld Yellow Crane copper-top photo: author/uploader spelling and current display need central rights/operations review; body remains conditional.
- Withheld Yueyang calligraphic screen: a CC0 2018 photograph exists, but the page does not need to republish the screen and current display is unconfirmed.
- Withheld historic Yellow Crane photographs: Commons status alone did not close author, first-publication and US copyright review.
- Rejected any three-tower “same scale” montage. A single real Yellow Crane image carries the Hero; the other towers appear at chapter level with their own captions and credits.
