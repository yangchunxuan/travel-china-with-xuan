## Known source conflict

The museum operator's live visitor guide visible on 2026-09-01 listed Tuesday closure and 09:00–16:30. An older 2025 Zhejiang travel booklet still listed Monday. The live operator guide is the fresher, more direct operating evidence; the old booklet is retained only to explain why copied weekday rules are unsafe. Travellers must check that live page and any newer dated campus notice.

## Deliberately not frozen

- A fixed harvest week, leaf colour, blossom, weather or crowd level.
- Any free-entry, reservation, passport or foreign-language-service promise.
- Walking time between Longjing and Meijiawu.
- Tea ceremony, tea picking, pan-firing or tasting availability.
- A claim that one village is quieter, more authentic or better for buying tea.

## Evidence layers

- **Official fact:** museum campuses, addresses, permanent-exhibition roles and official landscape/tea-area descriptions.
- **Long-term pattern:** working hillsides and residential lanes require more physical and social care than a museum gallery.
- **Homeground judgment:** choose one primary task first; add a second site only when it supplies a different kind of evidence.
