# Canonical boundary — china-hotel-reservation-proof-at-checkin

## Search task uniquely owned by this page

## Why a new canonical instead of updating an old page

The in-trip, standing-at-the-desk task (fifteen-minute sequence + cause triage + responsibility lanes + relocation asks) is distinct from pre-booking channel/rate decisions and from post-incident evidence assembly. Existing stay pages answer where to stay; this answers what to do when the stay itself cannot start. Merging it into any of them would bury the emergency checklist.
