## Hero assignment — preferred

Commission a current documentary photograph in one exact, source-supported case community:

### Option A: Caoyang New Village, Putuo District, Shanghai

- Exact subject: a public street or public-space edge where at least two everyday service layers are visible along one genuine pedestrian route—for example a public park edge plus ordinary market/repair/service frontage.
- Why it fits: the Shanghai planning authority has published an official community-life-circle map and a 2026 update for Caoyang.
- Required creator/rights: a named Homeground-commissioned photographer; written worldwide commercial web and social reuse licence; property permission if the camera is placed on private land or the image depends on a private frontage.
- Required caption data: exact road/public space, Caoyang New Village, Putuo District, Shanghai; capture date; photographer; licence record.
- Do not imply: that every visible facility is open to tourists, that the image represents all Chinese communities, or that the pictured route takes every person 15 minutes.

### Option B: Minzhu Village, Jiulongpo District, Chongqing

- Exact subject: the public approach connecting an ordinary market/service frontage and legal pedestrian crossing or public path.
- Why it fits: the Ministry of Commerce case describes micro-renovation joining several everyday services; the terrain can honestly show why straight-line distance is insufficient.
- Required creator/rights: same commissioned, written commercial terms as Option A.
- Required caption data: exact street/entrance, Minzhu Village, Jiulongpo District, Chongqing; capture date; photographer; licence record.
- Do not frame: older housing, working residents or small businesses as picturesque poverty, nostalgia or a contrast with a supposedly “modern” China.

## Privacy and authenticity requirements

- Prefer a wide public-realm view without identifiable residents.
- Do not photograph through residential windows, follow a person through a gate or stage residents as “local colour.”
- No visible faces of children, care recipients or vulnerable adults without explicit informed model releases.
- Exclude resident lists, health information, parcel labels, phone numbers, QR codes, access cards, vehicle plates and precise apartment numbers.
- Obtain specific venue permission for indoor community facilities, clinics, childcare, elder-care or dining spaces even if photography seems tolerated.
- Record source URL, original filename, creator, date, exact place, licence version, download date, transformation and final attribution in the asset log.
- A real photograph proves only the named place at the capture time. It does not prove current hours, current access, policy designation or a universal 15-minute journey.
