# SEO brief — China’s 15-minute neighbourhoods

Reviewed: **2026-08-13 (Asia/Shanghai)**
Canonical: `/guides/china-15-minute-neighbourhoods/`
Decision: one trilingual canonical identity; do not create separate pages for `15-minute city`, `community life circle` or `一刻钟便民生活圈` unless the search task becomes materially different.

## Primary query and search task

- Primary query: `China 15 minute neighbourhood explained`
- Search task: understand what China’s “15-minute” planning language means, distinguish the commerce-led convenient-living circle from the broader community-life-circle framework, and test whether one real walking route and facility are usable without treating a residential community as an attraction.
- Intent: informational with a practical field-verification step; not a city ranking, conspiracy rebuttal, property guide or promise that every need is reachable in exactly 15 minutes.

## Result gap and Homeground information gain

- Existing results commonly collapse `城市一刻钟便民生活圈` and `15分钟社区生活圈`, or explain policy without showing a traveller how to test one route.
- Homeground adds a two-framework distinction, target-versus-achievement guardrail, facility-access matrix, mobility-aware walking audit, privacy boundary and failure recovery.
- The three named local cases are used only to show implementation variance; the page refuses to turn a showcase into a national condition.
- The visitor leaves with a defensible conclusion about one usable route rather than a collection of policy slogans or street photographs.

## Unique asset plan

- Preferred asset: a rights-cleared, named public-street sequence in one community showing the same legal walking route at three points—anchor, barrier/crossing, and public service frontage.
- Companion asset: a simple route-audit diagram comparing planning radius with continuous usable path; it must not imply a precise national boundary.
- Evidence to retain: date, exact public viewpoint, route notes, access status and photographer/license. Avoid identifiable residents, access cards, children and care recipients.
- This brief does not authorize image creation or provenance changes.
