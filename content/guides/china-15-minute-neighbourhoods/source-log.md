## Editorial task and boundary

This page explains how a visitor can read a real Chinese community service network without treating “15 minutes” as an individual travel-time guarantee or the community as an attraction.

- It owns the national everyday-service-system explanation and the field method for testing one actual route.
- It distinguishes the Ministry of Commerce's `城市一刻钟便民生活圈` from the broader spatial-planning term `15分钟社区生活圈`.
- It does not own community-canteen eligibility, prices or meal-card rules; the planned `china-community-canteens-explained` page owns that task.
- It does not rank neighbourhoods, publish a walking tour, guarantee accessibility or imply that public support makes a facility open to tourists.

## Official and primary sources

### Ministry of Commerce — Construction guide

- URL: https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/gztz/art/2021/art_04807f8fe711403485c7d962a83a1172.html
- Checked: 2026-08-13
- Facts used: Defines an urban quarter-hour convenient-living circle as a community-based commercial and service cluster within roughly a 15-minute walk; lists basic and quality-improvement services; calls for local tailoring, reuse of existing space and attention to resident demand.
- Boundary: This commercial-service framework is not identical to every local spatial-planning use of “15-minute community life circle.” The radius is not a guarantee for an individual.
- Update trigger: Revised guide or replacement national policy.

### Ministry of Commerce — 2023–2025 action plan

- URL: https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ghjh/art/2023/art_a1495044fd694f0c920f1430b4dca1de.html
- Checked: 2026-08-13
- Facts used: Applies to eligible prefecture-level cities, uses pilots and assessment, calls for resident consultation and “one circle, one policy.”
- Boundary: A policy target is not proof that a specific community contains each listed service.
- Update trigger: National completion report, successor plan or new pilot list.

### Ministry of Commerce — 2025 expansion notice

- URL: https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/gztz/art/2025/art_989b4d7d0d5842db8177a7ea4d29236a.html
- Checked: 2026-08-13
- Facts used: Continued staged whole-area pilot expansion and movement toward eligible county communities.
- Boundary: Expansion language is not universal coverage.
- Update trigger: New pilot or assessment notice.

### Ministry of Commerce — 2026 work notice

- URL: https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/gztz/art/2026/art_ad0a1e1608be4bcd8ebf6e76ed8dbfbe.html
- Checked: 2026-08-13
- Facts used: Four national pilot batches and two whole-area leading-zone batches were at different stages on 2026-07-29; completion of 10,000 circles during 2026 is a target; favours local, gradual small-scale renewal and rejects one-size-fits-all demolition.
- Boundary: Do not report 10,000 as achieved. Do not infer current status of an individual city without its own notice.
- Update trigger: Year-end outcome or later Ministry of Commerce notice.

- Boundary: Recommended industry guide, not an individual access guarantee or the proposed future national standard.
- Update trigger: Replacement or amendment.

## Editorial judgments

- The useful visitor task is to trace services and actual connections, not collect facilities or enter every institution.
- Public street frontage is not evidence that an interior is open to tourists.
- A traveller-specific continuous route is more meaningful than the nominal 15-minute radius.
- Local old-neighbourhood renewal and new-community planning should be compared without treating either as backward, poor, picturesque or superior.

## Prohibited claims

- Every Chinese city or resident has every service within 15 minutes.
- The planning radius guarantees a 15-minute journey for wheelchairs, older adults or children.
- All community centres, clinics, schools, canteens and care facilities are public tourist attractions.
- China has solved community-service equality or accessibility.
- The 2026 target of 10,000 circles has already been reached.
- The Ministry of Commerce commercial circle and broader life-circle planning term are identical.
- The proposed national standard is already in force.
- Local apps universally work for foreign visitors.
- Older, lower-income or working communities are suitable as exotic documentary scenery.
