# SEO brief — China's 24 solar terms

- Reviewed: 2026-08-13 (Asia/Shanghai)
- Canonical owner: `/guides/china-24-solar-terms-weather-food-daily-life/`
- Page job: explain what the 24 solar terms are and help a traveller interpret weather language, food and local practices without turning the system into a forecast, wellness guide or event directory.
- Do not create: year variants, one page per solar term, city × solar-term pages, fixed annual event tables or medical/TCM advice pages.

`China 24 solar terms explained`

This query is broad enough to match how English-language search results label the subject, while the page earns differentiation through the traveller decision layer. The title and first answer should continue to use both `24 solar terms` and `twenty-four solar terms` naturally.

## Search task

The reader wants a fast definition, the astronomical mechanism and a usable rule for interpreting names, food and local events. The page should satisfy `understand` first, then help the reader decide what must be checked locally. It should not compete for annual date tables or medical advice.

## SERP composition observed on 2026-08-13

The sampled English SERP was dominated by encyclopedic lists, culture explainers and annual date tables. Many results map all 24 names to generalized weather or food statements; few make regional variation, current-host verification and the forecast boundary central.

This is a dated qualitative observation, not a ranking guarantee or traffic estimate.

## Result gap and Homeground information gain

- Keep the immediate answer that a solar term is an astronomical marker, not a local weather event.
- Preserve the three-layer model: astronomical marker, environmental memory and living practice.
- Retain the food-claim classifier: seasonal ingredient, community custom or commercial theme.
- Make the local owner/date/access check the practical payoff missing from list-style results.
- Keep health language explicitly cultural unless supported by clinical evidence.
- Avoid reproducing a 24-row annual date table; authoritative calendars own the exact civil time.
