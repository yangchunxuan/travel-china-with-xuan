## Language and naming decisions

- English uses `Zhou Family Old Taimen` and `Zhou Family New Taimen`, retaining the Chinese at first explanation in body copy.
- Chinese uses `周家老台门`, `周家新台门`, `百草园`, `三味书屋`, `咸亨酒店` and `孔乙己` consistently.
- Korean first mentions retain Chinese forms: `저우자 라오타이먼(周家老台門)`, `저우자 신타이먼(周家新台門)`, `바이차오위안(百草園)`, `싼웨이수우(三味書屋)`, `셴헝주뎬(咸亨酒店)`, `쿵이지(孔乙己)`.
- The collection is rendered descriptively in Korean as `『아침 꽃을 저녁에 줍다』(《朝花夕拾》)` while retaining the Chinese title; this avoids presenting a Homeground wording choice as the only published Korean edition title.
- No modern English or Korean translation is quoted.
