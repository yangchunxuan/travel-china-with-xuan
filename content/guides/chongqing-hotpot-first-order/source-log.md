# Source log and claim ledger — chongqing-hotpot-first-order

All sources were opened or their official files read on **2026-09-01**. Search-result pages and
traveller discussions informed the task definition but prove none of the rules below.

## 1. Current Sichuan–Chongqing regional standard — verified full text

- **Title:** DB50/T 10011—2024 / DB51/T 10011—2024, 火锅类餐饮企业加工操作规范
- **Publisher/register:** National Standard Information Public Service Platform; issued by the
  Chongqing and Sichuan market-regulation administrations
- **URL:** https://dbba.sacinfo.org.cn/stdDetail/99a1ec56165591631b46101374aaa5d4996d274a3c88502230d36c273570d6f4
- **Published:** 2024-12-10; **effective:** 2025-02-10; platform status read as current
- **Checked:** 2026-09-01
- **Supports:** hotpot as a format in which consumers cook ingredients in broth and usually use a
  dip; base may use animal and/or vegetable oil plus some combination of douban paste, chilli,
  spices, salt and vegetable products; red and clear base categories; a valid food-business permit
  should be displayed prominently; operators should prevent cross-contamination; diners should be
  encouraged to order appropriately; raw-material tools and containers should be separated; base and
  served dishes must not be reused; foods requiring thorough cooking should reach a centre
  temperature above 70 °C during food-service processing.
- **Cannot support:** the exact ingredients in today's broth, a consumer-readable seconds rule,
  portion sizes, grid meaning, spice grades, or a restaurant's compliance.

## 3. Chongqing local-gazette cultural account — verified with caveats

- **Title:** 〖重庆非遗〗重庆火锅
- **Publisher:** Chongqing Local Chronicles Office; excerpt credited to the Chongqing Culture and
  Tourism Research Institute's *Yuwei* series
- **URL:** https://dfz.cq.gov.cn/zqlswh/rwby_417819/202412/t20241219_13907530.html
- **Published:** 2024-12-19; **checked:** 2026-09-01
- **Supports:** an official local account of changing broths, ingredients and dipping-sauce forms;
  the documented contrast between a broth seasoned with chilli, Sichuan pepper, tallow, ginger and
  douban and northern clear-broth forms; the account's statement that the municipal project was
  listed in 2009.
- **Used with restraint:** the body uses the source to prove **variation**, not a complete menu.
- **Cannot support:** one uncontested origin event. Its “fiery, straightforward Chongqing
  personality” language is cultural rhetoric and is explicitly rejected as evidence about every
  resident.
