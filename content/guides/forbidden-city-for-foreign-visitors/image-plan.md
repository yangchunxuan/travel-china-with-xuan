# Image plan

**COPY DRAFT — EDITORIAL REVIEW AND ASSETS NEEDED**

Use only real, licensed or Homeground-owned photography with verified location and rights. Do not generate a documentary-looking Forbidden City scene with AI.

## Hero

- Purpose: make the correct southern arrival immediately understandable.
- Preferred image: a real horizontal photograph showing visitors approaching the Meridian Gate (Wumen) from the south, with enough architectural context to identify the gate.
- Alternative: a real exterior image of the Meridian Gate without identifiable visitors, provided the caption clearly calls it the only visitor entrance.
- Avoid: a Shenwumen or corner-tower image presented as the entrance, an unverified generic red gate, an empty staged composite, visible passport data or an image that implies there is no security approach.
- Crop target: 1600 × 1000 WebP at `public/images/guides/forbidden-city-for-foreign-visitors/hero-1600.webp`.
- Update all three `heroAlt` fields after the exact photograph and viewpoint are known.

## Essential route graphic

- Highest-priority supporting asset: a clean editorial diagram showing south at the bottom and north at the top.
- Required labels: Meridian Gate entrance, the central-axis sequence used in the article, one optional side-focus zone, Imperial Garden, Gate of Divine Prowess exit and East Prosperity Gate alternative exit.
- Add an external arrow to Jingshan Park and a neutral “confirm bus or pickup point” note outside the north gate.
- Base gate positions and open-area orientation on the Palace Museum's official visitor map. Redraw as an original Homeground schematic; do not publish a Google Maps screenshot or copy the museum map artwork.
- Do not present the minute allocations as official Palace Museum timings. Label the graphic “Homeground 3-hour planning route.”

## Entrance section

- One real photograph of the Meridian Gate approach or the final pedestrian route along the moat.
- The caption should explain that Donghuamen or Xihuamen may be approach landmarks but are not visitor entrances.
- Do not photograph or publish readable passport pages, booking QR codes or close identifiable faces without permission.

## Route section

- One real central-axis photograph with verified hall identification.
- A second optional image may show one named side gallery or palace cluster, but only if its location and current opening context are documented.
- Do not use a temporary exhibition image to imply permanent access.

## North-gate exit section

- One real photograph of Shenwumen from the visitor-exit side, ideally showing its relationship to the road and Jingshan direction.
- A transport-context image is useful only if the stop, date and route signage are legible and current; otherwise the route diagram is safer.
- Do not imply that taxis can always wait immediately outside the gate.

## Rights and editorial checks

- Record creator, source, licence, permitted channels, capture date if known and exact gate or hall identification.
- Keep Meridian Gate, Gate of Divine Prowess, East Prosperity Gate and corner-tower images separate; they are not interchangeable.
- Do not add or remove crowds in a documentary photograph.
- No hero asset is currently present. Guide generation and the site build are not expected to pass until the real image is supplied.
