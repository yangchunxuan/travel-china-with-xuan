## Editorial decisions

- The decision formula deliberately contains no prices, percentages or averages — three worked rows describe structures only and are labeled illustrative examples.
- Guarantee-card charging is described strictly as authorization bounded by published rate rules; no fee amounts asserted.
- Partial refunds described as computed by tier ladders where applicable; no specific ladder values claimed.
- No statement that refundable is always safer or non-refundable always cheaper.
