# Canonical boundary — refundable-or-nonrefundable-china-hotel-rate

## Search task uniquely owned by this page

Help a foreign traveller decide, before booking a China hotel night, whether the flexible-rate premium is worth paying — by first listing the dependencies that night sits behind (entry, prior transport, attraction reservations, weather-exposed segments) and then applying rate mechanics (deadline, clock, prepay/guarantee/no-show rules) to that specific dependency load.

## Why a new canonical instead of updating an old page

No existing Homeground page owns the pre-booking refundable-vs-non-refundable decision. Stay guides cover area/type choice; payment guides cover how to pay; the evidence-pack guide starts after something went wrong. The dependency-counting method and rate-mechanics explanation would not fit any of them without blurring their tasks.
