## High-volatility facts deliberately excluded from the body

- **Every individual performance date.** Only source-publication months and the boundaries of the
  historical sample appear; neither is a usable performance calendar.
- **Every programme title.** Titles change constantly, and the body says so and tells the reader to
  search by venue and troupe instead.
- **Every ticket price**, in every language, including the ranges seen in the listings.
- **The published enquiry telephone number.** Deliberately withheld: it is the correct channel and
  the body says where to find it, but a stale number printed in a guide is worse than none.
- **Availability of any kind.** The body states plainly that it cannot tell the reader today's
  seats.
- **Any claim that a particular venue is open, that the troupe is currently performing, or that a
  venue determines the start time.**
- **Any real-time or permanent calendar**, which is refused in writing with its reason.

## Freshness note for central

Rows 1 and 2 are the load-bearing ones. This article is essentially a pointer to two municipal
channels; if either is restructured or moved, the page's central section stops working even though
every sentence in it remains individually true. **A reviewer who finds either URL redirecting should
treat that as urgent**, not cosmetic.

Row 4 is the one most likely to be misread by a future editor. Seventy minutes is stated only as an
observation in cited historical examples, not a published standard or expected duration. The current
listing always controls, whether it repeats that number or not.

Row 5 exists to prevent another false regularity. The June 2026 monthly page lists both afternoon and
evening nanyin at the Nanyin Art Garden, and the May 2026 special-event page lists six starts in one
day. Do not reconstruct a venue-to-timeslot rule from older examples.

Rows 8 and 9 come from a January 2025 feature article. They are attributed to it by name and date in
the body. In particular, the sound-system description must never be converted into a blanket claim
about nanyin performances or a current equipment guarantee.

**Nothing in this article should ever be updated by adding an individual performance date, a price
or a telephone number.** If a future editor feels the pull, the correct response is to strengthen
the instructions for reading the official listings.
