# Dynamic facts — quanzhou-nanyin-first-performance-workflow

Reviewed 2026-08-22, Asia/Taipei.

This is a page about a schedule, written without publishing a schedule. The brief forbids a
real-time or permanent performance calendar, so the volatile layer is **excluded from the body
entirely** and replaced by a method for finding it. What remains published is the structure of the
official channels and a small number of regularities observed across four independent listings.

## High-volatility facts deliberately excluded from the body

- **Every performance date.** Not one appears in any language.
- **Every programme title.** Titles change constantly, and the body says so and tells the reader to
  search by venue and troupe instead.
- **Every ticket price**, in every language, including the ranges seen in the listings.
- **The published enquiry telephone number.** Deliberately withheld: it is the correct channel and
  the body says where to find it, but a stale number printed in a guide is worse than none.
- **Availability of any kind.** The body states plainly that it cannot tell the reader today's
  seats.
- **Any claim that a particular venue is open, or that the troupe is currently performing.**
- **Any real-time or permanent calendar**, which is refused in writing with its reason.

## Freshness note for central

Rows 1 and 2 are the load-bearing ones. This article is essentially a pointer to two municipal
channels; if either is restructured or moved, the page's central section stops working even though
every sentence in it remains individually true. **A reviewer who finds either URL redirecting should
treat that as urgent**, not cosmetic.

Row 4 is the one most likely to be misread by a future editor. Seventy minutes is an observation
across four listings, not a published standard. If a fifth listing shows a different duration, the
correct fix is to soften the sentence, **not** to add a second number.

Rows 8 and 9 come from a January 2025 feature article. They are attributed to it by name and date in
the body. If they are ever restated as bare facts, the attribution must go back in.

**Nothing in this article should ever be updated by adding a date, a price or a telephone number.**
If a future editor feels the pull, the correct response is to strengthen the instructions for
reading the official listings.
