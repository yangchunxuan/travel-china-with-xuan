## 1. UNESCO — Nanyin, Representative List: **VERIFIED, read in full**

- **Institution:** UNESCO, Intangible Cultural Heritage Section
- **Page title:** Nanyin
- **URL:** https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/nanyin-00199
- **Nomination file:** No. 00199 · **Inscription decision:** 4.COM 13.20
- **Published:** inscribed 2009 (4.COM); the page is a maintained record · **Retrieved:** 2026-08-22
- **Supports:**
  - **Inscribed in 2009** on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.
  - A **musical performing art central to the culture of the Minnan people of southern Fujian** and
    to Minnan populations overseas; **slow, simple and elegant melodies**.
  - **Distinctive instruments: a bamboo flute called the dongxiao, and a crooked-neck lute played
    horizontally called the pipa**, plus more common wind, string and percussion instruments.
  - **Three components:** one purely instrumental; one including voice; one of **ballads accompanied
    by the ensemble and sung in Quanzhou dialect, either by a sole singer who also plays clappers or
    by a group of four who perform in turn**.
  - The repertoire preserves ancient folk music and poems and **has influenced opera, puppet theatre
    and other performing art traditions**.
  - Performed at **spring and autumn ceremonies to worship Meng Chang, the god of music**, at
    weddings and funerals, and during festivities **in courtyards, markets and the streets**.
  - The file's own domain classification is **Performing arts**, and its recorded threatening factor
    is **urban development**.
- **Cannot support:** any performance date, venue, price or troupe; anything about how nanyin is
  programmed in Quanzhou today.
- **Dynamic:** no. This is a fixed inscription record.
- **Load-bearing role:** it is the source for the two vocal formats, which is what allows this page
  to refuse the "one fixed ensemble" claim the brief warns against.

## 2. Fujian Provincial People's Government portal — Quanzhou nanyin feature: **VERIFIED, read in full**

- **Institution:** 福建省人民政府门户网站 (Fujian Provincial People's Government portal), culture and
  tourism economy section
- **Page title:** 听一曲南音 品千年典雅——走进闽派文艺精品系列报道之泉州南音
- **URL:** https://www.fj.gov.cn/zwgk/ztzl/sxzygwzxsgzx/sdjj/wvjj/202501/t20250117_6703931.htm
- **Published:** 2025-01-17 · **Retrieved:** 2026-08-22
- **Supports:**
  - The Chinese names and behaviour of the three parts: **指 (指套)** has words, score and pipa
    fingering but is **generally not sung and is placed at the opening as pure ensemble playing**;
    **谱** is purely instrumental with titles mostly describing seasons, flowers, birds, insects and
    grasses; **曲 (散曲)** is by far the most numerous, covering longing, landscape, religion and
    more.
  - A documented programme order at one performance: an opening 箫指, then unaccompanied songs, a
    dongxiao solo, a duet, more songs, and **a closing 谱《梅花操》**.
  - **More than three thousand** scores, loose songs and suites have been collected and organised.
  - **The seating described as 最经典的表演形式 (the most classic performance form):** the singer
    holds the clappers in the centre; **pipa and sanxian to the right; dongxiao and erxian to the
    left**.
  - **The nanguan pipa is 曲项、横抱** — bent-necked and held horizontally; the notation 工ㄨ谱 is
    **also called the pipa fingering score**, and **the pipa leads the ensemble, playing the
    skeletal notes**.
  - Organological attributions: the **dongxiao follows the Tang chiba system**; the sanxian derives
    from a Qin-and-Han drum; the erxian retains the basic form of an early bowed instrument.
  - **"学者普遍认为"** — scholars generally hold that nanyin is closely related to Tang and Song
    music and is among the oldest surviving Chinese genres. **Explicitly an attributed position.**
  - Migration from the Central Plains across several dynasties brought musical culture into the
    Minnan region, where it fused with local music.
  - The small theatre on the upper floor of the nanyin venue is named for a shrine; **practitioners
    revere Meng Chang as the founding patron, titled Langjun**; performers stand on a gilded lion
    with palace lanterns beside them; **the room has no modern stage lighting or sound system** and
    the audience sits at no distance.
  - Ecosystem figures: **more than 400 nanyin societies, more than 70 transmission sites, more than
    6,000 people working in performance**; venues include the nanyin art garden, the municipal
    intangible heritage museum and a cultural salon, plus red-brick houses, teahouses and
    guesthouses. A temple-courtyard amateur society of about twenty, mostly retirees, is named after
    a classical nanyin score.
  - **1989**: nanyin entered Quanzhou primary and secondary classrooms. The local teachers'
    university runs what it describes as the country's first nanyin undergraduate programme and
    postgraduate provision.
  - A **national-level tradition-bearer born in 1946** is quoted: Quanzhou families sing nanyin at
    month-old banquets, birthdays, weddings and funerals, from birth to death; and **learning nanyin
    is learning Minnan speech**, which is why overseas families send children to study it.
  - The professional troupe's new work has won **national quyi awards**, and a nanyin-based song
    reached the **2023** national Spring Festival broadcast.
- **Cannot support:** current schedules, prices or availability; and it is a feature article, so its
  more lyrical framing ("a thousand years of elegance" in the headline) is **not treated as a
  factual claim** anywhere in the body.
- **Dynamic:** low for the descriptive material; the ecosystem counts are as-of-2025 figures and are
  attributed to the source rather than presented as current measurements.

## 3. Quanzhou Municipal Bureau of Culture, Radio, Television and Tourism — monthly schedule: **VERIFIED, read in full**

- **Page title:** 3月份泉州市文艺院团公益性文艺演出活动日程表
- **URL:** https://cbtb.quanzhou.gov.cn/xwdt/bjdt/202502/t20250226_3143384.htm
- **Published:** 2025-02-26 · **Retrieved:** 2026-08-22
- **Supports:**
  - That the city **publishes a monthly, dated, in-advance table** of public-welfare performances by
    municipal arts troupes, with columns for date, time, venue, programme, duration, troupe and
    ticket price.
  - **Nanyin is programmed by 泉州市南音传承中心** at two venues named for nanyin — a larger art
    garden and a small theatre — with **every nanyin entry in that month running 70 minutes**,
    evening starts at the larger venue and afternoon starts at the small theatre, and **prices
    differing between programmes within the same month**.
  - That **several separate troupes appear on the same schedule at separate theatres**, including
    the Gaojia and Dacheng opera centre, the Minnan song-and-dance centre, **the string puppetry
    centre at its own puppet theatres**, and the Fujian Liyuan opera centre at its own theatre.
    This is the basis for the "you booked at the wrong theatre" row.
- **Cannot support:** any month it does not cover; availability; and **no date, programme title or
  price from this page is reproduced in the body.**
- **Dynamic:** **high.** It is a one-month schedule. Used only as evidence of the channel's
  existence, structure and regularities.

## Absolute expressions audited after drafting

- **No performance date, programme title, ticket price or telephone number appears in any of the
  three languages.** Grepped and confirmed.
- **No claim that nanyin is Tang-dynasty music preserved intact.** The article has a dedicated table
  row saying the opposite, and every Tang-related statement in the body carries its attribution
  ("scholars generally hold", "is described as following").
- **No claim of a thousand unbroken years.** The article states in all three languages that no
  source consulted says this. The provincial source's headline uses "a thousand years" as a
  rhetorical phrase; it is reproduced only inside the source list as part of the page title, never
  as a claim.
- **No claim of a fixed ensemble.** Two vocal formats are given from source 1, and the seating is
  labelled a convention using the source's own word "classic".
- **No real-time or permanent calendar.** Refused explicitly, with the reason.
- **No claim that Homeground can book, hold or check seats.**
- The only categorical statements are negatives: that no source says X, and that this page will not
  publish Y.

## Sources deliberately not used

Ticketing platforms, travel-site "Quanzhou nanyin show" pages and reposted performance calendars
were found in quantity and **none was used**. The article names that class of source as the most
common reason a visitor arrives at a dark theatre.
