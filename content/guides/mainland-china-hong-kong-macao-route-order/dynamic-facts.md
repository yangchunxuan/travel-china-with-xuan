# Dynamic facts ledger

Recheck immediately before central release and advise travellers to recheck before booking/travel:

- each passport's mainland visa/visa-free basis and remaining entries;
- Hong Kong visit/transit requirements;
- Macao visit/transit requirements;
- mainland 240-hour or other transit-policy text;
- port, operator, terminal and opening information.

No individual eligibility, fixed port hour or guaranteed admission is published. Central legal/entry owner must sign off.
