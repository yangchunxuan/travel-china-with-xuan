# Canonical boundary — Xiamen and Gulangyu in one day

Canonical ID: `xiamen-and-gulangyu-one-day`

This page owns the strategic split between a Xiamen-city block and a Gulangyu-island block: which receives the best hours, how much each side can realistically hold and when to stop trying to cover both. `Xiamen and Gulangyu one day`, `Gulangyu or Xiamen`, `can I see both in a day` and `Xiamen one-day itinerary with Gulangyu` merge here.

It does not own passport booking, passenger-terminal selection, ferry check-in or return mechanics. Those stay with `xiamen-hubs-to-gulangyu-ferry-terminal`. It also does not become a complete Gulangyu attraction list, a Xiamen hotel guide or a live timetable page.
