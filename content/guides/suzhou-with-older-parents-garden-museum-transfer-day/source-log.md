## Official dynamic sources

- [Suzhou Museum notice dated 2026-09-16, published by Suzhou Municipal Government 2026-09-20](https://www.suzhou.gov.cn/szsrmzf/mszx/202609/2d1a50ec4496485d9f5c332de585dfdc.shtml): main building requires timed reservations, available seven days ahead counting the day the booking is made; the West building and named other venues then used no-reservation queue admission. The extended hours in this notice apply only to the stated Mid-Autumn/National Day dates. It does not establish December hours or guarantee a slot.
- [Suzhou Museum 2026 May Day notice](https://www.suzhou.gov.cn/szsrmzf/mszx/202604/3c3c5288f66d4d91859d80abe1d6e30e.shtml): main-building visitors show reservation information and the original ID or passport; the then-published luggage advice places the main-building luggage service opposite the entrance. Because this is a holiday notice, the public guide avoids promising that service for every later visit.
- [Suzhou Municipal Government garden notice, 2026-06-19](https://www.suzhou.gov.cn/szsrmzf/mszx/202606/face4650ec2245ce818aecc53eb4aaaa.shtml): Humble Administrator's Garden real-name timed online booking 1–7 days in advance; the 06:45 opening is specifically 2026-06-19 through 2026-10-07. Neither a December 06:45 opening nor same-day ticket supply follows from it.
- [Suzhou Landscape and Greening Bureau answer, 2026-03-04](https://www.suzhou.gov.cn/szsrmzf/zfzb/202602/0922ee651b354608b175f451211683d2.shtml): Humble Administrator's, Lingering and Lion Forest gardens require advance online reservation; other gardens then did not. A bundled third-party tour cannot override sold-out official admission. The public guide names only the garden it actually uses.
- [Suzhou Station onward travel guidance, 2026-04-02](https://www.suzhou.gov.cn/szsrmzf/mszx/202604/568a4eac0636443c968cfc195b9a3e29.shtml) and [official 12306 journey search](https://kyfw.12306.cn/otn/view/queryPublicIndex.html): old-city sites have rail/metro access, but departure and arrival stations, services, travel time and availability require the real date. No fixed Suzhou–Hangzhou train, door-to-door duration or station was verified for this guide.
