# Canonical boundary

## Owner

This page owns one narrow task: after a traveller has already decided to visit the Daocheng Yading Scenic Area, should the sleeping base be **Yading Village** inside the scenic-area transport chain or **Shangri-La Town (Riwa), Daocheng, Sichuan** on the gateway side?

The decision is made from confirmed arrival time, relative altitude exposure, one- or two-day sight order, luggage, current entry and park-transport conditions, the real cost of one hotel move, and failure recovery.

## It owns

- The town-to-village-to-inner-transport sequence needed to understand the two bases.
- When a relatively lower gateway night should veto a convenience gain higher inside the scenic area.
- A one-day, two-day and split-stay decision matrix.
- The exact property questions needed before relying on a village stay.
- Recovery from late arrival, closed or changed routes, failed pickup, a property mismatch or worsening symptoms.
- A reminder that **Shangri-La Town (Riwa), Daocheng, Sichuan** is not Shangri-La City in Yunnan.

## It does not own

- Whether Daocheng Yading is worth visiting, a complete Sichuan itinerary or a broad Daocheng destination guide.
- Ticket prices, booking tutorials, fixed entry hours, trail instructions, live weather, live roads or a permanent second-entry rule.
- Medical diagnosis, prevention or a promise that sleeping in either base prevents altitude illness.
- Hotel rankings, property recommendations, prices, inventory, ratings, foreign-guest acceptance, room type or accessibility guarantees.
- The generic `where to stay in Sichuan` or `where to stay in China` intent.

Daocheng county seat/Jinzhu Town may appear only as a late-arrival or disrupted-transport fallback. It is not promoted into a third equal base in this owner.

## Entity boundary

The central registry now resolves this owner to `attraction-daocheng-yading`, with `county-daocheng`, `town-shangri-la-daocheng`, `village-yading` and `province-sichuan` as controlled context. The scenic area and the village are distinct entities: the scenic area belongs to Daocheng County, while Yading Village belongs to Shangri-La Town. This prevents the graph from incorrectly treating the whole scenic area as a child of the village. The page must not map to Chengdu or to Shangri-La City in Yunnan.
