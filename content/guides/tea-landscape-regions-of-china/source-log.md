## Evidence and boundaries

1. [Traditional tea processing techniques and associated social practices in China](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-tea-processing-techniques-and-associated-social-practices-in-china-01884?RL=01884) — UNESCO ICH. Supports the nationwide living-knowledge frame, not certification of an individual workshop.
2. [Cultural Landscape of Old Tea Forests of Jingmai Mountain in Pu'er](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1665/) — UNESCO World Heritage Centre. Supports Jingmai's linked forests, villages and community practices, not all Pu'er production.
3. [Pu'er Traditional Tea Agrosystem](https://www.fao.org/giahs/giahs-around-the-world/china-puer-traditional-tea-agrosystem/en) — FAO GIAHS. Cross-checks layered vegetation, biodiversity and management pressures.
4. [Mount Wuyi](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/911) — UNESCO World Heritage Centre. Establishes the wider property and its mixed values; the listing is not a tea-quality certificate.
5. [Second list of China Nationally Important Agricultural Heritage Systems](https://www.moa.gov.cn/nybgb/2014/dliuq/201712/P020220424745802769272.pdf) — Ministry of Agriculture of China. Officially lists the West Lake Longjing Tea Culture System; it is a designation record, not a visitor-access or product-authenticity guarantee.
6. [West Lake Longjing green-tea processing technique](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/14605.html) — China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network. Supports the named production area, cultivation knowledge and distinctive manual pan-firing, not an exhaustive fixed visitor demonstration.
7. [Wuyi rock-tea processing technique](https://www.ihchina.cn/art/detail/id/14373.html) — China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network. Supports the connected processing sequence, not a claim that a brief visitor activity reproduces it.
8. [Wuyi traditional-village tea landscape](https://zjt.fujian.gov.cn/xxgk/gzdt/bmdt/202607/t20260715_7178552.htm) — Fujian Department of Housing and Urban-Rural Development. Dated 2026 context, not a permanent visitor programme.
9. [Third reserve list for China Global Important Agricultural Heritage Systems](https://www.agri.cn/zx/hxgg/202501/t20250124_8708188.htm) — Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs of China. Officially retains the Hunan Anhua Dark Tea Culture System in the reserve list; it does not itself document every landscape component.
10. [Qujiang tea-garden, village and old-road heritage](https://wwj.hunan.gov.cn/c100310/c100311/202007/t20200727_13078842.html) — Hunan Provincial Cultural Heritage Bureau. Documents one named Anhua component made up of tea gardens, vernacular buildings, an old road, wells and monuments; it is not generalized to all of Anhua.
11. [Qianliang tea processing technique](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/14624.html) — China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network. Supports the staged production description, not guaranteed participation.

## Public-link endpoint audit

- The two former China Agricultural Museum public URLs on `jgs.agri.gov.cn` still appeared in current search results, but their HTTPS certificate did not match the public hostname on 2026-08-13. They were therefore removed from the public Sources block rather than downgraded to insecure HTTP.
- West Lake Longjing is now covered publicly by the Ministry's official designation list plus the national ICH technique record. Anhua is covered by the Ministry's reserve-list notice plus the Hunan heritage authority's component-level Qujiang record. These replacements are first-party and narrower than the former summaries; their boundaries are stated above.
- UNESCO World Heritage pages returned automated-client 403 responses during command-line checking but remained current, indexed official records. The ICH pages were intermittently slow over TLS but returned current content. Neither class was treated as a dead link.
