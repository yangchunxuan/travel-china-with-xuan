## Explanatory image gaps for central assets

1. **Before/after bread:** the same verified restaurant order, first showing the supplied `饦饦馍`
   and then its broken pieces in the marked bowl. Caption must state the restaurant's requested size,
   not call it a citywide law.
2. **Service handoff:** hands returning the bowl and, if the shop uses one, its current
   order-identification object, only with diner and staff permission; crop faces and remove
   table/order identifiers.
3. **Four-style comparison:** use HTML, not a composite food photograph. Current availability and
   visual outcomes vary, so an editable table is more honest than staged bowls.
4. **Accompaniments:** sugar garlic, chilli paste and coriander photographed at one identified shop;
   caption says “offered here”, never “always included”.
5. **Community context:** an exterior or public interior of a verified qingzhen paomo restaurant,
   with permission and no worship space or identifiable diners. Do not use generic mosque imagery as
   decoration for a meal.

## Rights and ethics

- Do not lift food photos from restaurant menus, delivery apps or tourism accounts without written
  reuse permission.
- Do not photograph diners, workers or Hui residents as cultural props. Obtain release for any
  recognisable person, especially in a working kitchen.
- A halal sign or brand history cannot be inferred from beef/lamb imagery; verify the exact business
  and current rights before a caption makes that claim.
- No AI-generated documentary food, shop, worker or street image is acceptable.
