# Canonical boundary

## Owner

This page owns one decision **after the traveller has decided to visit Jiuzhaigou**: whether an arrival or departure night belongs in the outside-the-entrance lodging belt in Zhangzha Town, Jiuzhaigou County, or at the Huanglongjiuzhai rail gateway around Chuanzhusi Town, Songpan County.

It owns:

- the direct default for a confirmed full Jiuzhaigou park day;
- the conditions that make a rail-gateway transition night worthwhile;
- the distinction between the scenic area, the outside entrance/goukou lodging belt, Jiuzhaigou county seat, and Huanglongjiuzhai Railway Station;
- one-base versus one-move logic;
- accommodation consequences for late arrival, early train, family, older traveller, large luggage and a broken handoff;
- exact-property verification and accommodation recovery.

## Existing owners retained

- `chengdu-jiuzhaigou-transport-route` owns the Chengdu–Jiuzhaigou mode choice, exact rail station, named onward transfer, booking handoff, return and transport disruption. This page links to it and never republishes a timetable, fare, coach-booking walkthrough or complete station-to-valley route.
- `jiuzhaigou-one-day-sightseeing-bus-route` on a remote article branch owns the park-day sightseeing and internal bus order. This page consumes a confirmed park day and does not design that route.
- `chengdu-jiuzhaigou-huanglong-route-order` on a remote article branch owns the multi-stop Chengdu–Jiuzhaigou–Huanglong order and altitude buffer. This page does not choose the destination sequence.
- Destination and city/county pages retain broad destination orientation. This page does not target “Jiuzhaigou travel guide” or a hotel list.
- `foreigners-china-hotel` owns national foreign-guest registration and refusal recovery.
- `china-accessible-hotel-room-verification` owns measurements and the complete accessible room-chain audit.

## Explicit non-ownership

This page does not own or guarantee park tickets, current opening hours, exact trains or transfers, road status, prices, inventory, foreign-guest acceptance, room type, accessibility suitability, hotel quality, property rankings, or the best hotel. It never treats an OTA “near station” or “near entrance” label as geographic proof.

It also does not turn Songpan Station, Jiuzhaigou county seat/Nanping, Huanglong Scenic Area or an airport into equal third/fourth bases. They appear only where a real interruption or already-confirmed sight order makes them relevant.

## Entity handling

Metadata intentionally uses `destinations: ["china"]`, which resolves to `country-china` without fallback or an unmapped token. `county-jiuzhaigou-aba` exists only as a planning reference, not a runtime guide entity. `attraction-jiuzhaigou-valley`, `town-zhangzha-jiuzhaigou`, `transport-node-huanglongjiuzhai-station`, `town-chuanzhusi-songpan` and any county-level runtime mapping are future central decisions, not created or mapped in this article branch.

No homepage, destination Hub, Search Map, registry, shared entity map, template, sitemap or production setting is modified.
