## Accuracy boundary

This photograph is a **named Jinsha case**, not a generic image of every archaeological site museum in China. The caption and alt text must identify Jinsha Site Museum in Chengdu and say that it shows one model: a protective hall over excavated remains. It does not prove that every visible feature is currently accessible, that active excavation can be watched, or that other museums operate in the same way.

Suggested caption:

> Excavated remains inside the Relics Hall of Jinsha Site Museum in Chengdu. Jinsha is one example of a protective hall built around an archaeological site; Chinese site museums use several different arrangements.
