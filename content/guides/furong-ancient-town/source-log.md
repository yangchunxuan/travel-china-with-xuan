1. Hunan provincial government (EN), [Furong Town](https://enghunan.gov.cn/hneng/Tourism/TourHunan/xiangxi_1/TouristAttractions_13/201507/t20150724_1806806.html): Yongshun County, Xiangxi; more than 2,000 years; "an ancient town hanging on the waterfall"; originally Wangcun; renamed in 1997 after the film; once the palace of a Tusi chieftain.
