# Source log and claim ledger — Dujiangyan and Mount Qingcheng: same day or separate

Prepared: 2026-09-04 (Asia/Shanghai)

## Research method

The research started with the canonical collision audit, then separated four evidence classes:

1. World Heritage significance and physical components;
2. current admission, document and optional-service rules;
3. dated examples showing that capacity, weather and maintenance can change the plan;
4. current search-result and traveller-question evidence showing the decision language people use.

Only the first three classes support destination facts. Google, commercial pages and Reddit establish demand, competing answer shapes or traveller uncertainty; they do not establish current operations. No search volume, crowd count, conversion rate, fixed price, bus time or availability forecast is inferred.

## Source conflicts and limits

1. The live ticket page publishes prices and hours, but they are intentionally omitted from body copy because this page solves allocation, not price lookup, and future operations are unstable.
2. The portal describes an approximate Dujiangyan visit duration; the article does not treat it as universal for slow readers, parents, queues or all optional areas.
3. Dated official visitor information names Front Mountain and Back Mountain separately, and an official plan treats them as distinct planning areas. Neither source is upgraded into proof of current Back Mountain admission or operation, and UNESCO documents are not simplified into the same boundary system.
4. Current Back Mountain admission and operation were not closed by the live ticket portal reviewed for this draft. The article directs travellers to recheck them separately.
5. No official source closed a stable site-to-site bus number, boarding point, fare or duration. Those details are excluded.
6. Planning documents prove planning and management layers, not that a facility operates on the day.
