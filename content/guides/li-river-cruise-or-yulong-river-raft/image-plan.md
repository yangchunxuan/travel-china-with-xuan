# Image plan — Li River cruise or Yulong River raft

Prepared: 2026-09-04 (Asia/Shanghai)

## Image rule

Every image must identify the right waterway and support a specific decision. A scenic photograph cannot prove a current departure, vessel, fare, route opening, water level or passenger rule. No official-site image is reusable merely because it is public; official Li River and Yulong photographs without an open licence are excluded. No AI documentary image, unrelated `nuo`/raft scene, reseller screenshot or recognisable-person close-up is allowed.

## Known visual gap

No accurately licensed current photograph of the official Li River pure-electric sightseeing raft or a current Yulong rules board was found. That gap is explicit. The identity diagram explains the third product without pretending to be documentary evidence; current rules remain linked as text sources. A future central replacement must retain exact river/operator/product identity and full rights records.

## Attribution and derivative handling

- Store each Commons file page, original URL, author, source date, licence and production review date in `source-log.md`.
- Strip EXIF from public derivatives.
- Name the transformation: 16:10 crop, resize and WebP conversion.
- Preserve CC BY-SA notices for Yulong weirs and aerial derivatives; do not relabel them as Homeground-owned.
- Do not crop toward incidental people, logos or vessels in a way that creates an endorsement or current-product claim.
- SVG captions must call each diagram an editorial model and state that it is not a route map, timetable, operating status or operator interface.
