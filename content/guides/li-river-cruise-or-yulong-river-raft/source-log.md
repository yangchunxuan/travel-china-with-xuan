# Source log — Li River cruise or Yulong River raft

Prepared and reviewed: 2026-09-04 (Asia/Shanghai)

## Editorial answer

This is not a two-column “big boat versus bamboo raft” article. The official systems show three materially different products hidden inside that wording:

1. a star-rated large-vessel Li River landscape passage sold under the official Li River system;
2. a route-specific **pure-electric Li River sightseeing raft**, also under the Li River system;
3. a route-specific **hand-poled Yulong River raft**, sold through a separate wharf/operator system.

Homeground's primary decision is therefore to identify the product before comparing it. Choose the classic large-vessel Li River passage for a long, continuous corridor and more onboard shelter/service; choose a Yulong route for a smaller-scale, open-air, Yangshuo-countryside encounter when its exact wharf, endpoints and passenger rules fit; choose a Li River electric raft only when that named Li section is the desired experience. It is not shorthand for the other two.

## Search and traveller-question evidence

Current Google searches for `Li River cruise or Yulong River raft`, `Li River cruise vs bamboo raft`, `Yulong River route which wharf`, Chinese equivalents and Korean equivalents were reviewed on 2026-09-04. The result set now contains several recent commercial comparisons, so this page does not claim an empty SERP. Recurring weaknesses were:

- merging large Li River cruises with Li River electric rafts;
- calling every small craft a “bamboo raft” without naming the river or propulsion;
- treating Yulong as one route;
- freezing price/time and passport claims without a first-party date;
- presenting a one-way landscape passage as door-to-door luggage transport;
- offering no separate recovery when the two operators react differently to conditions.

Traveller questions reviewed on Reddit in 2024–2026 ask how to choose among Yulong wharves, how to book when official Chinese product cards are unclear, whether a Li River cruise can replace transport and whether a reverse direction exists. These questions support wording and information need only. No forum statement supports an operating fact.
