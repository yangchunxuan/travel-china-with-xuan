## Entity and wording rules

- “Li River cruise” means the selected official cruise product, not every vessel on the Li River.
- “Li River sightseeing raft” means the official pure-electric raft products on named Li River sections. It must not be described as a Yulong River hand-poled bamboo raft.
- “Yulong River raft” means a selected official Yulong route/wharf product. It is not one universal route.
- “Bamboo raft” is too ambiguous to support a booking decision on its own; the page always adds river, operator/wharf and endpoints.
- The classic star-rated Guilin-side-to-Yangshuo cruise is discussed as a one-way landscape passage. The current official route page also lists a Yangshuo–Yangdi return sailing, so the page requires travellers to match the live product and direction rather than claiming every Li River cruise has one direction.
- Route time, price, schedule, presale window, inventory, water level, vessel assignment, eligibility, refund handling and passport workflow are dynamic.
