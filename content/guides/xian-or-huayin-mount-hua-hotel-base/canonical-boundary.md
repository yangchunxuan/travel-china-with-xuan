# Canonical boundary

## Primary intent

Help a traveller who has already decided to visit Mount Hua (Huashan) place the night immediately before and/or after that visit: keep a continuing Xi’an base, move to Huayin before entry, sleep in Huayin after descent, or use Huayin on both sides. The decision is made from the confirmed first entry chain, the last workable descent-and-exit chain, luggage custody, the onward direction and a dated disruption fallback.

## This owner owns

- Xi’an day-return versus a Huayin pre-night, post-night or two-night staging pattern.
- The accommodation consequence of the booked entry type: ropeway users check at the Mount Hua Visitor Centre, while walkers use the Yuquanyuan–Huashan Gate chain.
- The fact that Huashan North Railway Station is a Huayin rail gateway, not the scenic-area gate or proof of a useful hotel pin.
- Whether one hotel move removes a fixed failure point or merely adds packing, luggage and check-in work.
- Recovery lodging when a delayed descent, suspended operation or missed rail connection makes the original sleep plan unusable.

## This owner does not own

- Whether Mount Hua deserves an extra Xi’an day, how many nights Xi’an needs, or a general Xi’an itinerary; those belong to the published Xi’an destination Hub.
- Which Xi’an neighbourhood to choose after deciding to stay in the city; `xian-where-to-stay-city-wall-or-dayanta` owns that comparison.
- The climbing route, peak order, ropeway choice, ticket purchase, sunrise, night-climb technique, fitness or mountain-safety decision.
- A permanent timetable, fare, opening hour, entry window, road status, ropeway or local-bus promise.
- Summit lodging inventory, named-hotel comparisons, live rooms, prices, ratings, foreign-guest acceptance or accessibility suitability.
- General China railway booking, ID, security and boarding steps; `china-high-speed-train-first-time-guide` owns them.

## Cannibalization controls

The Xi’an Hub may keep a short statement that Mount Hua can be a day trip or may justify a nearby night. Central integration may add a reciprocal link here for the actual sleep-location decision; this article-only commit does not edit the Hub. This owner must not expand into “is Mount Hua worth it?” or “how many days in Xi’an?”. Its title must preserve both `Xi’an or Huayin` and `for Mount Hua` so it does not compete with the broad Xi’an-area stay owner.

Do not confuse Mount Hua / Huashan (华山) with Mount Huangshan (黄山). `huangshan-summit-or-gateway-base` owns a different destination and must not donate summit-lodging logic to this page.
