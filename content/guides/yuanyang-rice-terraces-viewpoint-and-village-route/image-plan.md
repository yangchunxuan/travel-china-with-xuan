# Image plan — Yuanyang viewpoints and villages

**Status: exact-location, licensed documentary assets needed; no files bundled.**

## Hero

- Exact location: a named, currently public viewpoint at Duoyishu, Bada or Laohuzui. The source record must identify which platform/visitor area and the camera date.
- Required elements: terraces plus at least one part of the living system—village, forest edge, water-filled or cultivated fields. Public viewpoint context is preferred.
- Forbidden: Longji or another Asian rice terrace; an unverified roadside drone angle; fields digitally flooded or recoloured; residents presented as staged props; AI documentary scenery.
- Crop: 1600 × 1000 landscape; retain landform and living-system context. Do not clone away buildings, agriculture or clouds to create an ‘untouched’ scene.
- Alt EN: `A location-verified public viewpoint over Yuanyang Hani rice terraces, with villages and cultivated fields visible`
- Alt ZH: `从经地点核实的公众观景台俯瞰元阳哈尼梯田、村寨与耕作田地`
- Alt KO: `위치가 확인된 공개 전망대에서 본 위안양 하니 다랑논과 마을, 경작지`
- Rights: Homeground-owned original preferred. Otherwise exact location, creator, date, direct licence, derivative terms and hashes are mandatory.

## Supporting set

1. A clearly identified managed public route in Azheke or Qingkou, showing path boundaries without intruding on a home; place beside resident boundaries.
2. A verified mountain-road/visitor drop-off context at one named viewpoint; no dangerous shooting or parked-car obstruction.
3. A second viewpoint with genuinely different terrace geometry; caption the exact place and date, never “Yuanyang” generically.
4. Optional original selector matrix visual: sunrise / later-day / village / fog-recovery icons. It is a planning infographic, not a topographic or access map; cite UNESCO and official county road evidence.

No photograph can prove today's gate, visibility, ticket or season. Central must record those evidence limits with the licence.
