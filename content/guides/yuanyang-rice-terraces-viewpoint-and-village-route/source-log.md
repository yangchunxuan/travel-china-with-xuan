# Research and source log

Reviewed: 2026-08-13 (Asia/Shanghai).

## Evidence and current-state handling

- UNESCO establishes the 16,603-hectare World Heritage property, its integrated forest–water–village–terrace attributes, 82 villages and three representative terrace areas: Bada, Duoyishu and Laohuzui.
- FAO establishes the agricultural-heritage frame and forest–village–terrace–river relationship. Neither source proves today's visitor access.
- Yuanyang's 2026 heritage-management budget establishes ongoing protection and village/infrastructure work.
- County road and tourism documents from late 2025 establish road risk and active visitor-traffic management at named areas including Laohuzui. They are not permanent gate or ticket promises.
- Yunnan Transport's February 2026 notice establishes the Yuanyang–Lüchun expressway opening to the Nansha approach; the copy explicitly does not infer that upper scenic roads became simple.
- Homeground judgment: choose no more than two major viewpoints and one managed village; leave a second morning or non-view recovery for cloud.
