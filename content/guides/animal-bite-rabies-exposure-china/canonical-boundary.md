## Boundary with the general medical-emergency page

This page owns the condition-specific first action and continuity record. The general medical-emergency page owns calling 120, communicating the location, companion roles and the administrative hospital/insurance handoff. Neither page duplicates the other's full workflow.

## Official conflict rule

The 2023 China rabies-exposure standard governs the local clinical framework; WHO guidance supplies an international safety baseline. The two source sets agree on immediate thorough washing for about/at least 15 minutes and prompt professional assessment. If a future Chinese revision, an official translation or WHO guidance differs on classification or treatment, the article must show the dated difference and direct the traveller to a qualified clinician. It may never choose a more convenient regimen.
