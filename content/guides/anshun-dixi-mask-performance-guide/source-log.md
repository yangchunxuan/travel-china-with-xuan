## Source conflicts and corrections

1. **Formation and unchanged-history claims:** the 2026 government feature uses a confident 600-year military-drill narrative. Guizhou University research explicitly says the formation time has no direct documentary record. The guide keeps the military-colony context but rejects an exact unchanged-drill claim.
2. **Dixi/Nuo classification:** Gu Jiashun argues Dixi and Nuo are different; other scholars frame Dixi as military Nuo. The national register assigns separate project numbers. The guide reports these layers without declaring one camp universal.
3. **Forehead explanation:** the wearing arrangement is official-record fact; respect for the role is Gu Jiashun's explanation; the first historical cause remains unresolved.
4. **Tourism presentation:** the 2026 feature reports a current visitor format, while the 2020 academic study documents how tourism changed space and relationships. The guide treats a visitor excerpt as a useful but distinct context, not complete village ritual and not “fake.”
5. **Ticket versus performance:** official price-list inclusion is not converted into a time, seat or remedy guarantee.
