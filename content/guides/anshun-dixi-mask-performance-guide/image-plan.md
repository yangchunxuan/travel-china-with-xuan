## Deliberate image gaps

1. **No current Yanwutang image is used.** No exact 2026 venue image with a commercial-compatible licence and bounded caption was closed. The hero is therefore not labelled as the current hall.
2. **No identifiable close portrait is used.** The hero performers' faces are completely covered. Official-news portraits and social-media images were not assumed reusable.
3. **No village ritual phase is illustrated.** A public visitor-performance image cannot stand in for opening, closing or private/community ritual space.
4. **No object identity is invented.** The 2017 display lacks an object catalogue, so its caption does not assign character, maker, age or ritual history.
5. **No generic Nuo or other-region mask substitutes for Anshun Dixi.** Comparison is handled in editable text with source boundaries.
6. **No AI image fills a documentary gap.** If the exact current hall or a catalogued mask detail later becomes essential, defer that asset until a real, accurately located, rights-cleared source is available.
