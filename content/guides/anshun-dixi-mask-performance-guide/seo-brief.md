## Primary query

`why is the Anshun Dixi mask worn on the forehead`

The answer must distinguish the observable convention, a named practitioner's interpretation and the unresolved historical origin. It must not select an attractive practical explanation and present it as proven cause.

## Search task and answer shape

The reader first needs a direct cultural answer, then a repeatable observation method. The page therefore moves from the face-above-face arrangement to six linked channels—*lianzi*, *qingjin*, lead/group voice, gong/drum, weapon/back flags and whole body—before addressing literature, Dixi/Nuo classification and performance context. Operational facts remain dated and subordinate.

## Qualitative SERP observation on 2026-08-15

Sampled English, Chinese and Korean results were dominated by brief encyclopedia entries, heritage descriptions, tourism features and broad Nuo explanations. Chinese results answered the visible-mask question more often, but commonly collapsed a practitioner's or commentator's explanation into one universal origin story. The sample did not reveal a strong trilingual first-audience owner that combined the mask system, sound/body reading, classification uncertainty, tourism-context boundary and failure recovery.

Representative authoritative results used for factual grounding:

- [China ICH — Anshun Dixi](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/13391/) — official identity, protection unit and visible performance form.
- [National Ethnic Affairs Commission, 3 July 2026](https://www.neac.gov.cn/seac/xwzx/202607/1192282.shtml) — recent but bounded report of the Tianlong visitor excerpt.
- [Guizhou University — Zhang Dinggui](https://acc.gzu.edu.cn/2013/1017/c5649a42597/page.htm) — formation-date uncertainty and social context.
- [Acta Geographica Sinica, 2020](https://www.geog.com.cn/CN/abstract/article/0375-5444/47828) — peer-reviewed evidence of tourism-related space and relationship change at Tianlong.

This is a dated qualitative sample, not a search-volume, ranking, traffic or conversion claim.
