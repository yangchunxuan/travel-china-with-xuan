# Canonical boundary — Anshun Dixi mask and first-performance guide

Reviewed: 2026-08-15 (Asia/Shanghai)

## This page owns

- The first-audience answer to why the carved *lianzi* appears above a *qingjin*-covered face, with three evidence levels kept separate:
  - officially documented wearing convention;
  - Gu Jiashun's attributed respect-for-the-role interpretation;
  - no settled proof of the convention's first historical cause.
- A six-channel method for reading *lianzi*, *qingjin*, lead/group voice, gong/drum, weapon/back flags and whole-body movement as one system.
- A five-pass observation workflow for readers without Chinese-language or repertoire knowledge.
- A bounded role-family explanation that rejects a universal colour-code shortcut.
- The distinction between heroic-war literature and primary historical evidence.
- The relationship between Anshun Dixi and wider Nuo research without either a forced equals sign or a claim of total separation.
- A four-context distinction: village ritual sequence, Tianlong visitor excerpt, invited public presentation and contemporary creation.
- A dated Tianlong evidence statement and a no-performance recovery path that make no arrival-time guarantee.

## This page does not own

- A national or provincial encyclopedia of Nuo theatre, mask traditions or ritual drama.
- Anshun/Tianlong transport, accommodation, restaurant, ticket-price, opening-hour, identity-document or accessibility workflows.
- Fixed daily show times, cancellation/refund remedies, active-room guarantees or performer rosters.
- A directory or calendar of village ritual events, troupes, private homes or invitation routes.
- Photography, video, drone, portrait-release or commercial-production permission for any venue or participant.
- A carving tutorial, mask market, maker directory, conservation assessment or object-authentication service.
- Claims that a tourist excerpt is a complete village ritual, or that one context is inherently real and another fake.
- Claims that Dixi is an unchanged Ming military drill or that one practical observation proves the forehead convention's origin.

## Editorial boundary

- The page is a cultural-observation guide, not practitioner training, ritual access, an expert conservation assessment or an operational guarantee.
- It attributes community/practitioner interpretations and leaves unresolved history unresolved.
- Current tourism facts are dated, secondary and removable without harming the durable observation method.
- The hero and body photographs prove only what their source metadata and visible content support. They do not certify today's venue, programme, object identity or building age.
