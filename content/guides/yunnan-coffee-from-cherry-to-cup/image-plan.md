## Required hero commission

Commission one real documentary photograph at a cooperating coffee property in **Pu’er City or Baoshan City, Yunnan**. Select and name the property only after it has granted access and commercial publication rights; do not infer access from a news report, map listing or tourism post.

Preferred scene: ripe cherries in the foreground and a genuine, clearly separated drying or reception lot in the same working environment. A property-approved lot board may be visible only if it contains no personal data, confidential customer information, price, phone number or traceable contract code. Do not stage a false traceability system for the camera.

Hero delivery specification:

- landscape master of at least 4000 × 2500 px; editorial crop to 1600 × 1000 WebP;
- natural colour and documentary editing only; no object insertion or deletion;
- caption must state the exact physical stage shown and must not call one method representative of all Yunnan coffee;
- alt text must describe visible content, not make quality, sustainability or farmer-income claims.

## Evidence and rights record required with every commissioned frame

Keep these fields in the asset ledger before upload:

1. legal Chinese property/operator name and full street or village address;
2. decimal GPS coordinates captured on site, not copied from a broad map pin;
3. capture date and local time;
4. creator’s legal name, contact and original file identifier;
5. property manager or landholder’s signed permission for photography and worldwide commercial editorial web use;
6. written licence covering cropping, resizing, colour correction, EN/ZH/KO publication, social preview, archive and future page updates;
7. names and signed model releases for every identifiable adult; guardian and child release if a minor is ever considered, though the preferred plan excludes minors;
8. confirmation that visible brands, labels, machinery areas and work processes may be published;
9. caption verification by the property contact without granting that contact editorial control over the article.

## Supporting documentary shot list

| Priority | Real scene | Editorial purpose | Privacy and accuracy control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Ripe and unripe cherries being separated at reception | Shows why picking and reception affect a lot before processing | Obtain releases for hands/faces; do not claim every cherry in a whole crop was hand-selected. |
| 2 | One property’s washed, natural or honey workflow | Makes skin, pulp, mucilage and drying distinctions visible | Caption only the photographed method; record equipment and process confirmation from the operator. |
| 3 | Drying lot with genuine separation markers | Supports the drying and lot-identity sections | Remove or exclude names, customer codes, phone numbers, contract numbers and commercially sensitive data. |
| 4 | Hulling, density, screen or colour sorting | Shows how dried coffee becomes a green-bean preparation | Obtain facility permission and PPE/safety approval; do not photograph a restricted control screen. |
| 5 | Green lot and its matching roaster batch record | Connects trade identity to roasting | Use an operator-cleared sample record or shoot at an angle that protects commercial data. |
| 6 | Producer, processor or roaster portrait in their real workplace | Gives human context without romanticising labour | Record name, role, preferred caption and signed model release; no anonymous ‘local farmer’ caption. |

Pu’er and Baoshan should not be mixed into one implied continuous property. If images come from both, every caption and file record must name the correct city, property and stage.

## Non-negotiable exclusions

- no AI documentary image, generative fill, synthetic extension or composited worker;
- no stock image whose exact Yunnan location and rights cannot be proved;
- no invented farm, bag, lot code, tasting score, award, visitor route or branded product;
- no faces without releases, and no children in the default shot plan;
- no confidential invoices, order numbers, phone numbers, QR codes, vehicle plates, employee IDs or screens;
- no claim that a photographed property accepts walk-in visitors;
- no image sourced from social media, a platform listing or a news frame without explicit reusable commercial rights.
