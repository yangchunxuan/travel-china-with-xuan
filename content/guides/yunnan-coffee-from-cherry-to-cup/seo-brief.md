## Result gap and Homeground information gain

- Commercial results often attach a fixed flavour to Yunnan or a processing label; official results foreground aggregate percentages without helping a buyer evaluate one bag.
- Homeground adds a nine-stage evidence chain, lot-identity ladder, process-versus-flavour caution, drying/storage gate, green-lot questions, role separation, label checklist and failure recovery.
- It records the conflicting official 2025 `精品率` and deep-processing figures rather than manufacturing a clean time series, and distinguishes Yunnan-grown, Yunnan-processed and Yunnan-roasted.
