- Reviewed: 2026-08-22 (Asia/Shanghai)

- Canonical action: `create-new`, after full remote-ref audit found no four-section owner.
- Page job: resolve the choice among Badaling, Mutianyu, Jinshanling and Simatai before the reader enters a section-specific transport or ticket workflow.

## Primary query family

- `which Great Wall section should I visit`
- `best Great Wall section from Beijing`
- `Badaling vs Mutianyu vs Jinshanling vs Simatai`

The head query `Great Wall of China` is broad. This page does not pretend to own the complete history of the Wall; it earns relevance through a specific first-trip decision.

## Questions the page must settle

1. Which Great Wall section is best for a first visit from Beijing?
2. Is Badaling always too crowded?
3. Is Mutianyu better than Badaling for families?
4. Which section is best for serious hiking and photography?
5. Where can a visitor see the Great Wall at night?
6. Do cable cars make a section suitable for limited mobility?
7. Can Jinshanling and Simatai still be walked as one through-route?
8. Which choice is easiest without a private driver?
9. How do weather and season change the choice?
10. What must be rechecked on the day before departure?

## Information gain

- Compares the **complete trip shape**, not beauty adjectives.
- Separates section choice from transport ownership.
- Treats crowds as a date/entrance condition rather than a permanent label.
- Makes cable-car limits explicit instead of calling a whole attraction accessible.
- Treats night tours as dated products with limited open segments.
- Rejects old through-hike assumptions and unapproved wild-wall access.
- Gives an honest default (Mutianyu) while explaining when Badaling is the more intelligent choice.

## Cannibalisation boundary

- `/guides/beijing-to-badaling-great-wall-transfer/` owns Badaling rail/bus journey mechanics.
- `/guides/beijing-to-mutianyu-great-wall-transfer/` owns Mutianyu public/direct/private transfer mechanics.
- `/destinations/beijing/` owns Beijing stay length, area and overall attraction relationships.
- This page owns only the four-way section decision. Do not create `badaling-vs-mutianyu`, `best-great-wall-beijing`, `jinshanling-or-simatai` or year-specific night-tour canonicals.

## Snippet strategy

The direct answer names one use case per section in the first screen. The title carries all four entity names without claiming one universal winner. The description adds the comparison dimensions a traveller actually uses: transport, effort, crowds, scenery and night access.
