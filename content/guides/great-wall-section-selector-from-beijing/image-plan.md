# Image plan — great-wall-section-selector-from-beijing

## Rights and evidence boundary

All assets are original Homeground editorial graphics created locally on 2026-08-22. No outside photograph, logo, map tile, font file or identifiable person is embedded. The illustrations are schematic and are not a geographic map, reconstruction or documentary image of any named Great Wall section.

## Hero

- File: `public/images/guides/great-wall-section-selector-from-beijing/hero-1600.webp`
- Dimensions: 1600 × 1000.
- Visual: layered dawn ridges, a deliberately simplified wall line and four distinct route markers without section labels.
- Editorial purpose: establish “four choices, one decision” without presenting an uncertain photograph as a named location.
- Generation: deterministic local raster drawing; no generative AI.
- AI-generated or AI-assisted: **no**.

## Publication controls

- Do not label any ridge or tower in the background as a real section.
- Do not crop the hero into a card that removes the “editorial illustration” alt boundary.
- Do not add a stock wall photograph unless its exact section, creator, licence and date are documented.
- Do not use the graphic to imply that night tours, cable cars or any gate operate every day.
