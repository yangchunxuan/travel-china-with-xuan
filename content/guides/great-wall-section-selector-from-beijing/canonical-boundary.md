# Canonical boundary — Great Wall section selector from Beijing

Canonical: `/guides/great-wall-section-selector-from-beijing/`

Decision: `create-new`

Audit date: 2026-08-22 (Asia/Shanghai)

## Owner job

This page owns the **choice** among Badaling, Mutianyu, Jinshanling and Simatai for a Beijing-based visitor. Its durable comparison axes are:

- traveller and group fit;
- desired day shape;
- walking and exposure;
- relative transport complexity;
- crowd sensitivity;
- day visit versus dated night/overnight intent;
- the recheck points that follow from the choice.

## Existing owners that remain separate

- `/guides/beijing-to-badaling-great-wall-transfer/` owns the current Beijing-hub-to-Badaling transport workflow.
- `/guides/beijing-to-mutianyu-great-wall-transfer/` owns the current public/direct/private transfer workflow to Mutianyu.
- `/destinations/beijing/` owns Beijing nights, stay areas, gateways and the relationship among major attractions.
- Attraction operators own live tickets, operating hours, open towers, cable systems, weather closure and re-entry.

## Explicit exclusions

- No live opening board, ticket inventory or annual night-tour page.
- No permanent claim that any section is quiet, accessible or open after dark.
- No wild-wall or unapproved through-hike instructions.
- No detailed rail/bus duplication from the Badaling or Mutianyu transfer owners.
- No year in the canonical slug.
- No photograph labelled as a specific section unless exact location, creator, licence and evidence are documented.
