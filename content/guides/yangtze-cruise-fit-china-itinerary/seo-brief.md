Prepared 2026-09-26. This is a proposed content identity, not a live or indexed page. The saved GSC export covers a Jiangnan URL filter, and the saved Bing AI sample has no Yangtze grounding query. No search volume, AI referral volume or conversion value has been verified for this question.

## User decision and canonical boundary

Question: “Should I add a three-night Yangtze cruise to my China itinerary?” Answer with the four-calendar-day cost, Chongqing-to-Yichang route geometry, boarding and onward-travel margins, and the difference between private land touring and a shared ship programme.

The 6-, 12- and 17-day product pages own exact itinerary, inclusion, price and date-specific quote. The 12-day product FAQ already compares 12 versus 17 days; this guide must not recreate that comparison. Existing `best-2-week-china-tour` owns broad two-week route selection, and `china-rail-only-route` owns the general rail-versus-flight decision.

## Search and link plan

Use direct answer sentences and a short fit/skip comparison before details. Link the three existing private-tour products and relevant planning guides in each language. Treat the suggested query phrasings in metadata as editorial hypotheses, not measured keywords. After publication, measure the new canonical URL and query group in matching GSC windows, then separately inspect referral and inquiry behaviour. A draft, deployment, crawl, indexing and customer inquiry are different states.
