## Editorial judgments and recheck triggers

- Three ship nights span four calendar dates: boarding, two intervening dates and disembarkation. These are not four interchangeable sightseeing days.
- Choose the cruise when the river itself matters enough to use that block and the one-way Chongqing-to-Yichang path suits the rest of the trip. Cut it when the fixed sailing would strip needed time from higher-priority stops.
- Before quoting or publishing date-specific details, verify the actual operator's sailing, embarkation and disembarkation points, cabin and balcony type, check-in and end time, shore inclusions, language, fees, transfers and weather/navigation changes. Recheck again near travel.
- No live timetable, price, availability, universal boarding cutoff, guaranteed dock or invented customer experience appears in this page.
