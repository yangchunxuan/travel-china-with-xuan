## Editorial boundary

This page owns the method for choosing geographically coherent food regions, assigning each region a distinct meal or technique role, connecting those roles to the transport route, and retaining fallbacks for closures and dietary limits. It does not rank cities, restaurants or dishes; promise allergen safety; reproduce a complete private itinerary; or replace a live timetable.

## Homeground editorial judgments

- Assign a food region a role before adding a venue: staple, technique, meal format, market/season or shared-table experience.
- Add a second region only when it adds a different role and fits the transport direction without consuming the meal windows it was meant to create.
- A route that works only if one named restaurant is open is not resilient enough.
- For a serious dietary restriction, reduce uncertainty rather than increasing the number of “must-eat” stops.
- Replace a failed venue with the same regional role before replacing the region or adding a distant detour.
