## Canonical boundary

This page owns a gate-first decision: an already selected North or West Scenic Area entry determines the useful local rail/air hub, first-night base, authorised visitor-centre handoff and recovery. It does not rank attractions, publish live tickets or timetables, explain national rail boarding, or cover the South Slope.

The important 2026 correction is that `长白山西站` is now an operating rail identity after the Shenyang–Changbaishan high-speed railway opened on 2025-09-28. Older official guides that sent West Slope rail travellers through `长白山站` predate that opening and are not used as current timetable evidence.

- Shenyang–Changbaishan HSR opened on 2025-09-28 and its Jilin section includes both `长白山西站` and `长白山站`. Scope: station identity only. Checked 2026-08-13.
- Jilin transport guidance assigns substantial local connections separately at Changbaishan West and Changbaishan stations. Scope: existence of local transfer systems, not a promise that a particular route operates on a future date. Checked 2026-08-13.
- The 2025–2026 snow-season road-public-transport notice shows that winter services are issued seasonally. Scope: evidence for live rechecking; its attachment is not republished as a permanent timetable. Checked 2026-08-13.
- Changbai Mountain publishes scenic-area notices and weather-related adjustments on its official portal. Scope: visit-date operations. Checked 2026-08-13.
- CMA provides current forecasts and warnings. Scope: road and park-day weather recheck, not a prediction for an undated trip. Checked 2026-08-13.
