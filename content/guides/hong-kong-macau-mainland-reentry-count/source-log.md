- Canonical owner: employee 6 / this batch

- Fact boundary: this guide counts actual mainland exits and entries. It does not promise passport-specific admission to mainland China, Hong Kong or Macao.
- Freshness boundary: visa-free, transit, port and SAR admission rules are dynamic. Readers are directed to recheck the exact passport, date, ports and onward itinerary shortly before travel.
- Internal-link boundary: crossing guides own transport execution. This article owns only jurisdiction counting, matching each mainland arrival to an entry basis and recovering safely from an invalid route.

## Official sources

- Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Department of Consular Affairs: [China visa terms, entries and residence-permit guidance](https://cs.mfa.gov.cn/wgrlh/lhqz/) — reviewed 2026-08-13; supports the distinction among number of entries, entry validity and duration of stay, and notes that a valid foreigner's residence permit can support multiple exits and entries
- National Immigration Administration: [Exit and Entry Administration Law of the People's Republic of China](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147458/c155978/content.html) — reviewed 2026-08-13; supports border inspection, visa fields, admission discretion and the requirement for valid entry documents
- National Immigration Administration: [China's 240-hour visa-free transit policy interpretation](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c183412/content.html) — reviewed 2026-08-13; supports the third-country-or-region, passport, port, confirmed-onward-ticket and permitted-area boundary; no passport-specific eligibility is reproduced in the article
- National Immigration Administration: [China Immigration Service Hotline 12367](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147413/c194788/content.html) — reviewed 2026-08-13; supports the 12367 escalation channel without extending a fast-channel announcement for mainland and SAR residents to foreign visitors
- Hong Kong Immigration Department: [Visit visa and entry-permit requirements](https://www.immd.gov.hk/eng/services/visas/visit-transit/visit-visa-entry-permit.html) — reviewed 2026-08-13; supports separate Hong Kong admission by nationality and travel-document type
- Macao SAR Government: [Immigration clearance of non-residents of Macao](https://www.gov.mo/en/services/ps-1474/ps-1474b/) — reviewed 2026-08-13; supports Macao's separate passport, visa or authorization, onward-ticket and admission requirements
- Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macao Bridge Hong Kong Port: [Cross-boundary public transport services](https://www.hzmb.gov.hk/en/cross-boundary.html) — reviewed 2026-08-13; supports the separate Hong Kong–Macao and Hong Kong–Zhuhai shuttle routes used in the route-counting example
