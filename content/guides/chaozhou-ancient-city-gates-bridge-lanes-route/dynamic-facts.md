# Dynamic facts — chaozhou-ancient-city-gates-bridge-lanes-route

Reviewed 2026-08-22, Asia/Taipei. Nothing here may be presented in the body as a permanent rule.

This page is built to resist going stale: it is about static geography and a walking order, and it
publishes no hours, tickets, prices or schedules at all. The dynamic surface is therefore small,
but two items on it matter a great deal.

## Deliberately excluded from the body as too dynamic, or as another owner's

- Guangji Bridge opening hours, last admission, ticket price and any concession.
- The bridge's light show or drone-display calendar, and any festival programme.
- Any statement about whether the pontoon will be open on a particular date or at a particular hour.
- The intangible-heritage demonstration areas on the bridge, which rotate.
- Any restaurant, teahouse or shop.
- Any arrival transport, station or accommodation.
- The wall's exact length in metres and the gate tower's exact height, both of which appear only in
  sources that could not be opened.

## Freshness note for central

Item 1 is the page's hinge. If the daily pattern changes, or if the operator publishes a schedule
that can be cited directly, the bridge section, the decision table and the fallback table should be
revised together and the review callout updated in all three languages.

Item 6 is the standing repair job. The moment `www.chaozhou.gov.cn` or `www.xiangqiao.gov.cn`
becomes reachable, the two corroborated gate names should be verified directly, the review callout
amended, and this row closed.
