## Evidence and exact use

1. UNESCO World Heritage Centre, [Wulingyuan Scenic and Historic Interest Area](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/640/), opened in the owner's Chrome 2026-10-10: inscribed 1992; "more than 3,000 narrow sandstone pillars and peaks, many over 200 m high"; 26,400 ha.
2. Zhangjiajie World Geopark, [one-day route](https://www.zhangjiajieuggp.org.cn/html/2026/2026032620071642379.html), dated 2026-03-26: East Gate → eco-bus → Tianzi Mountain cableway up → 贺龙公园, 御笔峰, 仙女献花, 西海 → eco-bus to Yuanjiajie → 天下第一桥, 哈里路亚山原型, 后花园, 迷魂台 → Bailong Elevator down → Golden Whip Stream upstream or Ten-Mile Gallery downstream → stay in Wulingyuan.
3. Zhangjiajie World Geopark, [two-day route](https://www.zhangjiajieuggp.org.cn/html/2026/2026032620135033852.html), dated 2026-03-26: Day 2 South Gate → Huangshi Village on foot (1.5–2 h) or cableway → Golden Whip Stream 7.5 km, about 2 h → eco-bus to East Gate.
4. Hunan Provincial Government, [Embark on 10-day Trip to Explore "Greater Zhangjiajie"](https://enghunan.gov.cn/hneng/News/Localnews/202603/t20260306_33926625.html), 2026-03-06: recommends ascending Yuanjiajie by the Bailong Elevator "to admire the prototype of the floating Hallelujah Mountain in the movie Avatar".
5. Wulingyuan Scenic Area, [ticket page](https://www.hnzjj.com/index.php/Ticket/show/2.html), reviewed 2026-08-20 in the tickets guide: RMB 165 admission only; RMB 236 admission plus eco-bus; four consecutive days; optional RMB 3 insurance; cableways/elevator excluded.
6. Hunan Department of Culture and Tourism, [Wulingyuan transport guide](https://whhlyt.hunan.gov.cn/whhlyt/wldh2022jt/202208/t20220823_27716245.html): self-drive from the railway-station area to Wulingyuan "about 40 minutes". Old bus fare not reused.
7. Guinness World Records, [16 July 2015](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2015/7/breathtaking-cliff-face-elevator-in-china-recognised-as-worlds-tallest-outdoor-l-387951): Bailong Elevator 326 m, world's tallest outdoor lift.
8. The Irish Times, [26 January 2010](https://www.irishtimes.com/news/mountain-renamed-avatar-after-look-alike-peaks-in-film-1.1272985): Southern Sky Column at Yuanjiajie renamed "Avatar"; 1,074 m above sea level; Cameron quoted that the peaks were modelled on Huangshan.
9. Reuters via Sowetan, [29 January 2010](https://www.sowetan.co.za/news/2010-01-29-the-avatar-claim-false/): city government website reported the renaming ceremony and the 2008 Hollywood photographer; after an online backlash the city denied renaming; tourism chief Ding Yunyong said they only put up a comparison poster.
