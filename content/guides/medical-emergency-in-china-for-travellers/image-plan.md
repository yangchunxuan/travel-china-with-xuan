## Visual job

The diagram uses four language-light stages: a phone marked 120, a location pin, an abstract response vehicle and a handoff folder. A bold uninterrupted line places the emergency call before documents. A smaller dotted line into the folder communicates that identity and records support the handoff but never delay the call.

## Privacy, accuracy and safety review

- No patient, face, body, injury, passport, medical record, prescription, hospital number, insurance ID, payment card, QR code, address or live location appears.
- The response vehicle is generic and carries no agency, hospital, Red Cross or government mark.
- The image does not promise arrival, English service, treatment, admission, payment or insurance.
- Critical call instructions remain in accessible HTML; the image is orientation only.
