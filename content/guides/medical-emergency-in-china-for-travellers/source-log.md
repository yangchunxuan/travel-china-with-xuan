## Authority and conflict reconciliation

The NHC regulation is the primary owner for the national 120 number. The 2026 Beijing-government page is explicitly sourced to NHC and is used for the call fields, not as a Beijing-only phone number. State Council and NIA visitor guides corroborate the traveller-facing use of 120.

NIA's booklet lists a Red Cross emergency number 999, while the NHC response supplies the missing jurisdiction: Beijing also had 999 and the nationwide unified pre-hospital number remained 120. The article therefore does not tell travellers elsewhere to try 999. It also refuses English, timing, fee, destination and insurance claims found on local or commercial pages unless the exact locality and current official owner are separately verified.

## Demand research record

Google was sampled on 2026-09-01 for `medical emergency China tourist`, `ambulance number China`, `call 120 China English` and `China emergency room foreigner`. Results mixed embassy advice, insurance pages, hospital marketing and broad number lists. Visible questions repeatedly asked what to say, whether location would be understood, whether English was available and how payment worked. The durable information gain is a national-number call script plus explicit no-guarantee and failure-recovery paths; no search volume or service probability is asserted.
