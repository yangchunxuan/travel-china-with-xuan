## Homeground editorial judgments

### Direction

Chengdu → Chongqing → Zhangjiajie, or the exact reverse, is the coherent three-city default when the entry and exit gateways support forward travel. Starting in Chongqing and visiting both outer cities is treated as weaker because it often creates a repeated segment.

This is route geometry and opportunity-cost judgment, not a promise that a particular direct train operates.

### City cut test

Every city must retain at least one distinct, protected priority. Chongqing can be removed when it is only a transfer label. Chengdu can be removed when it creates an unsupported western detour. Zhangjiajie can be removed when the trip cannot protect meaningful outdoor time and recovery.

The page does not claim that one city is universally less valuable.

### Usable-day accounting

Arrival, departure and hotel-changing transfer blocks begin at 0 usable half-days. One half-day must be earned after the end-to-end chain leaves a stable local block; a full protected day receives 2.

The 5–6, 7–9 and 10+ night bands are warnings used to expose compression. They are not packages, minimum-stay rules or guarantees.

### Worked examples

- 9 nights = 10 calendar days. Subtracting arrival, departure and two zero-first intercity blocks leaves 6 protected full-day equivalents in the baseline.
- 7 nights = 8 calendar days. A Chengdu round trip with arrival, departure, two forward moves and one return leaves 3 protected full-day equivalents before any half-day is earned.

The examples are arithmetic demonstrations. A real trip may combine calendar blocks differently and must be recalculated from the actual bookings.
