## Planning judgments

- Nanjing–Suzhou–Shanghai–Hangzhou, or reverse, is the clean one-way chain when gateways allow.
- A Shanghai base is better only when hotel stability outweighs repeated station commuting.
- Short train rides do not erase terminal access and hotel moves.
