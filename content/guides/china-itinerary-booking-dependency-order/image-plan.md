## Why a documentary hero is not used

A station concourse or train photograph can prove a place or vehicle existed at capture time, but it cannot prove future inventory, a successful reservation request, an issued ticket or a safe dependency chain. The editorial diagram is therefore the more accurate hero for this planning task.

## Optional central documentary figures

1. A rights-cleared 12306-operated station departure board with all names, train numbers, times and live operational details unreadable or cropped out; use only to explain why dated verification is separate from route design.
2. A rights-cleared hotel cancellation-terms screen supplied by its owner with every property, guest, price, date, order number, QR code and platform account removed; use only if legal approves a generic contract-field explainer.
3. A named venue's public exterior and official temporary-notice board, captured with permission; never imply that a photograph proves current admission or opening.

No AI documentary photograph, fake ticket, fake app interface, copied booking screen, operator logo, readable passenger information or invented timetable may be added.
