## Sole canonical ownership

The decision and recovery path for a short-stay foreign visitor who wants to drive a rented small vehicle in mainland China: ask traffic management whether a current mutual-recognition route expressly covers that traveller and licence; otherwise verify and complete the temporary-permit route; then separately confirm that the rental company will accept the traveller and authority documents.

This page merges these query intents into one owner:

- whether a tourist can drive in mainland China with a foreign licence or IDP
- how a short-stay visitor applies for a temporary driving permit
- which documents should be confirmed before visiting a local vehicle-administration office
- why receiving a permit does not guarantee a rental handover
- what to do when the permit or rental plan fails

## Two independent gates

Gate one is public permission: the competent traffic-management authority determines eligibility, documents, vehicle category, learning or assessment requirements and validity. Gate two is commercial handover: the rental provider determines reservation, identity verification, deposit, payment, insurance and vehicle availability under its current terms. Passing either gate does not silently prove the other.

## Official conflict rule

National rules and central visitor guides set the framework, while Beijing, Shanghai and other local authorities publish operational details that can differ. If official Chinese and translated pages, or two local pages, differ, retain title, authority, language, update date and affected locality. Ask the vehicle-administration/traffic-management office that will issue the permit to confirm its current requirements; ask the rental provider to confirm handover separately. Do not select the easier version or carry one city's checklist into another city.

There is a material venue conflict to resolve before a rental trip: NIA's reference-only visitor page points generally to the office at the place of entry or residence, while Article 11 of the official temporary-entry regulation directs a driver of a rented Chinese vehicle to the traffic-management authority where the rental unit is located. The article discloses both and tells the traveller to obtain written confirmation from the authority in the intended rental location. It does not use the separate venue rule for a self-imported vehicle.
