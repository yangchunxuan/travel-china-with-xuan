## Evidence levels and editorial decisions

- **Official inventory fact:** inscription date, property composition, setting and recorded excavation campaigns.
- **Documented archaeological fact:** an object or structure’s stated find context, when tied to an official excavation account.
- **Research attribution:** Mausoleum 6’s association with Li Deming and the northern complex as an ancestral temple. Both retain explicit inference language.
- **Homeground editorial judgment:** museum first, then one mausoleum zone and the wider landscape; use a five-level confidence vocabulary in field notes.
- **Cannot infer:** every tomb occupant, every ceremony, the beliefs of all Xixia people, or untouched medieval fabric on every visitor-facing surface.
