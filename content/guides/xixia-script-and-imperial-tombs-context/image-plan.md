## Supporting assets

1. **Script fragment:** an officially identified Xixia-script stele fragment with object number, find location, holding institution and explicit web reuse permission. Crop must retain scale and broken edges. Do not use a decorative modern font sample as an excavated inscription.
2. **Provenanced object:** the gilded bronze ox or another object whose tomb number appears in the institutional record. Confirm whether the displayed item is the original and whether gallery photography may be republished.
3. **Site map:** request a reusable official map showing the nine mausoleums, subordinate tombs, northern complex and flood-control works. If reuse is not cleared, link the UNESCO map and render the article’s conceptual sequence in HTML.
4. **Reconstruction comparison:** only if the museum grants reproduction rights. The caption must say `reconstruction`, name its evidence base where available and never pair it with “what the site originally looked like” as certainty.
5. **Conservation detail:** a clearly documented modern protective edge, drainage intervention or visitor route can illustrate the difference between archaeological fabric and current management. Obtain site permission and date.

## Brand, object and location limits

- UNESCO, museum and scenic-area logos require separate brand permission; exclude them from image crops unless cleared.
- A Creative Commons landscape photograph does not grant rights to reproduce museum panels, maps or object photography by another rights holder.
- Do not geotag restricted excavation or storage locations beyond information publicly released by the management authority.
