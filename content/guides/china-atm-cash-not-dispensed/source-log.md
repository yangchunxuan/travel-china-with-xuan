## Editorial decisions

- The immediate action is to stop repeat withdrawals, preserve the machine and transaction facts, and contact the ATM owner through a verified official channel.
- The ATM bank and the card issuer are separate lanes: the former owns the machine-side event; the latter owns the cardholder account and formal dispute intake.
- Visa and Mastercard materials support a formal “cash not received” or “partial cash” category and show which transaction fields matter. Network manuals are not converted into consumer refund guarantees.
- The UnionPay FAQ describes an issuer-to-network-to-ATM-bank investigation for its stated card scenario. It is used for process architecture only, not universal outcome or timing.
- A pending authorization is not called a final debit. A cardholder-authorized withdrawal with no cash is not mislabeled as fraud.
- Privacy controls exclude PIN, CVV, one-time codes, full card number and unredacted balance from photographs or public posts.
