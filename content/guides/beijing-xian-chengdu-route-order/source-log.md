## Homeground editorial judgments

- The linear route is the default because Xi'an lies between the two outer gateway choices in this named three-city task and established rail links support forward travel.
- Every arrival, departure and hotel-change day starts at 0 usable half-days. One half-day must be earned by an end-to-end test; a transfer day never receives 2 in the conservative baseline.
- Night allocation follows protected priorities and recovery needs, not equal shares or a published universal template.
- If a same-gateway itinerary cannot absorb the additional return sector and final-flight buffer, the responsible repairs are a different gateway, more time or one fewer city.

These are planning judgments, not transport-operator guarantees or measured punctuality claims.
