## Stable facts used in the decision

- Dapeng Fortress was founded in 1394 as a coastal-defence settlement and was designated a Major Historical and Cultural Site Protected at the National Level in 2001.
- The current museum authority manages multiple named fortress buildings, including the South Gate Tower, Lai Enjue's former residence, Yiwen Building, Dai Family House and Luo Gui's former residence.
- The current fortress street network includes named public lanes and three surviving main gates; the north gate was blocked in the Qing period according to the official destination account.
- Jiaochangwei is a separate coastal village/tourism area next to the Pengcheng/fortress area.
- Official destination and route pages pair Dapeng Fortress with Jiaochangwei, but a recommended pairing is not evidence that every traveller should spend a full day there.

## Homeground editorial judgements

The following are planning mechanisms, not official rules:

- first-time visitors with only one or two Shenzhen days should not automatically give Dapeng a full day;
- the fortress is the default anchor for a history-led trip;
- Jiaochangwei is an optional addition unless coast, water or sunset is the primary objective;
- a same-day Hong Kong border crossing, flight or other fixed departure makes the excursion a poor fit;
- cut water/coast time before cutting the fortress on a history-led day;
- if interiors are unavailable and history is not a real priority, cancel the eastern excursion rather than filling it with food or leisure businesses by default;
- stay overnight only when a slower coast evening, water activity or another Dapeng Peninsula objective is independently important.
