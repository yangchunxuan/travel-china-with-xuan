# Research and source log

Reviewed: 2026-08-13 (Asia/Shanghai).

## Canonical task and fact classes

- Canonical task: distinguish the Qilian protected/candidate boundary from public visitor products, then select a lawful gateway and base.
- Current 2026 fact: National Forestry and Grassland Administration pages published in December 2025 and February 2026 still use `祁连山国家公园候选区`. The copy must not silently upgrade it to an established, unified national park.
- Planning evidence only: the 2019 pilot plan maps the proposed park and lists entrance communities. It does not prove current opening, ticketing, trail, shuttle or road access.
- Current public evidence: national and Zhangye forestry pages document the opened Natural Museum at Hongwansi, public nature-education infrastructure and the Mati entrance-community context.
- Long-term pattern: large elevation and road exposure justify buffers and layered clothing, but no range-wide weather promise is made.
- Homeground judgment: plan outside-in—base, named operator, signed visitor area—and reject access based only on a social-media coordinate.
