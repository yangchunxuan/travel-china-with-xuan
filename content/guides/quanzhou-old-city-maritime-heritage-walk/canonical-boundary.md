## Component / buffer / connective-fabric rule

1. **World Heritage component:** Kaiyuan Temple, Qingjing Mosque, Site of Deji Gate and Tianhou Temple are separately named component sites in the official 22-site list.
2. **Shared buffer zone:** the old city is included in a shared buffer zone to protect the setting and relationships among old-city components. Buffer-zone status is a protection and management category; it does not turn every parcel into a component site.
3. **Connective urban fabric:** West Street, Zhongshan Road and other public links help the traveller move between evidence nodes and read a living city. This page does not label them component sites unless an official component listing does so.
4. **Paired reading is not merged identity:** Deji Gate and Tianhou Temple can form one final spatial reading but remain two independent component sites with different evidentiary functions.

## Canonical promise

The page recommends a **reading sequence, not turn-by-turn or strictly north-to-south navigation**. The full default is Kaiyuan Temple → public old-city connective fabric → Qingjing Mosque → Deji Gate → Tianhou Temple. Exact movement around adjacent Deji Gate and Tianhou Temple depends on the currently permitted entrances. The shorter choice depends on the question: keep Kaiyuan + Qingjing for religious intersection; keep Qingjing + Deji + Tianhou for the port-facing urban edge. No shortened version claims to represent the complete 22-component maritime system.
