## Pre-visit recheck

1. Open the newest dated notice from the Quanzhou or Licheng authority or the venue's verified official channel.
2. Check each chosen venue's opening, reservation or ticket position and religious-event restrictions for the exact date.
3. If using a foreign passport, verify the current purchase and entry field before committing to a timed plan; no article workaround substitutes for the live rule.
4. Ask onsite about photography, filming, tripod and commercial-publication limits before recording religious activity or identifiable people.
5. Check holiday traffic controls and any West Street restriction still in force on that date.
6. Check weather warnings and the least mobile traveller's walking limit.
7. If a node fails, preserve the relevant evidence category rather than collecting unrelated attractions: Kaiyuan + Qingjing for religious intersection; Qingjing + Deji + Tianhou for the south port-facing edge. State what the remaining route can no longer prove.
