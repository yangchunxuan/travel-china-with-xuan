# Source log — quanzhou-old-city-maritime-heritage-walk

All web sources were reviewed on 2026-09-01 unless stated otherwise. Official tourism language is used only to document how an institution presents a place; it is not independent proof of current access, crowd levels, accessibility or operating certainty.

## Official, heritage and management sources

1. **UNESCO World Heritage Centre — Quanzhou: Emporium of the World in Song–Yuan China**
   URL: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1561/
   Supports: serial-property identity; 10th–14th-century integrated production, transport, marketing, institutional and social/religious system; inscription in 2021; religious, administrative, production and transport evidence must be read together. It does not make every old-city street a component site.

2. **UNESCO World Heritage Centre — revised nomination document / response material**
   URL: https://whc.unesco.org/document/180497
   Supports: official 22-component set and the reason the entire old city was included in one shared buffer zone for old-city components. This is the main source for the component/buffer distinction.

3. **UNESCO World Heritage Centre — maps for property 1561**
   URL: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1561/maps/
   Supports: the serial property's mapped property and buffer geography. The page is not a pedestrian navigation map and does not prove current public entrances.

4. **China Ministry of Culture and Tourism — inscription announcement (2021-07-26)**
   URL: https://www.mct.gov.cn/whzx/whyw/202107/t20210726_926669.htm
   Supports: official inscription and 22 representative sites plus associated environment. It is not a current operating notice.

5. **Quanzhou Municipal Government — World Heritage protection and management rules**
   URL: https://www.quanzhou.gov.cn/zfb/xxgk/zfxxgkzl/zc/gzk/202201/t20220126_2691339.htm
   Supports: legal definition of the integrated serial property and named 22-site list; old city in the shared buffer-zone scope; protection priority and management duties.

6. **Quanzhou Municipal Government — English World Heritage protection regulations**
   URL: https://www.quanzhou.gov.cn/gastronomy/en/about/unesco-heritage/202501/t20250110_3129273.htm
   Supports: English terminology for heritage site, buffer zone and associated environments; confirms the component list in an official English presentation. The Chinese legal text remains controlling.

7. **Quanzhou Municipal Government — Kaiyuan Temple**
   URL: https://www.quanzhou.gov.cn/lyb/lytj/rmjdtj/202202/t20220224_2699974.htm
   Supports: Kaiyuan's local Buddhist institutional role, economic and social connections, and specific material evidence of cultural contact. It does not support saying foreign merchants built the entire temple or all visible fabric is Song–Yuan.

8. **Quanzhou Municipal Government — Qingjing Mosque**
   URL: https://www.quanzhou.gov.cn/lyb/lswh/zdww/qgzdwwbhdwjj/202311/t20231130_2974304.htm
   Supports: foundation in 1009 outside the then southern city wall beside the moat, location in the southern commercial and foreign-community area, the 1310 Muslim-community renovation, and the surviving gate, prayer-hall remains, Mingshan Hall and inscriptions as distinct layers.

9. **Quanzhou Municipal Government — public-cultural-facility record for Qingjing Mosque**
   URL: https://www.quanzhou.gov.cn/zfb/xxgk/ztxxgk/ggwhty/whssml/202305/t20230516_2880606.htm
   Supports: official institutional identity and location. It is not used to freeze current hours, price, passport or weekly access.

10. **Quanzhou Municipal Government — Tianhou Temple**
    URL: https://www.quanzhou.gov.cn/lyb/lytj/rmjdtj/202202/t20220224_2699979.htm
    Supports: the institution's 1196 foundation, port-facing position, Mazu belief, merchant networks and state ritual. The official page says the extant main hall's principal structure took shape in the nineteenth century, so 1196 cannot date the visible hall.

11. **Quanzhou Municipal Government — Site of Deji Gate**
    URL: https://www.quanzhou.gov.cn/gastronomy/en/about/unesco-heritage/202412/t20241228_3123996.htm
    Supports: southern-gate location, initial 1230 construction, 1352 enlargement, later repairs, 2001 excavation and the site's relation to the southern commercial district. It is not evidence that an intact Song gate survives.

12. **Quanzhou Municipal Government — official old-city heritage route article (2024-02-07)**
    URL: https://www.quanzhou.gov.cn/lyb/lyxw/202402/t20240207_3004252.htm
    Supports: an official visitor sequence linking selected old-city heritage nodes. It is not turn-by-turn navigation and does not prove current opening.

13. **Licheng District Government — dated summer 2026 heritage check-in sequence (2026-07-15)**
    URL: https://www.qzlc.gov.cn/xxgk/ggwhty/fggzhgf/202607/t20260717_3310112.htm
    Supports: a summer-2026 promotional check-in list that placed Kaiyuan, Qingjing, Deji and Tianhou in sequence. The activity expired on 2026-08-31; it is used only as evidence that the district has published this grouping, not as current opening, route direction, walking time or operating evidence.

14. **Quanzhou Municipal Government — 2026 Spring Festival service changes (2026-02-11)**
    URL: https://www.quanzhou.gov.cn/zfb/xxgk/zfxxgkzl/qzdt/qzyw/202602/t20260211_3266879.htm
    Supports: dated proof that venue access and service can change around holidays and religious periods. Its dates and hours are not normal-day promises.

15. **Quanzhou Municipal Government — 2026 May Day old-city traffic controls (2026-04-26)**
    URL: https://www.quanzhou.gov.cn/zfb/xxgk/zfxxgkzl/qzdt/qzyw/202604/t20260427_3286541.htm
    Supports: dated proof of temporary traffic and visitor-management changes. It is not a permanent route map.

16. **Licheng District Government — West Street vehicle restriction (2026-05-20; stated validity to 2026-11-26)**
    URL: https://www.qzlc.gov.cn/xxgk/zfxxgk/lcqzfxxgkml/13cxjsgl/202605/t20260520_3293076.htm
    Supports: current dated vehicle-access control. It must be rechecked after expiry and does not establish permanent pedestrian conditions.
