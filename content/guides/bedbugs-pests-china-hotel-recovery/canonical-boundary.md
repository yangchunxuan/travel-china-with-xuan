# Canonical boundary — bedbugs-pests-china-hotel-recovery

## Search task uniquely owned by this page

Help a traveller who suspects bedbugs or sees obvious pests in a China hotel room: stop unpacking, contain luggage and clothing so the problem does not travel, record observations at low risk with honest evidence-strength distinctions, involve the duty manager and booking platform, choose between distant-room relocation / hotel change / professional treatment, and handle aftercare (laundry) plus responsible reviews — without diagnosing species or making public accusations.

## Why a new canonical instead of updating an old page

No existing page owns in-stay pest events. The fire-safety check owns arrival walk-throughs but not infestation response; the evidence pack starts after a commercial dispute exists; hotel guides own choosing/registering stays. Containment-first sequencing with evidence-strength tiers is a distinct task that would be buried inside any of them.
