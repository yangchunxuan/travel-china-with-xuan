## Editorial decisions

- The article never names hotels, never diagnoses insects from photos or bites, and provides no pesticide recipes or concentrations.
- Suspicion vs confirmation distinction is enforced throughout: live insect photographed in place > harbourage signs > moults >> bite marks alone; professional species confirmation is the only full proof.
- Health section points exclusively to qualified medical care; no medicines, no doses, no self-treatment claims beyond agency-attributed laundering guidance.
- Review-writing section enforces firsthand observations, timestamps, honest recording of hotel response; no doxxing, no unverified public accusations.
