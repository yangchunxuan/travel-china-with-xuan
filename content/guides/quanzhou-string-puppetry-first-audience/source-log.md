## Core evidence

2. [Puppetry (Quanzhou string puppetry)](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/13429.html), China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network: identifies the national project and protecting institution, describes string manipulation, repertoire and musical inheritance, and gives a variable range from more than ten to more than thirty strings in its overview.
3. [Quanzhou Marionette Heritage and Protection Center](https://www.cnqzmt.com/), direct official institution: institution identity and current first-party information.
4. [Official performance list](https://www.cnqzmt.com/shows_list.html), Quanzhou Marionette Heritage and Protection Center: the correct dynamic endpoint for named current programmes; no entries are copied into the evergreen body.
5. [Official contact](https://www.cnqzmt.com/contact.html), Quanzhou Marionette Heritage and Protection Center: direct route for reconfirming venue and access.
6. [Fujian culture authority, 2026 performance recognition](https://wlt.fujian.gov.cn/wldt/btdt/202601/t20260120_7082303.htm), Fujian Department of Culture and Tourism: current dated institutional context, not a recurring schedule.
7. [Heritage so young: youth telling the stories of Quanzhou](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/heritage-so-young-youth-telling-stories-quanzhou-world-heritage-city-red-brick-walls-and-ancient), UNESCO: independent institutional context for contemporary heritage interpretation in Quanzhou.
8. [Quanzhou Marionette Troupe](https://wepa.unima.org/en/quanzhou-marionette-troupe/), World Encyclopedia of Puppetry Arts / UNIMA: independent puppetry reference for history and institutional context.
9. [Hong Kong LCSD programme note (PDF)](https://www.lcsd.gov.hk/CE/CulturalService/Programme/pdf/program_note1_1855_1.pdf): a programme-specific description using a different string range; retained to demonstrate why no universal number is published.

## Evidence levels

- **Official form-level fact:** project identity, protecting institution, documented manipulation and musical/repertoire characteristics.
- **Safeguarding fact:** UNESCO recognizes a training strategy, not every visitor performance or product.
- **Direct-operator fact:** a named show, venue, date and booking condition appearing on the centre’s current channel; valid only for that listing and check date.
- **Independent reference:** UNIMA provides cross-checking context; the LCSD programme note documents its programme rather than setting a universal rule.
- **Homeground editorial judgment:** programme-selection ladder, four attention shifts, traveller scenarios and failure recovery.
- **Cannot infer:** authenticity, skill or age from string count alone; a puppet’s role from appearance alone; a permanent schedule from an archived notice.
