# Canonical boundary — Jingdezhen ceramic districts

Canonical ID: `jingdezhen-imperial-kiln-taoxichuan-sanbao`

This page owns the spatial choice among Taoyangli/Imperial Kiln, Taoxichuan and Sanbao: what each district is for, how dispersed it is and which combinations fit one or two days. `where to go in Jingdezhen`, `Imperial Kiln or Taoxichuan`, `Taoxichuan vs Sanbao` and `Jingdezhen ceramic district itinerary` merge here.
