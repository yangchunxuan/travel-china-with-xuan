### Why this image, and why deliberately not a better one

Commons has more dramatic Puzhehei photographs. This one was chosen because it is **honest about
the thing the article is about**. It shows the lakes with water plants present but not as a
spectacular carpet of bloom, under haze rather than perfect light — which is what a normal day here
often looks like, and which is precisely the article's argument. A picture of a flawless lotus
carpet would have contradicted the page's own thesis on sight, and would have done exactly what the
article warns readers about: presenting one unrepeatable day as the expectation.

It also does two structural jobs. It is taken **from the Qinglong Hill viewing platform**, which is
the shore-side alternative the article tells readers to reserve for free — so the header is
literally the view being recommended. And the water level, the extent of the plant cover and the
scale of the lake system are all legible from above in a way the article says they are not from a
boat at water height.

### An unusual disclosure carried into the body

Because the article tells readers that undated photographs are the most common cause of a wasted
trip here, it would be dishonest to place an undated photograph at the top and say nothing.
**The body therefore dates this image in all three languages** and states that it is used to show
the landscape rather than this year's flowers. That disclosure is in the "a photograph from last
July is not evidence about this July" callout.

### Licence and provenance checks performed

- Licence read from the Commons file page's own `extmetadata`, not inferred from a search thumbnail
  or a reverse-image result.
- CC BY-SA 3.0 requires attribution and share-alike on the derivative. Attribution appears in the
  `heroCredit` block of all three locales, in each language's sources block, and here; the
  derivative licence is stated as CC BY-SA 3.0 in each place.
- The image is a photograph. **No AI-generated image is used anywhere in this guide.** For a page
  about verifying real conditions, a generated landscape would be self-refuting.

### Privacy and content checks

- No identifiable people. A single small boat is visible at distance; its occupants are far below
  the threshold of recognition.
- No identity documents, bank cards, tickets, order screens or telephone numbers.
- No legible signage of any kind.

### Crop review

- **Desktop (wide):** the lake system fills the lower two thirds with the karst skyline across the
  middle; the pale sky at the top carries the headline overlay cleanly.
- **Mobile (390 px):** the shared template renders the same 1.6:1 ratio. The lakes, the plant
  patches and the peak line all stay in frame, and nothing essential falls into the outer 8% that a
  narrow viewport can clip.

### Age of the photograph

Taken June 2014, and **said so in the body**. It is used for landform and viewpoint, both of which
are stable. No claim in the article depends on the image, and the article explicitly instructs
readers not to draw conditions from any photograph.
