## 1. Puzhehei scenic area operator — visitor and boarding rules: **VERIFIED, read in full**

- **Institution:** 云南普者黑文化旅游开发有限公司 (Yunnan Puzhehei Culture and Tourism Development
  Co., Ltd.), the scenic area operator, which the site identifies as running the
  风景名胜区 / 国家湿地公园
- **Page title:** 旅游服务 (visitor services), containing 游览须知, 乘车须知 and 乘船须知
- **URL:** https://www.qbpzh.net/tourism.html
- **Published / updated:** undated live page; the site's news index carries items dated to
  August 2026, so it is current
- **Retrieved:** 2026-08-22 · **Rechecked:** 2026-08-23
- **Supports:**
  - **Scenic area opening: 1 May – 31 Oct 08:00–17:30; 1 Nov – 30 Apr 08:30–17:00.**
  - **Boat operation: 1 May – 31 Oct 08:00–17:30; 1 Nov – 30 Apr 08:00–17:30.**
  - **Boarding times can be reserved in advance on the official mini-programme**, after which the
    visitor should arrive on time at the corresponding wharf to check in and board.
  - **Qinglong Hill requires an advance reservation on the mini-programme; the reservation is free;
    once it succeeds the visitor may climb.**
  - **Boat rules:** life jacket worn throughout; do not put head, hands or feet outside the boat; no
    dangerous items; no swimming; no littering; **intoxicated or physically unwell people should not
    board**; follow crew instructions in an emergency; help lines published.
  - **Water-splashing etiquette and prohibitions**, including not splashing the elderly, children,
    crew or unwilling visitors, and not using oars for water fights; plus safety notes on slippery
    boarding, waterproofing electronics, bringing a change of clothes and avoiding a sudden cold
    soaking after strong sun.
  - **Visitor rules:** elderly and children must be accompanied by an adult throughout; **losses
    from damp, falling in the water or loss are the visitor's own responsibility**; buy from
    official platforms or ticket points rather than unlicensed sellers.
  - **The shuttle network:** five named lines with their stops and operating windows, including the
    main scenic-area line running between Qinglong Hill and the north, west, east and south
    entrances via stops that include 荷花大道 (lotus boulevard), plus dedicated village lines.
- **Cannot support:** current bloom state; water level; whether any route is running today; prices.
- **Dynamic:** yes for every operational item.

## Absolute expressions audited after drafting

The body contains **no bloom date, no bloom state, no water-level estimate, no price, no weather
figure and no claim about any specific day**. It does not infer boat access from rain, visible water,
a photograph, hill access or the night-tour listing. Three explicit refusals are stated in their
own section. The one categorical claim is a negative: that nobody can honestly say in advance when
the lotus will peak.

The phrase "the flowers do not read calendars" is the only rhetorical flourish, and it makes a true
point rather than a factual claim.

## Sources deliberately not used

Social posts, travel-site galleries and OTA seasonal pages carrying peak-bloom date ranges were
found in quantity and **none was used**. The draft names that class of source as the most common
cause of a wasted trip here, and applies the same scepticism to its own hero image by dating it in
the body.
