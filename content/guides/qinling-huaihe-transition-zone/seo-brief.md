## Search task

The reader wants a definition, map logic and visible differences. The page should correct the `hard line` mental model, compare mountain and lowland mechanisms, and provide a defensible observation method.

## SERP composition observed on 2026-08-13

The sampled SERP was sparse and definition-heavy: encyclopedia pages, academic abstracts, education material and general travel geography. Many results repeat the 0°C/800 mm shorthand without explaining that indicators do not perfectly coincide or showing how the western mountain and eastern lowland sections differ.

Representative results:

- [Wikipedia — Qinling–Huaihe Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qinling%E2%80%93Huaihe_Line) — concise definition and familiar climate/agriculture associations.
- [TravelChinaGuide — Qinling Mountains](https://www.travelchinaguide.com/attraction/shaanxi/xian/qinling-mountains.htm) — destination-led mountain overview using the north–south-divide frame.
- [Ecological Indicators — climate variation in the demarcation zone](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1470160X23014875) — academic climate-change analysis across eastern, central and western parts.
- [CAS Institute of Geographic Sciences — geographic structure and extent](https://igsnrr.cas.cn/sourcedb/zw/lw/202504/t20250409_7591749.html) — institutional research abstract for the approximately 1,750 km by 400 km transition-zone model.

This is a dated qualitative observation, not a ranking guarantee or traffic estimate.

## Result gap and Homeground information gain

- Lead with `useful shorthand, not a painted border`.
- Explain that long-term indicators, species ranges and land use operate at different scales.
- Separate the high-relief Qinling–Daba west from the lowland Jianghuai east.
- Give six observation lenses and require repeated patterns rather than one photograph.
- Keep the 1,750 km/400 km dimensions tied to one CAS research framework, not a universal legal boundary.
- Prevent canonical drift into heating policy, food identity or a live transport-view promise.
