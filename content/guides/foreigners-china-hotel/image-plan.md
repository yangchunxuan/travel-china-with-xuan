## Privacy-safe composition

- Prefer a wide reception or entrance view with an authorized staff member behind the desk and no active guest transaction.
- No passport, visa, identity document, registration form, booking confirmation, phone screen, QR code, payment card, room key number, room number or luggage tag may be visible.
- Computer and registration-system screens must be out of frame, turned away or physically blank; do not depend on blur that may fail in another crop.
- Avoid identifiable uninvolved guests. Obtain model releases for every recognisable person, including staff.
- Remove embedded GPS and other personal metadata from the final export.

## Acceptable fallback

A real hotel entrance with a visible staffed reception immediately inside, photographed with permission and with no guests or personal data. Record the exact property and city. A generic skyline, empty bedroom or unrelated lobby does not prove the operational feature this article discusses.

## Do not use

- AI-generated, composited or staged documentary hotel imagery.
- A fake passport exchange, rejection notice, platform screenshot or government-registration screen.
- Stock imagery whose property, city, rights or depicted reception function cannot be verified.
- A specific exterior labelled as a “foreigner hotel” or presented as proof that the property will accept every booking.
- Any crop containing readable personal, payment, booking, room or staff-system information.
