# Image plan

Status: **COPY DRAFT — EDITORIAL REVIEW AND ASSETS NEEDED**

## Editorial boundary

Use only real, licensed documentary photography. Do not generate a fake hotel, lobby, room, reception desk, booking screen or staged refusal scene. Do not present an AI-created image as a real check-in experience.

## Required hero

- **Target:** `/images/guides/foreigners-china-hotel/hero-1600.webp`
- **Size:** 1600 × 1000 px, landscape.
- **Scene:** a foreign traveller with ordinary luggage speaking calmly with staff at a genuinely staffed hotel reception desk in mainland China.
- **Editorial purpose:** show the practical check-in interaction without implying that the featured property caused a refusal.
- **Privacy:** no readable passport number, date of birth, room number, payment details, booking reference or registration-system screen. The traveller should retain control of the passport; avoid close-ups of personal documents.
- **Rights:** obtain photographer licence, property/location permission where required and model releases for recognisable people.

## Acceptable fallback hero

A real hotel entrance or reception area with staffed front desk, wayfinding and arriving luggage visible, photographed with permission. The caption must identify the actual city and property or describe it generically if the licence requires anonymity; it must not imply endorsement or a recorded refusal.

## Optional supporting images

1. A real staffed reception desk with bilingual check-in signage, with all guest and system data obscured.
2. A traveller viewing a genuine booking confirmation on a phone, with name, dates, price, QR codes and reservation number fully redacted.
3. A real late-evening hotel entrance showing whether reception is staffed, without identifying uninvolved guests.
4. A documentary image of an immigration-service or government-service sign relevant to accommodation registration, only where photography is permitted.

## Do not use

- AI-generated or composite documentary scenes.
- Fake rejection notices, fake platform screenshots or recreated government pages.
- A specific hotel exterior presented as a recommended “foreigner hotel.”
- Passport, visa or registration-form details that remain readable after export.
- Room photography that turns the article into a hotel ranking or value guide.

## Asset QA

- Confirm licence territory, duration, derivative rights and web/social use.
- Record city, property, capture date, photographer and release status.
- Verify that captions distinguish illustrative check-in imagery from a documented incident.
- Check all crops at 320 px and desktop widths.
- Export WebP without embedded personal metadata.
