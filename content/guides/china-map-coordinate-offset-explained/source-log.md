## Evidence synthesis and conflicts

- Amap's domestic Web Service documentation asks developers to identify coordinate source types and to use structured addresses. A separate international Web Service page documents WGS84 defaults for that overseas product. The copy therefore avoids saying that one provider always uses one coordinate default.
- Baidu's current geocoding documentation distinguishes exact and approximate address parsing and its Place Search documentation distinguishes a POI location from a navigation guidance point that may represent an entrance. These facts support separate address, property-centre and entrance diagnoses; they do not guarantee that every consumer listing exposes those fields.
- Tencent's currently accessible WebService pages distinguish input types and address match levels, but the pages use a legacy documentation path without a clear current revision date. The article uses only the stable conceptual distinction and makes no 2026 consumer-feature promise.
- Natural Resources rules establish approved treatment of protected geographic information and protect recognised algorithms and parameters. They do not prove that every wrong travel pin is caused by security processing and do not provide a correction value.
- The Surveying and Mapping Law and national geocoding-standard record provide the regulatory and standards context. Neither is treated as a consumer navigation accuracy guarantee.
