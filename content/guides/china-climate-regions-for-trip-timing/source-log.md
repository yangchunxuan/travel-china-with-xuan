## Editorial judgments

- Protect the weather-critical and least substitutable experience first.
- Screen heat, rain, cold, visibility and elevation separately, then overlay holiday and operator constraints.
- A mixed route can be coherent without every stop receiving its peak conditions.
- Current forecasts and named operating notices replace long-term assumptions close to travel.
