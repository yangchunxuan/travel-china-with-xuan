## Canonical boundary and search gap

This page covers the Beijing-hub-to-Badaling transport decision and return options. It does not compare Great Wall sections, teach attraction ticketing, promise a timetable, or duplicate the Beijing railway-station guide.

Searches reviewed: `Beijing to Badaling train or bus`, `Badaling 877 or S2`, and `Qinghe to Badaling`. Results often mix Beijing North, Qinghe, the former Huangtudian S2 terminal and the former Deshengmen 877 terminus, or quote vehicle time without the hotel first leg and scenic-area arrival point.

## Dynamic facts used

- Beijing Public Transport moved Bus 877 from Deshengmen to Beitucheng on 27 March 2025. No permanent first/last bus is stated. `checked_at: 2026-08-13`.
- Beijing's transport authority states that S2 has started at Nankou since 25 October 2025 and that Huangtudian has ceased operation. The dated timetable must still be checked. `checked_at: 2026-08-13`.
- A Capital Airport–Badaling direct bus was announced with dated 2026 operations; the article explicitly does not generalise it to daily, permanent or Daxing service. `checked_at: 2026-08-12`.
- Attraction operations, fares and railway inventory remain dynamic. `checked_at: 2026-08-12`.
