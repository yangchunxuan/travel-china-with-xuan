## Dynamic facts to recheck

- Exact property name, address, operator and public entrance.
- Whether the public, non-residents or foreign visitors may dine in.
- Resident, age, disability or other eligibility; local ID, registration, meal card or booking requirements.
- Meal windows, sell-out timing, menu, ordinary price, discount and subsidy rules.
- Cash, mobile-payment and card acceptance; transaction status after a failure.
- Kerbs, ramps, doors, lifts, queue, tray carrying, seating and toilet access.
- Ingredients, shared cooking/cross-contact, texture and any dietary information staff can actually confirm.
- Food-safety notices, operator licence/inspection display where applicable.
- Photography permission and privacy conditions.

## Editorial judgments

- The Chinese word `食堂` alone does not establish a public community-canteen service.
- Access and suitability are separate: admission does not make the meal safe for an allergy or usable for a mobility need.
- A subsidy normally follows a locally eligible person/service arrangement; a visitor should ask for ordinary price rather than seek another person's entitlement.
- A restricted, sold-out or closed property is a normal verification outcome. The responsible recovery is a public restaurant or prepared-food fallback, not confrontation.
- Community canteens should be described as meal and social infrastructure without romanticising, stigmatising or exoticising their users.

## Prohibited claims

- All community canteens are public, government-owned, subsidised, free or cheap.
- All foreign visitors are admitted or all foreign visitors are refused.
- Every person aged 60 or older receives a nationwide discount.
- Every `食堂` is a community canteen.
- One venue's price, menu, hours, payment or access is evergreen or national.
- Food-safety oversight certifies each dish as allergy-safe, halal, vegetarian or medically suitable.
- Community canteens are “poverty kitchens,” ideological spectacles or secret bargain restaurants.
- Observed satisfaction, social or usage associations prove nationwide causal health benefits.
- Diners, older people, staff, meal cards or payment screens can be photographed without consent.
