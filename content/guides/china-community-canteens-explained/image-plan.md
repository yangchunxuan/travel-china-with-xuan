## Commissioned documentary backup

Commission a named Homeground photographer at one currently verified, publicly accessible community canteen. The shot list should include:

1. Exterior frontage with exact Chinese property name and enough street context to verify the location.
2. Publicly posted ordinary-price or eligibility information, only with operator permission and without personal/customer data.
3. Street-to-door access showing kerb, ramp or threshold honestly.
4. A wide dining-space view only when the operator grants permission and no diner is identifiable, or every identifiable person signs an informed commercial model release.

Required paperwork:

- written worldwide commercial web/social licence or project ownership;
- exact address, capture date, photographer and operator contact in the asset log;
- property release for interior/private-premises photography;
- individual releases for every identifiable diner or staff member, with particular caution around older or vulnerable people;
- written confirmation that photographing posted notices is permitted.

## Privacy, dignity and authenticity requirements

- Do not select older diners, low-income residents, delivery workers or disabled people as visual proof of a social category.
- Do not stage a “cheap local meal” scene or ask diners to perform daily routines.
- Exclude names, faces without releases, meal-card numbers, resident eligibility, subsidy records, payment screens, order numbers, phone numbers, QR codes and precise home information.
- Never photograph through a care-facility or residential window, follow a user into a controlled area or capture a meal-assistance register.
- Do not claim an empty room proves low use or a busy frame proves universal demand; both are time-specific.
- A documentary photo must retain exact place, creator, date and rights. A generic cafeteria must not be captioned as a community canteen without property verification.
- No AI documentary replacement, fake room, fake menu, fake sign, face synthesis or composited “resident” is acceptable.
