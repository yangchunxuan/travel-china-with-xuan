# SEO brief — China community canteens

Reviewed: **2026-08-13 (Asia/Shanghai)**
Canonical: `/guides/china-community-canteens-explained/`
Decision: one canonical for visitor-facing community/senior meal-service access; generic university, school and workplace canteens remain outside this owner.

## Primary query and search task

- Primary query: `Can tourists eat at community canteens in China?`
- Search task: identify the facility type, determine whether a named property admits non-residents or foreign visitors at ordinary price, verify meal card/payment/access/dietary constraints, and recover safely if it is restricted, sold out or unsuitable.
- Intent: practical informational decision; not a cheap-eats list, benefit-eligibility loophole, nationwide price guide or promise of public access.

## Result gap and Homeground information gain

- Existing pages often prove that one named venue welcomed outsiders, then generalise that outcome to China.
- Homeground supplies a terminology decoder, national-versus-local policy boundary, property-level verification script, access-versus-suitability distinction, payment and mobility checks, respectful-use rules and fallback meal plan.
- The page explicitly separates ordinary public price from locally eligible discounts and refuses to treat older-adult meal infrastructure as a bargain attraction.
