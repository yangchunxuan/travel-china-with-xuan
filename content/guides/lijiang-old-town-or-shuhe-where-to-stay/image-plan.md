# Image plan

**COPY DRAFT — EDITORIAL REVIEW AND ASSETS NEEDED**

Use only real, licensable destination photography with reliable location provenance. Do not generate or simulate Lijiang photography with AI.

## Hero

- Purpose: communicate a real choice between two historic settlement bases, not a generic “beautiful old China” mood.
- Preferred treatment: an editorial diptych made from one verified Dayan street image and one verified Shuhe street or water-lane image. Both photographs must be real and separately licensed.
- Alternative: one strong horizontal photograph from either Dayan or Shuhe, provided the exact place is named in the caption and the image is not presented as representing both towns.
- Crop target: 1600 × 1000 WebP at `public/images/guides/lijiang-old-town-or-shuhe-where-to-stay/hero-1600.webp`.
- Avoid: an unverified “Lijiang old town” stock image, a Baisha photo labelled as Dayan or Shuhe, a hotel marketing image without reuse rights, or a mountain-view crop that implies every room has the same view.
- Metadata handoff: revise all three `heroAlt` fields after the final asset and exact location are known.

## Heritage-name section

- Worth making: yes. Create a simple editorial location diagram showing Dayan, Shuhe and Baisha as three components of one UNESCO property.
- Base labels and relative positions on the UNESCO pages in `source-log.md`.
- Show that Shuhe is north-west of Dayan, but do not convert the official 4 km geographic relationship into a road time or walking route.
- Do not use a Google Maps screenshot or invent administrative boundaries.

## Dayan comparison section

- One real, authorised photograph that shows a recognisable Dayan lane, waterway or larger urban-old-town context.
- A second optional image may show a genuine perimeter drop-off or the transition from vehicle access to pedestrian lanes, if the location and current use can be verified.
- Avoid using the busiest available crowd photo as proof that Dayan is always crowded.

## Shuhe comparison section

- One real, authorised photograph that shows a recognisable Shuhe street, water system or residential-scale context.
- A real entrance or managed drop-off image would be useful for the access section if its date and location are recorded.
- Avoid presenting an empty dawn photo as evidence that Shuhe is always quiet or “undiscovered.”

## Luggage and mobility section

- Best supporting image: a truthful close or medium view of stone paving, a bridge, steps or the final hotel approach in one named settlement.
- Caption it as one example, not as the condition of every lane in Dayan or Shuhe.
- Faces and identifiable hotel branding should be cleared or avoided unless rights are documented.

## Ordinary city or nature image

- Optional: a real Lijiang-plain or Jade Dragon Snow Mountain context image can sit near the transport section.
- Its role is geographical orientation only. Do not imply that the photographed view is visible from a named hotel or that Shuhe always reaches the mountain faster.

## Infographic decision

- A compact “Dayan or Shuhe?” decision graphic is useful if assets allow it, but the location diagram is the higher priority.
- Use only stable comparison points: heritage relationship, relative position, short-stay versus deliberate-base planning, and the need to verify the hotel drop-off.
- Do not include fixed taxi times, prices, crowd scores, hotel-rate ranges or a permanent bus timetable.

## Rights and editorial checks

- Record creator, source or archive reference, licence, permitted channels, capture date if known and exact location confirmation.
- Keep the provenance of Dayan, Shuhe and Baisha images separate; the three places are not interchangeable.
- Do not use AI to manufacture a real destination scene or to add/remove crowds in documentary photographs.
- No image asset is currently present. Guide generation and the site build are therefore not expected to pass until the hero file is supplied.
