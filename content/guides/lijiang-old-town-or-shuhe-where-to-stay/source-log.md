## Claim boundaries used in the copy

- Official fact: the serial World Heritage relationship, relative position, inscribed component areas, Dayan vehicle restriction and dated public-transport coverage.
- Dated operational fact: the 2026 airport summer-season stops, the May 2026 snow-mountain access notice and earlier official bus-network examples. Every timetable and pickup point still requires a final check.
- Homeground planning judgment: Dayan as the default for a short first stay, Shuhe when a specific property or slower base is intentional, choosing a perimeter room for easier luggage access and avoiding a split stay on a short trip.
- Not claimed: guaranteed quiet, fixed crowd levels, permanent hotel-price differences, a fixed taxi duration or fare, universal car access or prohibition in Shuhe, universal accessibility, a guaranteed mountain view, a single town-wide opening time, or a current universal entry-fee rule.
