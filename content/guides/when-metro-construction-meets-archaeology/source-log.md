# B21 source log — when-metro-construction-meets-archaeology

- Candidate: B21
- Research/review date: 2026-08-13
- Canonical user task: Help a traveller understand what can happen when a Chinese metro project encounters an older city below ground, and distinguish archaeology news from a passenger-facing disruption.
- Boundary: National process plus four bounded city cases. No construction technique, worksite access, timetable prediction, or claim that archaeology necessarily delays a project.

## Official / primary sources

1. [Cultural Relics Protection Law of the People's Republic of China (2024 revision)](https://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202411/t20241108_440882.html) — National People's Congress of China.
   - Evidence used: Article 43 mandatory pre-transfer investigation/exploration in designated potential-relic areas, pre-construction archaeology triggers and agreement of protection measures; Article 45 developer-budget principle; Article 46 chance-find protection and reporting.
   - Time boundary: Revised 2024; effective 2025-03-01; status reviewed 2026-08-13.
2. [Guangzhou Metro Line 12 Hengfu Road station A-entrance excavation completion letter](https://wglj.gz.gov.cn/gkmlpt/content/10/10315/post_10315789.html) — Guangzhou Municipal Cultural Heritage Bureau, issued 2025-06-09/published 2025-06-18.
   - Evidence used: 150 m²; five features; 24 artefacts/sets; completed area may proceed with other construction procedures; renewed stop/protect/report duty.
   - Boundary: One station entrance and one completed footprint, not all of Line 12.
3. [Beijing archaeology during the 14th Five-Year Plan](https://www.beijing.gov.cn/renwen/sy/whkb/202104/t20210425_2368940.html) — Beijing Municipal Government, 2021-04-25.
   - Evidence used: 2019–2020 work at four locations connected with Jin capital-wall protection and Lines 14/16 Lize station works; outer wall, moat and street remains.
   - Boundary: Public account of research results; it does not promise an in-station display.
4. [2021 performance report of Wuhan Municipal Institute of Cultural Relics and Archaeology](https://wlj.wuhan.gov.cn/ztzl_27/zxzt/swljbmyjsjsgjfyjsxx/202210/P020221026460810686848.pdf) — Wuhan Municipal Culture and Tourism Bureau.
   - Evidence used: pages 25 and 40; first-phase 500 m² archaeological work at Tanhualin station west entrance, national approval, expert acceptance; institute says the work supported on-time opening.
   - Boundary: The on-time statement is attributed to the institute; no national performance inference.
5. [Zhaojiadun excavation for Hangzhou Metro Line 15 Shushan South vehicle base](https://www.hzxcw.gov.cn/content_46825.html) — Hangzhou Publicity Office / Hangzhou Daily, 2026.
   - Evidence used: 2024–2025 excavation, 1,950 m², 119 features, late Majiabang focus with early Liangzhu remains and rare Houmaqiao-culture burial.
   - Boundary: A vehicle-base footprint, not a running tunnel or public visitor site.

## Independent research / cross-checks

6. [Safety risk assessment of heritage buildings in metro construction based on SPA theory: a case study in Zhengzhou, China](https://www.nature.com/articles/s40494-020-00439-3) — *npj Heritage Science* 8, 100 (2020).
   - Evidence used: Standing heritage beside metro construction needs a separate risk framework using building, metro, soil and management factors.
   - Boundary: Engineering-risk study; not evidence about buried finds or a national delay rate.
7. [Managing Cultural Heritage in China: A View from the Outside](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/china-quarterly/article/abs/managing-cultural-heritage-in-china-a-view-from-the-outside/C7311B0A5197F1AAA7641327BE18F8EA) — Zan and Bonini Baraldi, *The China Quarterly* (2012).
   - Evidence used: The analytical distinction between discovery, restoration, museum definition and public access supports the page's separation of archaeology from display.
   - Boundary: Historical management analysis; current legal procedure comes from the 2024 law, not this paper.

## Reconciliation and risk notes

- No source supports “archaeology always delays metros.” Wuhan provides an attributed counter-case; Guangzhou shows a bounded hand-back; no universal speed claim is made.
- No source supports “finds become a station museum.” The bodies expressly require a separate public-venue confirmation.
- Dates for construction, discoveries, opening and display are kept separate.
- Passenger disruption must come from a current metro-operator notice. No live service status is frozen into this explanatory page.
- At least three verifiable city cases required; four are supplied: Guangzhou, Beijing, Wuhan and Hangzhou.
