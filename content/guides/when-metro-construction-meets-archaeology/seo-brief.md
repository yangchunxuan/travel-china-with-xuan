# SEO brief — China metro construction and archaeology

- Reviewed: 2026-08-13 (Asia/Shanghai)
- Canonical owner: `/guides/when-metro-construction-meets-archaeology/`
- Dynamic class: medium; law and completed cases are durable, but construction state, service disruption and public display require current operator/heritage notices.
- Page job: explain the decision chain when a Chinese metro project encounters buried heritage and keep archaeology news separate from passenger operations.
- Do not create: a delay predictor, engineering manual, worksite-access guide or promise that finds become station museums.

## Primary query

`China metro construction archaeology`

Answer the process question through current law and bounded city cases, then give a source ladder for anyone trying to infer passenger impact.

## Search task

The reader needs three separate answers: what is underground, what the project must do, and what the public can actually see. A discovery report alone cannot answer the last two.

## SERP composition observed on 2026-08-13

The sampled results mixed spectacular single discoveries, general news, engineering papers and international metro-museum examples. China-specific process explanations grounded in the current national law were sparse, and headlines often encouraged the false shortcut from discovery to delay or display.

Representative results:

- [National People's Congress — 2024 Cultural Relics Protection Law](https://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202411/t20241108_440882.html) — current legal basis, effective 2025-03-01.
- [Guangzhou Cultural Heritage Bureau — Line 12 bounded excavation hand-back](https://wglj.gz.gov.cn/gkmlpt/content/10/10315/post_10315789.html) — a completed station-entrance footprint, not a whole-line claim.
- [Beijing Municipal Government — metro-linked archaeology](https://www.beijing.gov.cn/renwen/sy/whkb/202104/t20210425_2368940.html) — official research summary without a display promise.
- [Hangzhou — Line 15 vehicle-base excavation](https://www.hzxcw.gov.cn/content_46825.html) — 2024–2025 bounded case, distinct from an operating tunnel.
- [npj Heritage Science — heritage-building risk near metro works](https://www.nature.com/articles/s40494-020-00439-3) — independent engineering-risk lens, not a national delay rate.

This is a dated qualitative sample, not a ranking or traffic estimate.

## Result gap and Homeground information gain

- Lead with the three-question separation: underground evidence, project response and public access.
- Explain survey, exploration/excavation, protection decision, hand-back/redesign and separate interpretation/display.
- Use Guangzhou, Beijing, Wuhan and Hangzhou as bounded counterexamples to universal claims.
- Require a metro-operator notice before inferring passenger disruption.
- Supply recovery paths when a news article lacks footprint, date, authority or current service status.
