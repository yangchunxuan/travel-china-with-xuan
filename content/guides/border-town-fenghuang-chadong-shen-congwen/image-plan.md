The body deliberately uses no generic or wrong-location substitute.

## Brand, text and artwork limits

- Do not reuse book jackets, film/television stills, modern translations, illustrations or performance screenshots.
- A photograph licence covers the photograph, not a new claim about the age of architecture or the identity of a literary scene.
- If a museum label or manuscript becomes important, obtain separate reproduction permission for its text/artwork and keep quotations minimal.
- Hunan government pages are evidence links, not an image library.
