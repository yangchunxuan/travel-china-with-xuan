## Canonical boundary

This page owns the decision between Chadong as literary geography and Fenghuang as author hometown, protected former-home and memorial context. It does not own Zhangjiajie–Fenghuang transport, accommodation, a general Fenghuang itinerary, a complete Shen Congwen biography or a plot summary. It does not state that Fenghuang is the prototype of *Border Town*, and it does not promise that modern Chadong is a building-by-building survival of the novella.

## Core evidence

1. [Hunan Provincial Archives — Shen Congwen](https://sdaj.hunan.gov.cn/sdaj/wszt/xxsl/xxrw/200609/t20060928_1977903.html) records his birth in Fenghuang in 1902 and the later memorial relationship to the Tuojiang. It supports biography and commemoration, not a claim that Fenghuang is the novella's prototype.
2. [Hunan Provincial Cultural Relics Bureau — national protected sites](https://wwj.hunan.gov.cn/wwj/c100314/201406/t20140613_10483053.html) identifies Shen Congwen Former Residence as a Qing-period building in the sixth national list.
3. [Hunan Provincial Cultural Relics Bureau — 2025 protection inspection](https://wwj.hunan.gov.cn/wwj/c100310/c100311/202505/t20250530_33687539.html) confirms active heritage management. It does not establish that every visible component or furnishing is original.
4. [Journal of Jishou University — A Trinity View of the Literary Geography of *Border Town*](https://skxb.jsu.edu.cn/CN/Y2010/V31/I1/50) distinguishes Chadong from the principal literary space at Bixiju and supplies the romanization `Bixiju`.
5. [Journal of Jishou University — Spatial Production of the Ancient Town of Chadong](https://skxb.jsu.edu.cn/CN/Y2021/V42/I5/124) supports treating historical background, literary imagination and tourism production as related but distinct layers.
6. [Hunan Department of Culture and Tourism — Chadong Ancient Wharf](https://whhlyt.hunan.gov.cn/whhlyt/RedTourism/202206/t20220607_25264271.html) describes the wharf's location, approximate area, Republican-period origin, stone steps, later maintenance and protection status.
7. [Hunan Provincial People's Government — 2024 Chadong presentation](https://www.hunan.gov.cn/hnszf/hnyw/szdt/202410/t20241006_33468999.html) explicitly describes the Cuicui residence as a reconstructed simulation of a novel scene. It is evidence for tourism presentation, not for the historicity of Cuicui.
8. [Hunan Provincial People's Government — 2026 lettering provenance](https://www.hunan.gov.cn/hnszf/hnyw/szdt/202605/t20260502_33969228.html) distinguishes the newly installed authorized reproduction derived from a genuine 1981 inscription from the 1993 cliff imitation by Long Qinglian.
9. [Hunan Department of Civil Affairs — Chadong/Biancheng place-name history](https://mzt.hunan.gov.cn/mzt/sxdmx/202005/t20200513_12118025.html) supports the 2005 administrative renaming. Renaming shows modern reception and does not prove a one-to-one prototype.
10. [Ministry of Education language table](https://language.moe.gov.tw/001/Upload/Files/wxiao89/a.pdf) gives `jū` for 岨. Together with the Jishou University romanization, the article uses `Bixiju (碧溪岨, Bìxījū)` and does not present it as a verified modern attraction pin.
11. [KCI-indexed Korean study](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART003024669) was used to check the established Korean forms `선충원` and `『변성』`.

## Evidence layers and editorial decisions

- **History:** documented author biography, protected-property designation, dated material sites and documented inscription provenance.
- **Literature:** Bixiju, Cuicui, the ferry family and the selected/composed landscape of the novella.
- **Tourism-presentation:** renaming, reconstructed Cuicui spaces, performances, themed signs and modern inscription display.
- **Editorial-judgment:** Chadong is the stronger one-place choice for literary geography; Fenghuang is the stronger one-place choice for author biography and commemoration. This is Homeground's recommendation, not a scholarly or official ranking.
- **Cannot infer:** an exact GPS point for every literary name, a historical Cuicui, the exact novel ferry, uniformly original old streets, original furnishings throughout the former residence, or an unchanged daily ferry/performance schedule.

## Terminology decision: 碧溪岨

The first occurrence in every language keeps the Chinese form and `Bìxījū`. English uses `Bixiju`; Chinese uses `碧溪岨（Bìxījū）`; Korean uses `비시쥐(碧溪岨, Bìxījū)`. The spatial wording follows the academic article: a literary space associated with a rock-covered hill by an upper branch of the Chadong River. The body does not convert that description into a modern attraction address.
