## Evidence and dynamic facts

- NRA's 81-pair figure is explicitly dated March 2024 and is not presented as a 2026 count.
- The national rule is real-name ticketing for passenger trains and stations. A named non-long-distance public-welfare slow train or related station may temporarily not apply it only where the railway operator has announced the exception in advance and explained why; the train type alone never establishes an exception.
- Exact train numbers, stops, booking visibility, fares and onboard adaptations require current China Railway confirmation.
- The current real-name ticketing regulation allows published route-specific exceptions for some non-long-distance public-welfare slow trains. Online absence therefore proves neither cancellation nor permission to board.
- A Central South University student field report independently illustrates ordinary passenger use on one named train. It is not treated as peer-reviewed research. The Beijing University of Technology paper provides broader public-welfare railway theory; neither source proves a current train, route or timetable.
- Absence from one 12306 search is not treated as proof that a train has stopped, because some local public-welfare services require station confirmation.
