### Caption boundary

- **Allowed:** “A mushroom meal filmed by China News Service at a Kunming restaurant in 2020; the
  image does not identify each species or establish a current restaurant protocol.”
- **Not allowed:** the name of any plate or species; “safe mushrooms”; “fully cooked”; “the standard
  Yunnan set”; “today's restaurant”; or evidence that a timer, sourcing or sample-retention rule was
  followed.

## Explanatory images for central acquisition

1. **Wild versus cultivated menu evidence:** a current menu photographed with the operator's written
   reuse permission. Every highlighted item must be classified by the restaurant in writing; date,
   branch and menu version must be recorded.
2. **Kitchen handoff:** an operator-approved image showing a labelled ingredient tray moving to the
   kitchen. It must not expose supplier invoices, employee personal data or an unverified species.
3. **House cooking control:** a staged, staff-approved photograph of the exact timer/utensil process
   used by one named restaurant on one date. Caption it as that house's method, never a province-wide
   rule.
4. **Current warning sign:** a public-facing Yunnan CDC or market-regulator poster photographed where
   display and commercial reuse are permitted. Do not extract a copyrighted infographic merely
   because it appears on an official page.
5. **Emergency evidence kit:** an editorial still life of a blank receipt, menu note, sealed leftover
   container and phone showing `120`, using no real customer data and clearly labelled as an
   illustration rather than a poisoning scene.

Each commissioned image needs photographer, capture date, exact venue, written commercial-use terms,
visible-person consent, original file, hash, crop record and separately reviewed EN/ZH/KO alt and
caption. Staff handling food must be photographed without interrupting hygiene controls.

## Keep as HTML

- Restaurant evidence checklist, wild/cultivated/mixed decision table, one-pot/one-clock workflow,
  myth corrections, group adaptations, emergency steps and phrase card remain editable HTML.
- No AI-generated documentary restaurant, cook, patient, mushroom specimen or tourist photograph.
- No stock mushroom close-up is used as proof of species, edibility, Yunnan origin or preparation.
- No identifiable diner, patient or medical encounter is used without specific informed permission.
