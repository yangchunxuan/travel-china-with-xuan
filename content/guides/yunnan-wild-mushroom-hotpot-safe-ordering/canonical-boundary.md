## The one task this page owns

A foreign traveller choosing a **Yunnan restaurant meal sold as wild-mushroom hotpot** needs to
separate wild from cultivated mushrooms, decide whether the restaurant can explain its ingredients
and process, place a conservative first order, keep one reliable cooking sequence, reject unsafe
identification and timing shortcuts, and escalate suspected poisoning without delay.

This is an at-restaurant decision-and-recovery page. It does not promise that eating wild mushrooms
can be made risk-free. The lowest-risk choice presented is cultivated mushrooms or another meal.

## Original information gain

The page joins official public-health controls to the actual decisions a traveller makes at a table:

1. distinguish wild mushrooms, cultivated mushrooms and an unexplained mixed set;
2. treat a restaurant's name and licence as starting filters, not safety certification;
3. ask what the kitchen has identified, sourced, pre-treated and retained;
4. decline mixed wild species under current Yunnan guidance and choose a cultivated fallback;
5. keep one batch and one release decision; only the same confirmed recorded wild item may restart a
   complete process after a late addition, while a different or unknown addition stops the whole pot;
6. reject colour, insects, smell, silver, garlic and social-media photo tests;
7. avoid alcohol and over-ordering; and
8. stop eating and seek urgent medical help when suspicious symptoms appear.

Where official sources set a boundary, the copy attributes it. The suggested order and stop/go
decisions are labelled as Homeground editorial judgement rather than provincial guarantees.
