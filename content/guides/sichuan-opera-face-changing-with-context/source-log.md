## Evidence used

1. [Sichuan opera national ICH record](https://www.ihchina.cn/Article/Index/detail?id=13161) — China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network. Supports the five principal tune systems and the position of face-changing among a wider performance vocabulary.
2. [Sichuan Provincial Academy of Arts](https://www.scyishu.org.cn/) — provincial professional arts institution. Supports the continuing specialist research context, not a permanent public performance schedule.
3. [Review of the 2023 Sichuan opera showcase repertoire](https://www.scyishu.org.cn/index.php?a=show&c=index&catid=112&id=497&m=content) — Sichuan Provincial Academy of Arts. Documents named traditional, revised and newly created programmes in a dated showcase, supporting the instruction to identify a programme and version rather than expect a generic face-changing act.
4. [2026 Sichuan Opera Hundred Shows](https://www.yscz.org.cn/Home/News?id=db65b602-adf7-4c54-a144-8c7f09c2e270) — National Arts Fund centre. Used only as a dated example of named professional production and repertoire circulation.
5. [Sichuan Department of Culture and Tourism](https://wlt.sc.gov.cn/) — provincial government channel for current institution and programme rechecks.
6. [Actor craft in Sichuan opera](https://academic.oup.com/hong-kong-scholarship-online/book/22201/chapter-abstract/182370455) — Oxford Academic. Independent support for an actor-centred reading of voice, rhythm and movement.

## Public-link endpoint audit

- The national ICH record and the Academy's main site remained current and indexed. The ICH host was intermittently slow over TLS during command-line checks but returned the current Sichuan-opera record; it was not treated as dead.
- The former Academy repertoire-dictionary subdomain remained indexed, but its HTTPS certificate did not match the public hostname. The public Sources block now uses a reachable Academy article reviewing named repertoire and version types. The specialist dictionary remains useful research evidence, but it is not exposed as a user-facing HTTPS link until the Academy repairs the certificate.
- The Sichuan Department of Culture and Tourism homepage timed out from the review network but remained a current indexed provincial-government site. Oxford returned publisher access-control responses to automated checks. Both were preserved because neither condition established link death and replacing them would reduce authority.

The earlier Smithsonian video was removed from the public evidence set because
its mechanism-oriented framing conflicts with this page's decision not to
explain or expose guarded face-changing mechanics. Two commercial venue sites
were also removed from the public source block because one timed out and one
returned an access denial during final review. No current act, cast, schedule,
price or booking condition depends on either site.
