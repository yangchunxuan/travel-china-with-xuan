## Page promise

1. Separate the ancestral-temple institution from any individual hall or component.
2. Preserve the official chronology: the present west-axis group is principally the product of rebuilding begun in the 1980s and completed in 1994; the new south-axis group was built from 1998 to 2002.
3. Read the sixth national protected-site record (`Mazu Temple`, Qing, Putian) as a designation record, not a blanket date for every visible structure.
4. Use UNESCO's `is believed` language as belief tradition, never as proof of a fully verified secular biography.
5. Treat living practice as community action with participant agency, not a guaranteed visitor performance.
6. Give non-believers a Homeground low-interference minimum while stating that it is not an official site code.
