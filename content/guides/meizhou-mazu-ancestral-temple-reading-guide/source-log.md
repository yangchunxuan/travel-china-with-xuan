## Official evidence matrix

### 1. UNESCO — Mazu belief and customs

- URL: <https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/mazu-belief-and-customs-00227>
- Authority: UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage
- Supports:
  - inscription in 2009 on the Representative List;
  - oral traditions, religious ceremonies and folk practices as parts of the element;
  - community and social meaning;
  - exact epistemic boundary: Mazu **is believed** to have lived on Meizhou Island in the tenth century.
- Does not support:
  - a fully verified secular biography;
  - a 2026 event calendar, visitor entitlement or photography permission.
- Retrieval note: direct page fetch timed out in one review client; the current official indexed page and result content were independently retrieved on 2026-08-15.

### 2. China Intangible Cultural Heritage directory

- URL: <https://www.ihchina.cn/directory_details/11811>
- Authority: China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network / China National Center for Safeguarding Intangible Cultural Heritage
- Supports:
  - 2009 listing context;
  - classification as social practices, rituals and festive events;
  - the official Chinese project narrative and Meizhou location context.
- Boundary: the narrative's exact life dates and rescue account are attributed as project tradition, not promoted into independent secular proof.

### 3. Meizhou Mazu Ancestral Temple official introduction

- URL: <https://www.mzmz.org.cn/mzmzzm.html>
- Authority: ancestral-temple institution
- Supports:
  - location in Gongxia Village, north Meizhou Island;
  - west- and south-axis groups;
  - institution/building chronology;
  - the current west-axis group as principally rebuilt from the 1980s, completed in 1994;
  - the new south-axis group begun in 1998, completed in 2002;
  - modern plazas, exhibition and visitor-presentation components.
- Does not support:
  - an ordinary visitor route, photography code, accessible chain or component-level date for every visible building.

### 4. Meizhou Island Administrative Committee introduction

- URL: <https://mzd.putian.gov.cn/jggk/jdjs/202301/t20230120_1796135.htm>
- Authority: Meizhou Island Administrative Committee / tourism and culture department
- Supports:
  - ancestral-temple identity at the north end of Meizhou Island;
  - government corroboration of the west-axis, new hall group and visitor-presentation context.
- Boundary: its assertive biography wording is not used to override UNESCO's belief-tradition framing.

### 5. Sixth national list of major protected historical and cultural sites

- URL: <https://zwgk.mct.gov.cn/zfxxgkml/qt/202012/t20201206_918486.html>
- Authority: State Council record published by the culture authority
- Supports:
  - sixth-batch designation;
  - entry `597 / III-300 / Mazu Temple / Qing / Putian, Fujian`.
- Boundary: the list names and dates the protected property; it does not date every present component or validate every tourism label.

## Claims deliberately not published

- opening times, ferry times or transfer minutes;
- passport booking or check-in guarantees;
- accessible routes or facility promises;
- photography, video, flash, tripod or drone permission;
- ritual, festival, procession or performance availability;
- a half-day or any untested duration;
- participant identity or belief inferred from appearance;
- any claim that all visible buildings are thousand-year-old original fabric.
