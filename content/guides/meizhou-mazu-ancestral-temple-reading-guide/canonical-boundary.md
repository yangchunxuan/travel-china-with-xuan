1. `UNESCO: Mazu is believed to have lived...` remains belief-tradition wording.
2. The temple's long institutional history does not date every visible component.
3. The present west-axis group is principally the product of rebuilding from the 1980s through 1994.
4. The new south-axis group was built from 1998 through 2002.
5. The sixth national protected-site list records `Mazu Temple`, `Qing`, `Putian`; that is a protected-property record, not a blanket component date.
6. The observer framework is Homeground editorial guidance. Site instructions always take priority.
7. An old photograph's open licence does not create current venue, ritual or participant permission.
