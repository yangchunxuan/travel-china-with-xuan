# Canonical boundary — Shenzhen airport, railway-station and border-port selector

Reviewed: **2026-08-21 (Asia/Shanghai)**

## This page owns

- The pre-book and post-book choice of Shenzhen arrival/departure gateway across these principal international-visitor anchors:
  - Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport (`SZX`);
  - Shenzhen North Railway Station (`深圳北`);
  - Futian Railway Station (`福田` / `福田站`);
  - Shenzhen Railway Station (`深圳` / `深圳站`);
  - Futian Checkpoint (`福田口岸`) and Luohu Checkpoint (`罗湖口岸`) only as named border anchors handed to the existing cross-border route owner.
- The durable rule that an issued flight or railway record fixes the exact airport/station, while an unbooked traveller compares live inventory and both city-side transfers.
- The identity corrections `福田站 ≠ 福田口岸`, `深圳站 ≠ 深圳北`, and `深圳站 ≠ 罗湖口岸`.
- The relationship between a Shenzhen railway/checkpoint anchor and its different Hong Kong endpoint:
  - high-speed rail → Hong Kong West Kowloon;
  - Futian Checkpoint → Lok Ma Chau/East Rail;
  - Luohu Checkpoint → Lo Wu/East Rail.
- Luggage, early/late and separately ticketed-connection tests that can change the hub decision.
- Wrong-airport, wrong-railway-station and wrong-facility-name first response, including the handoff for a wrong checkpoint.
- A first-time traveller checklist that stops at identifying and reaching the correct Shenzhen passenger facility.

## This page does not own

- The complete Shenzhen–Hong Kong route choice, port ranking, border eligibility, immigration/customs procedure, crossing hours, queue forecast, Hong Kong last mile or wrong-port cross-border recovery.
- The choice of Futian, Luohu or Nanshan as an overnight base, property selection, hotel registration or room verification.
- China's national railway booking, passport, real-name, station-entry, security, boarding, seat-class, change/refund or missed-train workflow.
- A permanent train timetable, direction-to-station rule, flight/airline/terminal list, fare table, taxi duration, metro first/last time or checkpoint operating window.
- An exhaustive directory of Shenzhen's passenger stations, airport-area rail nodes, coach stations, ferry terminals or border checkpoints.
- A city × port, hotel × station or route × checkpoint permutation page.
- The Guangzhou–Shenzhen–Hong Kong regional sequence.

## Editorial boundary

- The page may name Futian and Luohu checkpoints only to prevent facility confusion and explain the Hong Kong-side handoff.
- It must not turn those identity notes into a second cross-border route guide.
- Dynamic services, operating hours, train allocations and terminals remain dated or traveller-verified; the durable decision method remains complete without them.
- A real Shenzhen North photograph identifies that station only. It cannot represent SZX, Futian Railway Station, Shenzhen Railway Station, a checkpoint or any current service condition.
