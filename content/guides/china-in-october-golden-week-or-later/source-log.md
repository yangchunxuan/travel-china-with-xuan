## Search-intent check

Google result pages were reviewed before drafting for:

- `China in October Golden Week or later travel`
- `China Golden Week train tickets hotels attraction reservations travel questions`

Current results and user-generated result snippets repeatedly asked:

- Is the end of October a good time to visit China?
- Is October a Golden Week in China?
- When should travellers avoid China in 2026?
- Is Beijing good in October?
- How should a traveller handle Golden Week when the international flight is already booked?
- How early can train tickets be bought, and what happens if the preferred train is unavailable?

The result set confirmed the working-title premise. It also exposed a content gap: many pages describe later October as uniformly cool, dry, quiet and cheaper, or describe every train, hotel and attraction as sold out. The article therefore uses an operational comparison and six-destination climate boundaries rather than those blanket claims. Search results and traveller discussions informed question selection only; they are not used as authority for holiday dates, ticket rules, attraction operations or weather facts.

## Claim layers used in the article

### 2026 facts

- Mid-Autumn Festival: September 25–27, 2026.
- National Day holiday: October 1–7, 2026.
- Make-up working days: September 20 and October 10, 2026.
- September 28–30 are ordinary working days, not an official combined holiday.

Primary source: State Council holiday notice, `https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202511/content_7047091.htm`.

### Current operator rules checked on 2026-08-10

- For most trains, China Railway's standard advance-sale period is 15 days including the travel day; station release times differ.
- Since July 20, 2026, more than 200 trains on the Beijing–Shanghai High-Speed Railway and Beijing–Shanghai Railway have been included in a ticket-reservation pilot. Covered trains shown in the 12306 app accept requests 17–60 days before departure, and the system attempts fulfillment 16–20 days before departure.
- A pilot reservation request is not a confirmed ticket. A fulfilled request must be paid by 23:00 on the fulfillment day or it is cancelled automatically. During the July 20–August 29 transition, the service accepts requests for travel on September 15 and later; from August 30, the regular 17–60-day request range applies.
- A 12306 waitlist request is not a confirmed ticket.
- Foreign-passport users should complete identity verification before the sales window and carry the original booking document.
- The Palace Museum normally opens tickets at 20:00 seven days before a visit and sells no same-day tickets.
- Badaling currently lists both real-name advance booking and on-site sales.
- The Emperor Qinshihuang's Mausoleum Site Museum requires real-name advance reservations, including for foreign visitors.
- Shanghai Museum rules currently differ by building, space and exhibition.

Pilot source: China Railway notice republished by the Beijing Municipal Government, `https://english.beijing.gov.cn/travellinginbeijing/transportation/railway/202607/t20260720_4772390.html`.

These are not permanent rules. Recheck official National Day notices and live booking pages when each window approaches.

### Long-term patterns

- Beijing has a short autumn and can be much cooler by late October.
- Shanghai is often near its seasonal transition into autumn around Golden Week.
- Xi'an and Chengdu cannot be described with Beijing's dry-autumn shorthand; autumn rain remains possible.
- Guilin is moving into a seasonally drier period, but cool-air changes and rain remain possible.
- Zhangjiajie's mountain conditions differ from its city conditions; visibility and foliage cannot be promised.

These climate statements are not a forecast for particular dates in October 2026.

### Homeground planning judgements

- Mid-to-late October is normally the lower-friction option for a first multi-city route.
- A fixed Golden Week arrival is workable when the traveller uses one base and moves between cities after the official holiday.
- Cancellable hotel terms, fewer chained bookings and one reservation-dependent anchor per day reduce route fragility.
- Later October improves flexibility but does not guarantee lower prices, empty sights or better weather.
