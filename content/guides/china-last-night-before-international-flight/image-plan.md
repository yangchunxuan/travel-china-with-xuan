# Image plan

Status: **COPY DRAFT — EDITORIAL REVIEW AND ASSETS NEEDED**

Use only real, licensed photographs with a traceable source and permission record. Do not generate airports, travellers, trains or flight-information displays with AI. Do not show a readable passport, boarding pass, baggage tag, QR code, booking reference or phone screen. Obtain appropriate releases for any clearly identifiable traveller, especially a child.

## Required hero

- File: `public/images/guides/china-last-night-before-international-flight/hero-1600.webp`
- Size: 1600 × 1000 px, WebP, landscape.
- Scene: international travellers with ordinary luggage checking a real departure board or wayfinding sign at a mainland China airport. The airport should be verifiable from the licence record or source metadata.
- Editorial purpose: show a final-departure decision, not a generic airport portrait or an endorsement of one airline.
- Composition: include travellers, luggage and enough terminal context to communicate international departure; preserve quiet space for responsive crops without depending on readable flight data.
- Preferred sources: an official mainland China airport or airline media library that grants reuse, a commissioned photographer, or a licensed editorial library with model-release information.
- Avoid: staged missed-flight distress, empty luxury lounges, private-jet imagery, a Hong Kong-only scene, obsolete health-control signage, prominent airline branding, and screens whose city, flight number or time could be mistaken for current advice.

## Graphic treatment

Do not publish a sample route or a city-by-city final-day timetable. If editorial wants a graphic, use a neutral backward-planning worksheet: international departure → airline's own check-in/bag-drop deadline → security and exit formalities → airport access → domestic arrival → disruption margin → last workable fallback. Label every time field as traveller-supplied or carrier-verified rather than a universal recommendation.
