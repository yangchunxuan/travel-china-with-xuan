Reviewed **2026-08-22**. Search volume and commercial probability are unavailable and were not guessed.

- [UNESCO — Yungang Grottoes](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1039/): supports the Datong-area cave complex and its fifth/sixth-century artistic context.
- [UNESCO — Longmen Grottoes](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1003/): supports the Luoyang-area cave complex, inscriptions and long patronage record.
- [UNESCO — Mount Wutai](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1279/): supports a sacred mountain landscape with temples and continuing Buddhist significance.
- [UNESCO — Mount Emei Scenic Area, including Leshan Giant Buddha](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/779/): supports the relationship between Buddhist heritage and mountain/river landscape.
- [Commons hero record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:27333-Luoyang,_Longmen_Grottoes.jpg): exact Longmen scene, creator and licence.
