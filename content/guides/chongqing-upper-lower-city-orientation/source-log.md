## Evidence

- Chongqing government sources identify the Kaixuan Road public lift as China's first urban vertical public-transport facility, record the Crown Escalator's 52.5-metre rise and include it among the city's distinctive public-transport modes. Separate official material supports the Li Zi Ba integrated design and community-design context.
- Independent urban research supports the need to measure walking in a three-dimensional street environment.
- Current openings are dynamic. The article makes no permanent operating-hours or fare promise.
- A 13 June 2026 official notice reports phased closures of Li Zi Ba's concourse, platform and Exits 1 and 2 during works expected to finish on 30 December 2026. Another June closure example and the current Chongqing Rail Transit service page support checking every exit and mechanical link on the day. One lift never proves an end-to-end step-free route.
