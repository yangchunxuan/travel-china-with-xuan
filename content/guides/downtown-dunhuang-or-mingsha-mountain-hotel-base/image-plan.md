## Truthfulness boundary

- a hotel, camp, glamping site, reception, road, entrance gate or room location;
- a view from any bookable room or property;
- current weather, sunset, sand condition, accessibility or crowding;
- current scenic-area opening, gate, ticketing, event or late-return transport;
- a property's foreign-guest acceptance, legal accommodation status, registration process, room availability, price, luggage handling or pickup;
