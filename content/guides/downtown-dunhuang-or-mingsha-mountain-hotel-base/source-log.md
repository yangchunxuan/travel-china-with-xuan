- Search-result and booking-platform prices, inventory, ratings, discount labels and “free cancellation” badges are excluded.

## Privacy boundary for any consultation CTA

The initial general form may collect only minimum non-sensitive information: dates, public base/property names, traveller and room counts, a confirmed slot or arrival/departure direction, and a broad budget band. A traveller may request a later family or accessibility discussion. They must not submit children's exact ages, mobility/medical/accessibility details, passport or ID data, booking/payment records, or identifiable supplier-sharing information in the initial form. Sensitive details belong only in an appropriate human follow-up; supplier sharing requires separate consent.

The CTA is not live inventory, booking or transport. It does not guarantee price, availability, foreign-guest acceptance, room type, accessibility, property identity or view, late reception/check-in, Mogao/Mingsha admission or opening, luggage handling, transfer, connection success or safety.
