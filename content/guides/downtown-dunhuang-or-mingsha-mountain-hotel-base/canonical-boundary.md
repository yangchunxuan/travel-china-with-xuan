# Canonical boundary

## Primary intent

Help a traveller who has already chosen Dunhuang and already knows the number of nights decide whether to keep one hotel base in downtown Dunhuang, use the Mingsha Mountain entrance area, choose a deliberately remote desert property, or accept the cost of a split stay. The decision begins with a confirmed Mogao Caves reporting slot and then tests the Mingsha evening, airport or railway handoff, luggage custody and named-property evidence.

The canonical task is therefore: **where should this traveller sleep so that a confirmed Mogao appointment, a conditional Mingsha evening and the arrival/departure chain can all work with the fewest unsupported handoffs?**

## This owner does not own

- a generic Dunhuang travel guide, a decision about whether Dunhuang is worth visiting, or how many days the city needs;
- the Mogao Caves booking, foreign-passport ticketing, visit mode, cave sequence or visit-day execution workflow;
- current Mingsha Mountain–Crescent Spring gates, opening hours, ticket prices, events or transport schedules;
- Xi'an–Lanzhou–Dunhuang route order, train selection, flight selection or a comparison of intercity gateways;
- a general decision between private transfer and public transport;
- hotel, camp or glamping rankings, a “best desert hotel” list, fixed room prices, ratings, inventory or an implied endorsement;
- the national legal explanation and full recovery workflow for foreign-passport hotel registration or refusal;
- a claim that “near Mogao,” “near Mingsha,” “desert,” an OTA pin or a view photograph proves the relevant first checkpoint, property identity, room view or usable route.

## Search intent and cannibalization decision

The allowed query cluster is narrow: `Dunhuang downtown or Mingsha Mountain where to stay`, `hotel near Mogao Caves Digital Exhibition Center`, and a Mogao-slot/Mingsha-evening hotel-base decision. The title and opening answer must keep the comparison and confirmed-slot frame visible.

Search results that merely list hotels near Mogao Caves often rank by distance to the protected cave-area pin, even though the official visitor chain begins at the Mogao Caves Digital Exhibition Center. Correcting that first-stop error is part of this owner's information gain; teaching the entire Mogao procedure is not. Likewise, a Mingsha-side stay is evaluated through the exact gate, road, reception and fallback chain, not through scenic atmosphere, price or rankings.

## Editorial decision boundary

“Downtown is the safer default” is a conditional editorial judgment when the fixed Mogao slot, city evening, luggage and onward transport must work together. “Mingsha entrance area can win” is conditional on repeated desert use and written proof of the exact property, relevant gate, road, staffed reception, actual room type, late contact, luggage and passport check-in. Neither statement is an official operator recommendation.

No neighbourhood label creates a guarantee. The page must preserve a usable alternative, the applicable cancellation/change terms, and the conditions that reverse the decision.
