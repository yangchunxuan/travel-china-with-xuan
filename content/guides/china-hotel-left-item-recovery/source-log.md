| `LI-01` | [Measures for the Security Administration of the Hotel Industry](https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/mobile/law/detail?LawID=792) | Article 8 requires hotels to keep guest property left behind properly and try to return it or announce it; suspicious and prohibited items follow a public-security route. | The regulation does not confirm that an item was found, set a hotel's contact workflow or guarantee shipment, timing, condition or return to a claimant. |
| `LI-02` | [Civil Code of the People's Republic of China](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/233181.html) | Articles 314–317 cover notice, return, proper custody of lost property and necessary custody expenses in the national civil-law framework. | These general provisions do not decide ownership, fault, storage condition, fees or liability in an individual hotel case. |
| `LI-03` | [Rules on acceptance inspection of mail and express items](https://www.spb.gov.cn/gjyzj/c200045/201507/603f78a94f6f4b4280126eb496ae92bf.shtml) | Requires sender and item information, inspection, suitable packaging and refusal of prohibited, excessive restricted or unsafe items. | Eligibility still depends on the exact object, carrier service and destination, especially for international carriage; the page does not approve any item for shipping. |
