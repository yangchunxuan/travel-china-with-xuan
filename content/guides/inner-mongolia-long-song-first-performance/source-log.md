## Evidence levels and editorial decisions

- **Official form-level fact:** heritage status, high-level musical description and distinction from short song/Morin Khuur.
- **Organizer fact:** a named event’s announced cast, programme, date and venue, valid only for the cited edition and subject to change.
- **Performance observation:** what is heard in a specific rendition; it should not be generalized into a rule for every region or singer.
- **Homeground editorial judgment:** prefer a traceable programme naming the form plus singer or song; accept a transparent mixed show as an introduction, not a dedicated recital.
- **Cannot infer:** credibility from clothing, horses, outdoor scenery, amplification, a Morin Khuur image or the promotional word “heritage” alone.
