# Research and source log

Reviewed: 2026-08-13 (Asia/Shanghai).

## Evidence decisions

- UNESCO establishes a serial World Heritage property and its component boundaries; it does not establish public access at every protected component.
- Current 2026 Yancheng government pages confirm Tiaozini, Chinese Milu Park and Red-crowned Crane Wetland Ecotourism Area as distinct public tourism/education products.
- The official wetland portal and Yancheng regulation establish the conservation frame and prohibited wildlife disturbance.
- Seasonal reports document breeding or migration conditions, not a guaranteed visitor sighting. No population number, flock size or visitor count is used as a planning promise.
- Homeground judgment: choose one base plus one public component; treat the museum as orientation and prepare a complete no-sighting day.
