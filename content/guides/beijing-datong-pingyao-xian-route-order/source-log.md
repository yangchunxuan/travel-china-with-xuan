## Editorial synthesis

- Datong and Pingyao are not substitutes: one primarily earns a base through Buddhist cave art/Northern Wei context, the other through historic urban fabric and commercial history.
- Both optional bases survive only when each retains a protected core visit after all hotel-to-hotel legs are verified.
- A one-night base that contains only check-in, sleep and checkout fails the route decision.
- Beijing → Datong → Pingyao → Xi'an and the reverse are hypotheses to test in 12306, not stored timetables.
