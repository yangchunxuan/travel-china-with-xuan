# Canonical boundary

Owns: Tianyi Pavilion as a private-library system—architecture, fire risk, access rules, family continuity, cataloguing and museum transformation.

Excludes: history of every Chinese library, rare-book scholarship guide, Ningbo itinerary, public reading-room services and ticket logistics.

Independent-task test: the visitor task is to understand why a library was restrictive, not to find open shelves; this requires more than a city-hub recommendation.
