## Result gap and Homeground information gain

- Answer with the water-route chain: catchment → inlet → storage/infiltration → overflow → downstream link.
- Explain why overflow is evidence of finite design capacity, not a failed `sponge`.
- Keep the 70% statement labelled as a 2015 policy objective, never a nationwide achieved result.
- Make prior wetness, soil/groundwater, maintenance and downstream drainage visible constraints.
- Give a safe observation boundary and tell readers not to enter basins or visit during active warnings.
- Use Jinxi Park only as an intended-feature example, not current performance certification.
