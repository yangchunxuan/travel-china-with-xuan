## Evidence and exact use

1. UNESCO, [Old Town of Lijiang](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/811/), read in the owner's Chrome: inscribed 1997; Lijiang plain at 2,400 m; three parts (Dayan Old Town including Black Dragon Pond, Baisha and Shuhe housing clusters); Dayan established in the Ming dynasty as a commercial centre; water from Yulong Snow Mount to Heilong Pool to canals, "still functions effectively today"; two-storeyed, tile-roofed, timber-framed houses combining Han and Zang elements, "representative of the Naxi culture"; Baisha from the Song and Yuan dynasties, 8 km north, with over 40 paintings on Buddhist, Taoist and Naxi subjects; Shuhe 4 km north-west.
